## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Building-side requirements for **liftwells, pits, machine and pulley rooms, service lifts** and **escalator** pits and machine spaces, plus the **warning notices**.
    - **Table 1** gives minimum well, entrance, pit, headroom and machine room sizes by **rated load** (630–1,600 kg) and **speed**. Smaller sizes are allowed if a **registered lift engineer** confirms in writing that the lift fits and can be safely maintained.
    - **Machine-room-less** lifts: the machine room and headroom rules do not apply if the model is approved by the **DEMS**; show it on the GBP.
    - **Pit access door** where the pit is deeper than **2 m** (and where practicable above 1.6 m): at least **1.4 × 0.6 m**, with a bilingual warning notice.
    - **Machine room access** from common areas, at least **2 m** high. Stairs where the level change exceeds **0.6 m**: width **≥ 900 mm**, tread **≥ 225 mm**, riser **≤ 175 mm**, **≤ 16** risers a flight, handrails both sides, barriers **1.1 m**.

=== "中文"

    - 建築物方面的要求：**升降機槽、機坑、機房及滑輪房、送貨升降機**，以及**自動梯**機坑及機房，另有**警告告示**。
    - **表 1** 按**額定載重**（630–1,600 公斤）及**速度**列出升降機槽、入口、機坑、頂部空間及機房的最小尺寸；如**註冊升降機工程師**書面確認可容納並可安全保養，可採用較小尺寸。
    - **無機房升降機**：型號經**機電工程署署長**批准，則機房及頂部空間要求不適用；須在圖則上註明。
    - 機坑深度超過 **2 米**（1.6 米以上在可行時亦須）須設**機坑出入門**：最少 **1.4 × 0.6 米**，並設中英文警告告示。
    - **機房通道**須經公用地方，高度最少 **2 米**；高差超過 **0.6 米**須設樓梯：闊 **≥ 900 毫米**、梯級深 **≥ 225 毫米**、級高 **≤ 175 毫米**、每段 **≤ 16** 級、兩邊扶手、欄障 **1.1 米**。

## Minimum dimensions 最小尺寸 (Table 1, selected, 1.0 m/s)

| Rated load 載重 | Persons 人數 | Car area max. 機廂面積 | Well W × D 槽 | Entrance W × H 入口 | Pit 機坑 | Headroom 頂部空間 | Machine room 機房 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 630 kg | 8 | 1.66 m² | 1,800 × 2,100 | 800 × 2,100 | 1,700 | 4,450 | 15 m² |
| 800 kg | 10 | 2.0 m² | 1,900 × 2,300 | 800 × 2,100 | 1,700 | 4,450 | 15 m² |
| 1,000 kg | 13 | 2.4 m² | 2,400 × 2,300 | 1,100 × 2,100 | 1,800 | 4,300 | 20 m² |
| 1,250 kg | 16 | 2.9 m² | 2,600 × 2,300 | 1,100 × 2,100 | 1,900 | 4,400 | 22 m² |
| 1,600 kg | 21 | 3.56 m² | 2,600 × 2,600 | 1,100 × 2,100 | 1,900 | 4,800 | 25 m² |

<small>Dimensions in mm. Faster lifts need deeper pits and more headroom (e.g. 1,000 kg at 3.5 m/s: pit 3,650, headroom 6,300). Interpolate between values. Above 1,600 kg add 0.16 m² car area and 0.6 m² machine room per 100 kg. 速度越高機坑及頂部空間越大；可內插。</small>

## Key requirements 主要規定

| Item 項目 | Requirement 要求 | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Liftwell enclosure 升降機槽 | Imperforate walls, floor and ceiling to [FS Code Part C](fsc-part-c-fire-resisting-construction.md); used only for the lift | 3.1, 3.4, 3.9 |
| Inspection / emergency doors 檢查門／緊急門 | Only for safety or servicing; inspection ≥ 1.4 × 0.6 m; emergency ≥ **1.8 × 0.5 m**; traps ≤ 0.5 × 0.5 m; bilingual notice, letters **≥ 25 mm** | 3.2 |
| Space below the pit 機坑下空間 | Avoid if possible; if accessible, pit base designed for **≥ 5 kPa** plus a solid pier under the counterweight buffer or counterweight safety gear | 3.6 |
| Pit 機坑 | Smooth, level, watertight; access door if > 2 m; anchorage points for fall arrest | 3.8 |
| Machine rooms 機房 | Lift equipment only (plus service lift / escalator machines, A/C, FSI); preferably above the well; no sprinklers normally; hooks with safe working load notice; access doors ≥ 0.6 × 1.8 m; trap doors ≥ 800 × 800 mm; steps > 500 mm need stairs and barriers | 3.11–3.17 |
| Service lifts 送貨升降機 | Similar rules; pit needed only where the lowest level served is at or near floor level | 4 |
| Escalators 自動梯 | Machine and return stations, steps, landings, clearances to obstructions | 5 |
