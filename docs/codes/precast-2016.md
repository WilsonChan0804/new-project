## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Design, production, handling, erection and quality control of **precast concrete**: façades, slabs, staircases, **volumetric** units, semi-precast slabs and connections. For MiC also see [JPN 8](../pnap/jpn-8.md).
    - **Temporary stages** govern many elements. Apply equivalent load factors to self weight: **demoulding 1.2–1.7**; yard handling **1.2**, **transport 1.5**, erection 1.2.
    - **Lifting inserts**: proprietary products, factor of safety **4** (**5** for repeated use), tested to ultimate load. Bracing members FoS **2**, connections and inserts **3**.
    - **Notional horizontal load** for stability: at least **1.5 %** of the characteristic dead load.
    - **Joints and connections** must give equivalent monolithic behaviour where assumed. The Jun 2026 amendment adds **testing of completed panel joints and window joints** (water-tightness).

=== "中文"

    - **預製混凝土**的設計、生產、搬運、安裝及品質控制：外牆、樓板、樓梯、**立體組件**、半預製樓板及接駁。組裝合成另見 [JPN 8](../pnap/jpn-8.md)。
    - 很多構件由**臨時階段**控制：自重乘以等效荷載系數，**脫模 1.2–1.7**；場內搬運 **1.2**、**運輸 1.5**、安裝 1.2。
    - **吊裝預埋件**：須為專利產品，安全系數 **4**（重複使用 **5**），並測試至極限荷載；支撐構件 **2**、支撐接駁及預埋件 **3**。
    - 穩定性**名義水平荷載**：不少於特徵恒載的 **1.5%**。
    - 假設等同整體澆築時，**接縫及接駁**須達到該表現；2026 年 6 月修訂加入**已完成外牆接縫及窗縫測試**（防水）。

## Load factors for temporary stages 臨時階段荷載系數 {#load-factors-for-temporary-stages}

| Demoulding 脫模 (Table 2.1) | Exposed aggregate 露石 | Smooth mould 光面模 |
|---|---|---|
| Flat, removable side forms, no rebates 平板無凹位 | 1.2 | 1.3 |
| Flat with rebates or reveals 有凹位 | 1.3 | 1.4 |
| Fluted with proper draft 坑紋 | 1.4 | 1.6 |
| Sculptured 雕塑形 | 1.5 | 1.7 |

| Stage 階段 (Table 2.2) | Factor 系數 |
|---|---|
| Yard handling 場內搬運 | 1.2 |
| **Transportation** 運輸 | **1.5** |
| Erection 安裝 | 1.2 |

| Item 項目 (Table 2.4) | Factor of safety 安全系數 |
|---|---|
| Bracing members 支撐構件 | 2 |
| Bracing connections; inserts cast into members 支撐接駁、預埋件 | 3 |
| **Lifting inserts**, normal 吊裝預埋件 | **4** |
| Lifting inserts, multiple use 重複使用 | 5 |

<small>Factors apply to flexural design of the element. Arrange inserts so the failure of one does not drop the element. 系數用於構件抗彎設計；預埋件佈置須確保單一失效不致整件墮下。</small>
