## Code of Practice for Oil Storage Installations 1992 貯油裝置作業守則 {#code-of-practice-for-oil-storage-installations-1992}

=== "English"

    - Design, construction, operation and maintenance of **petroleum storage tanks**, bunds, drainage, interceptors and pipelines.
    - **Safety distances** between tanks and to the boundary (Table 1); at least **10 m** from the outer boundary of the facility. Separate rules for installations under **7,000 m³**.
    - **Bund capacity**: at least **105 %** of the largest tank for **Class 1** products, **100 %** for Classes 2 and 3; drainage sized for the largest tank releasing its contents in **15 minutes**.
    - Foundations (granular pads, ring walls), tank bearing pads, corrosion protection, **leak detection**, electrical safety, and **external and internal inspections**, repairs and water tests.
    - **Marine pollution equipment** (booms, dispersant, skimmers, sorbents) and site security.

=== "中文"

    - **石油貯存缸**、圍堤、排水、截油器及管道的設計、建造、運作及保養。
    - 貯存缸之間及與邊界的**安全距離**（表 1）；與設施外邊界最少 **10 米**；總容量少於 **7,000 立方米**另有規定。
    - **圍堤容量**：**第 1 類**油品不少於最大油缸的 **105%**，第 2 及 3 類 **100%**；排水按最大油缸於 **15 分鐘**內全數洩出設計。
    - 地基（碎石墊層、環牆）、承墊、防腐、**滲漏探測**、電力安全，以及**內外檢查**、維修及注水測試。
    - **海上污染設備**（攔油索、分散劑、撇油器、吸油物料）及保安。

## Guide to Fire Safety Design for Caverns 1994 岩洞消防安全設計指引 {#guide-to-fire-safety-design-for-caverns-1994}

=== "English"

    - Only for **rock caverns used by public utilities** (sewage works, refuse transfer, service reservoirs) with a **low, trained population**, **low and controlled fire load**, **no sleeping risk**, and good management. Oil, gas and dangerous goods are **not covered**.
    - **Fire resistance**: generally **1–2 hours** depending on the escape route; **4 hours** where justified, e.g. for the place of safe passage and firefighting routes.
    - **Means of escape**: limit horizontal travel (up to **750 m** may be accepted in total); a **1 m** footway where the safe route is a roadway; special design where upward travel exceeds **10 m**.
    - Also covers detection and warning, sprinklers and fire service installations, compartmentation, and **smoke management**.

=== "中文"

    - 只適用於**公共設施岩洞**（污水處理、垃圾轉運、配水庫），且**人數少並熟悉環境**、**火警負荷低及受控**、**無睡眠風險**及有完善管理；油、氣及危險品**不包括在內**。
    - **耐火時間**：一般按逃生路線 **1–2 小時**；安全通道及消防路線等合理情況 **4 小時**。
    - **逃生途徑**：限制水平距離（總距離最多可接受 **750 米**）；以車路作安全通道須另設 **1 米**行人路；向上行超過 **10 米**須特別設計。
    - 另涵蓋火警偵測及警報、花灑及消防裝置、防火分隔及**煙霧管理**。
