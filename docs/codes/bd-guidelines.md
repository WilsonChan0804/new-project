## Bamboo scaffolds 竹棚架設計及建造指引 {#bamboo-scaffolds}

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/GDCBS.pdf) (first issued May 2006, revised Apr 2025) |

=== "English"

    - Materials, general forms, steel brackets, design, supervision, erection, maintenance, inspection and dismantling. Use with the Labour Department guidelines.
    - Double-layer scaffolds: **putlogs** at **3 m** horizontally, and **6.3 m** vertically below 100 m height (**4.2 m** at 100 m or more).
    - **Catch-fans** every **15 m** vertically (every **10 m** for demolition), projecting at least **1.5 m**, covered with zinc sheet.
    - Scaffolds over **15 m** high must be designed by a professional engineer. **Truss-out** scaffolds: at most **6 m** high.

=== "中文"

    - 物料、一般形式、鋼托架、設計、監督、搭建、保養、檢查及拆卸；須與勞工處指引一併使用。
    - 雙排竹棚**牽引**：水平 **3 米**；高度 100 米以下垂直 **6.3 米**（100 米或以上 **4.2 米**）。
    - **防墮板**每 **15 米**一層（拆卸工程每 **10 米**），伸出最少 **1.5 米**並鋪鋅鐵片。
    - 高度超過 **15 米**的竹棚須由專業工程師設計；**挑棚**高度不超過 **6 米**。

### Prevention of water seepage in new buildings (Mar 2005) 預防新建樓宇滲水指引 {#water-seepage}

!!! danger "Superseded for new submissions 已被取代"
    For new GBP, major revisions and A&A **submitted after 31 Mar 2025**, use the **enhanced guidelines in [APP-105 Appendix B](../pnap/app-105.md#paragraph-detail)** (Sep 2024). Key changes: waterproofing round bathtubs / showers **≥ 2 200 mm** (was 1 800 mm); behind basins / sinks / countertops **≥ 1 100 mm** or 300 mm above the fixture; whole kitchen floor incl. **open kitchens**; **sunken slab** rules; pipe-penetration membrane details; balconies and utility platforms treated as roofs; mock-up water tests for precast façades and curtain walls; RC roof guidance (C35 waterproof concrete, 150 mm slabs). The 2005 text below is kept for reference only. 2025 年 3 月 31 日後的呈交請用 APP-105 附錄 B；以下 2005 年內容只供參考。

[PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/GWS.pdf) · 6 pages · **2005**, superseded by APP-105 App. B. 2005 年版，已被取代。

| Area 位置 | Design 設計 (paras.) | Workmanship and testing 施工及測試 |
|---|---|---|
| **Roof / flat roof** 天台 | Design as a **water-retaining structure**; monolithic concrete kicker at perimeter (or full-height parapet); adequate **falls** and outlets; **overflows** for blocked outlets; expansion joints with monolithic kickers, flashings / copings and back-up drip trays; plant on dampers with membrane applied to plinth first; avoid conduits embedded in roof slab (paras. 2–3) | Surface prepared, dry and clean; membrane to manufacturer's spec, attention to joints, upturns, downturns, penetrations (4); supervision and **ponding test** (5) |
| **External wall** 外牆 | Waterproof junctions between adjoining buildings; precast panels: **slotted junction with water bar** and monolithic kicker (6) | Proper slump, pumping, compaction; tie holes grouted with waterproof mortar; water bars at expansion joints; key and additives for finishes (7); TCP supervision of formwork; tests after finishes; see Guidelines on External Finishes (Nov 2002) (8) |
| **Window** 窗 | Window hood with fall; **water bar under frame**; trimming bars at opening corners against diagonal cracks; frame joints, gaskets, sealants (9) | Avoid oversized openings (accurate or metal formwork); gap filled with **non-shrink waterproof grout**; finishes lapped and sealed (10); supervise lugs and anchors; **field water test** (11) |
| **Bathroom** 浴室 | Waterproofing on floor and walls: **≥ 300 mm** up every wall, **≥ 1800 mm** round bathtub and shower; concrete kicker under block walls; galvanised bars; floor drains to minimise falls; access under bathtub; under-tray for non-proprietary shower trays (12) | Sealant round bath and basin; protective waterproof plaster; tile joints; cement-based waterproofing under tiles; decorations must not damage the system (13) |
| **Kitchen** 廚房 | Waterproofing on floor and walls; monolithic worktop with splashback; **exposed** water and drain pipes (14) | Clean dry surfaces; gaskets and sealants at taps (15) |
| **Plumbing and drainage pipes** 水管及排水管 | Durable pressure-rated materials (16); brackets, pressure-reducing valves against water hammer; expansion collars; pipes grouped in ducts; **not embedded** in party walls (refers to old PNAP 211) (17) | Rust-free sleeves sealed and fire-stopped; careful jointing (18); competent plumber, TCP monitoring, water-tightness test (19) |
| **Car park floor** 停車場樓面 | Crack control (low w/c ratio, waterproof cement, reinforcement, joints); wheel loads as dynamic loads; proprietary coating; **car-wash area** with falls and channels; structural falls; **intercepting channels** at top and bottom of ramps; water-stops at movement joints, not at low points (20) | Follow concreting specs (21); ponding tests in wet areas (22) |
| **Basement** 地庫 | Materials suited to site (23); design as **water-retaining structure** (except pile caps, thick footings, diaphragm walls) with diversion; check construction sequence; water stops at construction joints and temporary supports; **cavity wall with channels**; hardcore / filter to relieve pressure (24) | External tanking maintenance-free; **water test before backfilling** (25); no free-dropping of concrete; vibrator access (26) |

### Maintenance and repair of drainage systems and sanitary fitments 排水系統及衞生設備保養及維修指引 {#drainage-maintenance}

[PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/Drainage-System-Guideline-Eng.PDF) · 1-page owners' leaflet. 業主單張。

| Item 項目 | Advice 建議 |
|---|---|
| **Drain pipes** 排水管 | Soil, waste, vent and underground pipes kept in good order; inspect regularly; rectify leaks, blockages and defects **immediately**. 定期檢查，即時修理。 |
| **Blockage** 淤塞 | Minor: high-pressure water jet or rodding; serious (e.g. cement): expose and replace the section. 輕微以高壓水或通渠；嚴重須更換。 |
| **Manholes** 沙井 | Keep accessible (no finishes, planters or furniture over them); stop foul air with **double-seal covers** or by repairing edges and cracks. 保持可達；用雙密封蓋。 |
| **Vent and dry pipes** 通風管 | Close inspection needed since leaks do not show as water; check all branches. 須近距離檢查。 |
| **Sanitary fitments** 衞生設備 | WCs, baths, basins, shower trays, sinks, floor drains: inspect **preferably every 3 months**; repair or replace leaking fitments and their pipes; clean per FEHD advice; investigate foul smells at once. 最好每三個月檢查一次。 |
| **Professional help** 專業協助 | Serious or unclear problems: appoint an **AP**; BD hotline **2626 1616**. 委任認可人士。 |

### Erection and maintenance of advertising signs 招牌豎設及保養指引 {#advertising-signs}

[PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/Ad_Signs_E.pdf) · 7 pages · advice from BD and other departments. 屋宇署及其他部門的建議。

**General (§2)**: get consent from owners / OC and the electricity supplier; check lease conditions; inspect and maintain regularly; owners may be liable for damage or injury; no obscene content. 取得業主／法團及電力公司同意；查核地契；定期保養。

!!! warning "This old guideline differs from the current statutory requirements 舊指引與現行規定不同"
    For signboards needing approval, the **current** rules are in **[APP-126](../pnap/app-126.md#paragraph-detail)**: e.g. clearance over a footpath **3.5 m** (**2.5 m** if projecting ≤ 600 mm), **5.8 m** within 1 m of the kerb or over a carriageway, no part within **1.5 m** of the street centre line, **≥ 1.2 m** from the common lot boundary, projecting signboards ≤ **4.2 m** and within a **40 m² × 600 mm** prism, fireman's switch **2.5–3 m** above ground. Use APP-126, not the figures below. 請以 APP-126 為準。

**Buildings Department (§3)**

| Topic 主題 | Requirement 要求 |
|---|---|
| Control 管制 (3.1) | A sign fixed to a building is normally "building works" under the BO; dangerous signs removed under **BO s.26(4)** or **PH&MSO s.105**; nothing over a street unless exempted. 屬建築工程；危險招牌可依條例移除。 |
| Appointments 委任 (3.2) | BA approval and consent where it is building works (now often minor works); **AP** to co-ordinate; **registered contractor** to erect. 委任認可人士及註冊承建商。 |
| Projection 伸出 (3.3.1) | Must not obstruct escape routes, stairs or service lanes, or required lighting / ventilation / open air; projection **≤ 4.2 m** from the main building line and not beyond the **street centre line**. 伸出不多於 4.2 米，不超過街道中線。 |
| Clearance 淨空 (3.3.2) | Over a pavement **≥ 3.5 m** high and **≥ 1 m** from the kerb; over a street **≥ 5.8 m**; over a tramway **≥ 7 m**. 行人路 3.5 米；街道 5.8 米；電車路 7 米。 |
| Spacing 間距 (3.3.3–4) | Adjacent signs **≥ 2.4 m** apart laterally; signs from opposite sides **≥ 3 m** apart. 相鄰 2.4 米；對面 3 米。 |
| Materials and structure 物料及結構 (3.4) | Durable, fire-resisting, rust-proofed; **≥ 2 support points**; not supported by another sign; **≥ 2 coats** of paint on steel; avoid electrolytic reaction (no steel screws into aluminium). 最少兩個支承點及兩層防銹漆。 |
| Other 其他 (3.5) | Display owner's details; repair defects; **remove when no longer needed**. 展示擁有人資料；不用時拆除。 |

**Other departments**

| Dept. 部門 | Advice 建議 |
|---|---|
| **EMSD** (§4) | Electricity (Wiring) Regulations (Cap. 406) and its CoP; installed by a **registered electrical contractor / worker**; labelled switches; ancillary gear in earthed metal or fire-resisting enclosure; HV parts out of reach. **Fireman's switch**: conspicuous, **≤ 3 m** above ground, below the sign (or notice showing it); push up OFF / down ON; on a white board about **300 × 250 mm** with **50 mm red border**, "NEON SIGN-FIREMAN'S SWITCH" in English and Chinese. 消防員開關不高於地面 3 米。 |
| **CAD** (§5) | No sign above airport height limits (Cap. 301). 不得超過機場高度限制。 |
| **AFCD** (§6) | Written permit for signs in country parks (Country Parks and Special Areas Regs reg. 10(1)(a)); permanent commercial signs generally not allowed; application with purpose, sketch, location plan, copy to the District Lands Office. 郊野公園須許可。 |
| **Tobacco / medical** (§7–8) | Health warnings (Cap. 371); Undesirable Medical Advertisements Ordinance (Cap. 231) ss. 3–4. 煙草健康警告；不良醫藥廣告。 |
| **TD** (§9) | Not on expressways or high-speed roads, junctions, crossings or curves; not confusable with traffic signs; must not obstruct traffic signs, lights, cameras or the Central–Mid-Levels escalator; consult TD where visible to drivers. 諮詢運輸署。 |
| **Marine** (§10) | Lights must not interfere with signal stations, navigation aids or vessel navigation; **no occulting flashing lights** on roof signs visible from the sea. 不得干擾航行。 |

### Greening in buildings 樓宇綠化指引 {#greening}

[PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/IGG_e.pdf) · 12-page illustrated guide. Building safety and structural integrity are the **prime consideration**. 樓宇安全及結構完整為首要考慮。

| Stage 階段 | Key points 要點 |
|---|---|
| **Approval needed?** 是否需要批准 | **No** if no new building works (no new structure, strengthening or drainage change). **Yes** if greening involves new building works, or in an existing building significantly affects load capacity, drainage or fire safety, or comes with a change in use. 涉及新建築工程或嚴重影響荷載、排水、消防時需要。 |
| **Scale** 規模 | Heavier, larger, permanent, extensive features (roof farming, garden features) matter more than light, movable, local ones (potted plants), especially on **long-span** or **cantilevered** structures. 重型及大範圍者影響較大。 |
| **Professionals** 專業人士 | Consult **AP / RSE** on feasibility and approval (Table 1); a **Registered Landscape Architect** for soil and plants. 諮詢認可人士／結構工程師及園境師。 |
| **Structural check** 結構核算 | Load depends on soil, plants and use; AP / RSE check record drawings and survey the structure. Special concern: (i) **inaccessible roofs** (designed for maintenance load only), (ii) long-span roofs / podiums, (iii) cantilevers, (iv) **water ponds** (heavy, cause seepage). 不可到達的天台只按維修荷載設計。 |
| **Drainage and waterproofing** 排水及防水 | Extra drainage if needed; discharge heavy rain; **surface fall ≥ 1:80**; inspect drainage, irrigation, waterproofing regularly; waterproofing sound before greening. 坡度不少於 1:80。 |
| **Fire safety** 消防 | Greenery must not reduce or obstruct a **refuge floor**. 不得阻礙庇護層。 |
| **Access and barriers** 通道及護欄 | Safe maintenance access; **protective barriers** at outer edges. 提供維修通道及護欄。 |
| **Construction** 施工 | Qualified contractors; adequate supervision; document changes (soil type and weight, plants, moisture, irrigation); check proprietary / lightweight systems against specs; hand design and maintenance information to owners. 記錄設計參數並交予業主。 |
| **Maintenance** 保養 | Regular inspection of greenery and building (Table 2); changes in plants or growing media may exceed the design load: get professional advice. 更改植物或泥土須諮詢專業人士。 |

**Table 1 (summary)**: *New building*: appoint AP / RSE; cater for greening in design; show it on building, structural and drainage plans. *Existing building*: consult professionals; assess structure, drainage and fire safety; submit plans if A&A or strengthening is needed; appoint a registered contractor. *Existing approved greening*: keep use consistent with design. *Unapproved greening*: get assessment; rectify defects; remove at once if overloading or safety concerns. 表 1 摘要。

### Surcharge on defaulted works (Jan 2014) 違責工程附加費 {#surcharge}

[PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/IG_ISDWe.pdf) · summary of BD internal guidelines. When owners fail to comply with orders, BD's contractor (BDC) does the works under a default works consultant (DWC); BD recovers cost and supervision charges plus a surcharge **≤ 20%** under **BO s.33** (as amended from **20 July 2012**). 業主不遵從命令時，屋宇署代做並收取不多於 20% 附加費。

| Case 情況 | Surcharge 附加費 |
|---|---|
| (a) Emergency works with **no** order / notice 無命令的緊急工程 | **Nil** 0% |
| (b) Owner takes back the works before BDC starts on site and complies 承建商動工前業主收回自做並遵從 | **Nil** 0% |
| (c) Default notification letter issued **before 20 Jul 2012** (unless surcharge notified later before works start) 2012 年 7 月 20 日前發出通知 | **Nil** 0% |
| (d) Owner proves **genuine practical difficulties** (tenant refuses access, obstruction of common parts, failed attempts to organise works) 證明確有實際困難 | **10%** |
| (e) Owner **old (≥ 70)**, infirm, disabled or mentally ill **and** has genuine difficulties 年長、體弱、殘疾或精神病並有實際困難 | **Nil** 0% |
| (f) Works under **Operation Building Bright** 樓宇更新大行動 | **Nil** 0% |
| (g) All other cases 其他 | **20%** |

Proof: age at the date of service of the order; medical certificate or SWD reference; documentary evidence of difficulties. Each case on its merits. 須提交證明文件。

### Prioritisation of actionable UBW on rooftops, flat roofs, yards, lanes and basements (Apr 2016) 天台、平台、天井、巷及地庫違建執法優次 {#tmb}

[PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/dev0620cb1-2487-1-AnnexG_e.pdf) · extract of BD internal guidelines.

| Point 要點 | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| Scope 範圍 | "Actionable" UBW includes structures on **rooftops, flat roofs, yards** (incl. lightwells), **lanes** (structures on or projecting over private lanes, e.g. extensions, retractable awnings) and **basements** (extensions below the lowest storey or into backfilled areas) — "**Top, Middle, Bottom**" (TMB) — whatever their risk or age. 不論風險或新舊均屬須取締。 |
| Approach 方式 | Mainly through **large scale operations (LSOs)**; exceptions: **new TMB** (incl. works in progress) and TMB with **obvious danger**, which are acted on at once — together with other TMB at the same level. 主要以大規模行動處理；新建及明顯危險者即時處理。 |
| Queue 輪候 | TMB reports each year form that year's queue for selecting target buildings. 每年報告組成當年輪候名單。 |
| Ranking 次序 | 2011 and 2012 queues: earliest date known to BD (incl. warning notices). After 2012: **current report date only**. 2012 年後按舉報日期。 |
