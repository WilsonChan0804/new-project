## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Limit state design** of glass panes and elements: curtain walls, windows, glass walls, fins, beams, columns, **balustrades**, roofs, canopies, floors and stairs, and their connections, sealants and tests.
    - **Laminated glass** is required for elements carrying **long-term load** (roofs, canopies, skylights, sloped glazing, stairs, floors, beams, columns) and for **glass balustrades**.
    - On façades, **tempered glass** must be **laminated** where the pane is over **2.5 m²** **and** any part is **5 m or more** above the accessible floor (for an IGU, the outer pane only).
    - Roofs, accessible canopies, skylights, stairs and floors need **multi-layer** glass and **redundancy**: with one pane broken, the rest must carry the **unfactored** loads.
    - All **tempered glass** must be **heat soaked** to BS EN 14179-1 (hold at **260 ± 10 °C** for at least **2 hours**) against nickel sulphide breakage.

=== "中文"

    - 以**極限狀態**設計玻璃板及構件：幕牆、窗、玻璃牆、玻璃肋、樑、柱、**玻璃欄河**、天幕、簷篷、地台及樓梯，以及其接駁、密封膠及測試。
    - 承受**長期荷載**的構件（天幕、簷篷、天窗、斜面玻璃、樓梯、地台、樑、柱）及**玻璃欄河**須用**夾層玻璃**。
    - 外牆**鋼化玻璃**若面積超過 **2.5 平方米**，**且**任何部分離可到達樓面 **5 米或以上**，須為**夾層玻璃**（中空玻璃只限外層）。
    - 天幕、可到達簷篷、天窗、樓梯及地台須用**多層**玻璃並具**冗餘度**：任何一片破裂後，其餘須能承受**未乘系數**的荷載。
    - 所有**鋼化玻璃**須按 BS EN 14179-1 進行**均質處理**（**260 ± 10 °C** 保持最少 **2 小時**），以減低硫化鎳自爆風險。

## Deflection limits 撓度限制 (cl. 5.5.3–5.5.4) {#deflection-limits-cl-553554}

| Support 支承 | Glass pane limit 玻璃板 |
|---|---|
| Four sides 四邊支承 | Shorter span / **60** |
| Three sides 三邊支承 | min. (b/60, a/30) |
| Two sides 兩邊支承 | Supported span / 60 |
| Point supported 點支承 | Longer span between supports / 60 |
| **Cantilever** 懸臂 | Span / **30** |

| Supporting member 支承構件 | Limit 限制 |
|---|---|
| Span ≤ 7.2 m 跨度 | Smaller of span/180 and **20 mm** |
| Span > 7.2 m | Span/360 |
| Cantilever member 懸臂構件 | Smaller of span/90 and 20 mm |

## Glass balustrades 玻璃欄河 (cl. 6.4) {#glass-balustrades-cl-64}

- The **handrail and balusters** carry the handrail loads; the glass is infill and must not be relied on to support the frame. 扶手及欄杆承受荷載，玻璃只作填充。
- Design for the [DIL barrier loads](dil-2011.md) or wind, whichever is worse, and **impact test** the glass. 按荷載守則或風壓設計，並須進行撞擊測試。
- Free-standing balustrades of **2 or more panels** in crowded areas: the top rail must **bridge a broken pane**, or the laminated glass must still carry the working load with one ply broken. Use **continuous bottom clamps**. 擠迫地方的獨立玻璃欄河須能跨越破裂玻璃。
