### Guidelines for Designing Access and Safety Provisions for the Maintenance and Repair (M&R) of External Air Conditioners (ACs) at Height<br><small>建築物外部高處空調機保養及維修通道及安全設施設計指引</small> {#cl-gdasp2016}

<small>**23 Dec 2016** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 1 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_GDASP2016e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_GDASP2016c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: ⚪ **Superseded by Appendix C of the 2019 DfS Code, now the AfEM Code 2021 已由 2019 年守則附錄 C 及 2021 年外部維修通道守則取代** → [Sep 2019 letter](2019.md#cl-copdsem2019) · [AfEM Code 2021](../codes/external-maintenance-2021.md) · [ADV-33](../pnap/adv-33.md) · Topic 主題: [Building design & use 樓宇設計及用途](topics.md#design)

- Design guidelines for safe **maintenance and repair of external air conditioners**: access preferably (a) from inside the unit through windows without climbing out, (b) from common areas via access doors, (c) via openable windows with climbing out; openings **≥ 500 × 900 mm** and not smaller than the AC. **AC platform** projection up to **900 mm** if justified (outer **150 mm** at least perforated); working space **400 mm** front or back and **300 mm** sides; **100 mm** ventilation gap; show platform and AC sizes on GBP (ADV-14, ADV-33).<br>**外置冷氣機**維修安全設計指引：通道以 (a) 由室內經窗而毋須爬出為優先；開口 **≥ 500 × 900 毫米**；**冷氣機平台**伸出可放寬至 **900 毫米**（外側最少 150 毫米須通花）；工作空間前或後 **400 毫米**、兩側 **300 毫米**；通風空隙 **100 毫米**。

### Measures to Deter Misuse of Industrial Buildings for Residential Use<br><small>防止濫用工業樓宇作居住用途的措施</small> {#cl-mdmibru2016}

<small>**17 Jun 2016** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 1 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_MDMIBRU2016e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_MDMIBRU2016c.pdf)</small>

- To deter **residential misuse of new industrial buildings**: (a) no modification for **non-provision of natural lighting and ventilation** to toilets except communal toilets; (b) so internal **pipe ducts** for toilets are **not GFA-exempt** except for communal toilets in common parts; (c) **high headroom** in small workshop units must be justified; (d) justify designs and facilities not usual in industrial buildings, or the proposal may not be accepted as an IB.<br>防止**新工業大廈被用作住宅**：(a) 除公用廁所外，不批准廁所**不設天然採光通風**；(b) 因此廁所**管道槽不獲豁免總樓面面積**；(c) 細小工場單位**高樓底**須有理據；(d) 非工業常見設計須解釋。
