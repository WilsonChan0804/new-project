### Fire Safety Precautionary Measures for Major Building Repair Works<br><small>進行樓宇大維修須採取的消防安全預防措施</small> {#cl-fspm2026}

<small>**29 Oct 2026** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: AD/Mandatory Building Inspection · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2026/CL_FSPM2026e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2026/CL_FSPM2026c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force – temporary measures pending BD long-term review 有效（臨時措施，屋宇署正檢討長遠安排）** → [FS Code Part F](../codes/fsc-parts-e-f-g.md) · Topic 主題: [Fire safety 消防安全](topics.md#fire)

- Issued on the day the Government released the Independent Committee report on the **Wang Fuk Court fire (Tai Po)**. Sets out **seven temporary, "extra-cautious" measures** for occupied buildings under large scale repair works, to take effect at once.<br>於政府公布大埔宏福苑火災獨立委員會報告當日發出，列出**七項即時實施的臨時「加倍審慎」措施**，適用於有人居住而正進行大型維修的樓宇。
- (a) No **combustible temporary materials** on scaffolding (non-fire-retardant nets, plastic sheeting, foam boards), especially in **lightwells and re-entrants**. (b) No storage of flammable materials, timber planks or scaffolding materials on scaffolding, especially above ground-level exits.<br>(a) 棚架上禁用**可燃臨時物料**（非阻燃安全網、膠布、發泡膠板），尤其天井及凹位；(b) 禁止在棚架上存放易燃物料、木板及棚架材料，尤其地面出口上方。
- (c) No combustible materials (e.g. foam boards) on windows and external walls. (d) **No access openings at any means of escape** (staircases, lift lobbies); if unavoidable, protect openings in escape staircases with a **temporary fire barrier** of equivalent rating.<br>(c) 窗戶及外牆禁用可燃物料；(d) **所有逃生途徑不得開設工作通道口**；如無法避免，樓梯的開口須以同等耐火的臨時防火屏障保護。
- (e) Windows at means of escape to have **fire-rated glazing or fire-resistant glass** (buildings with Fire Safety (Improvement) Directions). (f) Openable windows in staircases and lift lobbies of older buildings kept **closed** during the works. (g) Multi-building estates: repair external walls **in phases** (new works starting after 29 Oct 2026).<br>(e) 逃生途徑的窗須裝**耐火玻璃**（適用於已獲發消防安全（改善）指示的樓宇）；(f) 舊樓樓梯及升降機大堂的可開啟窗於工程期間須**關上**；(g) 多幢屋苑須**分期**維修外牆（適用於本通告後展開的工程）。
- B(C)R ss 3 and 16: suitable, tested materials and proper methods and precautions; breaches may lead to **prosecution and disciplinary action**. BD is reviewing long-term requirements and will consult the industry.<br>《建築物（建造）規例》第 3 及 16 條：物料須合適並經測試，須採取恰當方法及預防措施；違者或被**檢控及紀律處分**。屋宇署正檢討長遠規定並會諮詢業界。

### Submission of Electronic Test Reports in Standardised Test Report Formats<br><small>提交標準化格式的電子測試報告</small> {#cl-setrstrf2026}

<small>**20 Oct 2026** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2026/CL_SETRSTRF2026e.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [17 Dec 2025 letter](2025.md#cl-asmtr2025) · Topic 主題: [Structural codes, materials & testing 結構守則、物料及測試](topics.md#structural), [Submissions, approvals & e-submission 呈交、審批及電子呈交](topics.md#submission)

- From **1 December 2026**, submit **electronic test reports in standardised formats with digital signatures** for five HOKLAS tests: (a) concrete/grout cube strength; (b) concrete core strength; (c) physical tests of steel rebar (mass, tensile, bend, surface geometry); (d) rebar chemical composition; (e) in-situ soil density (sand replacement) and relative compaction.<br>由 **2026 年 12 月 1 日**起，五類 HOKLAS 測試須提交**標準化格式並附數碼簽署的電子測試報告**：(a) 混凝土／灌漿立方體強度；(b) 混凝土芯強度；(c) 鋼筋物理測試；(d) 鋼筋化學成分；(e) 泥土現場密度及相對壓實度。
- Builds on CEDD Public Works Laboratories practice (since Aug 2023) and the 17 Dec 2025 circular. Standard Forms jointly developed by PWL, Housing Authority and HKAS.<br>承接土木工程拓展署公共工程實驗所的做法（2023 年 8 月起）及 2025 年 12 月 17 日通告；標準表格由公共工程實驗所、房委會及香港認可處制定。
- ESH accepts digitally signed reports since **29 Aug 2026**; e-statements of certification by RC authorized signatories and RSE by end Nov 2026. BD uses CEDD **SmarTest** to check reports for **Forms BA13/BA14**.<br>電子資料呈交平台自 2026 年 8 月 29 日起接受數碼簽署報告；屋宇署以 SmarTest 系統自動核對報告，加快處理 BA13／BA14。
- Paper or unsigned e-reports after 1 Dec 2026 need **supplementary proof of authenticity** (e.g. a letter from the laboratory office bearer); BD intends to **phase out** them.<br>2026 年 12 月 1 日後提交紙本或無數碼簽署報告，須另交**真確性證明**；屋宇署擬逐步停止接受。

### Use of Tied-Back Wall Support System for Excavation Works<br><small>挖掘工程使用後拉式圍牆支撐系統</small> {#cl-utbwssew2026}

<small>**7 Jul 2026** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2026/CL_UTBWSSEW2026e.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [APP-57](../pnap/app-57.md) · Topic 主題: [Excavation, slopes & geotechnical 挖掘、斜坡及岩土](topics.md#geotech)

- **Tied-back walls** (anchored into ground outside the excavation) give a strut-free excavation. If tie-backs **encroach into an adjoining lot**, get acceptance from the authorities or the owner's permission **before** works start.<br>**後拉式圍牆**（錨入挖掘範圍外的土地）可免除橫撐。如後拉件**伸入毗鄰地段**，須於動工前取得當局接受或業主許可。
- **LandsD Information Note (7 Jul 2026)**: streamlined permission for **temporary ground reinforcements** (anchors, tie-backs, soil nails, dowels) in **adjoining Government land** for site formation and ELS – no need to prove exceptional geotechnical reasons. **Permanent** reinforcements are not covered.<br>**地政總署資料說明（2026 年 7 月 7 日）**：簡化審批**臨時**地基加固物伸入**毗鄰政府土地**作地盤平整及挖掘支撐，毋須證明特殊岩土理由；**永久**加固物不適用。
- Criteria (a)–(h): no sterilising future use or stability; land not needed during the works; embedded portion only, no surface works or effect on vegetation; **de-stress** pre-stressed elements; **retract and reinstate**, or leave in only if **easily breakable** (e.g. GFRP bars, GEO decides); temporary use; complete by the proposed date; before **OP**, retract or remove anchor heads.<br>條件 (a)–(h)：不影響日後發展或穩定；施工期間土地不需作他用；只限埋入部分、不涉地面及植被；預應力須**卸載**；用後**拔除並復原**，或只可留下**易於鑽破**物料（如玻璃纖維筋，由土力工程處決定）；屬臨時性質；按期完成；申請**佔用許可證**前須拔除或移除錨頭。
- Plans approved by BD (with GEO and PlanD comments); permission at **nil fee** (admin and excavation permit fees only); parent company signs a **Deed of Indemnity** and maintains affected slopes (GEO); AP/RSE/RGE certifies extent, period, retraction and safety. Not for land allocated to departments; roads go to **HyD**.<br>圖則須經屋宇署批准（附土力工程處及規劃署意見）；許可**免地價**（只收行政費及挖掘准許證費）；母公司須簽**彌償契據**並維修受影響斜坡；認可人士／結構／岩土工程師須證明範圍、工期、拔除及安全。已撥予部門的土地不適用；道路由**路政署**處理。
- Projections on or over streets may need **BO s 42 modification** of **s 31(1)**.<br>涉及伸出街道上空或之下者，或須按《建築物條例》第 42 條申請修改第 31(1) 條。

### Guidelines on Rodent-proofing Design in New Buildings and Mosquito/Rodent Prevention and Control Measures in Construction/Demolition Sites<br><small>新建樓宇防鼠設計及建築／拆卸地盤蚊患／鼠患防治措施指引</small> {#cl-grdnbmrpcmcds2026}

<small>**Jun 2026** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: Chief Officer/Technical Services · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2026/CL_GRDNBMRPCMCDS2026e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2026/CL_GRDNBMRPCMCDS2026c.pdf)</small>

### Prevention Measures at Construction Sites during Rainy and Typhoon Seasons<br><small>雨季和颱風季節期間建築地盤的預防措施</small> {#cl-pmcsrts2026}

<small>**13 May 2026** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: Chief Officer/Technical Services · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2026/CL_PMCSRTS2026e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2026/CL_PMCSRTS2026c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** · Topic 主題: [Typhoon, rainstorm & flood 颱風、暴雨及水浸](topics.md#weather), [Scaffolding, site safety & cranes 棚架、地盤安全及起重機](topics.md#scaffold)

- **Scaffolds**: competent person inspects before and after tropical cyclones and heavy rain (more often when signals are up). Measures: (a) enough ties at the cantilevered top; (b) at most **one floor** above the topmost floor; (c) adequate putlogs, ties, bracing, brackets; (d) when a signal or strong monsoon signal is hoisted, remove or lower and tie up screens and lower the scaffold above the top floor to **≤ half a floor**; (e) no loose materials. Remove idle scaffolds; replacements of protective materials follow **PNRC 85**.<br>**棚架**：熱帶氣旋及大雨前後由合資格人士檢查。措施：(a) 頂層懸臂部分有足夠拉結；(b) 高出頂層**不多於一層**；(c) 足夠橫桿、拉結、斜撐、鋼托架；(d) 懸掛信號時拆除或降低保護網，頂層以上棚架降至**不高於半層**；(e) 不放鬆散物料。更換保護物料須符合 PNRC 85。
- **Tower cranes**: slew jib downwind and free-slew; trolley at minimum radius, hook up; luffing jib at **15°** or per maker; tie per maker; lower the tower if needed. Secure **gondolas**.<br>**塔式起重機**：吊臂轉至下風並自由迴轉；小車移至最小半徑，吊鈎升至最高；俯仰臂置於 **15°** 或按廠方建議；需要時降低塔身。**吊船**須穩妥固定。
- Update emergency contacts with BD using the **ADM-3** form; inspect and repair after signals are lowered; duties under B(A)R regs 37 and 41.<br>以 **ADM-3** 表格更新緊急聯絡；除下信號後盡快巡查及修葺；注意《建築物（管理）規例》第 37、41 條責任。
- **Flood prevention** (DSD): avoid temporary works reducing drain capacity; protect stockpiles; maintain **silt traps**; design temporary site drains; keep road drainage working under TTAs; no **illicit connection** of surface water to foul sewers.<br>**防洪**（渠務署）：臨時工程不得減少排水量；遮蓋堆料；保養**隔沙池**；妥善設計臨時排水；臨時交通安排不得阻塞路面排水；不得把地面水**非法接駁**至污水渠。
