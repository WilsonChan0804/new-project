### Top-up Courses for Level 2 Representatives under the Code of Practice for the Mandatory Building Inspection Scheme and the Mandatory Window Inspection Scheme 2012<br><small>《強制驗樓計劃及強制驗窗計劃作業守則2012》2級代表補足資格培訓課程</small> {#cl-tclrcop2017}

<small>**11 Sep 2017** · To 致: RI, RGBC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: AD/Mandatory Building Inspection · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_TCLRCOP2017e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_TCLRCOP2017c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [MBIS/MWIS Code](../codes/mbis-mwis-2012.md) · Topic 主題: [Building & window inspection (MBIS/MWIS) 強制驗樓驗窗](topics.md#mbis), [Registration, CPD & conduct 註冊、持續進修及操守](topics.md#registration)

- After the Mar 2017 MBIS/MWIS Code amendments allowing an RI to appoint a **Level 2 representative** in the supervision team (App. VI Table 1), IVE runs **top-up courses** for (i) **TCP T1** (building or minor works) with 2 years' relevant experience and (ii) **authorised signatories** of RGBC or RMWC (Class I, Type A or B). With **5 years'** experience plus the course, they may act as Level 2 representatives; the **RI keeps overall responsibility**.<br>配合 2017 年 3 月守則修訂（註冊檢驗人員可委任**第 2 級代表**），IVE 為 (i) 具 2 年經驗的 **T1 技術人員**及 (ii) 承建商**獲授權簽署人**開辦**銜接課程**；具 **5 年**經驗並完成課程者可擔任第 2 級代表，**註冊檢驗人員仍負全責**。


### Application for Approval of Building Plans – Proof of Realistic Prospect of Control of the Land forming the Site<br><small>Application for Approval of Building Plans – Proof of Realistic Prospect of Control of the Land forming the Site</small> {#cl-aabp2017}

<small>**29 Jun 2017** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 1 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_AABP2017e.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: ⚪ **Superseded by the Sep 2021 letter listing acceptable situations 已由 2021 年 9 月通告取代** → [Sep 2021 letter](2021.md#cl-aagbpprpclfs-2021) · Topic 主題: [Submissions, approvals & e-submission 呈交、審批及電子呈交](topics.md#submission)

- A building plan site may include only land the applicant owns or has a **realistic prospect of controlling**. The BA considers all information, e.g. (a) **percentage of ownership** acquired, (b) steps to acquire the rest, (c) steps to get other owners' consent for joint redevelopment.<br>地盤只可包括申請人擁有或**有實際機會控制**的土地；當局考慮 (a) 已取得業權百分比、(b) 收購其餘業權的行動、(c) 取得其他業主同意合併重建的行動。


### Streamlined Procedures for Carrying out Minor Works before Demolition of Buildings<br><small>拆卸建築物前進行小型工程的簡化程序</small> {#cl-spmwdb2017}

<small>**26 Jun 2017** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 1 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_SPMWDB2017e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_SPMWDB2017c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [Demolition Code](../codes/demolition-2004.md) · [Minor works guide](../guides/minor-works.md) · Topic 主題: [Minor works & signboards 小型工程及招牌](topics.md#minorworks), [Submissions, approvals & e-submission 呈交、審批及電子呈交](topics.md#submission)

- **Minor works before demolition** (removing UBW, projections, windows; slab openings for debris; precautionary measures) – one-stop processing by **NBD1** if the same **project team** (AP, RSE, RSC (Demolition), RGE where applicable) does the demolition: submit **MW01 ≥ 7 days** before start **with the demolition plans** (showing the MW and TG protective measures; photos not needed if in the building appraisal report); submit **MW02** with **Form BA14A** (photos not needed if the whole building is demolished), or separately to NBD1 if completed before Form BA10. The RSC(D) must also be registered for the MW class/type, with the same AS.<br>**拆卸前小型工程**（清拆僭建物、伸出物、窗；開洞運送廢料；防護措施）：如由負責拆卸的同一**項目小組**進行，可由**新建築物科一組**一站式處理：**MW01 須於動工前 ≥ 7 日**隨拆卸圖則提交；**MW02** 隨 **BA14A** 提交；拆卸專門承建商須同時註冊相關小型工程類別。


### Amendments to Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013<br><small>《混凝土結構作業守則2013年》修訂</small> {#cl-acopsuc2013-2017}

<small>**13 Jun 2017** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_ACOPSUC2013_2017e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_ACOPSUC2013_2017c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🔵 **Incorporated into the Concrete Code 2020 Edition 已納入 2020 年版** → [Concrete Code](../codes/concrete-2013.md) · Topic 主題: [Structural codes, materials & testing 結構守則、物料及測試](topics.md#structural)

- Concrete Code amendments (Jun 2017): **"cementitious content"** (cement + silica fume + PFA or GGBS) replaces cement content; revised **minimum cementitious content** (Table 4.2); post-fire investigation of **spalling protection** in high strength concrete; **flange beam** longitudinal shear; shear in **circular columns**; beam-column joints; crack width for **0.1 mm** limits; alternative design of **exposed cantilevered slabs** by limiting steel stress.<br>混凝土守則修訂（2017 年 6 月）：以**膠凝材料含量**取代水泥含量；修訂最少含量；高強度混凝土火災後**剝落防護**檢查；翼樑縱向剪力；**圓柱**剪力；樑柱接頭；**0.1 毫米**裂縫；外露懸臂板替代設計。

### Matters requiring Attention under the Minor Works Control System (MWCS)<br><small>小型工程監管制度注意事項</small> {#cl-mramwcs2017}

<small>**27 Apr 2017** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: AD/Corporate Services · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_MRAMWCS2017e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_MRAMWCS2017c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [Minor works guide](../guides/minor-works.md) · [MW Technical Guidelines](../regulations/mw-technical-guidelines.md) · Topic 主題: [Minor works & signboards 小型工程及招牌](topics.md#minorworks)

- BD audits often find non-compliance. Ensure: (a) works fall within the **class, type and item you are registered for**; (b) forms complete with all documents; (c) licences for any **third-party IP** in submissions (you indemnify the Government); (d) **no unnecessary personal data**. Tell BD if works cannot start on the stated date or finish **within 6 months**. See the TG and the "Quick Guide for Minor Works" app.<br>審核常見違規。須確保：(a) 工程屬**已註冊的級別、類型及項目**；(b) 表格及文件齊全；(c) 已取得**第三者知識產權**許可；(d) 不提交不必要的**個人資料**。如未能按期動工或 **6 個月內**完成須通知屋宇署。


### Code of Practice for Foundations 2017<br><small>基礎作業守則2017</small> {#cl-cop-foundations2017}

<small>**24 Apr 2017** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_COP_Foundations2017e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_COP_Foundations2017c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🔵 **Promulgates the Foundations Code 2017 (now the 2024 Edition) 頒布地基守則 2017（現為 2024 年版）** → [Foundations Code](../codes/foundations-2017.md) · Topic 主題: [Structural codes, materials & testing 結構守則、物料及測試](topics.md#structural)

- Publication of the **Code of Practice for Foundations 2017** (review of the 2004 Code since 2012), usable at once. Major revisions: **bearing capacity equation** for shallow foundations and **plate load test** criteria; **settlement** estimation and acceptance; **Scheduled Areas 2 and 4** (marble); uplift, overturning and buoyancy with the **highest possible groundwater table** and overall stability; pile types; **re-use of foundations**; **monitoring plan**, vibration and settlement limits; **PR plan** for piling; more testing guidance.<br>頒布**《2017 年地基作業守則》**。主要修訂：淺地基**承載力公式**及**平板荷載測試**；**沉降**估算及準則；**附表地區第 2、4 號**（大理石）；以**最高可能地下水位**驗算上浮、傾覆、浮力；樁類；**重用地基**；**監測計劃**、振動及沉降限值；打樁**公關計劃**。
