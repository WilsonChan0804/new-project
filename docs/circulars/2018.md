### Granting of Modification or Exemption under Section 42 of the Buildings Ordinance for Transitional Housing Initiatives<br><small>根據《建築物條例》第42條就過渡性房屋措施批予變通或豁免</small> {#cl-gmebpthi}

<small>**Oct 2018** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/Corporate Services · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_GMEBPTHIe.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_GMEBPTHIc.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [BO s 42](../regulations/bo-part-3-miscellaneous.md) · Topic 主題: [Building design & use 樓宇設計及用途](topics.md#design), [Submissions, approvals & e-submission 呈交、審批及電子呈交](topics.md#submission)

- BD will favourably consider **BO s 42 modification/exemption** for **transitional housing** by **NPOs** in **old domestic buildings (completed before 1976)** with compensatory measures, after **THB endorsement**; for wholesale conversion of **industrial buildings** to transitional housing, exemption from **site coverage, plot ratio, open space and service lane** may be considered.<br>當局會酌情按《建築物條例》第 42 條，為**非牟利機構**在 **1976 年前落成的舊住宅**推行的**過渡性房屋**批出修改／豁免（須有補償措施及運輸及房屋局支持）；整幢**工業大廈**改作過渡性房屋，可考慮豁免**上蓋面積、地積比率、休憩用地及後巷**要求。
- Form **BA16** with plans and compensatory measures, mechanical ventilation assessment, NPO tenancy proof, written undertaking and **management plan**; under minor works an AP applies, works may start but cannot be certified before the permit. Reply within **10 working days** (final within 20). Permit **BD106** valid **6 months** for completion; void when the tenancy ends (reinstate); possible **annual AP inspection**; documents published on BD's website.<br>須以 **BA16** 申請，附圖則、機械通風評估、租約、承諾書及**管理計劃**；一般 **10 個工作天**回覆；許可證 **BD106** 有效期 **6 個月**，租約終止即失效並須復原；或須**每年由認可人士檢查**。
