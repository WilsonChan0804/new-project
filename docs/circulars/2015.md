### Safety Requirements for Liftwell Inspection and Emergency Doors<br><small>升降機槽的檢查門及緊急通道門的安全規定</small> {#cl-srlied201510}

<small>**6 Oct 2015** · To 致: Property management bodies · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_SRLIED201510e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_SRLIED201510c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🔵 **Incorporated into the Lifts and Escalators Code and APP-29 已納入升降機守則及 APP-29** → [Lifts & Escalators Code](../codes/lifts-escalators-2011.md) · [APP-29](../pnap/app-29.md) · Topic 主題: [Building design & use 樓宇設計及用途](topics.md#design)

- After a 2010 death when a meter reader opened a **liftwell emergency door** by mistake: for new buildings and A&A, liftwell **inspection and emergency doors** need **dual key-operated locks** (two designated keys simultaneously) and a permanent **figure-type warning sign** at the keyhole (revised CoP and **APP-29**).<br>2010 年抄錶員誤開**升降機槽緊急門**墮亡：新建及加建改建工程的升降機槽**檢查門及緊急門**須設**雙匙鎖**（兩條指定鎖匙同時操作）及匙孔旁**圖案警告標誌**（APP-29）。
