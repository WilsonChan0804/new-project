### Repairs to Curtain Wall, Glass Wall and Cladding<br><small>"修葺幕牆、玻璃牆及覆蓋層"</small> {#cl-cgwcr2019}

<small>**3 Oct 2019** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 1 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_CGWCR2019e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_CGWCR2019c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force (supersedes the Sep 2018 curtain wall letter) 有效（取代 2018 年 9 月通告）** → [APP-37](../pnap/app-37.md) · [APP-117](../pnap/app-117.md) · [APP-16](../pnap/app-16.md) · [ADM-19](../pnap/adm-19.md) · Topic 主題: [Submissions, approvals & e-submission 呈交、審批及電子呈交](topics.md#submission), [Structural codes, materials & testing 結構守則、物料及測試](topics.md#structural)

- Repairs to damaged **curtain walls, glass walls and cladding** are **not** minor works or exempted works – they need **approval and consent**, but get **ADM-19 fast track (≤ 30 days)** if the structural system is unchanged. Follow **APP-117**, **APP-37** and **APP-16**; the RSE assesses existing condition and integrity. Replacing individual components (glass panes, sealants, setting blocks) or cladding panels to the approved design needs **no structural calculations** or parent-structure check.<br>修葺受損**幕牆、玻璃牆及覆面**不屬小型工程或豁免工程，須**批准及同意**；如結構系統不變可按 **ADM-19 快速處理（≤ 30 日）**；須符合 APP-117、APP-37、APP-16。按原設計更換個別組件毋須結構計算。
- Submit together: (a) **supervision plan** (AP, RSE, RGBC per TM and Site Supervision Code); (b) for **tempered glass**, RSE/RGBC quality supervision plan for **heat soak** and the manufacturer's QA scheme (APP-37); (c) if glass thermal performance is worse than approved, provisional **OTTV** report (APP-67) and energy efficiency report (APP-156).<br>須同時提交：(a) **監工計劃書**；(b) 如用**鋼化玻璃**，熱浸品質監督計劃及廠商品質保證計劃；(c) 如玻璃隔熱較差，臨時 **OTTV** 報告（APP-67）及能源效益報告（APP-156）。

### Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019<br><small>《香港風力效應作業守則2019年》</small> {#cl-wc2019}

<small>**30 Sep 2019** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_WC2019e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_WC2019c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🔵 **Promulgates the current Wind Code 2019; grace period ended 31 Mar 2021 頒布現行風力守則；寬限期已結束** → [Wind Code 2019](../codes/wind-2019.md) · [Grace period extension](2020.md#cl-copwehk2019egp2020) · Topic 主題: [Structural codes, materials & testing 結構守則、物料及測試](topics.md#structural)

- Publication of the **Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019**, usable at once, from a BD consultancy reviewing the **2004 Code** against international codes. New features: unified **gust pressure** approach with size and size-and-dynamic factors; **torsional and across-wind** forces; load combination factors; **building acceleration and occupant comfort**; wind loads on **temporary structures**; **sheltering** by effective height; force and pressure coefficients; wind tunnel guidance; **directionality factors**; dominant openings; damping by aspect ratio. **Explanatory Notes** issued too.<br>頒布**《2019 年香港風力效應作業守則》**，即時可用。新特點：統一**陣風壓**方法及尺寸、動力系數；**扭轉及橫風**；荷載組合；**建築物加速度及舒適度**；**臨時構築物**；以有效高度計**遮擋**；力及壓力系數；風洞測試；**風向系數**；主要開口；阻尼比；另附**釋義**。
- **12-month grace period** for plans designed to the 2004 Code (later extended to 31 Mar 2021).<br>按 2004 年守則設計的圖則有 **12 個月寬限期**（其後延至 2021 年 3 月 31 日）。

### Submission of Vertical Greening Plans<br><small>呈交垂直綠化工程的圖則</small> {#cl-svgp}

<small>**14 Feb 2019** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_SVGPe.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_SVGPc.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [APP-19 greenery](../pnap/app-19.md) · [Wind Code](../codes/wind-2019.md) · Topic 主題: [Building design & use 樓宇設計及用途](topics.md#design), [Structural codes, materials & testing 結構守則、物料及測試](topics.md#structural)

- **Vertical greening (VG)** – demountable modular units on a supporting frame on an external wall – must be shown on **GBP**. If the supporting frame is **> 3 m** above adjoining ground, **structural plans** are required (frame layout, fixing of modules).<br>**垂直綠化**須在一般建築圖則顯示；支承架高出地面 **> 3 米**須提交**結構圖則**。
- **No structural details of modules** needed if: (a) each module **≤ 0.75 m** high and wide; (b) projection **≤ 0.2 m** from fixing points; (c) top **≤ 15 m** above ground; (d) proprietary system. AP/RSE still ensure safety; advise owners of **regular inspection and maintenance**.<br>如符合以下條件毋須提交組件結構細節：(a) 每件 **≤ 0.75 米**；(b) 伸出 **≤ 0.2 米**；(c) 最高點 **≤ 15 米**；(d) 專利系統。須提醒業主**定期檢查保養**。
