<small>**12 Dec 2025** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: Chief Officer/Technical Services · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2025/CL_RBGAOBD2025e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/2025/CL_RBGAOBD2025c.pdf)</small>
