### Training Courses leading to Equivalent Academic Qualifications for Technically Competent Persons (TCP) T1 to T3 and Authorized Signatory of Contractors for Registration under the Buildings Ordinance<br><small>獲取適任技術人員T1至T3及根據《建築物條例》註冊成為承建商獲授權簽署人的同等學歷資格的培訓課程</small> {#cl-tceaqtcpascrbo2024}

<small>**7 Nov 2024** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_TCEAQTCPASCRBO2024e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_TCEAQTCPASCRBO2024c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [Site Supervision Code / TM](../codes/site-supervision-2009.md) · Topic 主題: [Site supervision 地盤監督](topics.md#supervision), [Registration, CPD & conduct 註冊、持續進修及操守](topics.md#registration)

- Courses giving **academic qualifications equivalent** to those for **TCP T1–T3** under the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans 2009: HKIC/IVE **TCP T1 Training Course**; IVE **Professional Diploma in Construction Site Supervision**; IVE **Professional Diploma in Construction Engineering**.<br>可取得與《監工計劃書技術備忘錄》**T1 至 T3 技術人員**同等學歷的課程：建造業議會／IVE **T1 培訓課程**、IVE **建造地盤監督專業文憑**、IVE **建造工程專業文憑**。
- The Professional Diploma in Construction Engineering is also accepted for an **Authorized Signatory** of an RGBC or an RSC (demolition, foundation, GI field works, site formation) under **PNRC 38**. Group enrolment via HKIC 2100 9892, IVE 2436 8790.<br>建造工程專業文憑亦獲接納為一般建築承建商或專門承建商（拆卸、地基、土地勘測、地盤平整）**獲授權簽署人**的學歷（PNRC 38）。


### Further Streamlining Measures for Structural Works<br><small>Further Streamlining Measures for Structural Works</small> {#cl-fsmsw2024}

<small>**31 Oct 2024** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_FSMSW2024e.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🔵 **Incorporated into APP-18, ADM-19, PNRC 52 and Form BA8A 已納入 APP-18、ADM-19、PNRC 52 及 BA8A 表格** → [APP-18 (Annex C1)](../pnap/app-18.md) · [ADM-19 (App. G)](../pnap/adm-19.md) · Topic 主題: [Submissions, approvals & e-submission 呈交、審批及電子呈交](topics.md#submission), [Structural codes, materials & testing 結構守則、物料及測試](topics.md#structural)

- **Two-stage pile foundation plan submission**: an RSE may split a pile foundation submission into two stages; required information in **APP-18 Annex C1**.<br>**樁基圖則分兩階段呈交**：註冊結構工程師可分兩階段呈交，所需資料見 **APP-18 附件 C1**。
- **Concurrent approval and consent** for **secondary structural elements** (extended from the 27 Feb 2023 letter): curtain wall, window wall, cladding, space frame, precast element, canopy, skylight, balustrade, external ceiling, grille/louvre, ventilation duct, maintenance platform and similar – submit revised **Form BA8A** with the plans (ADM-19 revised).<br>**次要結構構件同時批准及同意**範圍擴大：幕牆、窗牆、覆面、空間框架、預製件、簷篷、天窗、欄杆、外部天花、格柵、通風管、維修平台等，隨圖則提交新 **BA8A 表格**。
- **One Form BA16** for modification of **B(A)R reg 33(1)** covering all minor amendments of superstructure works (including secondary elements) after first approval and consent, if ADM-19 App. G criteria are met.<br>符合 ADM-19 附錄 G 時，只需提交**一份 BA16** 申請修改 B(A)R 第 33(1) 條，涵蓋上蓋所有輕微修訂。

<small>**30 Jun 2024** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/Corporate Services · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_BDLSESH2024e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_BDLSESH2024c.pdf)</small>

### Qualified Supervision of Building Works Involving Mobile Plants and Tower Cranes by Adopting Smart Site Safety System<br><small>採用「安全智慧工地」系統為涉及流動機械及塔式起重機的建築工程提供合格監督</small> {#cl-qsbwimptcassss2024}

<small>**28 Mar 2024** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_QSBWIMPTCASSSS2024e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_QSBWIMPTCASSSS2024c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟡 **Extended to all works types from 1 Jul 2025 by the 31 Mar 2025 letter 已由 2025 年 3 月 31 日通告擴大至所有工程類別** → [31 Mar 2025 letter](2025.md#cl-eassssqsatbwimptc2025) · [APP-162](../pnap/app-162.md) · Topic 主題: [Scaffolding, site safety & cranes 棚架、地盤安全及起重機](topics.md#scaffold), [Site supervision 地盤監督](topics.md#supervision)

- Mandatory **Smart Site Safety System (4S)** measures: **Mobile Plant Alert System** (360° sensors; danger zone set by the Safety Officer, **≥ 2 m** from the plant) and **Tower Crane Alert System** (all loading zones at all levels; danger zone **≥ 7 m radius** from the hook; clearance **≥ 3 m**).<br>強制**智慧工地安全系統**：**流動機械警報系統**（360° 感應，危險範圍 **≥ 2 米**）及**塔式起重機警報系統**（危險範圍以吊鈎計 **≥ 7 米半徑**，淨空 **≥ 3 米**）。
- From **1 July 2024**, **s 17(1) item 6** conditions on the **first approval or major revision of superstructure plans** when building works cost **> $30 million**; on consent, the AP must **declare** whether the cost exceeds $30 million (B(A)R reg 10) – no declaration may lead to refusal under **s 16(3)(b)**. AP/RSE/RGE still oversee the RC stream (Site Supervision Code Tables 4.1–4.3).<br>由 **2024 年 7 月 1 日**起，造價 **> 3,000 萬元**項目於**首次批准上蓋圖則**時附加條件；申請同意時認可人士須**聲明**造價，否則可按第 16(3)(b) 條拒絕。

<small>Related 相關: [Site Supervision Code](../codes/site-supervision-2009.md)</small>
