If TCP T1 full-time factory supervision is disrupted by lockdowns, video supervision may be approved case by case with AS audits **biweekly** and **≥ 50 %** of tempered glass panes supervised by TCP T3 by video; assistants must not be RC supervisory staff; log books and DVDs kept.<br>工地抽查提高至**≥ 2%（每 10 批最少 1 件）**及**每類組件 ≥ 2%**；如封城影響 T1 全時間監督，可個別批准視像監督，獲授權簽署人**每兩星期**抽查，**≥ 50%** 鋼化玻璃由 T3 以視像監督。
