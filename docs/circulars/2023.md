### Qualified Supervision for Off-site Fabrication of Simple Structural Steel Elements<br><small>為場外預製的鋼結構工程提供合格監督</small> {#cl-qsofssse2023}

<small>**15 Dec 2023** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_QSOFSSSE2023e.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [Steel Code](../codes/steel-2011.md) · [Site Supervision Code](../codes/site-supervision-2009.md) · Topic 主題: [Structural codes, materials & testing 結構守則、物料及測試](topics.md#structural), [Site supervision 地盤監督](topics.md#supervision)

- **Simple structural steel elements** (cast-in embeds, brackets, balustrades, railings, A/C support frames, minor-works type steelwork) often fabricated in **Mainland factories**. Approval conditions (BO s 17(1) item 6) require an RSE's **QCS** (qualifications as **TCP T3**) to inspect fabrication **at least weekly**.<br>**簡單結構鋼構件**（預埋件、托架、欄杆、冷氣機架等）常於**內地廠房**製造；批准條件要求結構工程師委派**品質控制監督**（資歷同 **T3**）**每星期最少一次**監督製造。
- Alternative accepted: **on-site audit checks** instead of factory visits – sample **≥ 5 %, minimum 5 assemblies per batch**; RSE's checklist covers size and dimensions, **mill certificates**, visual and **NDT of welds** by a HOKLAS lab, bolt size and quality; results logged and kept on site.<br>可改為**工地抽查**：每批抽樣 **≥ 5%，最少 5 件**；清單包括尺寸、**鋼材證明書**、HOKLAS 實驗所**焊縫無損檢測**、螺栓；記錄存放工地。
- RSE gives the BA **one month's written notice**; other element types by application (decision in 14 days). All other approval conditions and Steel Code QC requirements still apply.<br>結構工程師須**一個月前書面通知**；其他構件須申請（14 日內決定）；其餘條件及鋼結構守則品質要求不變。

### Compliance with the Buildings Ordinance Concerning Erection of Unauthorised Building Works<br><small>遵從《建築物條例》的規定有關建造違例建築工程</small> {#cl-cboceubwrbprc2023}

<small>**6 Nov 2023** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: AD/Corporate Services · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_CBOCEUBWRBPRC2023e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_CBOCEUBWRBPRC2023c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [ADV-37](../pnap/adv-37.md) · [PNRC 79](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnrc/Pnrc79.pdf) · [BO s 40](../regulations/bo-part-2-enforcement.md) · Topic 主題: [Unauthorised works & enforcement 違例建築及執法](topics.md#enforcement)

- After **landslides in the Sep 2023 rainstorm** exposed blatant UBW: **do not advise or assist** anyone to erect UBW. Duties of PBPs and PRCs: BO **ss 4(3), 4B, 9(5), 9AA(6)**; conduct per **ADV-37** and **PNRC 79**.<br>**2023 年 9 月暴雨山泥傾瀉**揭露嚴重違例建築：**不得建議或協助**任何人搭建違例建築；訂明人士及承建商責任見《建築物條例》第 4(3)、4B、9(5)、9AA(6) 條及 ADV-37、PNRC 79。
- Tell clients to get **approval and consent (s 14(1))** or use the **Minor Works Control System**. Penalties: knowingly breaching s 14(1) – **$400,000 and 2 years**, plus **$20,000 per day** (s 40(1AA)); minor works without a PBP/PRC – **$100,000** (s 40(1AB)); disciplinary action under ss 7 and 13.<br>須提醒客戶取得**第 14(1) 條批准及同意**或循小型工程監管制度進行。罰則：明知違反第 14(1) 條 **罰款 40 萬元及監禁 2 年**，每日另罰 **2 萬元**；小型工程不委任訂明人士 **罰款 10 萬元**；亦可被紀律處分。

### Mandatory Building Inspection Scheme (MBIS) Inspection of Unauthorised Building Works (UBW)<br><small>強制驗樓計劃違例建築工程（僭建物）的檢驗</small> {#cl-mbisiubw2023}

<small>**28 Sep 2023** · To 致: Registered Inspectors · Issued by 發出: AD/Mandatory Building Inspection · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_MBISIUBW2023e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_MBISIUBW2023c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [MBIS/MWIS Code para 3.7](../codes/mbis-mwis-2012.md) · Topic 主題: [Building & window inspection (MBIS/MWIS) 強制驗樓驗窗](topics.md#mbis), [Unauthorised works & enforcement 違例建築及執法](topics.md#enforcement)

- After an **unauthorised canopy fell** from a building that had completed MBIS inspection: RIs must follow the **MBIS/MWIS Code (2023 Ed.)**, alert owners of emergencies and notify the BA; inspect and assess **UBW within para 3.7.1** for obvious hazard or imminent danger, and **advise owners to remove all UBW** found (para 3.7).<br>一座已完成強制驗樓的樓宇有**僭建簷篷墮下**：註冊檢驗人員須按守則（2023 年版）提醒業主緊急情況並通知當局；檢驗第 3.7.1 段範圍內的**違例建築**並**建議業主清拆**。

<small>**29 Aug 2023** · To 致: RMWC (Company) · Issued by 發出: AD/Corporate Services · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_CARMWCRS2023e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_CARMWCRS2023c.pdf)</small>

### Precautionary Measures for Repair Works of External Walls in Buildings<br><small>大廈外牆修葺工程的防護措施</small> {#cl-rwew2023}

<small>**28 Aug 2023** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RI, RGBC, RSC, RMWC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 2 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_RWEW2023e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_RWEW2023c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [Demolition Code](../codes/demolition-2004.md) · Topic 主題: [Scaffolding, site safety & cranes 棚架、地盤安全及起重機](topics.md#scaffold)

- After rendering fell from a bamboo scaffold onto a **double-decker bus**: scaffolds for external wall repairs must have **closely boarded or metal-plated platforms**, **toe-boards and guard-rails**, protective coverings or **screen nets**; do not pile removed rendering or concrete on platforms. Registered professionals in charge check the precautions (BD bamboo scaffold guidelines, LD CoP).<br>外牆維修時批盪從竹棚架掉下擊中**雙層巴士**：棚架須有**密鋪工作台**、**踢腳板及護欄**、保護網；不得在工作台堆放鑿下物料；負責的註冊人士須檢查。
