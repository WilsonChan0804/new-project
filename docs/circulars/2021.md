### Application for Approval of General Building Plans Proof of Realistic Prospect of Control of the Land Forming a Site<br><small>建築圖則審批申請有實際機會控制組成地盤的土地的證明文件</small> {#cl-aagbpprpclfs-2021}

<small>**27 Sep 2021** · To 致: AP, RSE, RGE, RGBC, RSC · Issued by 發出: AD/New Buildings 1 · [English PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_AAGBPPRPCLFS_2021e.pdf) · [中文 PDF](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/tc/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/circular/CL_AAGBPPRPCLFS_2021c.pdf)</small>

Status 狀態: 🟢 **In force 有效** → [B(P)R site and lot](../regulations/bpr-18a-22.md) · Topic 主題: [Submissions, approvals & e-submission 呈交、審批及電子呈交](topics.md#submission)

- A GBP site may include only land the applicant **owns or has a realistic prospect of controlling**. Typical acceptable situations (Appendix): (a) authorised by the registered owners; (b) a registered owner authorised by all others; (c) **District Lands Conference** in-principle approval of a land grant; (d) in-principle approval of a licence by a department; (e) acceptance of LandsD basic terms or payment of the land exchange admin fee; (f) lease from the registered owner who authorises development; (g) **IO** for A&A forming a new building on common parts; (h) binding provisional sale and purchase agreement with all owners; (i) ownership enough for a **compulsory sale order** application (solicitor's letter and LR proof; possible B(P)R 20/21 modification on a **notional site** basis with a **2-year non-renewable** limit).<br>一般建築圖則的地盤只可包括申請人**擁有或有實際機會控制**的土地。附錄列出可接受情況：(a) 獲登記業主授權；(b) 業主之一並獲其他業主授權；(c) **地政總署地區地政會議**原則上批准批地；(d) 部門原則上批出牌照；(e) 已接受基本條款或繳付換地行政費；(f) 向業主租用並獲授權發展；(g) 法團於公用部分加建新建築物；(h) 與所有業主簽訂具約束力臨時買賣合約；(i) 擁有足夠業權申請**強制售賣令**（可按「**概念地盤**」修改 B(P)R 第 20、21 條，限期 **2 年**不可續）。
- Submit bulky proof (e.g. LR records) on a non-rewritable **CD/DVD** with PDFs and an index file, labelled and signed by the AP.<br>大量證明文件（如土地註冊處記錄）宜以不可重寫光碟提交，附 PDF 及索引。
