# Minor works forms<br><small>小型工程表格</small>

Source: [BD minor works forms](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/resources/forms/form_mw.html) (sample filled forms checked). From **29 May 2026** these are submitted online through the **RMWMS** ([APP-60](../pnap/app-60.md)). Legal basis: [B(MW)R Cap. 123N](../regulations/bmwr-123n.md#key-table-class-requirements). 由 2026 年 5 月 29 日起經「註冊及小型工程管理系統」網上呈交。

## MW01 Notice of Commencement of Class I Minor Works<br><small>展開第 I 級別小型工程通知</small> {#mw01}

| | |
|---|---|
| **Revision 版本** | 02/2026 |
| **Purpose 目的** | Notifies BA before Class I minor works start, with the appointed prescribed building professional(s) and prescribed registered contractor. 在第 I 級別工程展開前通知，列明訂明建築專業人士及訂明註冊承建商。 |
| **When 何時** | **At least 7 days** before commencement. 展開前最少 7 天。 |
| **By 由誰** | AP (or RI for prescribed repair items), RSE / RGE if needed, and the PRC 認可人士（或註冊檢驗人員）等及承建商 |
| **Legal basis 法律依據** | [B(MW)R ss. 27–28, 30](../regulations/bmwr-123n.md#part-by-part) |
| **Related 相關** | [APP-147](../pnap/app-147.md) · [Minor works guide](../guides/minor-works.md) |
| **Submit with 附交文件** | Plans and details (PR2 / PR3); structural calculations (PR4) where needed; supervision plan (PR5 / SP); photos of the existing condition (PR1); A&A loading / stability certification where needed. 圖則、計算書、監工計劃書、施工前照片等。 |

## MW06-1 / MW06-2 / MW06-3 Inspection and certification of prescribed building or building works, and completion of associated alteration or strengthening works<br><small>訂明建築物或建築工程的檢驗及證明，以及相關改動或加固工程完工證明書</small> {#mw06}

| **Revision 版本** | 09/2021 |
| **Purpose 目的** | Validates existing works under the **Minor Amenity Feature Validation Scheme** (MW06-1 Class I, MW06-2 Class II, MW06-3 Class III) or the **Household Minor Works Validation Scheme** (MW06-3), after inspection and any strengthening. 經檢驗及加固後，檢核現有適意設施（第 I／II／III 級別）或家居小型工程（第 III 級別）。 |

| **By 由誰** | PBP (MW06-1) / PRC 訂明建築專業人士或承建商 |

## SP Supervision Plan 監工計劃書 {#sp}

Same form as for new building works (04/2021); required for **Class I** minor works with MW01. See [SP in the new building forms page](nb-aa.md#sp) and the [Site supervision code](../codes/site-supervision-2009.md). 第 I 級別小型工程須連同 MW01 呈交。

## VSR Safety Inspection Check List 安全檢查清單 {#vsr}

Checklist (04/2022) for the Household Minor Works Validation Scheme and the Minor Amenity Feature Validation Scheme; submitted with [MW06-1/2/3](#mw06). 家居小型工程及適意設施檢核計劃的安全檢查清單，連同 MW06 呈交。
