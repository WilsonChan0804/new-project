## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - APs, RSEs and RGEs must notify BA of a change of **business address within 14 days** (B(A)R 45) (para. 1).
    - Use **Form BA24**, online via the Electronic Forms Submission System or sent to the BD Registration Unit (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊岩土工程師須在**營業地址更改後 14 天內**通知建築事務監督（B(A)R 第 45 條）（第 1 段）。
    - 使用**表格 BA24**，可經電子表格呈交系統網上遞交或寄交屋宇署註冊組（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: every AP, RSE and RGE on the registers when their **business address** changes. 註冊人士更改營業地址。
