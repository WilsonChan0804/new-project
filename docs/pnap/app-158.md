## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - AP, RSE and RGE **supervise** and the RC gives **continuous supervision** per the supervision plan; **quality supervision** ensures works follow the BO, approved plans and conditions (para. 1).
    - Under the **Site Supervision Code**, each stream devises **checklists** of typical and project-specific items for its TCPs, identifying **critical stages and activities** for more frequent supervision (para. 2).
    - This PNAP adds common inspection items for **superstructure, ELS and site formation** works (para. 3 and appendices).

=== "中文"

    - 認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊岩土工程師負責**監督**，註冊承建商按監工計劃書進行**持續監督**；**質量監督**確保工程符合條例、核准圖則及條件（第 1 段）。
    - 根據**地盤監督守則**，各方須為其適任技術人員訂立包括典型及項目特定項目的**檢查表**，並確定須較頻密監督的**關鍵階段及工序**（第 2 段）。
    - 本備考補充**上蓋、挖掘及側向承托及地盤平整**工程的常見巡查項目（第 3 段及附錄）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **quality supervision** (part of the **supervision plan** system) for building and street works: AP / RSE / RGE (periodic) and RC (continuous) checklists and records, with typical items for **superstructure**, **ELS** and **site formation** (Appendix A). 品質監督。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | AP / RSE / RGE **supervise**, the RC gives **continuous** supervision per the supervision plan; quality supervision ensures works follow the BO, approved plans and conditions. 監督責任。 |
| 2 | Per the **Site Supervision Code 2009**, AP / RSE / RGE / AS devise **checklists** (typical plus project-specific items, incl. approval conditions) for their **TCPs**; the Code covers foundations, GI field works, soil nails, **critical stages** and **critical activities**. 核對表。 |
| 3 | **Appendix A** adds common items for superstructure, ELS and site formation; add others as needed (curtain walls, glass walls, prestressing, steelwork, waterproofing). 附錄 A。 |
| 4 | Each operates their part of the plan, inspects **personally** as needed, and ensures TCPs do their duties. 親自巡查。 |
| 5 | Keep **checklists and records on site** for BD's **Site Monitoring Section**; sample record in **Appendix B**. 記錄存放地盤。 |
| 6 | Similar note to RCs. 承建商另有作業備考。 |

## Appendix A: quality supervision items 附錄 A：品質監督項目 {#appendix-a}

| Item | Stage 階段 | Stream 負責 |
|---|---|---|
| **QC1–QC11** In-situ RC | Element locations and sizes; **rebar** quality (CS2) and **fixing** (cover, starter bars, [ADV-15](adv-15.md)); **concrete** quality (CS1, cubes, high-strength coring); placing, compaction, curing (**transfer structures**: sequence, temporary works, method); **formwork and falsework** stability, no early striking; concrete after striking; **couplers**; cast-in embeds and anchors; in-situ strength (cores, **rebound hammer**, [APP-167](app-167.md)) | **RSE + RC** (formwork split per Supervision Code 4.7–4.9) |
| **QB1–QB8** General building works | Setting out of basement / G/F (lanes, **EVA**, set-backs, dedicated and surrendered areas, storey heights, FRS, staircases, smoke vents) and upper floors (balconies, UPs, barriers, **refuge floors**); fitting out (exit routes, fireman's lift lobbies, **temporary refuge spaces**, GFA-exempt features, fire barriers, fire doors and smoke seals, roof waterproofing); **windows** (prescribed glazing and openable areas, [APP-116](app-116.md)); **BFA** dimensions and features; waterproofing application and tests; **curtain wall** mock-up and site water tests (Glass Code Annex A); **precast façade** water tests (Precast Code 2.8.2.8, 4.3) | **AP + RC** |
| **QE1–QE9** ELS | Temporary planking location (AP\*, RSE, RC) and installation (RSE, RC); **grout curtain** and **pumping tests** (RGE, RC); strut erection / removal (AP\*, RSE, RC); **preloading** (RSE, RC); excavation stages, **backfilling**, **instrumentation and monitoring** (AP\*, RSE, RGE, RC) | as listed; RGE only where geotechnical qualified supervision is imposed |
| **QSF1–7** Site formation, general and cut / fill | Monitoring; site drainage; rainstorm protection; cut / fill setting out; excavation stages; backfilling ([APP-15](app-15.md)); records | per original table |
| **QRS1–6** Rock slopes | Setting out; excavation ([APP-17](app-17.md)); scaling and trimming; verify joint assumptions; treatment layout and material tests | per original table |
| **QRW1–9** Retaining walls | Location; excavation; **bearing stratum**; rebar; concrete; drainage and filter ([APP-54](app-54.md)); waterproofing and insulation (B(P)R 48); backfilling | per original table |
| **QRF1–4** Reinforced fill | Setting out; drainage and filter; backfilling; records | per original table |

<small>\* AP ensures checks are done by the right professionals and RC and coordinates follow-up; # AP checks compliance with general layouts on approved plans. 符號說明。</small>

## Appendix B: sample record 附錄 B：記錄樣本 {#appendix-b}

| Field 欄目 | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| Header | BD ref., project, type of works, TCP name (as in the supervision plan), grade, inspection frequency, date |
| Each item | Item no. (from checklist), location / details, result **S** (satisfactory; very minor items fixed at the same visit), **NS** (not satisfactory), **NA**, remedial remarks, photos |
| NS items | Recorded in the site supervision report; **Form B** if material safety concern, or if work cannot be verified because it was covered up |
