## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The **MOE Code 1996** gives guidance on B(P)R 41(1); it clarified anomalies, upgraded standards, removed overlaps with the FRC Code and added **Part III** for PPE (paras. 1–3).
    - In force **1 Dec 1996** (Part III from 28 Jun 1996); works under way or consented by then may use the earlier edition (para. 4). BA recognised difficulties with the **fire engineering approach** and existing buildings (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - **1996 年走火守則**為 B(P)R 第 41(1) 條提供指引；釐清含糊之處、提高標準、刪除與耐火結構守則重複的內容，並新增公眾娛樂場所的**第 III 部**（第 1–3 段）。
    - **1996 年 12 月 1 日**生效（第 III 部自 1996 年 6 月 28 日起）；當日或之前已展開或已獲同意的工程可沿用舊版（第 4 段）；建築事務監督承認**消防工程方法**及現有建築物的應用有困難（第 5 段）。

!!! warning "Superseded for new works 新工程已不適用"
    Listed in [APP-153 Appendix A](app-153.md#appendix-a); the MOE Code 1996 is replaced by **FS Code 2011 Part B**. Still relevant to buildings consented **1 Dec 1996 – 1 Apr 2012** and to Cap. 502 / 572 upgrading. 由消防守則 B 部取代。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | MoE requirements are in **B(P)R 41(1)**; the **MOE Code** gives guidance. 法定依據。 |
| 2 | The revised **MOE Code 1996** is published. 新版守則。 |
| 3 | Clarifies anomalies, upgrades some standards, removes duplication with the FRC Code; adds **Part III** for **places of public entertainment**. 新增娛樂場所部分。 |
| 4 | In operation **1 Dec 1996**, except: (a) **Part III** from **28 Jun 1996** (PNAP 194); (b) works carried out or consented on or before 1 Dec 1996 may use the earlier edition. 生效日期。 |
| 5 | The BA recognises difficulties with **fire engineering** and existing buildings; more guidance may follow. 消防工程方法。 |
| 6 | Regular review. 定期檢討。 |
