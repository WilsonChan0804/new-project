## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - RCHE and RCHD are **domestic** uses under the BO, but the BA may grant a **modification** to treat them as **non-domestic** for **B(P)R 19–22** (site classification, **site coverage**, plot ratio) and to waive **open space** under **B(P)R 25** (para. 2).
    - Six conditions (para. 2(a)–(f)): **no adverse SWD comments** (including access for ambulant residents); **no FSD objection**; full BO compliance including structure, fire safety, **headroom, natural lighting and ventilation**; compliance with **B(P)R 49(1) and 49B** (hardship cases considered); compliance with **Cap. 459A s.20 / Cap. 613A s.21** and **not in a basement**; **not in an industrial building**.
    - **Incentive schemes** (LandsD and SWD, time-limited): RCHE/RCHD **count for GFA**, but the BA may allow a **higher plot ratio** to hold the extra GFA of eligible premises, subject to PlanD's advice on the OZP (paras. 3–4).
    - The **para. 2** measures apply to **new buildings and A&A works** for new or extra homes; the **para. 4** plot ratio bonus is for **new developments** under the incentive schemes only (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 安老院及殘疾人士院舍在條例下屬**住用**，但建築事務監督可批出**變通**，就《建築物（規劃）規例》**第 19–22 條**（地盤分類、**上蓋面積**、地積比率）視作**非住用**，並豁免第 **25 條**的**休憩用地**（第 2 段）。
    - 六項條件（第 2(a)–(f) 段）：**社會福利署無負面意見**（包括行動不便院友的通道）；**消防處無反對**；全面符合條例，包括結構、消防、**淨高、自然採光及通風**；符合規例**第 49(1) 及 49B 條**（確有困難可考慮）；符合**第 459A 章第 20 條／第 613A 章第 21 條**且**不在地庫**；**不在工業大廈內**。
    - **鼓勵計劃**（地政總署及社署，有時限）：院舍**須計入總樓面面積**，但建築事務監督可批准**較高地積比率**以容納合資格院舍的額外樓面面積，並考慮規劃署對分區計劃大綱圖的意見（第 3–4 段）。
    - **第 2 段**措施適用於提供新院舍或增加院舍的**新建築物及改動加建**；**第 4 段**地積比率安排只適用於鼓勵計劃下的**新發展項目**（第 5–6 段）。

!!! tip "Applies / does not apply 適用／不適用"
    **Applies**: RCHE (Cap. 459) and RCHD (Cap. 613) premises in new buildings or A&A works. 適用於新建築物或改動加建中的安老院及殘疾人士院舍。
    **Does not apply**: premises in **basements** or **industrial buildings**; the plot ratio bonus does **not** apply to A&A works in existing buildings or to homes outside the incentive schemes. 不適用於地庫或工業大廈；地積比率安排不適用於現有建築物改動加建或計劃以外的院舍。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Purpose: facilitating measures and the BA's considerations for **modifications** to help provide RCHE and RCHD. 目的。 |
| 2 | Modification to treat the premises as **non-domestic** for **B(P)R 19, 20, 21, 22**, and **no open space** under **B(P)R 25**, if all of (a)–(f) are met: |
| 2(a) | **No adverse comments from SWD**, including on access for ambulant residents. 社署無負面意見。 |
| 2(b) | **No objection from FSD**. 消防處無反對。 |
| 2(c) | Complies with the BO and regulations in **all aspects**: structural suitability, fire safety, adequate **headroom**, **natural lighting and ventilation**. 全面符合條例。 |
| 2(d) | Complies with **B(P)R 49(1)** (no dangerous trades in a domestic building) and **49B** (no cinema with an elderly or disability home); hardship cases may get a modification. 符合規例第 49(1) 及 49B 條。 |
| 2(e) | Complies with **Cap. 459A s.20** or **Cap. 613A s.21** (location and height limits of the home in the building) and is **not in a basement**. 符合院舍規例位置要求，不在地庫。 |
| 2(f) | **Not in an industrial building**. 不在工業大廈。 |
| 3 | Two time-limited **incentive schemes** (LandsD Practice Notes 5/2023 and 10/2023; SWD Guidance Notes). 兩個鼓勵計劃。 |
| 4 | RCHE/RCHD **count for GFA**; during the pilot period the BA may permit a **higher plot ratio** for eligible premises, taking PlanD's advice and the OZP into account; the scheme parameters and para. 2 criteria must also be met. GFA calculation guideline: Annex of the LandsD / SWD notes. 可批較高地積比率。 |
| 5 | Para. 2 applies to **all new developments** and **A&A works** for new or additional RCHE/RCHD. 第 2 段適用範圍。 |
| 6 | Para. 4 applies **only** to eligible premises in **new developments** under the incentive schemes. 第 4 段適用範圍。 |

## Explanation 解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    Treating the home as **non-domestic** matters because domestic site coverage and plot ratio limits in the **First Schedule** are lower, and domestic buildings need **open space**. With the modification, a care home podium can be built to non-domestic site coverage in a composite building, without the open space a domestic floor would need.

    The modification does **not** take the home out of GFA. Under the incentive schemes the extra GFA is **on top of** the normal plot ratio, but only with PlanD's support and within the OZP. Check the home's **height above ground** early: the care home regulations restrict how high a home may be (Cap. 459A s.20), and the home cannot be in a basement.

=== "中文"

    視作**非住用**的重要性在於：**附表一**的住用上蓋面積及地積比率較低，住用建築物亦須提供**休憩用地**。獲變通後，綜合用途建築物內的院舍平台可按非住用上蓋面積興建，且毋須提供住用樓層所需的休憩用地。

    變通**不會**令院舍免計總樓面面積。在鼓勵計劃下，額外樓面面積可**在一般地積比率以外**增加，但須獲規劃署支持並符合分區計劃大綱圖。應及早核對院舍**離地高度**：院舍規例限制院舍所在高度（第 459A 章第 20 條），而院舍不可設於地庫。

## Common pitfalls 常見錯誤 {#common-pitfalls}

- Assuming the modification also **exempts GFA**. It does not; only the incentive schemes allow extra plot ratio (para. 4). 以為變通可豁免總樓面面積。
- Placing the home on floors too high above ground for Cap. 459A, or partly in a **basement** (para. 2(e)). 院舍位置過高或在地庫。
- Proposing a home in a building with a **cinema** or dangerous trades (para. 2(d)). 與戲院或危險行業同一建築物。
- Expecting the plot ratio bonus for **A&A conversion** of an existing building (para. 6). 以為現有建築物改建亦可享地積比率安排。
- Not getting **SWD and FSD** comments before applying (para. 2(a)–(b)). 未取得社署及消防處意見。
