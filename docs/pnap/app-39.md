## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Submitted plans are public records; under **BO s.36G** the BA may allow inspection and copies on payment, unless the **copyright owner denies access** (para. 1).
    - BA considers the **Privacy Ordinance (Cap. 486)**, **Copyright Ordinance (Cap. 528)**, Code on Access to Information and **confidence** (para. 2).
    - Only records of **completed** works are available: the latest approved plans, calculations and documents, and submitted minor works records (para. 3), for purposes such as checking construction or compliance (para. 4). Application procedures follow (paras. 6–13).

=== "中文"

    - 呈交的圖則屬公共紀錄；除非**版權擁有人拒絕**，建築事務監督可根據**條例第 36G 條**在收費後准許查閱及發出副本（第 1 段）。
    - 建築事務監督會考慮**《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）**、**《版權條例》（第 528 章）**、《公開資料守則》及**保密責任**（第 2 段）。
    - 只提供**已完成**工程的紀錄：最新的核准圖則、計算及文件，以及已呈交的小型工程紀錄（第 3 段），用途限於確定建造或合規等事宜（第 4 段）；申請程序見第 6–13 段。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: public **inspection and copying of building records** (plans, calculations, forms) under **BO s.36G**, only for **completed** building and minor works, at the **Building Information Centre (BIC)** or online via **BRAVO**. 查閱及複印建築記錄。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Plans submitted under the BO are public records; BA may permit inspection and copies on request and fee (**s.36G**), unless copyright owners deny access. 公共記錄。 |
| 2 | BA considers the **Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance**, **Copyright Ordinance**, the **Code on Access to Information** and confidence. 考慮因素。 |
| 3 | Not until works are **completed**; available: latest approved plans, calcs and documents of completed projects; latest submitted plans / descriptions and photos of completed minor works. 只限已完成工程。 |
| 4 | Purposes: (a) construction or works matters; (b) whether works complied with the BO or other law; (c) other public-interest matters; with a **declaration**. 查閱目的。 |
| 5 | Purpose (c) needs special circumstances or the **copyright owner's authorization**. 其他目的。 |
| 6 | Services at the **BIC** or via **BRAVO**. 服務渠道。 |
| 7 | In person: **BIC1** for inspection; electronic records within **3 working days**; walk-in first come first served; paper / microfilm by appointment; copies via **BIC2**. 親身查閱。 |
| 8 | **One-Stop Service** (**BIC3**): BD finds and copies; result and fee within **10 days**. 一站式服務。 |
| 9 | Copies of **occupation permits**: **BIC4**. 佔用許可證副本。 |
| 10–11 | **BRAVO**: register online or by **BIC5**; inspect and order after paying. 網上系統。 |
| 12–13 | Fees per **B(A)R 42**; collect at BIC or **free delivery** in HK (not PO boxes, Closed Area, Lantau, outlying islands). 費用及送遞。 |
| 14 | Forms via **EFSS**, mail, email, fax or in person. 表格。 |
| 15 | **Copyright**: a new AP / RSE / RGE using the former one's plans must get the **copyright owner's consent**. 更換專業人士的版權問題。 |
