## At a glance (last version) 重點摘要（最後版本） {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - MiC needs double walls between modules and thicker walls for lifting, so GFA concession is given (para. 1).
    - **6%** of the **MiC floor area** may be disregarded from GFA on application under **BO s.42**, and is **not** counted in the **10% cap** of [APP-151](app-151.md) (para. 2).
    - Revoked if MiC is no longer used; adjusted if MiC floor area is reduced (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 組裝合成需要雙層牆及較厚外牆，故給予寬免（第 1 段）。
    - 按 **s.42** 申請，**組裝合成樓面面積的 6%** 可不計入總樓面面積，且**不受** APP-151 **10% 上限**限制（第 2 段）。
    - 不再採用則撤銷；面積減少則調整（第 3 段）。

## What it said 內容 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it said 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | MiC benefits; double walls and thicker enclosure walls | 背景 |
| 2(a) | **6%** of MiC floor area disregarded from GFA (BO s.42 application). *MiC floor area*: within the external walls of the combined modules, plus MiC balconies and joints | 6% 寬免 |
| 2(b) | Not subject to the **10% overall cap** (APP-151) | 不計上限 |
| 3 | Revoked or adjusted if MiC dropped or reduced | 撤銷或調整 |
