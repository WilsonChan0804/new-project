## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Contractors must work safely, and APs, RSEs and RGEs must ensure works under their supervision are done safely (para. 1).
    - Beyond the Supervision Code, integrate safety into management, training and **procurement**; if safety costs are not **separately priced and paid**, safety may suffer (para. 2).
    - The **Pay for Safety Scheme** (PFSS) separates safety items for pricing and payment; private developers are encouraged to adopt it (para. 3 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 承建商須以安全方式施工，認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊岩土工程師須確保其監督的工程安全進行（第 1 段）。
    - 除監督守則外，應把安全納入管理、培訓及**採購**；如安全費用未有**獨立定價及支付**，安全或受影響（第 2 段）。
    - **支付安全計劃**把安全項目獨立定價及付款；鼓勵私人發展商採用（第 3 段起）。
