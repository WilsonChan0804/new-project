## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Contractors must work safely, and APs, RSEs and RGEs must ensure works under their supervision are done safely (para. 1).
    - Beyond the Supervision Code, integrate safety into management, training and **procurement**; if safety costs are not **separately priced and paid**, safety may suffer (para. 2).
    - The **Pay for Safety Scheme** (PFSS) separates safety items for pricing and payment; private developers are encouraged to adopt it (para. 3 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 承建商須以安全方式施工，認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊岩土工程師須確保其監督的工程安全進行（第 1 段）。
    - 除監督守則外，應把安全納入管理、培訓及**採購**；如安全費用未有**獨立定價及支付**，安全或受影響（第 2 段）。
    - **支付安全計劃**把安全項目獨立定價及付款；鼓勵私人發展商採用（第 3 段起）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: private construction contracts (AP to **recommend** to clients). 私人工程合約（建議）。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contractors, AP, RSE, RGE must ensure works are carried out safely | 安全責任 |
| 2 | Beyond the TM for Supervision Plans and CoP for Site Supervision, price safety distinctly so it is not squeezed | 明確定價 |
| 3 | **PFSS** (since 1996): employer pays for certified safety items and training at **pre-priced rates** in the BQ / SoR | 計劃內容 |
| 4 | References: DEVB Site Safety Manual, Cap. 59AF, Safety Partnering Programme, CIC PFSS guidelines, LD / OSHC training | 參考 |
| 5 | AP to recommend PFSS to clients | 建議採用 |
