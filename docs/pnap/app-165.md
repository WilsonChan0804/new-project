## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - A third-party **certification body (CB)** issues a **certificate of conformity (CoC)** confirming a product meets a **Product Conformity Certification Scheme (PCCS)**; CBs must be **HKCAS**-accredited or MRA partners (para. 1).
    - Certification involves product evaluation, **factory audit** and independent lab testing of samples (para. 2); CBs run **periodic surveillance audits**; a CoC is usually valid **3 years**, then re-certification (para. 3).
    - The BA accepts a valid **CoC** for **fire rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partition walls** (HKISC passive fire protection scheme) **instead of test reports** (para. 4). Submit it with the **Schedule of Building Materials and Products** (APP-13) on completion; BD **audits** a random sample (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 第三方**認證機構**發出**符合證明書**，確認產品符合**產品合格認證計劃**；認證機構須獲 **HKCAS** 或其互認夥伴認可（第 1 段）。
    - 認證包括產品評估、**工廠審核**及獨立實驗所測試樣本（第 2 段）；認證機構定期進行**監察審核**；證明書一般有效 **3 年**，其後須重新認證（第 3 段）。
    - 建築事務監督接受**防火門組件及非承重防火間隔牆**的有效**合格證書**（香港鋼結構學會被動防火產品計劃），**代替測試報告**（第 4 段）；完工時連同 APP-13 的**建築物料及產品附表**呈交，屋宇署會**抽查**（第 5–6 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Product certification: an accredited **certification body (CB)** issues a **Certificate of Conformity (CoC)** under a product conformity certification scheme (PCCS); list on the HKCTC website. 產品認證制度。 |
| 2 | CB evaluates product information, **audits the factory** and has samples tested by an independent accredited lab. 工廠審核及抽樣測試。 |
| 3 | Periodic **surveillance audits**; a CoC is usually valid **3 years**, then re-certification; failures lead to suspension or withdrawal. 證書一般有效 3 年。 |
| 4 | BA accepts valid CoCs for **fire rated doorsets** and **non-loadbearing fire partitions** (PCCS for passive fire protection, Issue 3) **in place of test or assessment reports**. 代替測試報告。 |
| 5 | On completion submit CoCs with the **Certificate and Schedule of Building Materials and Products** (APP-13); test reports not needed. 完工時呈交。 |
| 6 | BD **randomly audits** CoCs; data go to the **Central Data Bank** (ADM-20). 抽查及中央資料庫。 |
| 7 | BD will advise owners of other PCCSs and watch for schemes for other products. 其他產品。 |
| 8 | A similar practice note is issued to registered contractors. 承建商作業備考。 |
