## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - A third-party **certification body (CB)** issues a **certificate of conformity (CoC)** confirming a product meets a **Product Conformity Certification Scheme (PCCS)**; CBs must be **HKCAS**-accredited or MRA partners (para. 1).
    - Certification involves product evaluation, **factory audit** and independent lab testing of samples (para. 2); CBs run **periodic surveillance audits**; a CoC is usually valid **3 years**, then re-certification (para. 3).
    - Certified products can support compliance submissions (later paras.).

=== "中文"

    - 第三方**認證機構**發出**符合證明書**，確認產品符合**產品合格認證計劃**；認證機構須獲 **HKCAS** 或其互認夥伴認可（第 1 段）。
    - 認證包括產品評估、**工廠審核**及獨立實驗所測試樣本（第 2 段）；認證機構定期進行**監察審核**；證明書一般有效 **3 年**，其後須重新認證（第 3 段）。
    - 經認證的產品可用作合規證明（其後各段）。
