## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The BO has **no provision for BA to certify materials or products**; BA relies on APs, RSEs, RGEs and **independent laboratories**; completion certification per APP-13 (paras. 1–2).
    - BA recognises **HOKLAS**-accredited labs and those of **MRA partners**; check each test is **within the lab's scope** (para. 3).
    - Materials normally tested by accredited labs include concrete, reinforcement, structural steel and others listed in para. 4.

=== "中文"

    - 條例**沒有條文讓建築事務監督為物料或產品發出證明**；建築事務監督倚賴認可人士、註冊結構工程師、註冊岩土工程師及**獨立實驗所**；完工時的證明見 APP-13（第 1–2 段）。
    - 建築事務監督認可 **HOKLAS** 及其**互認協議夥伴**認可的實驗所；須確保測試**在其認可範圍內**（第 3 段）。
    - 一般須由認可實驗所測試的物料包括混凝土、鋼筋、結構鋼及第 4 段所列其他物料。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: acceptability and **testing of building materials and products**, especially concrete, rebar, couplers, curtain walls and **fire rated** products. The BA issues **no certificates** for products; it relies on AP / RSE / RGE and **accredited laboratories**. 建築物料測試。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Guidance for advising manufacturers, suppliers, contractors and owners. 目的。 |
| 2 | AP / RSE / RGE / RC supervise material selection and certify compliance; the BO has **no provision for BA certificates** or reports on products; completion certification per [APP-13](app-13.md). 屋宇署不發產品證書。 |
| 3 | Labs accredited by **HOKLAS** or **MRA partners**, tests within their scope. 認可實驗室。 |
| 4 | Normally tested by accredited labs: (a) **concrete cubes and cores**; (b) **steel reinforcement**; (c) **splices / couplers**; (d) **curtain wall** systems; (e) **fire rated** products. 須測試的物料。 |
| 5 | Fire rated products meet **B(C)R 90** by reference to the **FS Code 2011**. 防火產品。 |
| 6 | Fire properties tested or assessed to FS Code standards; reports per **E16.1–E16.2**; **expired** reports not accepted. 報告須有效。 |
| 7 | Certification bodies recognised under E16.1: **HKCAS** or its MRA partners. 認證機構。 |
