## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The **Wind Code 2019** (Sep 2019) is a comprehensive wind code; the **Explanatory Notes** are guidance only, not part of the Code (para. 1).
    - After the grace period ended **31 Mar 2021**, only 2019 Code designs are accepted, though projects already approved under the **2004 Code** may continue with it (para. 2).
    - A&A after the grace period: follow **APP-117** for new elements and checks of existing structure (para. 3). Consent may be refused if more than **2 years** have passed since approval (BO s.16(3)(d)) (para. 4). Amendments to the Code and the Explanatory Notes in **Dec 2023** (para. 5). A wind tunnel test that departs from Section 6 needs its **methodology accepted** first (**45 days**), with a **QA plan**, witnessed by the RSE or an **independent engineer** (MHKIE, **5 years**), and a signed report (paras. 6–9).

=== "中文"

    - **2019 年風力守則**（2019 年 9 月）是全面的風力守則；**說明附註**只屬指引，不是守則的一部分（第 1 段）。
    - 寬限期於 **2021 年 3 月 31 日**屆滿後，只接受按 2019 年守則的設計，但已按 **2004 年守則**獲批的項目可繼續沿用（第 2 段）。
    - 寬限期後的改動加建：新構件及現有結構檢核按 **APP-117**（第 3 段）。批准後超過 **2 年**可拒絕同意動工（第 16(3)(d) 條）（第 4 段）；守則及解釋附註於 **2023 年 12 月**修訂（第 5 段）；不按第 6 節進行的風洞測試須先獲接納**測試方法**（**45 天**），附**品質保證計劃**，由註冊結構工程師或**獨立工程師**（香港工程師學會會員、**5 年**經驗）見證，並提交簽署報告（第 6–9 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | The **Wind Code 2019** with **Explanatory Notes** (not part of the Code). 2019 年風力守則。 |
| 2 | Effective **30 Sep 2019**; 2004 Code accepted only until **31 Mar 2021**; plans approved before then may keep their original code for later submissions. 寬限期。 |
| 3 | A&A after the grace period: new elements and checks of existing structure per **APP-117**. 改動加建。 |
| 4 | Consent may be refused if more than **2 years** after approval (BO s.16(3)(d)). 兩年限制。 |
| 5 | Amendments: Code (App. A) and EN (App. B), **Dec 2023**, incl. hoarding and covered walkway net pressures **0.63–0.77 kPa** (≤ 2.5 m to 10 m). 2023 年修訂。 |
| 6 | Wind tunnel tests not following Section 6: submit a **methodology proposal**; decision within **45 days**; ADM-19 enquiry possible. 風洞測試方法。 |
| 7 | Include a **quality assurance plan** (e.g. ASCE Manual 67, AWES QA Manual). 品質保證。 |
| 8 | Test **witnessed** by the RSE or an **independent engineer** (Corporate Member HKIE or equivalent, **5 years** experience). 見證。 |
| 9 | Report: procedures, all readings, structural and dynamic properties, and an RSE statement on supervision and compliance. 測試報告。 |

## Appendices in full 附錄詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision December 2023 (general revision). 已按 2023 年 12 月版本核對。

Extra points in the main text 正文補充:

- Para. 2 footnote 1: the "keep the original code" concession also covers plans designed with a **loading allowance for future structures**.
- Para. 5: amendments came from BD's **Technical Committee**, which collects industry feedback on the 2019 Code and EN.
- Para. 6: the RSE may first make an [ADM-19](adm-19.md) pre-submission enquiry with a preliminary test proposal; BD may hold pre-submission conferences with the RSE and the laboratory.
- Para. 8: the independent engineer must **not** be staff of the wind tunnel laboratory, and must check the QA procedures were followed.

### Appendix B: amendments to the Explanatory Notes (Dec 2023) 釋義修訂

| Item 項目 | Change 修訂 |
|---|---|
| EN cl. 2.2.3 | Existing rule kept: across-wind base moment check **not needed** if H/min(B,D) < 5, H < 100 m and N > 0.5 Hz. **New**: for several towers on a common podium, the across-wind fundamental frequency may be found by (a) the tower alone extended to the base, ignoring the podium; (b) the tower plus the podium within the least of **20 m**, **three bays**, and the midline to the next tower, measured from the tower's wall/column edge, if there are no large slab openings there (openings may be considered separately); or (c) the tower plus a podium portion per recognised engineering principles. Or use the dominant across-wind mode from an integrated model of all towers and podium, especially where (b) can't define the tributary extent. 多座塔樓共用平台的基本頻率。 |
| EN cl. 2.5 | Hoarding, covered walkways, contractor sheds, bamboo sheds, tents, marquees (non-residential): at least **37%** of Code pressures (unchanged). **New Table 2-1** for construction site hoarding and covered walkways: z ≤ **2.5 m: 0.63 kPa**; **5 m: 0.70 kPa**; **10 m: 0.77 kPa** (all-inclusive design net pressure). Notes: interpolate linearly; C<sub>p</sub> and S<sub>s</sub> included; beneficial self-weight of steel may be counted; add a topography factor where local topography is adverse. 圍板及有蓋行人道設計淨壓力。 |
| EN cl. 4.3.1 | "Accidental dominant open scenario" is now "**not a compulsory requirement**" of the Code (was "out of scope"). |
| EN cl. 6.4 | Without a building removal investigation, shelter benefit is limited to **80% of the loads of the Standard Method**, compared as **total base moments** (was "80% of the along-wind value"). 遮擋效益上限。 |
| Figure B-1 | Correction in the top-left figure (see the PDF). |
| Appendix C2 | Typo: the outlying high damping value was for a **250 m** cubic building (was 200 m). |
| Appendix E3.1 | Torsion procedure updated to refer to Figure E-7(a)–(d), resultants including F<sub>4-i</sub>, Figure E-7(e) and (f). |

### Appendix C: wind tunnel methodology proposal 風洞測試方法建議書內容 (12/2023)

| Item 項目 | Essential information 必要資料 (2019 Code clause) |
|---|---|
| General | Laboratory name and location; site location; building plans and elevations with floor levels; H/B and H/D ratios and frequency modes; test scope (wind load, acceleration, cladding / curtain wall pressures, etc.) (cl. 6.1.1, 6.1.2) |
| Topographic model | Scale, extent, materials; blockage ratio; approach wind profiles (mean speed and turbulence intensity); number and interval of wind directions (cl. 6.1.3, 6.1.5, 6.1.6) |
| Proximity model | Scale and extent; source of surrounding building data; materials; Reynolds number and blockage ratio; wind directions; matching height of peak gust pressure; topographic multiplier; matching peak gust pressure of the 2019 Code (cl. 6.1.3–6.1.6) |
| Instrumentation | Measurement method; sampling frequency, length and full-scale duration; number and density of pressure taps; extras (cl. 6.1.1, 6.3) |
| Wind climate / directionality | Source of climate data; directionality factors; analysis method (Sector, Up-crossing, Storm Passage or other) (cl. 6.5.2) |
| Testing configurations | Existing configuration; **removal configuration** with the principle used and number of buildings / lots removed (cl. 6.4) |
| Post-processing | Theory (modal analysis / random vibration, pressure integration, peak cladding pressure, peak acceleration); load combination factors and number of load cases; vertical distribution of storey loads (cl. 6.6) |
| Quality assurance | Plan per Appendix D |

### Appendix D: quality assurance plan 品質保證計劃內容 (12/2023)

| Item 項目 | Essential information 必要資料 |
|---|---|
| 1. Models | Fabrication and checking procedures; limits and tolerances of modelling, testing and analysis; inspection and endorsement by the RSE (with flowchart) |
| 2. Instrumentation | Calibration details and intervals; checks that equipment (including wind profile generation) works before and during the test |
| 3. Data analysis / reporting | Sample calculations to check software input and output; verification of results and error control; reporting and follow-up (e.g. re-testing); full records including photos of set-up, model and apparatus; preparation, checking and endorsement of reports |
| 4. Supervision | Names and posts of supervisors for each stage; monitoring and checking frequency; name of the **independent engineer** acting for the RSE |
