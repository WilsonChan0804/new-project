## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Soil nails are hidden once built, so reliable **supervision, testing and certification** at each stage are needed, with acceptance criteria (paras. 1–2).
    - CEDD LPM particular specifications, standard drawings, checklists and GEO (2003) guidance on **non-destructive testing of nail length** may be used (para. 3).
    - Design for **buildability**: nails **> 20 m** risk loose or permeable ground and buried streams, causing hole collapse and **grout loss** (para. 4). Difficult ground: loose fill, colluvium, rockfill, soil pipes, buried streams, open joints, mixed soil and rock, **groundwater flow** (para. 5). The RGE's site formation submission must include any **site trial**, the **pull-out test** procedure and the **non-destructive test** method for nail length (para. 6). Supervision qualifications and NDT details are in the **TM and Site Supervision Code** (para. 7).

=== "中文"

    - 泥釘建成後不可見，因此各階段須有可靠的**監督、測試及核證**程序，並訂明驗收準則（第 1–2 段）。
    - 可參考土木工程拓展署防止山泥傾瀉計劃的特別規格、標準圖則、檢查表，以及土力工程處（2003）的**泥釘長度非破壞性測試**指引（第 3 段）。
    - 設計須考慮**可建性**：**> 20 米**的泥釘較易遇到鬆散或透水地層及埋藏河道，導致鑽孔塌陷及**灌漿流失**（第 4 段）。困難地層包括鬆散填土、崩積土、石填、土管、埋藏河道、張開節理、泥石交替及**地下水流**（第 5 段）；註冊岩土工程師的地盤平整呈交須列明**現場試驗**、**拉拔測試**程序及檢驗泥釘長度的**非破壞性測試**方法（第 6 段）；監督人員資歷及測試細節見**技術備忘錄及地盤監督守則**（第 7 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Soil nails are hidden once built: reliable **supervision, testing and certification** are needed. 泥釘品質監督。 |
| 3 | Use CEDD's particular specifications, standard drawings and supervision checklists (LPM Programme), and GEO guidance. 參考資料。 |
| 4 | Design for **buildability**: nails **over 20 m** more likely to meet loose or permeable ground and buried streams (hole collapse, **grout loss**). 可建性。 |
| 5 | Difficult ground includes loose fill, colluvium, rockfill, soil pipes, buried streams, open discontinuities, mixed soil and rock, **groundwater flow** (may need drainage). 困難地層。 |
| 6 | Site formation submission must include: (i) **site trial** if needed; (ii) **pull-out test** procedure; (iii) **NDT** method to verify installed nail length. 呈交內容。 |
| 7 | Supervision requirements, site supervisor qualifications and NDT details: **TM for Supervision Plans** and the **Site Supervision Code**. 監督要求。 |
