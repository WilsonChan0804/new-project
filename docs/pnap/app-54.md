## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - A retaining wall is a permanent structure retaining earth or fill; anchor plates on rock or earth faces are not. Design to **B(C)R 23**, APP-63 and **Geoguide 1**; rock is not "earth" for earth-pressure calculations (paras. 1–2).
    - Except minor walls (B(C)R 22), **drainage and filters** follow B(C)R 24: clean, durable, clay-free material; **geotextile filters** acceptable in non-aggressive conditions (Geoguide 1 s.8.5, GEO Pub. 1/93) (paras. 3–4).
    - **Backfill** to Geoguide 1 and App. A Table 1; no peat or organics, toxic, combustible, metal, rubber or plastic, volume-change (marine mud, swelling clay) or soluble material (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 擋土牆指承托泥土或填料的永久構築物；承托岩面或泥面的錨板不算；按 **B(C)R 第 23 條**、APP-63 及**岩土指南第 1 號**設計；計算土壓時岩石不視為「泥土」（第 1–2 段）。
    - 除小型擋土牆（B(C)R 第 22 條）外，**排水及濾層**須按第 24 條：潔淨、耐用、不含黏土的物料；非侵蝕性環境可用**土工布濾層**（岩土指南第 1 號第 8.5 節、GEO 刊物 1/93）（第 3–4 段）。
    - **回填料**須符合岩土指南第 1 號及附錄 A 表 1；不得含泥炭或有機物、有毒、易燃、金屬、橡膠或塑膠、體積易變（海泥、膨脹黏土）或可溶物料（第 5–6 段）。

## Paragraph by paragraph and Appendix A (full) 逐段及附錄 A 詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision February 2021 (general revision). 已按 2021 年 2 月版本核對。

| Para. 段 | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| 1 | A retaining wall is a **permanent structure on land retaining earth or fill**. Anchor plates supporting a rock or earth face are **not** retaining walls. |
| 2 | Design and construction per **B(C)R s. 23**, [APP-63](app-63.md) and **Geoguide 1** (2nd Ed.). Rock faces are **not "earth"** for earth pressure calculations. |
| 3 | Except minor retaining walls (B(C)R s. 22), drainage and filters per **B(C)R s. 24**. Filters are clean, sound, durable and free of clay and organics. In non-aggressive conditions, **geotextile filters** of resistant polymers may replace granular filters in permanent works. |
| 4 | Filter design: Geoguide 1 s. 8.5; GEO Publication No. 1/93. |
| 5 | Backfill in-situ properties meet the design (Geoguide 1) and Table 1. |
| 6 | Backfill must **not** contain: (a) peat, vegetation, timber or degradable matter; (b) dangerous or toxic material; (c) combustible material; (d) metal, rubber, plastic or synthetics; (e) material with large volume change (marine mud, swelling clay, collapsible soil); (f) soluble material. |
| 7 | Not chemically aggressive (e.g. excess **sulphate** attacks concrete and steel). |
| 8 | Remedial or preventive works to existing walls: use past performance; designs to **Geotechnical Manual for Slopes s. 7.3.3** are acceptable. |
| 9 | **Monitor during construction**: wall performance and effects on groundwater, the site, and nearby buildings, structures, land, streets and services. |
| 10 | Demolishing a retaining wall needs **demolition plans** and documents under **B(A)R 8(3) and (4)**. |
| 11 | Routine maintenance of slopes and walls may be minor works ([APP-147](app-147.md)). |

### Appendix A, Table 1: backfill grading and plasticity (Rev. 2/2021) 回填物料級配及塑性要求

| Requirement 要求 | Crushed rock products 碎石 | Soil 泥土 |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum size 最大粒徑 | **200 mm** | **75 mm** (note 2) |
| % passing 63 µm sieve | **0** | **0–45** |
| Coefficient of uniformity 均勻系數 | **≥ 5** | **≥ 50** (note 4) |
| Liquid limit 液限 | N/A | **≤ 45%** (note 5) |
| Plasticity index 塑性指數 | N/A | **≤ 20%** (note 5) |

Notes:
1. Tests per Geospec 3 sections 6 and 8 ([APP-64](app-64.md)).
2. Soil backfill may include up to **5%** rock fragments ≤ 200 mm if they don't affect compaction or damage the wall.
3. Maximum particle size **≤ ⅔ of the compacted layer thickness**.
4. The value of 50 applies to soils from in-situ rock weathering. Alluvial sands and gravels need a coefficient of uniformity ≥ 5 and must **not be gap-graded**.
5. LL and PI checks are not needed if particles < 63 µm are **< 30%** by weight.
6. Grade **without dispersants**.
