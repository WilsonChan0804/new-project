## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - A retaining wall is a permanent structure retaining earth or fill; anchor plates on rock or earth faces are not. Design to **B(C)R 23**, APP-63 and **Geoguide 1**; rock is not "earth" for earth-pressure calculations (paras. 1–2).
    - Except minor walls (B(C)R 22), **drainage and filters** follow B(C)R 24: clean, durable, clay-free material; **geotextile filters** acceptable in non-aggressive conditions (Geoguide 1 s.8.5, GEO Pub. 1/93) (paras. 3–4).
    - **Backfill** to Geoguide 1 and App. A Table 1; no peat or organics, toxic, combustible, metal, rubber or plastic, volume-change (marine mud, swelling clay) or soluble material (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 擋土牆指承托泥土或填料的永久構築物；承托岩面或泥面的錨板不算；按 **B(C)R 第 23 條**、APP-63 及**岩土指南第 1 號**設計；計算土壓時岩石不視為「泥土」（第 1–2 段）。
    - 除小型擋土牆（B(C)R 第 22 條）外，**排水及濾層**須按第 24 條：潔淨、耐用、不含黏土的物料；非侵蝕性環境可用**土工布濾層**（岩土指南第 1 號第 8.5 節、GEO 刊物 1/93）（第 3–4 段）。
    - **回填料**須符合岩土指南第 1 號及附錄 A 表 1；不得含泥炭或有機物、有毒、易燃、金屬、橡膠或塑膠、體積易變（海泥、膨脹黏土）或可溶物料（第 5–6 段）。
