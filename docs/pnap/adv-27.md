## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Natural streams and rivers (perennial or intermittent, with natural beds and banks) have ecological and landscape value; **Ecologically Important Streams (EIS)** are listed by AFCD (paras. 1–2).
    - Follow **ETWB TC(W) No. 5/2005** to avoid or minimise impacts of construction works on streams (para. 3 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 天然河溪（常年或間歇性，河床及河岸屬天然）具生態及景觀價值；**具重要生態價值的河溪**由漁護署公布（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須按**環境運輸及工務局技術通告（工務）第 5/2005 號**避免或減低建造工程對河溪的影響（第 3 段起）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: works **in or affecting** natural streams / rivers (perennial or intermittent), not nullahs, drainage channels, fishponds. 天然河溪；不包括人工渠道。
