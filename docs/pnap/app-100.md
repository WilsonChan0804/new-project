## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - GRP water tanks are building works. GRP is strong but **flexible**, so design is driven by **stiffness**, **creep** and environment (paras. 1–2).
    - Structural plans (except small tanks, para. 7): design standards, material specs, general arrangement with **max capacity**, dimensions and thicknesses, fabrication details, support fixings, plus **calculations** for tank and base (para. 3).
    - Before consent: lab reports showing panels withstand **6 × design hydrostatic pressure**, and the manufacturer's documents (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 玻璃強化聚酯水箱屬建築工程；物料強度高但**剛度低**，設計受**剛度**、**蠕變**及環境影響（第 1–2 段）。
    - 結構圖則（小型水箱見第 7 段）：設計標準、物料規格、顯示**最大容量**、尺寸及厚度的總體佈置、製造細節、支座固定，並附水箱及底座的**計算**（第 3 段）。
    - 申請同意前：提交化驗報告，證明板件能承受**設計靜水壓力的 6 倍**，以及製造商文件（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **glass reinforced polyester (GRP) water tanks** (building works under the BO). Simplified rules for tanks **≤ 9 m³ and ≤ 2 m head**, simpler still for **indoor ≤ 4 m³ and ≤ 1.2 m**; some works in existing buildings are **minor works**. 玻璃纖維水缸。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | GRP tanks are BO building works; guidance on plans and tests. 屬建築工程。 |
| 2 | High strength but **low stiffness**: design governed by stiffness; allow for **creep** and environment. 剛度控制設計。 |
| 3 | Plans: (a) design standards; (b) material specs; (c) general arrangement, **max. capacity**, dimensions, thicknesses; (d) fabrication of main parts; (e) **fixing and supports**; with calculations for tank and base. 圖則內容。 |
| 4 | Before consent: (a) test reports showing panel properties and resistance to **6 × design hydrostatic pressure**; (b) manufacturer's **QC** details. 動工前文件。 |
| 5 | After completion, filled to overflow: (a) **leakage**: none visible after **48 h**; (b) **deflection** after **2 h** full: sides **≤ 1.0%** of tank depth, bottom **≤ 10 mm**. 性能測試。 |
| 6 | Submit test reports with the AP / RSE's signed **statement**. 提交報告。 |
| 7 | **≤ 9 m³ and ≤ 2 m head**: paras. 3–5 normally not needed; show location, dimensions, capacity, weight; standards and materials; supports with calculations. 小型水缸。 |
| 8 | **Indoor ≤ 4 m³ and ≤ 1.2 m**: only location, dimensions, capacity, weight, plus floor calculations. 室內小水缸。 |
| 9 | Certain GRP tank works in existing buildings are **minor works** ([APP-147](app-147.md)). 小型工程。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A roof tank 3 × 2 × 2 m (12 m³, 2 m deep): full requirements apply (over 9 m³). After assembly, fill to overflow: no leaks after 48 h; side deflection ≤ **20 mm** (1% of 2 m) and bottom ≤ **10 mm** after 2 h. 天台水缸例子。
