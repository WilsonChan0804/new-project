## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Offices need lighting and ventilation per **B(P)R 30 and 31**; where reg. 31 cannot be met, **B(P)R 34(a)** requires mechanical ventilation supplying fresh air to all parts at **≥ 5 air changes per hour** (para. 1).
    - For **large or complex** centralised, ducted systems serving many rooms or floors, engage a **Registered Professional Engineer (Building Services)** (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 辦公室須按 **B(P)R 第 30 及 31 條**提供照明及通風；如未能符合第 31 條，**第 34(a) 條**規定須設機械通風，向房間各部分供應新鮮空氣，**每小時換氣 ≥ 5 次**（第 1 段）。
    - 如屬服務多個房間或樓層的**大型或複雜**中央風管系統，應聘請**註冊專業工程師（屋宇裝備）**提供意見（第 2 段）。
