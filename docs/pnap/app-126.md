## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Signboards (display surfaces and fixings) are **building works** (para. 1). Projection over a street is allowed under **BO s.31(1)(aa)** if not a danger to structure, traffic or the public, not a fire hazard, and not obstructing traffic signs, MOE / MOA, or required lighting and ventilation (para. 2).
    - Comply with construction (App. A), position and dimensions (App. B), **FSD / TD / HyD** (Apps C–E), airport height, OZP, railway, harbour-facing and monument requirements; checklist App. F (para. 3).
    - Within the site: signboards exceeding App. B limits count as **buildings** (GFA and SC); signboards > **3 m** on ground or roof must not create enclosed floor space unless counted in GFA / SC (paras. 4–5).
    - Not minor works or exempted: needs **approval and consent**, built by an **RGBC**; no supervision plan normally, but provide public safety measures during erection (paras. 6–7).
    - **Fast-track**: AP and RSE certify compliance; **≥ 11 sets** of plans with App. I certificate and App. F checklist via centralised processing (ADM-2); plans processed within **30 days** (paras. 8–10). Minor works route: para. 16.

=== "中文"

    - 招牌（展示面及固定件）屬**建築工程**（第 1 段）；如不危及結構、交通或公眾，不構成火警危險，亦不阻擋交通標誌、逃生途徑／救援通道或規定的照明通風，可根據**條例第 31(1)(aa) 條**伸出街道（第 2 段）。
    - 須符合構造（附錄 A）、位置及尺寸（附錄 B）、**消防處／運輸署／路政署**（附錄 C–E）、機場高度限制、分區計劃大綱圖、鐵路、面向海港及古蹟的要求；清單見附錄 F（第 3 段）。
    - 地盤內：超出附錄 B 限制的招牌視為**建築物**（計算樓面面積及上蓋面積）；地面或屋頂上高於 **3 米**的招牌不得形成圍封樓面空間，除非計入樓面面積／上蓋面積（第 4–5 段）。
    - 非小型工程或豁免工程：須取得**批准及同意**，由**註冊一般建築承建商**施工；一般毋須監工計劃書，但施工時須有公眾安全措施（第 6–7 段）。
    - **快速處理**：認可人士及註冊結構工程師核證合規；以中央處理方式（ADM-2）呈交 **≥ 11 套**圖則，連附錄 I 證明書及附錄 F 清單；**30 天**內處理（第 8–10 段）；小型工程途徑見第 16 段。

## Paragraph by paragraph (full) 逐段詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

| Para. 段 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Signboards (display surface and fixings) for advertisement, announcement or display are **building works** under the BO. |
| 2 | Projection over a street allowed under **BO s.31(1)(aa)** if not: a danger to structure, a traffic hazard, a fire hazard, obstructing traffic signs / signals / markings / monitoring equipment, reducing **MoE or firefighting access**, reducing required **lighting / ventilation**, or a danger to the public. |
| 3 | Comply with **App. A** (construction), **App. B** (position / dimension), **App. C–E** (FSD, TD, HyD); airport height (Cap. 301), OZP, railway protection, harbour-facing / airport-vicinity illumination, monuments; submission checklist **App. F**. |
| 4 | Within site: wall / projecting signboards **exceeding App. B** limits are treated as a **building** (GFA and SC accountable). |
| 5 | Signboards **> 3 m** high on ground or roof must not create enclosed floor space unless counted in GFA / SC (or occupied by machinery / ducts). |
| 6 | Unless done as **minor works** or **designated exempted works**, prior **approval and consent** needed; details per **App. G**; erected by an **RGBC**; vinyl fixing detail **App. H**. |
| 7 | **Supervision plan** generally not required (unless a new building), but public safety measures (shoring, platforms) must be provided. |
| 8–10 | **Fast track**: AP / RSE certify compliance; **≥ 11 sets** of plans with **App. I** certificate and **App. F** checklist (ADM-2 centralised processing); processed in **30 days**; unconventional / non-conforming cases excluded — AP told within **28 days**. |
| 11 | Consent may be applied for **concurrently** ([ADM-19](adm-19.md) criteria). |
| 12 | Fixing to **common parts**: DMC, BMO liabilities; consent of management / OC / owners. |
| 13 | Misrepresentation or misconduct: BO s.4, 7, 40 sanctions. |
| 14 | No land title; **LandsD** permission (App. J) esp. over government land; **EMSD** (App. K); other legislation (App. L). |
| 15 | Light pollution: follow the ENB / EPD / EMSD *Guidelines on Industry Best Practices for External Lighting Installations*. |
| 16 | **Minor works** alternative ([B(MW)R](../regulations/bmwr-123n.md) Sch. 1, [APP-147](app-147.md)). |
| 17 | Display the **BD reference number** (e.g. BD 2/9876/10) — as for MW ([APP-147](app-147.md)) and validated signboards ([APP-155](app-155.md)). |
| 18 | **Maintenance**: owner given a maintenance manual (**App. M**); inspections **annually and after typhoons**. |
| 19 | Provide **anchorages** for future signboards in new buildings, shown on plans. |
| 20 | From **1 Sep 2021**, give signboard information per **App. N** with BA12 / 13 / 14 for the GeoInfo Map database. |

### Appendix A – construction requirements 構造要求

| Topic 主題 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| Supports 支承 | Fixed to **approved structural members**; never to unauthorised structures or **another signboard**; **no cantilevered slabs** (any size); no projecting structures / balconies (except small signboards per App. G 2(c)) |
| Design 設計 | Structural calculations for frame, display surface and fixings; loads per B(C)R; **full design wind pressure** for the frame; check overall building stability where a projecting signboard is **≥ 10 %** of the building's wind exposure area; allowable stresses **+25 %** for wind only |
| Frames 支架 | Not impair supporting members; members assessed (in-situ strength, photos); laterally tied — **guy wires are not structural members**; **through bolts** preferred to anchors |
| Vinyl surfaces 膠布 | Data: dimensions, elongation, breaking and adhesive strength, weight, tie capacity / spacing; FoS **2**; wind **70 %** of design pressure for vinyl and its fixings; **full** pressure for other surfaces incl. **LED boards** |
| Materials 物料 | B(C)R quality; avoid bi-metallic corrosion; blast-clean / wire-brush and paint steel; steel yield **≥ 250 MPa**; qualified welders; **stainless mechanical anchors**, one of each type **pull-out tested to 1.5 × safe tensile capacity**, records submitted on completion |
| Fire 防火 | Frame **non-combustible**; display surface metal or non-combustible non-brittle — otherwise plastics, vinyl, LED: **Class 2** surface spread of flame (BS 476-7) or NFPA 701 Test Method 2 (vinyl); wood: **Class 3** + BS EN 1995-1-2; combustible wall signboards: openings behind protected by fixed lights or blocked to the floor's FRR (C10.2 infill then not needed); FS Code Part E |

### Appendix B – position and dimensions 位置及尺寸

| Rule 規則 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| General 一般 | No obstruction of **MoE, EVA, firefighting access**, required **lighting / ventilation / open space**, or trees |
| **Clearance** 淨空 | Within **1 m** of the footpath edge or over a carriageway: **≥ 5.8 m**; over a footpath: **≥ 3.5 m** (**≥ 2.5 m** if projecting **≤ 600 mm**); over a tramway: **≥ 7 m** |
| Street centre line 街道中線 | No part within **1.5 m** horizontally of the street centre line; **≥ 3 m** from an opposite signboard |
| Spacing 間距 | Signboards projecting **> 600 mm**: **≥ 2.4 m** laterally from adjacent projecting signboards (incl. guy wires, frames), throughout the height; **≥ 1.2 m** from the common boundary with an adjoining lot |
| SBD setback 後移範圍 | No signboard projecting > 600 mm in an [APP-152](app-152.md) setback area up to **15 m** above street level |
| **Wall signboard** 牆身招牌 | Projects **≤ 600 mm**; at shopfront head structurally independent of roller shutters / A/C; enclosed space not for storage |
| **Projecting signboard** 伸出式招牌 | Projects **> 600 mm**, max. **4.2 m**; within a virtual prism of **≤ 40 m²** vertical planar area and **≤ 600 mm** width; several in the same vertical plane: total **≤ 40 m²** and total height **≤ 20 m** (existing + proposed) |
| **Roof signboard** 天台招牌 | **≥ 1.5 m** from the inside face of the parapet / curb; setback accessible |

<small>Not "external walls" for App. B: B(P)R 7 projections, canopies, outer walls of balconies outside the site, APP-150 A/C platforms, APP-2 curtain walls. App. B 所指外牆不包括上述構件。</small>

### Appendices C–E – FSD, TD, HyD 消防處、運輸署、路政署

| Dept. 部門 | Requirements 要求 |
|---|---|
| **FSD** (App. C) | Horizontal clearance **≥ 4.5 m** from central divider / highway structure / opposite kerb; headroom **5.8 m** over carriageway, **5 m** above adjacent elevated roads; **2.4 m** lateral between projecting signboards (nothing in that gap above or below); **1.5 m** from centre line; **no lateral / diagonal guy wires** (fire ladder access); no lamps on combustibles; HV parts out of reach; roof exits and **openable windows unobstructed**; **fireman's switch** **2.5–3 m** above ground, below the sign or near the main entrance, out of public reach, up OFF / down ON, white 300 × 250 mm board, 50 mm red border, "SIGNBOARD – FIREMAN'S SWITCH / 招牌 – 消防員開關掣"; prevent welding sparks; extend hydrant / hose reel, extinguishers and sprinklers to signboards not wholly non-combustible (not for MW / exempted works) |
| **TD** (App. D) | Not over **expressways** (unless Commissioner's permission) or roads **≥ 70 km/h**; not confusable with traffic signs; not obstruct signs, signals, CCTV, VMS; moving images not to distract drivers (consult TD); not at junctions, roundabouts, crossings, curves |
| **HyD** (App. E) | Not fixed to / obstruct street lights, signs, highway structures, trees, furniture; not above **expressways and trunk roads**; **2 m** clearance to highway structures, **3 m** to street lighting; outside the **144°** cone (72° from vertical) of street light beams — site survey of lamp posts |
