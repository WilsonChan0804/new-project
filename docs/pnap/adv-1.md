## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Avoid **asbestos-containing material (ACM)**; removal only by **registered asbestos personnel** (para. 1).
    - Use in building operations is prohibited (Cap. 59AD); import, use and supply banned under **APCO (Cap. 311)** from **4 Apr 2014**; existing ACM unaffected (para. 2).
    - Where ACM is present or suspected (UBW clearance, repair, demolition): appoint a **registered asbestos consultant** early, test samples at a **registered asbestos laboratory**, and submit investigation report and abatement plan to **EPD** (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 避免使用**含石棉物料**；只可由**註冊石棉人員**移除（第 1 段）。
    - 建築工序禁止使用（第 59AD 章）；自 **2014 年 4 月 4 日**起根據**《空氣污染管制條例》（第 311 章）**禁止進口、使用及供應；現有建築物的含石棉物料不受影響（第 2 段）。
    - 如有或懷疑有含石棉物料（清拆違建、維修、拆卸）：及早委任**註冊石棉顧問**，由**註冊石棉化驗所**測試樣本，並向**環保署**提交調查報告及消減計劃（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: works where **ACM is present or suspected**: demolition, repair, **UBW clearance**, A&A of older buildings. Low-risk non-friable ACM may be exempt from consultant / contractor (GN 3021, 3022 of 1997). 涉及或懷疑含石棉的工程。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Avoid ACM; removal only by **registered asbestos personnel** | 避免石棉 |
| 2 | Use banned under **Cap. 59AD**; from **4 Apr 2014** import, use and supply banned under **APCO Cap. 311** (existing ACM not affected) | 禁止使用 |
| 3(a)–(b) | Appoint a **registered asbestos consultant** early for investigation; samples to a registered laboratory | 調查 |
| 3(c) | ACM confirmed: submit to **EPD** an **Asbestos Abatement Plan** and/or **Operation and Maintenance Plan** | 清除計劃 |
| 3(d)–(g) | **Registered asbestos contractor** under consultant supervision; **28-day notice** to EPD; worker protection (LD); proper waste disposal | 施工要求 |
| 4 | Abate **before other building works**; keep scavengers out until ACM inventoried, labelled, segregated; consultant certifies completion and notifies EPD | 先清除後施工 |
| 5–6 | Advice: LD Occupational Safety and Health Branch; EPD Asbestos Management and Control Section (registers) | 查詢 |
| 7 | Exemption for low-risk non-friable ACM | 豁免 |
| 8–9 | References: LD and EPD codes, PPECC Practice Note 2/97, CoP for Demolition | 參考 |
