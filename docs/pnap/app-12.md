## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Permanent **prestressed ground anchors** need long-term monitoring that owners rarely sustain, so BD treats them as **short-lived temporary works** and may refuse them in permanent buildings (para. 1).
    - Where exceptionally accepted, follow **App. A**: GEOSPEC 1 Model Specification and the GEO **Prior Approval System (PASA)** for proprietary anchor systems (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 永久**預應力地錨**須長期監測，業主甚少能持續執行，因此屋宇署視之為**短暫的臨時工程**，可拒絕將其納入永久建築物（第 1 段）。
    - 在例外情況下接受時，須按**附錄 A**：GEOSPEC 1 標準規格及土力工程處對專利地錨系統的**預先批核制度（PASA）**（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **permanent prestressed ground anchors** in building works (site formation, retaining structures). BD treats anchors needing long-term monitoring as **short-lived temporary works** and may refuse them; accepted only in **exceptional circumstances** (para. 1). 永久預應力地錨只在特殊情況下接受。

    **Not here 不在此列**: temporary anchors for ELS during construction (normal ELS submissions). 施工期間的臨時地錨。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Para. 1 | Permanent anchors impose **long-term monitoring** cost and hard remedial works; owners rarely comply, so BD regards them as **temporary works** and may refuse them in permanent buildings. 視作臨時工程。 |
| Para. 2 | If accepted, follow **Appendix A**. 須按附錄 A。 |
| App. A 1–2 | Follow **GEOSPEC 1**; use the GEO **Prior Approval System (PASA)** of Approved Anchor Systems and Types (list from the Geotechnical Information Unit); PASA does not reduce AP / RSE duties. 預先批核制度。 |
| App. A 3 | Submission: (a) plan of location, inclination, length; (b) **Anchor Class**, working load, free length, with calculations; (c) which anchors get **Suitability / Extended Acceptance Tests**; (d) compressive movement allowance; (e) anchor pad details; (f) **long-term monitoring** schedule, access and the **maintenance party's agreement**. 呈交資料。 |
| App. A 4 | Plans and sections with boundaries and geotechnical data, RSE's calculations; adjacent utilities and structures, showing **no damage**. 鄰近設施。 |
| App. A 5 | Before consent: additional plans naming the system and types, replaceable materials, monitoring schedule and **interpretation guidelines**; installer from the **Nominated Anchor Contractors** list; s.17 conditions possible; consent may be refused. 動工前補交。 |
| App. A 6 | Keep a certified copy of the **Prior Approval Documents** on site. 文件存放地盤。 |
| App. A 7 | After installation: **as-built** drawing and records (system, materials, design loads, lengths, strata, grout, dates, stressing and test records, monitoring results, future schedule and guidelines). 竣工記錄。 |
| App. A 8 | Monitoring for the **whole service life**; consult maintenance parties first and give them as-built details. 終身監測。 |
| App. A 9 | Test **grease** in anchor heads (chloride, nitrate, sulphide); GEO **Corrigenda 1/98 and 2/98** (hexane extraction, 5 g ± 0.05 g, blank determination, ppm of the aqueous extract). 黃油測試。 |
| App. A 10 | Anchors **outside the site boundary** only in **exceptional** cases (obstruct utilities, inhibit development). 不得伸出地界。 |
| App. A 11 | Into government land: early **LandsD** permission; into private land: the adjoining **lot number on Form BA5** to show the owner's acquiescence. 越界須許可。 |
