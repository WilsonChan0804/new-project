## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - MOE Code para. 14.5(e) requires a **vision panel** in an inner room (other than a toilet) whose only exit is through another room (para. 1).
    - It may be replaced by alternatives such as a **fire alarm audible in the room** (para. 2). It does not apply to habitable rooms escaping through the **living / dining room or kitchen** of domestic flats (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 走火守則第 14.5(e) 段規定，唯一出口須經另一房間的內室（洗手間除外）須設**觀察窗**（第 1 段）。
    - 可改用替代措施，例如在房內設**可聽見的火警警報**（第 2 段）；不適用於住宅單位內經**客廳／飯廳或廚房**逃生的居住房間（第 3 段）。
