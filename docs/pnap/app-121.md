## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - MOE Code para. 14.5(e) requires a **vision panel** in an inner room (other than a toilet) whose only exit is through another room (para. 1).
    - It may be replaced by alternatives such as a **fire alarm audible in the room** (para. 2). It does not apply to habitable rooms escaping through the **living / dining room or kitchen** of domestic flats (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 走火守則第 14.5(e) 段規定，唯一出口須經另一房間的內室（洗手間除外）須設**觀察窗**（第 1 段）。
    - 可改用替代措施，例如在房內設**可聽見的火警警報**（第 2 段）；不適用於住宅單位內經**客廳／飯廳或廚房**逃生的居住房間（第 3 段）。

!!! warning "Superseded for new works 新工程已不適用"
    Listed in [APP-153 Appendix A](app-153.md#appendix-a): it does **not apply** to buildings designed to the **FS Code 2011**. Current rule: [FS Code B11.5](../codes/fsc-part-b-means-of-escape.md#clause-by-clause) (inner rooms, vision panel or alarm). Still relevant to older buildings and Cap. 502 / 572 upgrading. 按 2011 年消防守則設計的建築物不適用。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | MOE Code 1996 para. 14.5(e): **vision panel** needed in an **inner room** (not a toilet) whose only exit is through another room. 內房須設視窗。 |
| 2 | May be replaced by alternatives such as a **fire alarm audible** in the room. 可用火警鐘代替。 |
| 3 | Not for **habitable rooms in flats** escaping through the living / dining room or kitchen. 住宅單位房間不適用。 |
