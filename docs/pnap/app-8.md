## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Chimneys and flues must meet **B(C)R s.44**: **non-combustible** materials not unduly affected by heat, condensation or combustion products; positioned and shielded to avoid igniting the building; terminating so fumes do not enter windows, fresh air inlets or exhausts (paras. 1–2).
    - **Insulation** depends on fuel type and rate, nearby materials and surrounding uses: provide **calculations and justification** (BS 5854 gives guidance) (para. 3).
    - **Steel chimneys**: design to the **Steel Code 2011**, clause 13.2, with attention to **wind-excited oscillation** (simplified method for circular chimneys, cl. 13.2.8) (para. 4).
    - **Construction**: corrosion protection and avoidance of **bimetallic action** (Steel Code 13.2.7); **flashing or weather hoods** where flues pass walls or roofs; follow BS EN 1856-1 and BS 5854 (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 煙囪及煙道須符合 **B(C)R 第 44 條**：以**不可燃**物料建造，不會因熱力、凝結或燃燒產物而受損；位置及遮擋須避免燃點建築物；出口須令煙氣不會進入窗戶、新鮮空氣入口或排氣口（第 1–2 段）。
    - **隔熱**視乎燃料種類及用量、附近物料及周圍用途，須提供**計算及理據**（可參考 BS 5854）（第 3 段）。
    - **鋼煙囪**：按《2011 年鋼結構作業守則》第 13.2 條設計，注意**風致振動**（圓形煙囪可用第 13.2.8 條簡化方法）（第 4 段）。
    - **建造**：防銹及避免**雙金屬作用**（第 13.2.7 條）；煙道穿過牆身或屋頂處設**泛水或防雨罩**；按 BS EN 1856-1 及 BS 5854（第 5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: design and construction of **chimneys and flues** under **B(C)R s.44**; installation or alteration of fuel-burning equipment may also need **EPD** approval (APCO). 煙囪及煙道。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Comply with **B(C)R s.44**; detailed requirements here. 法定依據。 |
| 2 | **Non-combustible** materials not unduly affected by heat, condensation or combustion products; termination and fire safety per **s.44(2)–(4)**. 不燃物料。 |
| 3 | **Insulation** depends on fuel type and rate, nearby materials and uses; justify by calculation; guidance in **BS 5854:1980**. 隔熱計算。 |
| 4 | Steel chimneys to the **Steel Code 2011** clause **13.2**; analyse **wind-excited oscillation** aerodynamically; circular chimneys may use the simplified method in **13.2.8**. 鋼煙囪設計。 |
| 5 | Construction and corrosion: Steel Code, **BS EN 1856-1:2009**, BS 5854; (a) **corrosion protection and bimetallic action** (13.2.7, Annex A); (b) **flashing or weather hood** where it passes through walls or roofs. 防腐及泛水。 |
| 6 | Fuel-burning equipment and chimneys under the **Air Pollution Control Ordinance**; EPD prior approval may be needed; plans referred to EPD; food / offensive trade licences may add requirements. 環保署審批。 |

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Flue outlets near **windows or fresh air intakes** of the same or adjoining buildings.
        - No heat insulation calculation for kitchen or boiler flues.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 煙道出口靠近本身或毗鄰建築物的**窗戶或新鮮空氣入口**。
        - 廚房或鍋爐煙道沒有隔熱計算。
