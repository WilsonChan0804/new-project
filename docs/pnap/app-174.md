## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Amendments to the **Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016**: **App. A (Nov 2020)** and **App. B (Jun 2026)** (para. 1).
    - Nov 2020 examples: cover for corrosion now refers to the **Concrete Code 2013** (cl. 2.4.3); connections filled to prevent corrosion, cracking, spalling **and water seepage** (cl. 2.4.4.1) (App. A).

=== "中文"

    - **2016 年預製混凝土結構作業守則**的修訂：**附錄 A（2020 年 11 月）**及**附錄 B（2026 年 6 月）**（第 1 段）。
    - 2020 年 11 月例子：防銹保護層改為參照 **2013 年混凝土守則**（第 2.4.3 條）；接駁位須填實以防銹蝕、爆裂、剝落**及滲水**（第 2.4.4.1 條）（附錄 A）。

## Amendments in full 修訂詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, first issue **June 2026**. See also the [Precast Code page](../codes/precast-2016.md). 已按 2026 年 6 月版本核對。

### Appendix A: November 2020 二〇二〇年十一月

| Clause 條文 | Amendment 修訂 |
|---|---|
| 2.4.3 | Cover for **fire**: FS Code. Cover for **corrosion**: now the **Concrete Code 2013** (was B(C)R). |
| 2.4.4.1 | Connections must be filled to prevent corrosion, cracking, spalling **or water seepage**. |
| 2.6.1 | Materials: B(C)R **and** Concrete Code 2013; design properties from the Concrete Code 2013. |
| 2.6.2.1 | **Alkali-silica reaction** measures: control cement content; low-alkali cement; cement replacement such as pfa; reactive alkali **≤ 3.0 kg Na₂O eq / m³**; **new**: expert advice before using reactive aggregate; non-reactive aggregate per CS1; or limiting moisture access. The supplier gives the AP/RSE a mix design and HOKLAS certificates proving the alkali limit. |
| Fig. A6 note | Source credit added (Journal of Southeast University, May 2013). |

### Appendix B: June 2026 二〇二六年六月

| Clause 條文 | Amendment 修訂 |
|---|---|
| **2.7.9.4, Fig. 2.5a** | Bearing strength now includes a **confinement factor m** = √(A<sub>2</sub>/A<sub>1</sub>) ≤ 2: dry **0.27 m f<sub>cu</sub>** (was 0.4 f<sub>cu</sub>); bedded **0.40 m f<sub>cu</sub>** (was 0.6 f<sub>cu</sub>); cast-in steel bearing plate (each dimension ≤ 40% of the concrete) **0.80 m f<sub>cu</sub>**. This matches the Concrete Code strut-and-tie provisions ([APP-142](app-142.md) App. C). 承壓強度加入約束系數。 |
| **2.8.2.8, Table 2.7a** | Joint testing: **on-site field testing of completed panel joints and installed windows** for dampness or leakage is the most realistic test. Follow **AAMA 501.2** — see the table below. |
| Annex A | Added AAMA 501.1, 501.2, 501.3; ASTM C719, C794, C920, C1087, E283, E330, E331, E547; ANSI Z97.1; BS 6213. |

**Table 2.7a: field water test of completed joints** 接縫現場防水測試

| Item 項目 | Requirement 要求 |
|---|---|
| Samples | **100%** of joints |
| Nozzle pressure | **205–240 kPa** |
| Hose diameter | **19 mm** |
| Duration | **5 minutes per 1.5 m** of joint, moving the nozzle slowly |
| Nozzle distance | **305 mm ± 25 mm** from the test section |
| Failure | Dampness or leakage on interior surfaces during the test or **within 2 hours** after |
| If failed | Remedy all failed joints and retest until all pass |
