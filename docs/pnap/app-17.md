## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - For **B(P)R 47** (domestic buildings near retaining walls), only a **widely jointed** rock face (joint spacing **> 600 mm**) with **no unfavourably oriented joints** or defects counts as a "**massive rock face**" (para. 1).
    - Other rock slopes are treated as **cuttings under B(P)R 27** (clear space **¼ of height, min. 2.5 m**) (para. 1).
    - The Appendix sets out **site formation plan** requirements for rock faces: site investigation (mapping, drillholes, packer tests, CCTV), **qualified geotechnical engineers** for rock face surveys, and lessons from the **Fei Tsui Road** landslide (para. 2).
    - Take measures to prevent **uncontrolled rockfalls** during construction, especially where public safety is affected (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 就 **B(P)R 第 47 條**（靠近擋土牆的住用建築物）而言，只有節理間距 **> 600 毫米**、**沒有不利走向節理**或缺陷的岩面才當作「**整體岩面**」（第 1 段）。
    - 其他岩石斜坡按 **B(P)R 第 27 條路塹**處理（須留高度 **¼、最少 2.5 米**的空間）（第 1 段）。
    - 附錄訂明涉及岩面的**地盤平整圖則**要求：地盤勘測（測繪、鑽孔、壓水試驗、閉路電視）、由**合資格岩土工程師**進行岩面勘察，並吸取**翡翠道**山泥傾瀉的教訓（第 2 段）。
    - 施工期間須防止**失控墮石**，尤其影響公眾安全時（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: site formation involving **rock faces**, and the meaning of **"massive rock face"** in [B(P)R 47](../regulations/bpr-39-49e.md#clause-by-clause) (domestic buildings near retaining walls) versus [B(P)R 27](../regulations/bpr-24-37.md#clause-by-clause) (cuttings). 岩面及「整塊岩面」定義。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Para. 1 | Only a **widely-jointed** rock face (joint spacing **> 600 mm**) with **no unfavourably oriented** joints or defects is a **"massive rock face"** (B(P)R 47); other slopes fall under **B(P)R 27**. 整塊岩面定義。 |
| Para. 2 | Appendix sets submission requirements and good practice, with lessons from the **Fei Tsui Road** landslide (13 Aug 1995). 附錄要求。 |
| Para. 3 | Prevent **uncontrolled rockfalls** during construction, especially near the public. 防止落石。 |
| App. 2–3 | Site investigation (reconnaissance, aerial photos, mapping, drillholes, packer tests, impression packers, CCTV); Geotechnical Manual for Slopes Tables 5.6–5.7; Geoguides 1, 2, 4. 勘測及分析。 |
| App. 4 | Joint patterns are only known near the end of excavation, so **state assumptions** clearly on the site formation plans. 列明假設。 |
| App. 5(a) | **Controlled blasting**; **pre-split or smooth-face** methods for the final face where suitable. 控制爆破。 |
| App. 5(b) | Expanding agents: state how uncontrolled flow is prevented; assess effect on stability. 膨脹劑。 |
| App. 5(c) | Immediately after excavation, **scale** and have the face **surveyed** by a qualified geotechnical engineer / engineering geologist (e.g. RPE (Geotechnical)), including **seepages** and weak, impermeable zones (e.g. **kaolinitic clay** layers). 合資格人士勘察。 |
| App. 5(d) | Submit large-scale **engineering geological drawings** (Geoguide 3 descriptions), support and drainage details, **calculations** and **photographs**. 呈交工程地質圖。 |
| App. 6 | Fei Tsui Road: an unfavourably oriented **persistent weak, impermeable layer** was the main cause. 翡翠道山泥傾瀉教訓。 |
