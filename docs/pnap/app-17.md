## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - For **B(P)R 47** (domestic buildings near retaining walls), only a **widely jointed** rock face (joint spacing **> 600 mm**) with **no unfavourably oriented joints** or defects counts as a "**massive rock face**" (para. 1).
    - Other rock slopes are treated as **cuttings under B(P)R 27** (clear space **¼ of height, min. 2.5 m**) (para. 1).
    - The Appendix sets out **site formation plan** requirements for rock faces: site investigation (mapping, drillholes, packer tests, CCTV), **qualified geotechnical engineers** for rock face surveys, and lessons from the **Fei Tsui Road** landslide (para. 2).
    - Take measures to prevent **uncontrolled rockfalls** during construction, especially where public safety is affected (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 就 **B(P)R 第 47 條**（靠近擋土牆的住用建築物）而言，只有節理間距 **> 600 毫米**、**沒有不利走向節理**或缺陷的岩面才當作「**整體岩面**」（第 1 段）。
    - 其他岩石斜坡按 **B(P)R 第 27 條路塹**處理（須留高度 **¼、最少 2.5 米**的空間）（第 1 段）。
    - 附錄訂明涉及岩面的**地盤平整圖則**要求：地盤勘測（測繪、鑽孔、壓水試驗、閉路電視）、由**合資格岩土工程師**進行岩面勘察，並吸取**翡翠道**山泥傾瀉的教訓（第 2 段）。
    - 施工期間須防止**失控墮石**，尤其影響公眾安全時（第 3 段）。
