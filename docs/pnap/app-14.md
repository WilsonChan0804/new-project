## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Under **B(P)R 49B**, cinemas totalling **≤ 2 000 persons** may be on any storey of a **non-domestic** building or part; cinemas may share exits, foyers, waiting space, vestibules and projection rooms (para. 1).
    - **Shared exits** with other non-domestic uses are allowed if (para. 2): every cinema box has **dedicated smoke extraction** and FSI to FSD; boxes are **fire-separated** (doors of equal FRP, no protected lobby needed); foyers separated or smoke-extracted; shared exits reached from **common circulation**, not through private premises; directly connected stairs fully protected; exits sized for the **combined** occupancy; in composite buildings, **separate from domestic** exits; exits discharge to streets **≥ 4.5 m**, two exits per floor to **different thoroughfares**; **no basement** cinemas.
    - Entrance may be through circulation of adjoining non-domestic areas such as a **shopping arcade** (para. 3).
    - **Exit widths** from MOE tables; cinemas **≥ 12 m** above pavement need the higher widths; shared exits sized on the total of the cinema and the sharing area; exit stairs not to narrow downwards (paras. 4–6).

=== "中文"

    - 根據 **B(P)R 第 49B 條**，合共容納 **≤ 2 000 人**的電影院可設於**非住用**建築物或部分的任何樓層；電影院之間可共用出口、大堂、等候地方、前廳及放映室（第 1 段）。
    - 可與其他非住用部分**共用出口**，條件是（第 2 段）：每個影院設**專用排煙系統**及符合消防處要求的消防裝置；影院以**防火結構分隔**（門的耐火時效相同，毋須防護門廊）；大堂分隔或設排煙；共用出口須經**公用通道**到達，不得穿過私人處所；直接連接的樓梯須全面防護；出口按**合計**人數計算；綜合用途建築物須與**住用部分出口分開**；出口通往闊 **≥ 4.5 米**的街道，每層兩個出口通往**不同街道**；**不得設於地庫**。
    - 入口可經毗鄰非住用地方（例如**商場**）的通道（第 3 段）。
    - **出口闊度**按走火通道守則表格計算；位於行人路面以上 **≥ 12 米**的影院須按較高要求；共用出口按影院及共用地方的總人數計算；出口樓梯不得向下收窄（第 4–6 段）。

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Cinema exits leading through **shops** or private premises.
        - Exit widths sized for the cinema alone when exits are **shared** with a mall.
        - Cinema in the **same building** as a school, child care centre or elderly home (B(P)R 49B(4)).

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 影院出口須穿過**商店**或私人處所。
        - 與商場**共用**出口時只按影院人數計算闊度。
        - 影院與學校、幼兒中心或安老院位於**同一建築物**（B(P)R 第 49B(4) 條）。
