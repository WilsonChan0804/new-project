## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Bridges and highway structures built with private developments follow HyD's **Structures Design Manual for Highways and Railways**, and plans always go to **ACABAS** (appearance committee) via centralised processing (paras. 1–2).
    - **Wholly or partly within a lot**, or supported by / connected to a private building (e.g. a footbridge between two buildings, or from a building to government land): processed under the **BO** and must also meet **B(C)R** (paras. 3–4).
    - **Free-standing, wholly outside the lot** on government land or over highways: **exempt from the BO**; HyD is the controlling authority (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 私人發展附帶興建的橋樑及公路構築物須按路政署**《公路及鐵路結構設計手冊》**設計，圖則經中央處理系統送交**橋樑及有關構築物外觀諮詢委員會**審議（第 1–2 段）。
    - **全部或部分位於地段內**，或由私人建築物承托／與之相連（例如兩幢建築物之間或由建築物連往政府土地的行人天橋）：按**條例**處理，亦須符合 **B(C)R**（第 3–4 段）。
    - **完全位於地段外**、建於政府土地或公路上的獨立構築物：**豁免受條例規管**，由路政署管制（第 5–6 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **bridges and highway structures** built with private developments (e.g. under lease). **Wholly or partly in a private lot**, or supported by a private building: processed under the **BO**. **Wholly outside** the lot (free-standing on government land): **exempt from the BO**, controlled by the **Director of Highways**. 私人發展的天橋及公路結構。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Such structures may be inside, outside or partly outside the lot. 位置。 |
| 2 | Design to HyD's **Structures Design Manual for Highways and Railways**; plans examined by **ACABAS** via centralised processing. 設計手冊及外觀委員會。 |
| 3 | Under the BO if wholly / partly in a lot or supported by a private building: (a) bridges between two private buildings supported by either; (b) bridges supported by a private building at one end and government land at the other. 受條例規管情況。 |
| 4 | These must also satisfy the **B(C)R** and BD codes and PNAPs. 另須符合建造規例。 |
| 5–6 | Free-standing structures wholly on government land or over highways: **exempt**; HyD vets plans, permits works, foundation tests, materials, liaison. 路政署管制。 |
| 7 | HyD standards: (a) **Transport Planning & Design Manual Vol. 2**; (b) **General Specification for Civil Engineering Works**; (c) **Geoguide 1** walls; (d) HyD / EMSD **lighting**; (e) **Geotechnical Manual for Slopes**; (f) **landscape** treatment (consult HyD Landscape Unit). 路政署標準。 |
| 8 | Submit initially to **BD**: **3 sets** of plans, **2** of calculations, **2** of SI, forwarded to HyD; HyD certifies completion. 經屋宇署轉交。 |
| 9 | Manuals from the Government bookshop. 參考資料。 |
