## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Bridges and highway structures built with private developments follow HyD's **Structures Design Manual for Highways and Railways**, and plans always go to **ACABAS** (appearance committee) via centralised processing (paras. 1–2).
    - **Wholly or partly within a lot**, or supported by / connected to a private building (e.g. a footbridge between two buildings, or from a building to government land): processed under the **BO** and must also meet **B(C)R** (paras. 3–4).
    - **Free-standing, wholly outside the lot** on government land or over highways: **exempt from the BO**; HyD is the controlling authority (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 私人發展附帶興建的橋樑及公路構築物須按路政署**《公路及鐵路結構設計手冊》**設計，圖則經中央處理系統送交**橋樑及有關構築物外觀諮詢委員會**審議（第 1–2 段）。
    - **全部或部分位於地段內**，或由私人建築物承托／與之相連（例如兩幢建築物之間或由建築物連往政府土地的行人天橋）：按**條例**處理，亦須符合 **B(C)R**（第 3–4 段）。
    - **完全位於地段外**、建於政府土地或公路上的獨立構築物：**豁免受條例規管**，由路政署管制（第 5–6 段）。
