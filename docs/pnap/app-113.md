## At a glance (last version) 重點摘要（最後版本） {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - B(P)R 31: a prescribed window faces external air only if it faces a street or an uncovered **RHP**, with inclined planes unobstructed (para. 1).
    - Interim **modification** for tall buildings facing another block on the same site (para. 2): window faces an RHP of B(P)R 31(2) size, RHP **≥ 30 m** long, **≥ 4.5 m** clear between buildings.
    - Windows **angled ≤ 15°** from the wall accepted if a notional scheme meets B(P)R 31(2) (para. 3). Interim pending a BD consultancy (para. 4), which led to APP-130.

=== "中文"

    - B(P)R 31：訂明窗須面向街道或無上蓋的**矩形水平面**，斜面不受阻（第 1 段）。
    - 高層建築面向同一地盤另一座：窗面向符合 31(2) 的 RHP，RHP 長 **≥ 30 米**，建築物間淨距 **≥ 4.5 米**，可考慮修改（第 2 段）。
    - 窗與外牆成 **≤ 15°** 而概念方案符合 31(2)，可接受（第 3 段）。屬過渡措施，其後由 APP-130 取代（第 4 段）。

## What it said 內容 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it said 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prescribed window faces external air only via a street or an unobstructed RHP with clear inclined planes (B(P)R 31) | 訂明窗要求 |
| 2 | Modification if: (a) window faces RHP of 31(2) size; (b) RHP ≥ **30 m** long; (c) ≥ **4.5 m** clear between buildings | 可修改條件 |
| 3 | Non-compliant windows need proof of equal performance; windows angled **≤ 15°** accepted with a notional complying scheme (amended Nov 2000) | 斜角窗 |
| 4 | Interim measure pending BD's lighting and ventilation consultancy | 過渡措施 |
