## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Encourages **babycare rooms** for the public and **lactation rooms** for staff in commercial buildings (para. 1).
    - A babycare room has separate **nappy-changing** and **feeding** areas with **private breastfeeding** spaces; at least **one per building** recommended (para. 2).
    - In **common areas**, easily reached (G/F with level access or by lift), **separate from toilets**, free of foul air, with clear **directional signs** (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 鼓勵在商業樓宇為公眾提供**育嬰間**及為員工提供**哺集乳室**（第 1 段）。
    - 育嬰間須有獨立的**換尿片區**及**餵哺區**，並設**私隱哺乳間**；建議**每幢樓宇最少一間**（第 2 段）。
    - 應設於**公用地方**並方便到達（地下有平路通道或經升降機），**與洗手間分開**，避免臭氣，並有清晰**指示牌**（第 3–4 段）。
