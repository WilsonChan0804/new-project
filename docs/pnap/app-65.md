## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - B(P)R 40: common staircases serving **> 2 tenants** need adequate **natural lighting** (para. 1).
    - **Guidelines** (para. 2): clear glass; window glass per storey **≥ 1/10** of the staircase enclosure plan area; ≤ **12 storeys**: windows face **open air**; > 12 storeys: face an uncovered space **2.3 m** base × **4.5 m** deep (service lane width counts); scissor stairs: end-wall windows **≥ 1.3 m** wide, or side windows **≥ 650 mm** wide, two per storey.
    - **Waiver** of natural lighting (para. 3) if: permanent **artificial lighting ≥ 30 lux** at floor; automatic **emergency lighting ≥ 2 lux** (FSI Code); and **proper management** (management company and **DMC** maintenance clause) (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - B(P)R 第 40 條：供 **2 名以上租客**共用的樓梯須有足夠**天然照明**（第 1 段）。
    - **指引**（第 2 段）：用透明玻璃；每層樓梯窗玻璃面積 **≥ 樓梯間平面面積 1/10**；**12 層**或以下：窗面向**露天地方**；12 層以上：面向底邊 **2.3 米**、深 **4.5 米**的無遮蓋空間（可計通道闊度）；剪刀梯：端牆窗闊 **≥ 1.3 米**，或側窗闊 **≥ 650 毫米**、每層兩個。
    - **豁免**天然照明（第 3 段）的條件：永久**人工照明 ≥ 30 勒克斯**；自動**緊急照明 ≥ 2 勒克斯**（消防裝置守則）；及**妥善管理**（管理公司及**公契**保養條款）（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **common staircases** in buildings for **more than two tenants** ([B(P)R 40](../regulations/bpr-39-49e.md#clause-by-clause)): natural lighting guidelines, or **artificial lighting** instead (para. 3). 多於兩名租客共用的樓梯。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | B(P)R 40: adequate **natural lighting** to common staircases. 樓梯須天然照明。 |
| 2(a)–(b) | **Clear glass**; glass per storey **≥ 1/10** of the staircase **plan area**. 清玻璃；玻璃 ≥ 樓梯平面 1/10。 |
| 2(c) | Buildings **≤ 12 storeys**: windows face an uncovered **open air** space (B(P)R 2(1)). 12 層或以下面向露天。 |
| 2(d) | **> 12 storeys**: face an uncovered, unenclosed space with base **≥ 2.3 m** and depth **≥ 4.5 m** (service lane width counts). 12 層以上：2.3 × 4.5 米。 |
| 2(e) | **Scissor stairs**: even light to each flight, e.g. end-wall window centred, **≥ 1.3 m** wide; or side-wall windows **≥ 650 mm**, at least **2 per storey**, with a reduced separating wall. 剪刀梯。 |
| 3 | **Waiver** of natural lighting if: (i) permanent artificial lighting **≥ 30 lux** at floor; (ii) automatic **emergency lighting ≥ 2 lux** (FSI Code); (iii) proper **management** of maintenance. 可以人工照明代替。 |
| 4 | Management accepted if a **management company** maintains the systems and the duty is in the **DMC**. 公契須訂明保養。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A 20-storey block, staircase enclosure 3.0 × 5.5 m = **16.5 m²** plan: clear glass **≥ 1.65 m²** per storey, facing a space at least **2.3 m × 4.5 m**. If the stair is internal, provide **30 lux** lighting plus **2 lux** emergency lighting, with maintenance in the DMC (para. 3–4). 20 層樓梯例子。

## Key table: natural lighting or waiver 天然照明或豁免 {#key-table-natural-lighting-or-waiver}

| Option 方案 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| Natural lighting 天然照明 | Clear glass ≥ **1/10** of stair plan area per storey; open air (≤ 12 storeys) or 2.3 × 4.5 m space (> 12 storeys) |
| Waiver 豁免 | Artificial **≥ 30 lux** + emergency **≥ 2 lux** + management company and DMC maintenance clause |
