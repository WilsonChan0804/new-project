## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Subsurface conditions cannot be fully predicted, and unconventional schemes are increasingly common, so **BO s.17(1) item 6(g)** allows BA to require a documented **performance review** for certain works outside scheduled areas (para. 1).
    - Imposed as an approval or consent condition for specific sites (examples in para. 3); it must show the works were **inspected and monitored**, **design assumptions remain valid**, ground conditions were reviewed and any design changes justified (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 地下情況難以完全預測，加上非傳統工程方案日漸普遍，**條例第 17(1) 條第 6(g) 項**容許建築事務監督就附表地區以外的某些工程要求提交有文件紀錄的**表現檢討**（第 1 段）。
    - 在指定地盤作為批准或同意條件（例子見第 3 段）；檢討須證明工程已**妥善巡查及監測**、**設計假設仍然有效**、已覆檢施工時發現的地質情況，並為任何設計改動提供理據（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: geotechnical works where the BA imposes a **performance review** (BO s.17(1), Column B item **6(g)(ii)**) as an approval or consent condition: sites in **Scheduled Areas 1, 2, 4**, and others with uncertain geology, sensitive groundwater or **unconventional designs**. The review must be accepted **before OP / TOP**. 表現檢討。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Subsurface conditions cannot be predicted precisely; complex geology and **unconventional schemes** need verification during and after construction; s.17(1) item 6(g) allows a performance review outside the Scheduled Areas. 背景。 |
| 2 | Show works were **inspected and monitored**, **design assumptions valid**, ground conditions reviewed, and design changes made and **plans amended and approved**; submit before **OP / TOP**, else refusal under **s.21(6)(f)**. 檢討內容及時間。 |
| 3(i) | **Geology to verify**: slopes with past continuous movement; abnormal weaknesses (e.g. persistent **kaolinitic clay** layers). 地質須核實。 |
| 3(ii) | **Groundwater affected**: sloping sites where congested foundations, **bored pile or diaphragm walls** may dam groundwater, e.g. **downslope of Mid-levels**, Hong Kong Island hills, Kowloon foothills. 地下水受影響。 |
| 3(iii) | **Unconventional design**: retaining walls **> 15 m** retained height; excavation in **reclaimed land**; reliance on **horizontal drains** for large drawdown; **permanent underground drainage** against basement buoyancy; technologies **not tried in Hong Kong**. 非傳統設計。 |
