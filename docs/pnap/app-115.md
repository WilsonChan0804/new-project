## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Subsurface conditions cannot be fully predicted, and unconventional schemes are increasingly common, so **BO s.17(1) item 6(g)** allows BA to require a documented **performance review** for certain works outside scheduled areas (para. 1).
    - Imposed as an approval or consent condition for specific sites (examples in para. 3); it must show the works were **inspected and monitored**, **design assumptions remain valid**, ground conditions were reviewed and any design changes justified (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 地下情況難以完全預測，加上非傳統工程方案日漸普遍，**條例第 17(1) 條第 6(g) 項**容許建築事務監督就附表地區以外的某些工程要求提交有文件紀錄的**表現檢討**（第 1 段）。
    - 在指定地盤作為批准或同意條件（例子見第 3 段）；檢討須證明工程已**妥善巡查及監測**、**設計假設仍然有效**、已覆檢施工時發現的地質情況，並為任何設計改動提供理據（第 2 段）。
