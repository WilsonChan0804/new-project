## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - One copy of every plan is **coloured** to distinguish existing from new works (B(A)R 14(3)) (para. 1).
    - Use the **App. A colours**; on amendment plans, identify proposed works the same way on **all sets** (paras. 2–3).

=== "中文"

    - 每份圖則須有一份**著色**，以區分現有及新建工程（B(A)R 第 14(3) 條）（第 1 段）。
    - 採用**附錄 A 顏色**；修訂圖則須在**所有套數**以相同方式標示擬議工程（第 2–3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: one coloured copy of every plan submitted for approval (B(A)R 14(3)); for **amendment plans**, colour proposed works on **all sets**. Recommended, not a ground for disapproval. 所有呈交批准的圖則。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| 1 | B(A)R **14(3)**: one copy of every plan coloured to distinguish existing from new works and parts of new works | 規例要求 |

| 3 | Consistent colouring speeds processing | 加快審批 |
| App. A notes | (a) ducts, light-wells, lift shafts **uncoloured**; (b) lighter washes for elevations and large areas; (c) light wash for **phased** development; (d) colour **legend** on all sets; plot screening 100 | 附註 |
