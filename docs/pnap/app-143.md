## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Sets **QA, qualified supervision and quality audit** for key precast elements made in factories; excludes minor items like non-structural blocks, features and planters (para. 1).
    - AP and RSE give **periodic**, the RC **continuous** supervision (BO s.4(3)(a), 9(5)(a), 9(6)(a); B(A)R 37, 41); follow the **Precast Concrete Code 2016** (paras. 2–3).
    - Approval conditions include manufacture in an **ISO 9001-certified factory** (s.17(1) item 6) and further QA and audit requirements (para. 4 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 訂明預製廠生產的主要預製構件的**品質保證、合資格監督及品質審核**；不包括非結構預製磚、建築裝飾及花槽等次要構件（第 1 段）。
    - 認可人士及註冊結構工程師作**定期**監督，註冊承建商作**持續**監督（條例第 4(3)(a)、9(5)(a)、9(6)(a) 條；B(A)R 第 37、41 條）；須遵從 **2016 年預製混凝土守則**（第 2–3 段）。
    - 批准條件包括由具 **ISO 9001 認證**的工廠生產（第 17(1) 條第 6 項）及其他品質保證和審核要求（第 4 段起）。

## Paragraph by paragraph (full) 逐段詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision April 2022 (general revision). 已按 2022 年 4 月版本核對。

| Para. 段 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | QA, qualified supervision and quality audit for key precast elements made in a **precasting factory**. Does **not** apply to minor elements such as non-structural blocks, architectural features and planters. 不適用於非結構預製件、建築裝飾及花槽。 |
| 2 | AP and RSE give **periodic supervision** (BO s. 4(3)(a), B(A)R 37); the RC gives **continuous supervision** (BO s. 9(5)(a), 9(6)(a), B(A)R 41). |
| 3 | Follow the [Precast Concrete Code 2016](../codes/precast-2016.md) for design, construction and QC. |
| 4(a) | Factory with **ISO 9001** (BO s. 17(1) item 6). |
| 4(b) | Manufacturer's **QAS** submitted **≥ 14 days** before production (B(A)R 10), with RSE's written confirmation that it is adequate. |
| 5 | QAS covers: (a) QC tests of materials; (b) lab equipment calibration; (c) casting yard equipment; (d) production process; (e) testing procedures; (f) inspection and (g) audit by in-house staff and **independent parties** (footnote 1: an HKAS-accredited certification body or inspection body, not employed by the factory). |
| 6(a)–(b) | Qualified supervision by RSE and RC for fabrication, erection and examination. **Precast Concrete Supervision Plan** submitted **≥ 14 days** before production (footnote 2: names, qualifications, ID, frequency, appointment confirmation and contacts of QCS and QCC). |
| 6(c) | **RSE's QCS** (at least grade **T3**, RSE stream) supervises factory production as for in-situ RC under [APP-158](app-158.md), at least **once a week**. |
| 6(d) | **RC's QCC** (at least grade **T1**, RC stream) gives **continuous** supervision in the factory. |
| 6(e) | Names, qualifications, production, inspection, audit and test details in log books kept at the factory, a copy at the site office, shown to BD on request. |
| 7 | RSE and the RC's **AS** visit in person to inspect the **first batch** (footnote 3: to verify QAS and QA/QC are followed and the line works; covers Code cl. 3.1–3.14 and 4.1), then audit **at least monthly**: **in person every quarter**; the months in between (2nd, 3rd, 5th, 6th …) in person or by **videotelephony**. Audit reports endorsed by RSE and AS to BD **within 14 days** of each audit (including the first visit). Reports cover the QCS's and QCC's supervision. Minimum content: Appendix A. |
| 8 | Videotelephony audits: RSE assisted by the QCS and AS by the QCC in the factory; standard not lower than in person; items recorded at the time in the reports; reports and **non-rewritable DVD-ROMs** (colour, **≥ 480p**, footnote 4) to BD within 14 days, each disc marked by RSE / AS. |
| 9 | **Alternative for the RSE**: first visit in person, then **on-site audits** of delivered elements instead of monthly factory audits. Give the BA **one month's written notice**. Report within 14 days of the first visit and of each site audit. Minimum content: Appendix B. (The AS still audits at the factory.) |
| 10 | Flow chart: Appendix C. Standard supervision plan, typical items and sample record: Appendix D. |
| 11 | A **separate RSE** may be appointed for elements that do not affect the parent structure's stability (e.g. precast façade or staircase). Forms **BA4 and BA5** with the plans; the project RSE notifies the BA in writing that he/she knows of the appointment and the split of duties; the separate RSE certifies completion under **B(A)R 25(3)**. |
| 12 | Separate RSE's **assessment report** on the effect on the parent structure, with a statement from the project RSE. |
| 13 | Separate RSE supervises production, erection and connections (ties, anchor plates, cast-in embeds), unless these were installed before his/her appointment; then the project RSE shows them on superstructure plans and supervises them. |
| 14 | Separate RSE ensures paras 4–9 are met and makes the submissions. |
| 15 | A similar practice note is issued to RCs. |

### Appendix A: factory audit by RSE and AS 廠房品質審核 (Rev. 4/2022)

| Item 項目 | Scope 範圍 |
|---|---|
| 1 Formwork | Smoothness, cleanliness and dimensions of steel moulds; release agent |
| 2 Reinforcement | Size, pattern, fixing and layout; spacers; cover; test reports |
| 3 Concrete | Placing and compaction; curing; test reports |
| 4 Finished products | Surface and defects; sizes, dimensions and tolerances; starter bars |
| 5 Records | QCS log book (audited by RSE); QCC log book (audited by AS) |
| 6 Others | Anything else the RSE / AS considers essential |

### Appendix B: RSE's on-site audit (alternative) 地盤品質審核 (Rev. 4/2022)

Sample at least **1%** of elements, minimum **one per 20 batches** delivered (a batch = same type of element, e.g. façades, beams, columns, bearing walls, delivered on the same day).

| Item 項目 | Scope 範圍 |
|---|---|
| 1 Reinforcement | **Open up** concrete at **3 locations**, each **100 × 300 mm**, to check bar size, pattern, layout and cover; **covermeter** at **6 locations**, 6 readings each in a **450 × 450 mm** area; test reports |
| 2 Concrete | Strength by non-destructive test (e.g. **rebound hammer**) at **3 locations**; test reports |
| 3 Finished products | Surface and defects; sizes, dimensions and tolerances; starter bars |
| 4 Records | QCS log book |
| 5 Others | Anything else the RSE considers essential |

### Appendix C: flow chart 流程圖 (4/2022)

1. Submit the QAS and supervision plan ≥ 14 days before production.
2. RSE and AS make the first visit in person and report within 14 days. If not satisfactory, fix the problems and inspect again; if satisfactory, mass production of acceptable items starts.
3. **AS**: quarterly audits in person, other monthly audits in person or by video, report within 14 days of each.
4. **RSE**: either the same as the AS, **or** a one-month notice to the BA and then on-site audits of delivered elements, report within 14 days of each.
5. Continue until production ends.

### Appendix D: supervision plan form and annexes 監督計劃表格

- **Form**: addressed to the BA. It lists the QCS (**weekly**) and QCC (**full time**) with ID / TCP no. and signatures (no CV needed if a TCP no. is given), is signed by the RSE and the AS of the RC, and includes personal data notes.
- **Annex 1** lists typical items (each project sets its own checklists and frequencies):
  - Materials: **PC1** rebar (delivery records, sampling and testing to CS2); **PC2** concrete / grout (cubes to CS1).
  - Production: **PC3** mould condition and periodic rechecks; **PC4** rebar fixing, cover and starter bars to [ADV-15](adv-15.md); **PC5** cast-in items (starter bars, dowels, lifting inserts, window frames with lugs, recesses, sleeves); **PC6** placing, compaction and curing; **PC7** enough strength before demoulding.
  - Finished products: **PC8** condition after demoulding; **PC9** storage and stacking; **PC10** special tests such as water penetration and tile pull-off (Code cl. 4.3); **PC11** in-situ strength (coring or rebound hammer); PCn other items.
  - Code cl. 3.2–3.14 gives the detail.
- **Annex 2** is a sample QCS record with these marks:
  - **S** (satisfactory): includes a very minor non-conformity fixed during the same inspection.
  - **NS** (not satisfactory): logged in the log book, plus **Form B** of the Supervision Code where safety is a concern or the work was covered up before it could be verified.
  - **NA** (not applicable).
