## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Site formation proposals may be **split** for parallel processing (paras. 1–2): **Part A** site formation details (slopes, drainage, layout and size of retaining structures); **Part B** structural details of retaining structures.
    - The **AP or RGE** co-ordinates and signs Part A; the **RSE** Part B (B(A)R 12; APP-141) (para. 3).
    - Submit together, or Part A first and Part B once soil-structure interaction is defined (para. 4); simpler arrangements for simple proposals (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 地盤平整建議可**分拆**以平行處理（第 1–2 段）：**A 部**地盤平整細節（斜坡、排水、擋土構築物佈局及大小）；**B 部**擋土構築物結構細節。
    - A 部由**認可人士或註冊岩土工程師**統籌及簽署，B 部由**註冊結構工程師**負責（B(A)R 第 12 條；APP-141）（第 3 段）。
    - 可同時呈交，或在土壤與結構相互作用確定後先交 A 部再交 B 部（第 4 段）；簡單建議另有簡化安排（第 5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: site formation proposals. **Simple** proposals (simple retaining structures, NTEH, slope drainage without major retaining structures) may be a single submission. 地盤平整建議；簡單個案可單一呈交。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parallel processing of site formation and structural details | 平行審批 |
| 2 | **Part A** site formation: slope works, drainage, general layout and size of retaining structures (B(A)R **8(1)(bb)**); **Part B** structural details of retaining structures | 分兩部分 |
| 3 | AP or **RGE** co-ordinates and signs Part A; **RSE** Part B (B(A)R **12**; APP-141 App. B Task 4) | 簽署責任 |
| 4 | Submit together, or Part A first and Part B once **soil-structure interaction** is defined; or full sets marked Part A / Part B | 呈交次序 |
| 5 | Simple cases: single submission (simple retaining structures, **NT exempted houses**, slope drainage, box culverts, sand traps, catchpits) | 簡單個案 |
| 6 | Enough sets for consultation (ADM-2, ADM-8, APP-141) | 圖則套數 |
| 7 | Piling in site formation: monitoring per APP-18 and APP-137 | 打樁監測 |
