## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Where the FSI Code requires a **permanent water supply** to fire service installations, the permanent connection must be made **before occupation** (para. 1).
    - If FSI is satisfactory but the connection is not yet made, FSD issues its certificate with a **rider** that it is valid only after connection; the **OP will not be issued** until the rider is fulfilled (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 如消防裝置守則規定須為消防裝置提供**恆久供水**，須在**佔用前**完成恆久接駁（第 1 段）。
    - 如消防裝置合格但未接駁，消防處的證明書會附**附帶條件**，只在接駁後生效；在條件達成前**不會發出佔用許可證**（第 2 段）。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Planning the OP date without allowing time for the **permanent WSD connection** to FSI tanks.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 預計佔用許可證日期時，沒有預留時間完成消防水缸的**水務署恆久接駁**。
