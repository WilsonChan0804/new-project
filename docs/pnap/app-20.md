## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - When Government acquires part of a lot for **street widening**, compensation may be **bonus PR / SC under B(P)R 22(2)**, cash, or a land exchange (para. 1).
    - For the bonus, the BA may count the **surrendered area as site area** for the **first building** on the reduced site, to calculate permitted PR and SC (para. 3).
    - Conditions (para. 4): each case on its merits; extra PR only if density is **not excessive**; a **block plan (in quadruplicate)** showing the surrender area; plans endorsed; **Agreement to Surrender** executed and **registered** in the Land Registry.
    - A similar principle applies to open space under B(P)R 25 (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 政府收回部分地段作**擴闊街道**時，補償可以是 **B(P)R 第 22(2) 條的額外地積比率／上蓋面積**、現金或換地（第 1 段）。
    - 如採用額外地積比率，建築事務監督可把**交回的面積當作地盤面積**，只適用於縮減後地盤上的**第一幢建築物**，以計算准許地積比率及上蓋面積（第 3 段）。
    - 條件（第 4 段）：逐案考慮；只在密度**不過高**時給予額外地積比率；附顯示交回範圍的**地盤平面圖（一式四份）**；圖則須加註；簽立**交回土地協議**並在土地註冊處**登記**。
    - B(P)R 第 25 條空地亦採用相若原則（第 5 段）。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Claiming the bonus for a **second** redevelopment: it applies to the first building only.
        - No registered **Agreement to Surrender** before approval.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 在**第二次**重建時申請額外地積比率：只適用於第一幢建築物。
        - 批准前沒有登記**交回土地協議**。
