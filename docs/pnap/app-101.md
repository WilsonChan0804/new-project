## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - B(P)R 20(3) limits **100% SC non-domestic podiums to 15 m** high (para. 1).
    - Relaxation considers function, site constraints, environment, public interest and risk of abuse (para. 2). Favourable examples: **public transport termini**, better **aesthetic or environmental** effect, **special design or use**, **lease floor-height** requirements (para. 3).
    - If granted: podium **≤ 4 storeys** and **≤ 20 m** above ground (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - B(P)R 第 20(3) 條把 **100% 上蓋面積的非住用平台**限於 **15 米**高（第 1 段）。
    - 放寬時考慮功能、地盤限制、環境、公眾利益及濫用風險（第 2 段）；正面考慮的例子：**公共交通總站**、**美觀或環境**效果較佳、**特殊設計或用途**、**地契對樓層高度**的要求（第 3 段）。
    - 如獲批准：平台 **≤ 4 層**及距地面 **≤ 20 米**（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **non-domestic podiums** claiming the full site coverage allowed by **B(P)R 20(3)**, which is limited to podiums up to **15 m** high. Use this note when asking to **relax** that height. 申請放寬第 20(3) 條平台 15 米高度限制。

    **Not here 不在此列**: domestic floors and towers above the podium (normal First Schedule SC); OZP building height limits ([JPN 5](jpn-5.md)). 平台以上住用樓層；大綱圖高度限制。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | B(P)R 20(3) limits full SC of a **non-domestic podium** to **15 m** height; this note explains how the BA handles relaxation requests. 平台全覆蓋限 15 米。 |
| 2 | BA considers **functional requirements, site constraints, environmental impact, public interest** and **likelihood of abuse**. 考慮因素。 |
| 3 | Favourable consideration, e.g.: a **public transport terminus** or similar; better **aesthetic or environmental** effect; buildings of **special design or use**; **lease** floor-height requirements. 可正面考慮的例子。 |
| 4 | If modified: podium ≤ **4 storeys** and ≤ **20 m** above ground. 放寬後：不超過 4 層及 20 米。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A podium with a **PTI** on G/F needs 9 m headroom, so 3 storeys reach **18 m**. Apply on **BA16** citing para. 3 (PTI): acceptable, as it is ≤ 4 storeys and ≤ 20 m. A 5-storey, 22 m retail podium without special reasons would not be. 公共交通交匯處平台 18 米可接受。
