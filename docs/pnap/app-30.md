## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Parts of Mid-levels are **Scheduled Area No. 1** (BO Sch. 5) with extra geotechnical controls; an **RGE** must be appointed from early planning (paras. 1–2).
    - **Ground investigation** there needs BA **approval and consent** (s.41(3)), with plans and lab testing per B(A)R 8(1)(l) (para. 3).
    - GI to **Geoguide 2 and 3**, usually with qualified supervision under s.17(1)(6)(e) (para. 4). **Demolition plans** need approval (para. 5); **bulk excavation** is limited to the **BEL** set with GEO, provisional BEL in **10 working days**, shown on site formation plans (paras. 6–9); **foundation plans go in with site formation plans** (B(A)R 11A) (para. 10); a **performance review** may be a condition (para. 11); **one extra set** of GBP, SF, foundation and ELS plans (para. 13).

=== "中文"

    - 半山區部分地方屬條例附表 5 的**附表所列地區第 1 號**，受額外岩土管制；須在規劃初期委任**註冊岩土工程師**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 該區**土地勘測**須取得建築事務監督**批准及同意**（第 41(3) 條），並按 B(A)R 第 8(1)(l) 條呈交圖則及化驗（第 3 段）。
    - 土地勘測須符合**岩土指南第 2 及 3 號**，一般會根據第 17(1)(6)(e) 條要求合格監督（第 4 段）。**拆卸圖則**須獲批准（第 5 段）；**大規模挖掘**以與土力工程處訂定的**挖掘限制水平**為限，臨時限制水平於 **10 個工作天**內發出，並須在地盤平整圖則上顯示（第 6–9 段）；**地基圖則須與地盤平整圖則一併呈交**（第 11A 條）（第 10 段）；可施加**表現檢討**條件（第 11 段）；須**多交一套**圖則（第 13 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Mid-levels is **Scheduled Area No. 1** (BO Schedule 5): extra geotechnical control. 半山為附表地區第 1 號。 |
| 2 | Appoint an **RGE** for all geotechnical aspects, from early planning. 須委任註冊岩土工程師。 |
| 3 | **Ground investigation needs BA approval and consent** (BO s.41(3)); GI plans with lab testing per B(A)R 8(1)(l). 土地勘測須批准及同意。 |
| 4 | GI to **Geoguide 2 and 3**; site supervision by an experienced engineer or geologist usually required (BO s.17(1)(6)(e)); see APP-49. 勘測標準及監督。 |
| 5 | **Demolition plans** (B(A)R 8(3)–(4)) must be submitted for approval. 拆卸圖則須批准。 |
| 6 | **B(C)R s.25**: bulk excavation limited to the **bulk excavation limit (BEL)** to minimise cumulative effects on hillside stability. 挖掘限制水平。 |
| 7 | Ask GEO for a **provisional BEL** early; issued within **10 working days**. 臨時挖掘限制水平。 |
| 8 | Submit the BEL assessment (with RSE input) to BD **for agreement before** SF, foundation, ELS or other affected plans; follow **GEO TGN 50** (deemed to satisfy B(C)R s.25). 須先就限制水平取得同意。 |
| 9 | Show the excavation envelope and BEL on **site formation plans** (B(A)R 8(1)(bb)(vii)). 在圖則顯示。 |
| 10 | **Foundation plans with site formation plans** (B(A)R 11A); otherwise refusal under BO s.16(1)(a), (e) or (i). 地基與地盤平整圖則一併呈交。 |
| 11 | **Performance review** may be imposed as a condition; if it fails to validate the design, OP may be refused (BO s.21(6)(f)). 表現檢討。 |
| 12 | Further controls possible: debris resistance, works sequence, groundwater drainage (BO ss.16(1)(q), 17(1)(6)(f), 28A–D). 其他管制。 |
| 13 | **One extra set** of GBP, SF (with reports), foundation (with pile caps) and ELS plans. 多交一套圖則。 |
| 14–15 | Mid-levels Study report (1982) on the CEDD website; factual data at GEO's Geotechnical Information Unit (ADM-7). 資料來源。 |
| 16 | Boundary plan of Scheduled Area No. 1 at the GIU, the BD Building Information Centre and the BD website. 界線圖。 |
