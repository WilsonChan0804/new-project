## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Under the **Hostels in the City Scheme**, EDB-confirmed eligible conversions of **commercial buildings and hotels** to student hostels get a **pragmatic** approach to modifications (paras. 1–2).
    - BA may (para. 3): treat the hostel as **non-domestic for SC / PR** (B(P)R 19–22); waive **open space and service lane** (regs 25, 28); waive **unit kitchens** (reg. 45) if a communal canteen or pantry is provided. Other domestic requirements (natural L&V) and hostel fire safety still apply (para. 4).
    - **GFA**: favourable exemption for **hostel facilities** (Appendix); previously exempted areas stay exempt; redundant exempted facilities (and ex-hotel lay-bys and back-of-house) may be converted via modification of reg. 23(3)(a). Hostel facilities up to **5% of hostel GFA**, or all redundant facilities if more (paras. 5–8).
    - Partial conversion allowed per Scheme guidelines (EDB with DPFO); since the **2025 Policy Address**, the same concessions apply to **newly built** student hostels (paras. 11–13).

=== "中文"

    - 在**城中學舍計劃**下，經教育局確認合資格、由**商業大廈及酒店**改裝的學生宿舍，申請變通時建築事務監督會採取**務實**態度（第 1–2 段）。
    - 建築事務監督可（第 3 段）：把宿舍視為**非住用以計算上蓋面積／地積比率**（B(P)R 第 19–22 條）；豁免**休憩用地及後巷**（第 25、28 條）；如提供公用飯堂或茶水間，可豁免**單位廚房**（第 45 條）；其他住用要求（自然照明通風）及宿舍消防要求仍須遵從（第 4 段）。
    - **樓面面積**：**宿舍設施**（附錄）可獲正面考慮豁免；原已豁免的面積繼續豁免；多餘的已豁免設施（及前酒店上落客貨區和後勤設施）可透過變通第 23(3)(a) 條改作宿舍設施；宿舍設施上限為**宿舍樓面面積 5%**，如多餘設施較多則可全數改裝（第 5–8 段）。
    - 可按計劃指引部分改裝（教育局及發展項目促進辦事處）；自 **2025 年施政報告**起，**新建**學生宿舍亦享相同寬免（第 11–13 段）。

## Paragraph by paragraph (full) 逐段詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision September 2025 (para. 3 amended, paras 13–15 added). 已按 2025 年 9 月版本核對。

| Para. 段 | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | The scheme encourages converting **commercial buildings and hotels** into student hostels. For eligible hostels (criteria confirmed by **EDB**), the BA takes a pragmatic approach to modifications and exemptions. |
| 3(a) | Treat the hostel (or hostel part) as **non-domestic** for SC and PR under B(P)R 19–22. 計算上蓋面積及地積比率時視作非住用。 |
| 3(b) | Allow **no open space** (reg. 25) and **no service lane** (reg. 28). Footnote 1: the service lane waiver is on case merits, e.g. the existing building has no lane and the servicing strategy is acceptable. |
| 3(c) | Allow **no kitchen in each unit** (reg. 45). Footnote 2: a communal canteen, kitchen or pantry must be provided. |
| 4 | Other domestic requirements (e.g. **natural lighting and ventilation**) and fire safety requirements for hostels / dormitories still apply. 其他住用要求及消防要求仍適用。 |
| 5 | **Hostel facilities** (Appendix) are favourably considered for GFA exemption. |
| 6 | Areas already exempted or disregarded stay so after conversion, so hostels need not lose bulk. Redundant exempted facilities may be converted, by modification of reg. 23(3)(a), to mandatory features, plant rooms, green / amenity features or hostel facilities. Footnote 3: subject to the [APP-151](app-151.md) prerequisites for A&A works resulting in a new building. |
| 7 | Former **licensed hotels** without a valid licence under Cap. 349: lay-bys and back-of-house areas disregarded under reg. 23A(3) may become hostel facilities, exempted by modification of reg. 23(3)(a). |
| 8 | **5% rule**: if redundant facilities are **< 5%** of the hostel's total GFA, hostel facilities may be up to **5%**. If they are **> 5%**, all redundant facilities may become hostel facilities. Hostel facilities **> 5%** are considered case by case. 宿舍設施一般以總樓面面積 5% 為限。 |
| 9 | Only hostels supported by the scheme qualify (www.studenthostel.gov.hk). |
| 10 | All other building regime requirements and those of LandsD, PlanD, FSD, TD, etc. still apply. These concessions do not affect other regimes. |
| 11–12 | **Partial conversion** is allowed if the scheme's Application Guidelines are met (decided by EDB with the **Development Projects Facilitation Office**, DEVB). "Total GFA of the student hostel" then means the hostel portion only. |
| 13 | **New (2025 Policy Address): newly built hostels.** (a) Non-domestic for SC / PR; (b) no open space or service lane (footnote 4: lane on case merits, e.g. no existing lane pattern); (c) no unit kitchens (with a communal facility); (d) hostel facilities exempt **up to 5%** of hostel GFA. Subject to the APP-151 prerequisites, but **not** to the overall cap on GFA concessions. 不受整體寬免上限限制。 |
| 14 | Treated as **non-residential** for [JPN 1](jpn-1.md), [JPN 2](jpn-2.md), [APP-42](app-42.md) and [APP-104](app-104.md). |
| 15 | Paras 13–14 apply to new building plans or major revisions submitted **on or after** the issue of this revision (September 2025). |
| 16 | **Loss of status**: if no longer run as a scheme hostel, the exemptions are **revoked**. The owner must appoint an AP to get approval for the change in use, or BD may take enforcement action. 不再作宿舍用途則撤銷寬免。 |

### Appendix: hostel facilities that may be exempted from GFA (7/2025) 附錄：可豁免樓面面積的宿舍設施

For exclusive use of the hostel: (a) linen store; (b) laundry room; (c) study / collaboration / leisure room (rooms for self-study or group schoolwork); (d) gymnasium / sports room; (e) communal kitchen / pantry / canteen; (f) staff rest room; (g) staff toilet; (h) other supporting facilities accepted by EDB and the BA.
