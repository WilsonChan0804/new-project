## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The AP must give the RGBC, RSC and RMWC a copy of the **approved plans**, the RSE's approved **structural details** and the **supervision plan** (B(A)R 36) (para. 1).
    - A copy suffices, but it must be an **exact reproduction** bearing BA's stamp, signature and date (para. 2); supply the latest plans **promptly** for continuous supervision (para. 3).
    - For **Class I minor works**, give the prescribed registered contractor the prescribed plans and, if required, the supervision plan (B(MW)R 55) (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 認可人士須向註冊一般建築承建商、註冊專門承建商及註冊小型工程承建商提供**核准圖則**、註冊結構工程師的核准**結構詳圖**及**監工計劃書**的副本（B(A)R 第 36 條）（第 1 段）。
    - 提供副本即可，但須為附有建築事務監督印章、簽署及日期的**準確複製本**（第 2 段）；須**盡快**提供最新圖則，以便持續監督（第 3 段）。
    - **第 I 級小型工程**須向訂明註冊承建商交付訂明圖則及（如需要）監工計劃書（B(MW)R 第 55 條）（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: the AP's duty to give **approved plans, structural details and supervision plans** to the RGBC, RSC and RMWC (**B(A)R 36**), and for **Class I minor works** (**B(MW)R s.55**). 認可人士須向承建商提供圖則。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **B(A)R 36**: supply (a) a copy of **approved plans** (B(A)R 30); (b) **approved structural details** by the RSE; (c) any **supervision plan**. 須提供三類文件。 |
| 2 | One **copy** is enough, but an **exact reproduction** of the plans as **stamped, signed and dated** by the BA. 須為批准圖則的準確副本。 |
| 3 | No time limit stated, but supply the **latest** plans **promptly** for continuous supervision. 須盡快提供最新圖則。 |
| 4 | **B(MW)R s.55**: for **Class I** minor works, give the prescribed registered contractor (a) the prescribed plans and details (s.30(c)) and (b) the supervision plan if the TM requires. 小型工程。 |
