## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **AAR**: cement alkalis react with certain aggregate minerals in the presence of moisture, forming an expansive gel that **cracks** concrete, speeds rebar corrosion and reduces strength (paras. 1–2).
    - Needs **reactive aggregate** (opal, cristobalite, volcanic glass, micro-crystalline quartz…), **enough alkali** and **moisture** (paras. 3–4).
    - Reduce risk by limiting **cement content**, using **low-alkali cement** and **PFA**; follow the App. A specification items (paras. 5–7).

=== "中文"

    - **鹼性骨料反應**：水泥中的鹼質在有水分時與某些骨料礦物反應，產生膨脹膠體，令混凝土**爆裂**、加速鋼筋銹蝕並降低強度（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須同時有**活性骨料**（蛋白石、方石英、火山玻璃、微晶石英等）、**足夠鹼質**及**水分**（第 3–4 段）。
    - 可透過控制**水泥用量**、使用**低鹼水泥**及**煤灰**減低風險；並採用附錄 A 的規格條款（第 5–7 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: reinforced concrete in private developments exposed to **moisture**: specify the **AAR control** clauses (Appendix A), limiting reactive alkali to **3.0 kg Na₂O eq. per m³**. Not needed if the element stays dry for its design life (para. 8). 受潮混凝土須控制鹼骨料反應。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Paras. 1–2 | **AAR**: alkalis (mainly from cement) react with certain aggregate minerals in moisture, forming an expansive **gel** → cracking, rebar corrosion, strength loss. 機理。 |
| Para. 3 | Needs all three: **reactive minerals**, **enough alkali**, **moisture**. 三項條件。 |
| Para. 4 | Reactive minerals include opal, tridymite, cristobalite, volcanic glass, micro- / cryptocrystalline and poorly crystalline quartz. 活性礦物。 |
| Para. 5 | Control cement content, use **low-alkali cement**, and **PFA**. 控制措施。 |
| Para. 6 | Public Works Laboratories studies since 1992; clauses adopted in Works Branch contracts. 研究背景。 |
| Paras. 7–8 | Put the clauses in private contracts; plans state reactive alkali **≤ 3.0 kg/m³** Na₂O eq.; AP / RSE enforce it unless the element stays dry. 圖則註明上限。 |
| App. 1–2 | Control by limiting reactive alkali to **3.0 kg/m³** unless alternatives are approved. 上限。 |
| App. 3 | **Na₂O eq. = A + B + C**: A = acid-soluble alkalis of cement, admixtures and water; B = **⅙** of PFA total alkalis; C = **0.76 × chloride ion** of aggregate. Cement: average of latest **25 daily** results + 2 SD (BS 4550-2); PFA: 25 **weekly** + 2 SD; admixtures BS 1881-124; water APHA 3500; aggregates BS 812-4. 計算方法。 |
| App. 4 | Supplier submits **HOKLAS** certificates (≤ 6 months old), the mix calculation, and **quarterly** updates. 證書每季更新。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    Mix: cement 380 kg/m³ with acid-soluble alkali 0.60% → 2.28 kg; admixture and water 0.05 kg; PFA 100 kg/m³ at 2.4% total alkali → 2.4 × 1/6 = 0.40 kg; aggregate chloride 0.05 kg/m³ → 0.04 kg. Total **2.77 kg/m³ ≤ 3.0 ✓**. 計算例子。
