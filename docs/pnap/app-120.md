## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Concrete batching plants have grown larger and moved near buildings and streets (para. 1). BA regards a CBP **fixed to and founded on the ground, with utility supply and a control room**, as a **building** needing approval and consent (para. 3).
    - **New CBPs** and associated cement, sand and aggregate facilities: AP and RSE submit plans, complying also with the **town plan**; for the common type (App. A), approval and consent can be processed **together** (App. B) (para. 4(a)). Later paras. deal with existing plants.

=== "中文"

    - 混凝土拌合廠規模漸大，並移近建築物及街道（第 1 段）；建築事務監督認為**固定及建基於地面、接駁公用設施並設有控制室**的拌合廠屬**建築物**，須取得批准及同意（第 3 段）。
    - **新拌合廠**及相關水泥、沙石設施：由認可人士及註冊結構工程師呈交圖則，並須符合**城市規劃圖則**；常見類型（附錄 A）可**同時**處理批准及同意申請（附錄 B）（第 4(a) 段）；其後各段處理現有拌合廠。
