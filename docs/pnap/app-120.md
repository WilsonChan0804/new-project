## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Concrete batching plants have grown larger and moved near buildings and streets (para. 1). BA regards a CBP **fixed to and founded on the ground, with utility supply and a control room**, as a **building** needing approval and consent (para. 3).
    - **New CBPs** and associated cement, sand and aggregate facilities: AP and RSE submit plans, complying also with the **town plan**; for the common type (App. A), approval and consent can be processed **together** (App. B) (para. 4(a)). Apply for an **OP or TOP** on completion (para. 4(b)); new CBPs erected **after 31 Mar 2002** without approval face **UBW enforcement** (para. 4(c)); CBPs erected **before 1 May 2002** are tolerated if an **RSE appraisal report** was submitted by **30 Oct 2002** and the plant is maintained (para. 4(d)). Self-certification may be explored for plants with silos **< 50 t** and height **< 15 m** (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 混凝土拌合廠規模漸大，並移近建築物及街道（第 1 段）；建築事務監督認為**固定及建基於地面、接駁公用設施並設有控制室**的拌合廠屬**建築物**，須取得批准及同意（第 3 段）。
    - **新拌合廠**及相關水泥、沙石設施：由認可人士及註冊結構工程師呈交圖則，並須符合**城市規劃圖則**；常見類型（附錄 A）可**同時**處理批准及同意申請（附錄 B）（第 4(a) 段）。完工後須申請**佔用許可證或臨時佔用許可證**（第 4(b) 段）；**2002 年 3 月 31 日後**未經批准興建的新拌合廠會被**以違例建築工程執法**（第 4(c) 段）；**2002 年 5 月 1 日前**興建的拌合廠，如已於 **2002 年 10 月 30 日**前呈交**註冊結構工程師評估報告**並妥善保養，不會被執法（第 4(d) 段）。日後或探討為料倉 **< 50 噸**及高度 **< 15 米**的拌合廠設自行認證（第 5 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Concrete batching plants (CBPs) were once site machinery, but many have grown into large permanent installations. 混凝土拌合廠。 |
| 2 | APs and RSEs judge whether plans are needed; BD advises on enquiry. 是否須呈交。 |
| 3 | A CBP **fixed to and founded on the ground**, connected to utilities and with a **control room**, is a **building**: approval and consent needed. 屬建築物。 |
| 4(a) | New CBPs and associated cement, sand and aggregate facilities: plans by **AP and RSE**, complying with the **town plan**; common types (App. A) may get approval and consent **together** (App. B). 新拌合廠。 |
| 4(b) | **OP or TOP** on completion. 佔用許可證。 |
| 4(c) | New CBPs erected **after 31 Mar 2002** without approval and consent: **enforcement** as UBW. 執法。 |
| 4(d) | Existing CBPs before **1 May 2002**: no action if an **RSE appraisal** was submitted by **30 Oct 2002** and the plant is maintained; other departments may still act. 現有拌合廠。 |
| 5 | Possible future self-certification for **silos < 50 t** and **height < 15 m**. 自行認證。 |
