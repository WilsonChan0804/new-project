## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Because of high accident and health risks, the Buildings (Amendment) Ordinance 1995 **banned hand-dug caissons**: any shaft dug by a person inside it (paras. 1–3).
    - BA may refuse plans under **s.16(1A)**. Exemption only if shown to the BA personally: depth **≤ 3 m** with inscribed diameter **≥ 1.5 m**, or it is the **only practical or safe method**, e.g. narrow or steep sites (para. 3).
    - Then specify stringent safety in the method statement and follow the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations, HKIE guidance notes and the OSHC code (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 由於意外及健康風險高，1995 年《建築物（修訂）條例》**禁止人工挖掘沉箱**，即由人在井內挖掘的豎井（第 1–3 段）。
    - 建築事務監督可根據**第 16(1A) 條**拒批圖則；只有在向建築事務監督本人證明以下情況才獲豁免：深度 **≤ 3 米**而內切圓直徑 **≥ 1.5 米**，或屬**唯一可行或安全的方法**，例如狹窄或陡斜地盤（第 3 段）。
    - 如獲豁免，須在施工方法說明訂明嚴格安全要求，並遵從《建築地盤（安全）規例》、香港工程師學會指引及職安局守則（第 4–5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: any foundation or earth-retaining structure dug by a person **inside the shaft** (a **hand-dug caisson**, BO s.2(1)). Banned by the **Buildings (Amendment) Ordinance 1995**; plans refused under **s.16(1A)** from **1 Feb 1996** unless an exemption applies. 人手挖掘沉箱已禁止。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | High accident rate and health hazards; the **1995 Amendment Ordinance** (19 Jan 1995) bans them. 禁止原因。 |
| 3 | Definition; **s.16(1A)**: BA may refuse plans. Exemption only if the BA is **personally** satisfied: (a) depth **≤ 3 m** and inscribed diameter **≥ 1.5 m**; or (b) the **only practical method** or **no other safe alternative** (e.g. narrow or steep sites with no room for machines). 豁免條件。 |
| 4 | In exceptions: stringent **safety requirements** in the method statement; RC aware and compliant; **Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations**. 安全要求。 |
| 5 | HKIE **Guidance Notes on Hand-dug Caissons**; OSHC **Code of Safe Working Practices**. 參考資料。 |
| 6 | **12-month grace**; from **1 Feb 1996** no approval except under s.16(1A). 寬限期。 |
| 7–8 | APs and RSEs urged to **avoid** them; similar note to contractors. 盡量避免。 |
