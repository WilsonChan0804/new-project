!!! warning "Repealed 已廢除"

## At a glance (last version) 重點摘要（最後版本） {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Falling objects from buildings under construction: extra measures during **superstructure works** (para. 1).
    - Always a hoarding / covered walkway ([APP-23](app-23.md)) (para. 2).
    - During frame erection: hoarding, covered walkway, **gantry and catch platform**; façade **double netting** (inner heavy-duty nylon, thread ≥ **3.5 mm**, ≤ **50 mm** mesh; outer fine net) (para. 3).
    - Not needed if the building is **under 30 m**, or set back so a **10°** plane from the top edge stays within the site (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 建築中樓宇墮物：**上蓋工程**期間的額外措施（第 1 段）。
    - 必須設圍板／有蓋行人道（第 2 段）。
    - 搭建結構框架時：圍板、有蓋行人道、**門廊及承接平台**；外牆**雙層防護網**（內層尼龍網線徑 ≥ 3.5 mm、網眼 ≤ 50 mm；外層細網）（第 3 段）。
    - 樓高 **30 米以下**，或後移令 **10°** 斜面不超出地盤界線，則毋須設置（第 4 段）。

## What it said 內容 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it said 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Falling-object incidents remain high; enhance precautions during superstructure works | 背景 |
| 2 | Hoarding / covered walkway always required ([APP-23](app-23.md)) | 圍板 |
| 3(a) | Frame erection: hoardings, covered walkways, gantry, **catch platform** (design per APP-21 paras 22–24) | 承接平台 |
| 3(b) | Façade screen of **two layers**: heavy-duty nylon (≥ 3.5 mm thread, ≤ 50 mm c/c) + fine nylon; may be removed locally for cladding / curtain wall | 雙層防護網 |
| 4 | Exemptions: height **< 30 m**, or **10°** setback plane inside the site | 豁免 |
| 5 | A&A, minor works and concrete repair at external walls: adequate protection; see Minor Works Technical Guidelines (amended Dec 2010) | 加建及小型工程 |
