## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - HK is a major importer of **tropical rainforest timber**, much wasted in construction and dumped (paras. 1–2).
    - Accept changes to traditional methods; BD is reviewing requirements that encourage hardwood use (paras. 3–5). Actively reduce hardwood use, e.g. use **other materials for hoardings** (para. 6). Question whether a **covered walkway** is needed, e.g. where buildings are set back (para. 7); replace timber **formwork** with profiled steel sheeting, precast units and other systems (paras. 8–9); use non-timber **wall panels, doors, frames and skirtings** (paras. 10–11); **separate and reuse** timber waste (para. 12); otherwise use **renewable sources** (para. 13).

=== "中文"

    - 香港是**熱帶雨林木材**的主要進口地，大部分用於建築業並造成浪費及棄置（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須接受改變傳統施工方法；屋宇署正檢討會鼓勵使用硬木的規定（第 3–5 段）；應積極減少使用硬木，例如**以其他物料造圍板**（第 6 段）。考慮是否真的需要**有蓋行人道**，例如建築物已後移（第 7 段）；以壓型鋼板、預製件等取代木**模板**（第 8–9 段）；**牆板、門、門框、踢腳線**改用非木材料（第 10–11 段）；木材廢料**分類再用**（第 12 段）；無替代時採用**可再生來源**木材（第 13 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Hong Kong is a major importer of **tropical hardwood**, much of it wasted in construction. 熱帶硬木消耗。 |
| 3–5 | Accept changes to traditional methods; Government leads; BD reviewing rules that encourage hardwood. 改變傳統方法。 |
| 6 | **Hoardings**: use other materials, e.g. reusable **steel** (as ArchSD and HD require). 圍板改用鋼。 |
| 7 | **Covered walkways**: not essential on every site, especially where buildings are set back. 有蓋行人道未必需要。 |
| 8–9 | **Formwork** is the largest user: consider profiled steel sheeting, precast units and other systems. 模板替代。 |
| 10–11 | **Internal finishes**: non-timber wall panels, doors, frames, skirtings, architraves. 內部裝修。 |
| 12 | Specify that timber waste is **separated and reused**. 分類再用。 |
| 13 | Where no alternative, require timber from **renewable sources**. 可再生來源。 |
| 14 | Long-term changes to construction methods after industry consultation. 長遠改變。 |
