## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **App. A** lists legislation and publications of interest to the building industry (not exhaustive), e.g. Cap. 123 and its regulations (123A–123Q) and other ordinances, codes and guides (para. 1).
    - Available on department websites or from the **Government Bookstore / ISD** (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - **附錄 A** 列出與建築業有關的法例及刊物（並非盡列），例如第 123 章及其規例（123A–123Q）及其他條例、守則和指南（第 1 段）。
    - 可在各部門網站瀏覽或經**政府書店／政府新聞處**購買（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: reference list only, **not exhaustive** (Appendix A, Rev. 11/2014). Check the current e-Legislation and BD codes. 參考清單，並非詳盡。
