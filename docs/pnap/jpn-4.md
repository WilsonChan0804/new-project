## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - **Three regimes** control development intensity: **B(P)R** (BD), the **statutory plan / OZP** (PlanD), and the **lease** (LandsD). Each used to check GFA with its own definitions. JPN 4 **streamlines** this (paras. 2–3).
    - **Max PR/GFA** (para. 4): for sites under **new or amended statutory plans gazetted on or after 18 Oct 2021**, the **OZP restriction becomes the sole PR/GFA reference**. If the B(P)R First Schedule is stricter, the BA may grant a **s.42 modification** to bring it up to the OZP level.
    - **One GFA calculation** (paras. 6–8): **BD is the sole agent** that verifies GFA under B(P)R 23(3)(a). PlanD and LandsD **rely on BD's verified figure**. LandsD still checks use-specific GFA (e.g. retail / office minimums) itself.
    - **Aligned GFA accountability** (Appendix): **Group I** (exempt under all three regimes, regardless of the lease), **Group II** (exempt if the lease has a **specific exemption clause**: (A) nil premium, or (B) premium assessed), **Group III** (case by case, aligned in new leases).
    - Not for buildings with an OP. **No GFA transfer** between completed and uncompleted phases (para. 10).

=== "中文"

    - 發展密度受**三個制度**管制：**《建築物（規劃）規例》**（屋宇署）、**法定圖則／分區計劃大綱圖**（規劃署）及**地契**（地政總署）；以往三者各自以本身定義核對總樓面面積，JPN 4 將其**精簡**（第 2–3 段）。
    - **最高地積比率／總樓面面積**（第 4 段）：**2021 年 10 月 18 日或之後刊憲的新訂或經修訂法定圖則**涵蓋的地盤，以**大綱圖限制為唯一的地積比率／總樓面面積依據**；如 B(P)R 附表 1 較嚴，建築事務監督可根據**第 42 條變通**提高至大綱圖水平。
    - **單一計算**（第 6–8 段）：**屋宇署是唯一**按 B(P)R 第 23(3)(a) 條核實總樓面面積的部門，規劃署及地政總署**依據屋宇署核實的數字**；地政總署仍自行核對特定用途的總樓面面積（例如零售／辦公室最低面積）。
    - **一致的總樓面面積計算**（附錄）：**第 I 組**（三個制度均豁免，不論地契）、**第 II 組**（地契有**特定豁免條款**才豁免：(A) 免補地價，(B) 須評估補地價）、**第 III 組**（個別考慮，在新地契中統一）。
    - 不適用於已取得佔用許可證的建築物；已完成與未完成期數之間**不得轉移**總樓面面積（第 10 段）。

### Group II — Exempt by all three only if the lease has a specific exemption clause 第 II 組：地契有特定豁免條款才豁免

| Sub-group 分組 | Items 項目 |
|---|---|
| **(A) Nil premium** (admin fee if appropriate) 免補地價 | Caretaker's quarters and offices (residential) 看更宿舍及辦公室 · **Wider common corridors and lift lobbies** 加闊走廊及升降機大堂 · **Communal sky gardens** 公用空中花園 · **Communal podium gardens** (non-residential) 公用平台花園 · **Acoustic fins** 隔音鰭 · **Wing walls, wind catchers, funnels** 翼牆、捕風器、風斗 · Owners' corporation / committee offices 業主立案法團辦事處 |
| **(B) Premium assessed** + admin fee 須評估補地價 | **Curtain walls** 幕牆 · **Balconies** 露台 · **Utility platforms** 工作平台 · **NSPEW** 非結構預製外牆 · **Noise barriers** 隔音屏障 · **MiC buildings** 組裝合成建築物 · **Residential recreational facilities** (clubhouse) 住客康樂設施 |

<small>For existing leases without the clause, adding it needs a **lease modification** with premium assessed case by case. Green features and larger lift shafts apply to **new developments / redevelopments only** (footnote 4). 現有地契沒有條款者須修訂地契；環保設施及較大升降機槽只適用於新發展／重建。</small>

### Group III — Case by case; aligned and written into new leases 第 III 組：按個別情況，並在新地契中統一

1. **Public transport terminus** 公共交通交匯處
2. **Government accommodation** (other than PTT) 政府設施
3. **Covered public passageways and walkways** required by Government 政府要求的有蓋公眾通道
4. **Covered private car parks, loading/unloading** and their ancillary areas (driveways, ramps, car lift shafts, lobbies, staircases solely serving the car park) 有蓋私家停車場及上落客貨區

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "Para. 4 — OZP as the sole PR/GFA reference 大綱圖為唯一依據"
    …where a maximum PR / GFA restriction is stipulated in new or amended statutory plans gazetted on or after the effective date of this JPN, the restriction so stipulated under the planning regime will become the sole reference, save for exceptional cases, in terms of PR / GFA control when departments vet the GBPs… if the permissible PR restriction stipulated in the First Schedule to the B(P)R is more stringent than the maximum PR / GFA restriction stipulated in the relevant statutory plan, the Building Authority (BA) may, on application, favourably consider exercising discretion under section 42 of the BO to grant modification to permit the PR restriction stipulated in the First Schedule to B(P)R to be exceeded to a level on par with the maximum PR / GFA restriction under the planning regime…

    **中譯（非官方）：** ……如在本 JPN 生效日期或之後刊憲的新訂或經修訂法定圖則訂明最高地積比率／總樓面面積限制，則除特殊情況外，各部門審核一般建築圖則時，會以規劃制度下訂明的限制作為地積比率／總樓面面積管制的唯一依據……如 B(P)R 附表 1 訂明的准許地積比率較法定圖則的限制更嚴，建築事務監督可應申請從寬考慮根據《建築物條例》第 42 條行使酌情權，批出變通以容許超出附表 1 的地積比率，以達至與規劃制度下的最高地積比率／總樓面面積限制相同的水平……

!!! quote "Para. 6 — BD as sole agent for GFA verification 屋宇署為唯一核實部門"
    Under the streamlined arrangements on compliance checking process and given BD's statutory duty to check and approve GBPs… BD will become the sole agent responsible for verifying the GFA computation in accordance with regulation 23(3)(a) of the B(P)R for building facilities, features and structures of developments for the purposes of processing GBP under the three regimes. For items where GFA computations have been verified by BD, PlanD and LandsD will rely on such verified computations (i.e. follow BD's practice in GFA calculation and without undergoing their own computation)…

    **中譯（非官方）：** 在精簡的核對程序下，鑑於屋宇署負有審核及批准一般建築圖則的法定職責……屋宇署會成為唯一負責按 B(P)R 第 23(3)(a) 條核實發展項目建築設施、特色及構築物總樓面面積計算的部門，以供三個制度處理一般建築圖則之用。對於屋宇署已核實的項目，規劃署及地政總署會依據該核實結果（即沿用屋宇署的計算方法，而不另行計算）……

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    Before JPN 4, the same building could have **three different GFA figures**: one for BD, one for PlanD and one for the lease. That caused delays and arguments. JPN 4 says:

    1. **Which limit?** For newer OZPs, the **OZP figure** is the one that matters, and BD will lift the B(P)R limit to match if needed.
    2. **Who calculates?** **BD only**. The other departments accept BD's verified GFA.
    3. **What is exempt?** The **Appendix** lines up the three regimes. Group I is exempt everywhere. Group II is exempt only if the **lease allows it**, and some of these items cost **premium** (balconies, utility platforms, clubhouses, curtain walls, MiC). Group III is decided case by case.

    For a project, check the **lease** early. Many green features are only free of GFA under the lease if there is an **exemption clause**. Otherwise a **lease modification and premium** may be needed.

=== "中文"

    在 JPN 4 之前，同一幢建築物可能有**三個不同的總樓面面積數字**（屋宇署、規劃署、地契各一），導致延誤及爭議。JPN 4 訂明：

    1. **用哪個上限？** 較新的大綱圖以**大綱圖數字**為準，如有需要，屋宇署會把 B(P)R 上限提高至相同水平。
    2. **誰計算？** **只由屋宇署**計算，其他部門接受屋宇署核實的總樓面面積。
    3. **甚麼可豁免？** **附錄**把三個制度對齊：第 I 組三處均豁免；第 II 組只在**地契容許**時豁免，部分項目須**補地價**（露台、工作平台、會所、幕牆、組裝合成）；第 III 組按個別情況決定。

    處理項目時應及早核對**地契**：許多環保設施只有在地契有**豁免條款**時才在地契下不計總樓面面積，否則可能須**修訂地契及補地價**。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Assuming a BD exemption (e.g. **balcony** under JPN 1) is automatically free under an **old lease**. It is **Group II(B)**: you need a lease clause or modification, plus **premium**.
        - Doing separate GFA calculations for PlanD and LandsD. BD's verified GFA is used, except LandsD's **use-specific** GFA checks.
        - Applying para. 4 to sites under **older OZPs** (gazetted before 18 Oct 2021). It only applies to new or amended plan restrictions.
        - **Transferring GFA** from a completed phase to a later one. This is not allowed (para. 10).

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 以為屋宇署的豁免（例如 JPN 1 的**露台**）在**舊地契**下自動適用：露台屬**第 II(B) 組**，須有地契條款或修訂地契，並須**補地價**。
        - 為規劃署及地政總署分別計算總樓面面積：一般採用屋宇署核實的數字，只有地政總署的**特定用途**總樓面面積須另行核對。
        - 把第 4 段套用於**舊大綱圖**（2021 年 10 月 18 日前刊憲）涵蓋的地盤：只適用於新訂或經修訂的限制。
        - 由已完成的期數**轉移總樓面面積**至較後期數：不准許（第 10 段）。
