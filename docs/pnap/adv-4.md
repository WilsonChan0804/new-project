## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Poorly managed sites cause **noise, dust, water and waste** nuisance; APs, RSEs and RGEs should prevent or minimise it (para. 1).
    - **App. A** checklist gives practical advice, legal sanctions and responsible departments, e.g. quieter methods such as **non-percussive piling**; percussive piling is controlled by **EPD permits** (7 am – 7 pm, not public holidays) (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 管理不善的地盤會造成**噪音、塵埃、污水及廢物**滋擾；認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊岩土工程師應防止或減低影響（第 1 段）。
    - **附錄 A** 檢查表列出實用建議、法例及負責部門，例如採用**非撞擊式打樁**等較寧靜方法；撞擊式打樁受**環保署許可證**管制（上午 7 時至下午 7 時，公眾假期除外）（第 2 段）。
