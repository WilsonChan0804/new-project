## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Site investigation** (B(C)R s.2) covers desk studies, surveys and **ground investigation (GI)**, i.e. boreholes, trial pits and tests (paras. 1–2).
    - Follow GEO technical guidance (TGN 1, **Geoguide 2 and 3**) and the **Foundations Code 2017 (2024 Ed.)** (para. 3).
    - **GI field works** are specialised works and must be done by an **RSC (GIFW category)**; lab tests and field density tests are excluded (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - **地盤勘測**（B(C)R 第 2 條）包括文件研究、測量及**土地勘測**（鑽孔、試坑及測試）（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須按土力工程處技術指引（技術指引第 1 號、**岩土指南第 2 及 3 號**）及 **2017 年基礎作業守則（2024 年版）**進行（第 3 段）。
    - **土地勘測外勤工程**屬專門工程，須由**註冊專門承建商（土地勘測外勤工程類別）**進行；化驗及現場密度測試除外（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **site investigation** (desk study, surveys, ground investigation) for building works, especially foundation and site formation; GI field works by a **RSC (GIFW)**; digital **AGS** reports (from Sep 2025). 地盤勘測及土地勘測。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Site investigation** (B(C)R s.2): documentary studies, surveys and **ground investigation**; GI = boreholes, test pits, field and lab tests. 定義。 |
| 3 | Follow **GEO TGN No. 1** documents (Geoguides 2 and 3 etc.) and the **Foundations Code 2017 (2024 Edition)**. 技術指引。 |
| 4 | **GI field works** (site operations, not lab tests or field density tests) are a **specialised works** category: only **RSC (GIFW)** ([APP-96](app-96.md)); lab and field density tests by **accredited laboratories** ([APP-64](app-64.md)). 須由註冊專門承建商進行。 |
| 5 | RSC (GIFW) compile the **GI report** for foundation, SF and ELS plans (B(A)R 8(1), 9) and the GI for the **geotechnical assessment** with GBP (B(A)R 8(1)(ba)). GI 報告。 |
| 6 | RSC is responsible for **all information** in the report; **cores and samples** kept for BD / GEO inspection until completion is acknowledged. 保存岩芯。 |
| 7 | **Quality supervision** per the TM and **Site Supervision Code 2009 (2024 Edition)**. 品質監督。 |
| 8 | Submit GI and lab reports **digitally**: **PDF** per GI station (unique names, e.g. SSB-BH1) with photos, plus **AGS data** in ASCII, unsplit, with CEDD local additions. 數碼格式。 |
| 9 | Similar note to registered contractors. 承建商另有作業備考。 |
