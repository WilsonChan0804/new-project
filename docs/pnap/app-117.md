## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Every **structural A&A submission** needs an **assessment of structural viability**, based on the AP/RSE's **inspection** of the affected elements, with **site photos** where possible (para. 2).
    - **New structural elements**: designed to **current** regulations and codes, with test results and any remedial works for existing elements (para. 3).
    - **Existing elements** may be checked to the **codes they were originally designed to**, **except** (para. 4):
        - **Wind**: use the **current wind code** if wind-resisting walls/frames lose **≥ 5% stiffness**, or wind exposure area increases **≥ 10%**;
        - **Storage floors**: check with the **current minimum imposed loads**;
        - **Flat slabs** designed by working stress: check **shear** with the **1987 concrete code** if altered or more heavily loaded.
    - If original plans are unavailable, compare **new vs original** loads, moments and shears, and **open up** elements to verify reinforcement and concrete strength if needed (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 每項**結構改動加建呈交**均須附**結構可行性評估**，以認可人士／註冊結構工程師對受影響構件的**檢查**為基礎，並盡量附**現場照片**（第 2 段）。
    - **新結構構件**：按**現行**規例及守則設計，並附現有構件的測試結果及補救工程（第 3 段）。
    - **現有構件**可按其**原設計時的守則**檢查，**但以下情況除外**（第 4 段）：
        - **風力**：抗風牆／框架**剛度減少 ≥ 5%**，或受風面積**增加 ≥ 10%**，須用**現行風荷載守則**；
        - **貯物樓層**：按**現行最小外加荷載**檢查；
        - 以工作應力法設計的**無樑樓板**：如被改動或承受額外荷載，須按 **1987 年混凝土守則**檢查**剪力**。
    - 如沒有原核准圖則，可比較**新舊**荷載、彎矩及剪力；如有需要須**鑿開**構件核實鋼筋及混凝土強度（第 5 段）。

## Key clause 主要條文

!!! quote "Para. 4 — Checking existing works 現有構件的檢查"
    The structural adequacy of an existing building or part thereof, as may be affected by proposed A&A works may, subject to the following requirements, be checked according to the then prevailing Building Regulations and codes of practice to which they were designed: (a) Wind Calculations — In the case of A&A works involving: i. partial or total removal of existing major wind resisting walls or frames, which would result in a reduction in their stiffness by 5% or more, or ii. the extension of building dimensions which would result in an increase of 10% or more of the wind exposure areas of a building, the structural adequacy of the building due to wind should be checked based on the current wind code. (b) Imposed Loads in Storage Areas — When existing floors used for storage purposes are affected, their structural adequacy should be checked using the minimum imposed loads stipulated in the current Building (Construction) Regulations. (c) Shear in Flat Slabs — When flat slabs, which were originally designed based on working stress method, are structurally altered or subject to any additional load, the shearing stresses in the slabs should be checked using the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Concrete - 1987.

    **官方中文本：** 對現存樓宇或其中可能受到改建工程影響的部分，可按當年設計時的建築物規例及作業守則進行結構承載能力的檢查。唯下列情況例外：(a) 風力計算——當改建工程涉及下列情況時，應根據現行風荷載守則對結構在風荷載下的承載能力進行驗算：i. 主要抗風力牆或框架被部分或全部移除，並導致剛度減少5%或以上，或 ii. 樓宇尺寸擴大，並導致樓宇的受風面積增加10%或以上。(b) 貯物區的外加荷載——如現作貯物用途的樓層受到影響，應按現行《建築物（建造）規例》規定的最小外加荷載檢查其結構承載能力。(c) 無樑樓板的剪力——無樑樓板，其原設計為工作應力法，若需進行結構性改動或需承受任何額外荷載時，樓板的剪應力應依據《1987年混凝土的結構使用》作業守則作檢查。

## Key table: which code to use 使用哪套守則

| Situation 情況 | Code to check against 檢查依據 |
|---|---|
| New structural elements 新結構構件 | **Current** regulations and codes 現行規例及守則 |
| Existing elements affected (general) 受影響的現有構件（一般） | Codes **originally designed to** 原設計守則 |
| Wind walls/frames lose **≥ 5%** stiffness, or wind area **+≥ 10%** 抗風剛度減少 ≥ 5% 或受風面積增加 ≥ 10% | **Current wind code** 現行風荷載守則 |
| Existing **storage** floors affected 現有貯物樓層 | **Current** minimum imposed loads (B(C)R) 現行最小外加荷載 |
| **Flat slabs** (working stress) altered or loaded more 無樑樓板 | Shear check to **CoP Structural Use of Concrete 1987** 1987 年守則檢查剪力 |

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    When you alter an old building, it would be unfair (and often impossible) to bring every existing member up to today's codes. So APP-117 lets you check them to the **original codes**, except where the change hits a known weak point: **wind stability** (removing walls or enlarging the building), **storage loads** (which are higher today), and **punching shear in old flat slabs**.

=== "中文"

    改動舊樓時，要求每個現有構件都符合今天的守則既不公平，亦往往不可能。因此 APP-117 容許按**原設計守則**檢查，但在已知弱點受影響時除外：**風力穩定性**（拆牆或擴建）、**貯物荷載**（現時要求較高）及**舊式無樑樓板的衝切剪力**。
