## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Plans forming the basis of an **Exclusion Order** (Cap. 7) are normally excluded by **BO s.16(3A)** from the 2-year lapse rule in s.16(3)(d); consent is still given if works can finish within the order period (para. 1).
    - **Material variation** needing an amending order from the **Lands Tribunal**: floor space reduced **> 10%**, change of use, or other Cap. 7 premises brought in (para. 2). BD approval does not mean acceptance under Cap. 7 (para. 3).
    - BA may grant a **one-month penalty-free extension** for commencement if asked before expiry, e.g. vacant possession pending legal proceedings (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 作為**不包括令**（第 7 章）根據的圖則，一般根據**條例第 16(3A) 條**不受第 16(3)(d) 條兩年失效規定限制；如工程仍可在命令期限內完成，仍會給予同意（第 1 段）。
    - 須向**土地審裁處**申請修訂命令的**重大更改**：樓面面積減少 **> 10%**、更改用途，或牽涉其他受第 7 章規管的處所（第 2 段）；屋宇署批准不代表符合第 7 章（第 3 段）。
    - 如在期限屆滿前申請，建築事務監督可批准**一個月免罰延期**動工，例如正透過法律程序收回空置管有權（第 4–5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: redevelopment projects with an **Exclusion Order** under the **Landlord and Tenant (Consolidation) Ordinance Cap. 7**, or a Re-development Order under the **Demolished Buildings (Re-development of Sites) Ordinance Cap. 337**. 涉及《業主與租客（綜合）條例》豁除令或重建令的重建項目。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Plans forming the basis of an Exclusion Order are normally excluded by **BO s.16(3A)** from **s.16(3)(d)** (review of plans approved over 2 years); consent applied for after 2 years is given if the works can still finish within the **Re-development Order** period. 不受兩年覆核限制。 |
| 2 | **Material variation** to approved plans: (a) floor space reduced by **> 10%**; (b) **change of use** of any part; (c) involving **other premises** under Cap. 7 (e.g. extending the project). Apply to the **Lands Tribunal** for an amending Exclusion Order. 重大更改須向土地審裁處申請。 |
| 3 | BO approval does not mean the plans are accepted under Cap. 7. 不代表符合第 7 章。 |
| 4 | **Penalty-free extension of 1 month** for starting works may be granted by the BA if requested **before** the commencement period expires. 可延期一個月。 |
| 5 | Examples: no vacant possession despite diligent legal proceedings; no entry to adjoining property for shoring; consent to demolish withheld for reasons beyond control. 延期理由例子。 |
