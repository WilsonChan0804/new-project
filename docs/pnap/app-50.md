## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Temporary buildings (for a temporary purpose or of short-lived materials) are subject to **all BO provisions**: approval and consent, AP / RSE supervision, registered contractor, completion certificates, and a **temporary OP** (para. 1).
    - Submit **Form BA17** (replacing BA5 and BA8) with BA5 certificate and BA4 appointments; approval and consent are given together on **Form BD107**, stating how long the building may remain (para. 2).
    - Works must start within **3 months** of BD107 (else renew on **BA9**); BA17 processing period **60 / 30 days** (first submission / resubmission) (paras. 3–4).
    - A **temporary OP (BD105)** is needed before occupation (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 臨時建築物（作臨時用途或以非耐用物料建造）須遵守**《建築物條例》所有條文**：批准及同意、認可人士／註冊結構工程師監督、註冊承建商、完工證明書及**臨時佔用許可證**（第 1 段）。
    - 以**表格 BA17**（取代 BA5 及 BA8）連同 BA5 證明書及 BA4 委任通知呈交；批准及同意以**表格 BD107** 一併發出，並列明可保留的期限（第 2 段）。
    - 須在 BD107 發出後 **3 個月**內展開工程（否則以 **BA9** 申請續期）；BA17 審批期限 **60／30 天**（首次呈交／再呈交）（第 3–4 段）。
    - 佔用前須取得**臨時佔用許可證（BD105）**（第 5 段）。
