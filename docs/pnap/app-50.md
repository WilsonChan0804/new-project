## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Temporary buildings (for a temporary purpose or of short-lived materials) are subject to **all BO provisions**: approval and consent, AP / RSE supervision, registered contractor, completion certificates, and a **temporary OP** (para. 1).
    - Submit **Form BA17** (replacing BA5 and BA8) with BA5 certificate and BA4 appointments; approval and consent are given together on **Form BD107**, stating how long the building may remain (para. 2).
    - Works must start within **3 months** of BD107 (else renew on **BA9**); BA17 processing period **60 / 30 days** (first submission / resubmission) (paras. 3–4).
    - A **temporary OP (BD105)** is needed before occupation (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 臨時建築物（作臨時用途或以非耐用物料建造）須遵守**《建築物條例》所有條文**：批准及同意、認可人士／註冊結構工程師監督、註冊承建商、完工證明書及**臨時佔用許可證**（第 1 段）。
    - 以**表格 BA17**（取代 BA5 及 BA8）連同 BA5 證明書及 BA4 委任通知呈交；批准及同意以**表格 BD107** 一併發出，並列明可保留的期限（第 2 段）。
    - 須在 BD107 發出後 **3 個月**內展開工程（否則以 **BA9** 申請續期）；BA17 審批期限 **60／30 天**（首次呈交／再呈交）（第 3–4 段）。
    - 佔用前須取得**臨時佔用許可證（BD105）**（第 5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **temporary buildings** under B(P)R 50(1)(a): (a) for a **temporary purpose**, and / or (b) of **short-lived materials**. **All** BO provisions still apply: approval, consent, AP / RSE supervision, registered contractor, completion and TOP. 臨時建築物仍受條例全面規管。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Two categories (temporary purpose; short-lived materials); all BO procedures apply. 兩類臨時建築物。 |
| 2(a) | Submit **Form BA17** (replaces BA5 application and BA8), **BA5** certificate of preparation by AP / RSE, and **BA4** appointment. 呈交 BA17、BA5、BA4。 |
| 2(b) | On approval, **Form BD107** issued if consent can also be granted; it states **how long** the building may remain. 發出 BD107 註明存在期限。 |
| 3 | Start within **3 months** of BD107 (BO s.20), or apply to renew consent on **BA9**; may remain until BD107 expires. 3 個月內動工。 |
| 4 | BA17 combines approval and consent; time limits run **concurrently**: **60 days** first submission, **30 days** resubmission. 60／30 天。 |
| 5 | **TOP (Form BD105)** before occupation, applied for on **BA12**; BD107 conditions (max. persons, removal, expiry) carry into the TOP. 佔用前須臨時佔用許可證。 |
| 6 | Remove on expiry or cancellation, else **s.24(1) order**; occupation after expiry: prosecution under **s.40(1)**. 期滿須拆除。 |
| 7 | **Extension**: written application with original BD107 and BD105 (formerly Forms 32 and 24) and an AP / RSE certificate that it will last the extended term, **max. 5 years**. 延期最多 5 年。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A site office and show suite in a temporary building: BA17 + BA5 + BA4 submitted on day 0; BD107 by **day 60** (approval and consent together); start within 3 months; on completion apply on **BA12** for a BD105 TOP limited to, say, 80 persons ([APP-95](app-95.md) safety conditions apply to a show flat). 臨時售樓處流程。
