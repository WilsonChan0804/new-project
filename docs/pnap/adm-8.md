## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Present structural plans and calculations clearly: indexed drawings with titles and numbers (paras. 1–2).
    - First drawing: **general notes and material specs**: codes, concrete constituents (PFA, GGBS), reinforcement, grades and strengths, **cover, corrosion and fire resistance**, construction and testing (para. 3).
    - Show key plans, layouts, sections, elevations; **imposed loads** on floor plans (with variations diagrammed); for A&A, existing member details from approved plans; geotechnical items as needed (paras. 4–7).

=== "中文"

    - 結構圖則及計算須清晰編排：圖則須編索引並有標題及編號（第 1–2 段）。
    - 第一張圖：**一般註釋及物料規格**：守則、混凝土成分（煤灰、磨細礦渣）、鋼筋、等級及強度、**保護層、防銹及耐火要求**、施工及測試（第 3 段）。
    - 顯示總圖、平面、剖面、立面；在樓面圖註明**外加荷載**（不同荷載以圖示）；改動加建須顯示取自核准圖則的現有構件細節；有需要時附岩土資料（第 4–7 段）。
