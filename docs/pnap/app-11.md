## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Street improvement schemes approved **before 1982** (before the Roads (Works, Use and Compensation) Ordinance, Cap. 370) remain valid (para. 1).
    - They only indicate **desirable** improvements: no representation that Government will implement them or resume land, and **no basis for compensation claims** (paras. 2–3).
    - BO approval of plans that **conform** does not imply implementation or compensation; approval of plans that **do not conform** is **without prejudice** to resumption under other laws (paras. 4–5).
    - **Enquire with the Highways Department** before submitting plans for affected lots (para. 6).

=== "中文"

    - 在 **1982 年前**（第 370 章《道路（工程、使用及補償）條例》制定前）批准的街道改善計劃仍然有效（第 1 段）。
    - 計劃只顯示**理想**的改善，不代表政府會實施或收回土地，亦**不構成索償基礎**（第 2–3 段）。
    - 根據《建築物條例》批准**符合**計劃的圖則，並不表示會實施或補償；批准**不符合**計劃的圖則，**不影響**政府根據其他法例收回土地（第 4–5 段）。
    - 呈交受影響地段的圖則前，應**先向路政署查詢**（第 6 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: lots affected by a **street improvement scheme approved before 1982**, when the Roads (Works, Use and Compensation) Ordinance Cap. 370 was enacted; such schemes remain valid. 1982 年前核准的街道改善計劃所影響的地段。

    **Not here 不在此列**: road works gazetted under **Cap. 370** itself (see [ADM-19 App. A](adm-19.md)); setbacks on narrow streets under [B(P)R 26](../regulations/bpr-24-37.md#clause-by-clause). 第 370 章道路工程；窄街後移。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Schemes approved **before Cap. 370 (1982)** stay valid; these principles apply to them and the lots affected. 舊計劃仍然有效。 |
| 2 | Street improvement plans only **indicate desirable improvements**; no promise to implement or to **resume** property. 只屬指示性。 |
| 3 | An approved plan gives **no basis for compensation or damages**. 不構成索償基礎。 |
| 4 | Approving building plans that **conform** to the scheme does not mean it will be implemented or compensation paid. 符合計劃的圖則獲批不代表會實施。 |
| 5 | Approving plans that **do not conform** is without prejudice to Government action under other law, including **resumption**. 不符合計劃仍可能被收回。 |
| 6 | Before formal submission, **ask Highways Department** about Government's intention to avoid abortive work. 呈交前先向路政署查詢。 |
