## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Geoguide 1** sets good practice for permanent retaining walls: RC, gravity (crib, gabion, mass concrete) and cantilevered walls (para. 1).
    - The 1st edition (1982) used **global** safety factors; the 2nd edition (1993, continuously updated on the CEDD website) uses **partial factors**, with guidance on parameters and groundwater (para. 2).
    - Either edition accepted **in its entirety**; **no mixing**; state the edition used; the **2nd edition is strongly recommended** (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - **岩土指南第 1 冊**訂明永久擋土牆的良好作業標準，涵蓋鋼筋混凝土牆、重力式（框格、石籠、素混凝土）及懸臂式擋土牆（第 1 段）。
    - 第一版（1982 年）採用**整體**安全系數；第二版（1993 年，土木工程拓展署網站持續更新）採用**分項系數**，並就參數及地下水提供指引（第 2 段）。
    - **全面**按任何一版設計均可接受；**不可混用**；須註明所用版本；**強烈建議採用第二版**（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: design of **permanent retaining walls** (RC, gravity: crib, gabion, mass concrete; cantilevered) under **Geoguide 1**. 永久擋土牆設計。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Geoguide 1 sets good practice for permanent retaining walls. 擋土牆設計指引。 |
| 2 | **1st edition (1982)**: limit state with **global** factors of safety. **2nd edition (1993)**: limit state with **partial** factors, more rational and economical, with guidance on parameters, groundwater and design–construction links; updated version on CEDD's website. 第一版整體安全系數；第二版分項系數。 |
| 3 | Designs wholly to the 1st **or** 2nd edition are acceptable; **mixing** them is **not**; state the edition used; the **2nd edition is strongly recommended**. 不可混用；建議第二版。 |
