## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Contractor's sheds (site office, store, staff room, canteen) need a **permit under B(P)R 53** (para. 1).
    - **Self-certification**: sheds meeting **Appendix A** may be certified by an **RGBC or RSC** (Form **BA18** Parts I & II); others within the site by an **AP and RSE** (Parts I & III, with structural documents like B(A)R 8(1)(d), (i), plus RGE geotechnical documents where relevant) (paras. 3–5).
    - BA issues the permit on receipt, with random audit checks; renewal needs the original **Form BD108** and confirmation that certification remains valid (paras. 6–7).
    - No permit if a shed over a **hoarding / covered walkway** relies on it for support or obstructs its clear width; **combustible** sheds **≥ 3 m** from other buildings and the site boundary; fire safety per Appendix B (paras. 8–10).

=== "中文"

    - 承建商屋棚（地盤辦公室、貯物室、員工室、飯堂）須根據 **B(P)R 第 53 條**取得**准許證**（第 1 段）。
    - **自我核證**：符合**附錄 A** 的屋棚可由**註冊一般建築承建商或註冊專門承建商**核證（表格 **BA18** 第 I、II 部）；地盤內其他屋棚由**認可人士及註冊結構工程師**核證（第 I、III 部，附相當於 B(A)R 第 8(1)(d)、(i) 條的結構文件，如有需要另附註冊岩土工程師的岩土文件）（第 3–5 段）。
    - 建築事務監督收到申請即簽發准許證，並隨機抽查；續期須附原本**表格 BD108** 及核證仍然有效的確認（第 6–7 段）。
    - 在**圍板／有蓋行人道**上方的屋棚如依賴其支承或阻礙其淨闊度，不會獲發准許證；**可燃**屋棚須距其他建築物及地盤界線 **≥ 3 米**；消防設施按附錄 B（第 8–10 段）。

## Key table: Appendix A: RGBC / RSC self-certification criteria 承建商自我核證準則 {#key-table-appendix-a-rgbc-rsc-self-certification-criteria}

| Criterion 準則 | Limit 限制 |
|---|---|
| Size 大小 | Single storey, height < 3 m, floor area < **230 m²** 單層，高 < 3 米，面積 < 230 平方米 |
| Stilts 架空 | Base ≤ **1.5 m** above ground 底部離地 ≤ 1.5 米 |
| Geotechnical 岩土 | Site gradient ≤ 15°; area within 10 m < 15°; no slope > 30° or > 1.5 m high, no retaining wall > 1.5 m within 10 m |
| Location 位置 | Not on a cantilever or above hoarding / covered walkway; within site, not readily accessible to public |

## Key table: Appendix B: fire safety 消防規定 {#key-table-appendix-b-fire-safety}

| Shed 屋棚 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| Single storey < 230 m² (contractor) 單層 < 230 平方米 | One 4.5 kg CO₂ or 9 L water extinguisher at each entrance 每個入口一個滅火筒 |
| > 1 storey, < 230 m² (AP/RSE) | Extinguisher at each entrance + at least one **open staircase** |
| Single storey > 230 m² (AP/RSE) | Extinguisher at each entrance + **fire engine access within 30 m** travel distance |
| Other designs 其他設計 | FSD case-by-case 消防處個別考慮 |

## Paragraph by paragraph (full) 逐段詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision October 2007 (para. 5 and Form BA18 revised). Appendix B cites the MOE Code 1996, now replaced by the [FS Code 2011](../codes/fire-safety-code-2011.md). 已按 2007 年版本核對。

| Para. 段 | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Sheds for site office, storage, staff room or canteen need a **B(P)R 53 permit**. The professional input needed depends on size. |
| 3 | **Self-regulation**: the BA issues permits on certification by an RGBC / RSC, or by an AP and RSE. |
| 4 | Sheds meeting **Appendix A**: certified by the **RGBC or RSC** on Form **BA18 Parts I and II** with shed details. |
| 5 | Other sheds within the site: certified by an **AP and RSE** (who need not be the project's), on **BA18 Parts I and III**, with structural documents comparable to B(A)R 8(1)(d) and (i). Where Appendix A item (c) (geotechnical) applies, add **RGE** documents comparable to B(A)R 8(1)(bb) showing no effect on or from nearby slopes or retaining structures. |
| 6 | The permit is issued on receipt of a complete application. The BA **audits** random cases, but the certifiers are responsible for stability and safety. |
| 7 | **Renewal**: written application with the **original Form BD 108** and written confirmation that the BA18 certification remains valid for the extended period. |
| 8 | No permit for a shed over a hoarding or covered walkway if it **depends on that structure** for support or its supports **obstruct the clear width**. |
| 9 | Combustible sheds **≥ 3 m** from adjoining buildings and the site boundary, with that space kept clear. |
| 10 | Fire safety per Appendix B. |

**Form BA18**:
- Part I: application — applicant, site and lot, plans, duration, use, number of persons housed, latrine / WC / kitchen provision.
- Part II: RGBC / RSC confirmation that an Appendix A shed will be built under its supervision and is safe until a stated date.
- Part III(a): AP certifies the structural details and calculations (B(A)R 12(3), (4)), the fire safety provisions, and where applicable the RGE geotechnical documents.
- Part III(b): RSE certifies the structural details and calculations.
- Part III(c): contractor confirms construction under its supervision.
