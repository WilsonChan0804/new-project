## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - An **oil storage installation** is an above-ground tank (or group) with any tank **≥ 110,000 L** for petroleum products (BO s.2(1)); needs **approval and consent** before construction (paras. 1–2).
    - Deemed to satisfy B(OSI)R if it meets the **Code of Practice for Oil Storage Installations 1992** (para. 3).
    - Must not operate without a **licence**; A&A and new tanks need a **licence endorsement**; submit **operation instructions** (6 English + 1 Chinese copies) and other documents before licensing (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - **貯油裝置**指在地面以上貯存石油產品、任何一個容量 **≥ 110,000 公升**的貯油缸（或一組貯油缸）（條例第 2(1) 條）；建造前須取得**批准及同意**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 符合 **1992 年貯油裝置作業守則**即視為符合 B(OSI)R（第 3 段）。
    - 未領**牌照**不得運作；改動加建及新增貯油缸須**牌照加簽**；發牌前須提交**操作指引**（英文 6 份、中文 1 份）等文件（第 4–5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **oil storage installations**: any above-ground tank of **≥ 110,000 litres** for petroleum products, or a group of tanks in the same cluster or bunded area where any one is that size (BO s.2(1)); construction, licensing, inspection, A&A, repair and **demolition**. 容量 11 萬公升或以上的地面油缸。

    **Not here 不在此列**: smaller tanks (Dangerous Goods law, FSD); underground caverns ([APP-71](app-71.md), [Oil caverns code](../codes/oil-storage-caverns.md)). 較小油缸；地下岩洞。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Definition: tank (or group) **≥ 110,000 L** above ground for petroleum products. 定義。 |
| 2 | Approval and **consent under BO s.14(1)** before construction. 須批准及同意。 |
| 3 | Deemed to satisfy the **B(OSI)R** if it meets the **Code of Practice for Oil Storage Installations 1992**. 符合 1992 守則即當作符合。 |
| 4–5 | **Licence** needed before operation; A&A (incl. a new tank) needs a licence **endorsement**. Submit **operation instructions** (6 English + 1 Chinese copies) and general arrangement plans for agreement. 須領牌；操作指引中英文。 |
| 6 | **OP and certificate of general inspection** before a new licence; licence **renewed annually** with **external inspection** certificates of tanks (reg. 8(1)(b), App. B) and of associated works (reg. 8(1)(c), App. C). 每年續牌。 |
| 7 | **Internal general inspection** of every tank by the **10th year**, then **every 5 years**; certificate (App. A) to the licensee, then to the BA. 第 10 年內部檢查，其後每 5 年。 |
| 8 | Repairs and A&A: 2 copies of RSE-signed proposals for **written authorization** (reg. 10); extensive repairs or non-exempted works also need **s.14(1)** approval. 須書面授權。 |
| 9 | **Typhoon** precautions for partly built tanks. 颱風預防。 |
| 10 | **Demolition**: method statement agreed before or with the consent / authorization application, following **Appendix D**. 拆卸方法須獲同意。 |

## Appendix D: demolition of oil storage installations 附錄 D：拆卸油缸 {#appendix-d}

| Item | Requirement 要求 |
|---|---|
| 1 Environment | EPD contaminated land guidance; noise practice notes; nuisance control; asbestos report and plan (APCO) if needed |
| 2 Hoarding | Enclose the area; **fire screen** if part stays in operation |
| 3–4 Gas freeing | Institute of Petroleum code; **permit-to-work**; pipes and tanks certified **gas-free** by a trained competent person |
| 5 Sludge | Identify and dispose under the Chemical Waste Regulations |
| 6–7 Waste water | Avoid spillage; keep drains and **oil interceptors** working; seaboard valves locked except licensed discharge (WPCO) |
| 8 Bunds | Demolish bund floors and walls only after tanks and pipes are removed |
| 9 Programme | Plan around parts still operating; follow agreed operation instructions |
| 10 Drainage | Keep contaminated water out of storm drains; storm drain plan on site |
| 11 Weather | Precautions for extreme weather |
| 12–13 Contamination report | Before works: past activities, chemical inventory, processes, **soil sampling plan**; identifies soil needing treatment |
| 14 Soil tests | After demolition, test for **hydrocarbons and lead** |
