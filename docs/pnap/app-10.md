## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - An **oil storage installation** is an above-ground tank (or group) with any tank **≥ 110,000 L** for petroleum products (BO s.2(1)); needs **approval and consent** before construction (paras. 1–2).
    - Deemed to satisfy B(OSI)R if it meets the **Code of Practice for Oil Storage Installations 1992** (para. 3).
    - Must not operate without a **licence**; A&A and new tanks need a **licence endorsement**; submit **operation instructions** (6 English + 1 Chinese copies) and other documents before licensing (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - **貯油裝置**指在地面以上貯存石油產品、任何一個容量 **≥ 110,000 公升**的貯油缸（或一組貯油缸）（條例第 2(1) 條）；建造前須取得**批准及同意**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 符合 **1992 年貯油裝置作業守則**即視為符合 B(OSI)R（第 3 段）。
    - 未領**牌照**不得運作；改動加建及新增貯油缸須**牌照加簽**；發牌前須提交**操作指引**（英文 6 份、中文 1 份）等文件（第 4–5 段）。
