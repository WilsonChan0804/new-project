## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Forms specified under **BO s.22(4)** are listed in **App. A** and must be used **unaltered**; otherwise the application, notice or certificate may be invalid (paras. 1–2).
    - Online submission: **EFSS** and e-Counter; **ESH** Stages 1–3 (Jun 2022, Mar 2023, Jun 2024) for new building and A&A forms; the **RMWMS** (launched **29 May 2026**) for registration and minor works forms (paras. 3–6).
    - Electronic plan format per **ADM-17**; download the latest forms from the BD website and fill them on computer so the **QR code** captures the data (paras. 7–8).

=== "中文"

    - 根據**條例第 22(4) 條**指明的表格列於**附錄 A**，必須**原樣**使用，否則申請、通知或證明書可能無效（第 1–2 段）。
    - 網上呈交：**電子表格呈交系統**及電子櫃位；**電子呈交平台**第 1–3 階段（2022 年 6 月、2023 年 3 月、2024 年 6 月）處理新建築物及改動加建表格；**註冊及小型工程管理系統**（**2026 年 5 月 29 日**推出）處理註冊及小型工程表格（第 3–6 段）。
    - 電子圖則格式見 **ADM-17**；應從屋宇署網站下載最新表格並以電腦填寫，使資料儲存於 **QR 碼**（第 7–8 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: all applications, notices and certificates that the BO or its regulations require in a **specified form** (BO **s.22(4)**). Use the forms **unaltered**; otherwise the submission may be **invalid**. Many can be submitted online via **EFSS / e-Counter**, **ESH** or the **RMWMS** (from **29 May 2026**). 指明表格。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Forms specified under **s.22(4)** (Appendix A); must be used unaltered or the submission may be **invalid**. 不得修改。 |
| 3 | **EFSS** on BD's website and **e-Counter** (receipt@bd.gov.hk). 網上表格系統。 |
| 4 | **ESH** Stages 1–3 (30 Jun 2022, 31 Mar 2023, 30 Jun 2024) for common NB and A&A forms. 電子呈交平台。 |
| 5–6 | **Registration and Minor Works Management System (RMWMS)** from **29 May 2026**; Appendix A shows which forms go online by which system. 新系統。 |
| 7–8 | Electronic format per [ADM-17](adm-17.md); latest versions on BD's website; fill on computer so the **QR code** stores data; paper copies at BD HQ, West Kowloon. QR 碼。 |
| 9 | MBIS / MWIS forms: **PNBI-3**. 強制驗樓表格另見。 |

## Appendix A: main specified forms 附錄 A：主要指明表格 {#appendix-a}

| Group 類別 | Forms 表格 |
|---|---|
| **Registers** 註冊 | **BA1 / 1A / 1B** (AP, RSE, RGE, RI registers: inclusion, retention, restoration); **BA2 / 2A / 2B / 2C** (RGBC / RSC: registration, renewal, restoration, TD / AS approval); **BA24** change of address |
| **Plans and consent** 圖則及同意 | **BA4** appointment of AP / RSE / RGE; **BA5** application for approval and certificate of preparation; **BA6** stability certificate (A&A); **BA7** urgent works; **BA8** consent; **BA8A** concurrent consent; **BA9** renewal of consent; **BA10** appointment of RC and commencement; **BA11** RC ceasing; **BA16** modification / exemption; **BA21** temporary acting AP / RSE / RGE |
| **Completion** 完工 | **BA12** (new temporary building / part, TOP); **BA13** (new building, OP); **BA14** (works not resulting in a new building); **BA14A / 14B** (demolition, normal / streamlined); **BA14C** (streamlined A&A) |
| **Use and special works** 用途及特別工程 | **BA15** material change in use; **BA17** temporary building permit; **BA18** contractor's shed; **BA19** hoardings and covered walkways; **BA20** demolition TCPs; **BA22** groundwater drainage works (s.28B); **BA23** oil storage licence |
| **Minor works contractors** 小型工程承建商 | **BA25–25E** (company), **BA26–26D** (individual): registration, renewal, restoration, extra classes, signatories, review |
| **Minor works** 小型工程 | **MW01 / 02** Class I start / completion; **MW03 / 04** Class II; **MW05** Class III; **MW06-1 / -2 / -3** validation schemes; **MW07 / 08** change of appointment; **MW09–10** acting / cessation; **MW11 / 12** additional works; **MW31**; **MW32** signboard submission number |
| **Signboards** 招牌 | **SC01 / SC01C**, **SC02 / SC02C**, **SC03** (validation of unauthorised signboards, Classes I–III) |

<small>Online availability (EFSS / e-Counter, RMWMS, ESH) is shown per form in the original Appendix A. 各表格的網上呈交渠道見原文附錄 A。</small>
