## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Fast-track construction with **different concrete grades** for vertical and horizontal elements needs earlier quality control and an **alert system** for RSE and RC (para. 1).
    - APP-158 App. A lists in-situ concrete supervision items: quality, placing, compaction, curing, condition after striking, and strength by coring or **rebound hammer tests (RHT)** (para. 2).
    - A condition on the **first superstructure approval** with mixed grades requires **early-age RHT** on higher-grade elements (App. A); other projects are encouraged to adopt it (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 快速施工並在垂直及水平構件採用**不同混凝土等級**，須及早進行品質控制，並為註冊結構工程師及註冊承建商設**警報機制**（第 1 段）。
    - APP-158 附錄 A 列出現場混凝土監督項目：品質、澆灌、搗實、養護、拆模後狀況，以及以鑽芯或**回彈錘測試**驗證強度（第 2 段）。
    - 涉及不同等級的**首次上蓋圖則批准**會附加條件，要求對較高等級構件進行**早齡期回彈錘測試**（附錄 A）；其他項目亦應考慮採用（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: in-situ RC **superstructure** with **different concrete grades**: a s.17 condition on **first superstructure approval** requires **rebound hammer tests (RHT)** on the **higher-grade** elements at **7–10 days**. Other projects are encouraged to adopt it. 不同混凝土強度的上蓋結構早期回彈錘測試。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Para. 1 | Fast-track works and mixed grades need **earlier** quality checks and an **alert system**. 目的。 |
| Para. 2 | Quality supervision per [APP-158](app-158.md) App. A (concrete quality, placing, compaction, curing, post-striking condition, in-situ strength by cores or RHT). 品質監督。 |
| Para. 3–4 | **s.17(1) item 6** condition on first superstructure approval with different grades; App. A details; similar note to contractors. 批准條件。 |
| App. 1 | RHT on higher-grade elements **between day 7 and day 10** (next working day if a holiday), to **BS EN 12504-2:2021**. 第 7 至 10 天。 |
| App. 2–4 | Qualified supervision by RSE and RC; RSE selects elements and frequency; RSE's **TCP T3** witnesses **≥ 10%**; tests by RC's **TCP T1** or above (or a HOKLAS lab); records on the **Annex form** kept on site. 監督及記錄。 |
| App. 5 | Frequency: (1) vertical elements of higher grade than their **base support** (pile cap, footing, raft, transfer plate / beam) **50%**; (2) vertical elements higher than the beams / slabs **above** **10%**; (3) **transfer beams 50%**; (4) beams higher than adjoining beams / slabs **10%**; test **kickers** too; random selection. 測試頻率。 |
| App. 6 | **Alert level** (without engineering assessment): RHT strength **< 55%** of characteristic strength (with **PFA / GGBS**) or **< 65%** (without). Actions: (a) check plans, concrete orders and delivery, concreting and curing, supervision records; (b) **extra RHT or cores**; (c) tighten supervision on similar elements; (d) review, report to BD and **suspend** concreting if needed. 警戒水平及跟進。 |
| App. 7 | Alternative: **maturity method** on day 7 (Concrete Code 11.7.5.4), same points and alert level. 成熟度法。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A C60 column on a C45 transfer plate: test **50%** of such columns (and their kickers) at day 7–10. Without PFA the alert is **< 65% × 60 = 39 MPa**; with PFA **< 55% × 60 = 33 MPa**. A reading of 36 MPa on a non-PFA column triggers record checks, extra RHT or cores, and closer supervision. 計算例子。
