## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Permissible SC and PR depend on a site **under the applicant's title or realistic control**; boundaries and setting-out support the area and site class (para. 1).
    - GBP must substantiate site parameters with a **detailed lease plan or Land Survey Plan (LSP)** (B(A)R 8(1)(h)(i); ADV-33) (para. 2); the AP keeps them accurate and updated before completion certification (para. 3).
    - Common proofs: DSO **lease plans** with areas, boundaries and coordinates; **LSPs** by an Authorized Land Surveyor; others in para. 4.

=== "中文"

    - 允許的上蓋面積及地積比率取決於**申請人擁有業權或有實際控制前景**的地盤；地界及定界支持所申報的面積及地盤類別（第 1 段）。
    - 一般建築圖則須以**詳細地契圖則或土地測量圖**證明地盤參數（B(A)R 第 8(1)(h)(i) 條；ADV-33）（第 2 段）；認可人士須在核證完工前確保參數準確並已更新（第 3 段）。
    - 常見證明：地政處的**地契圖則**（面積、地界及坐標）；由認可土地測量師擬備的**土地測量圖**；其他見第 4 段。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: every GBP submission relying on a site area for **site coverage and plot ratio** (B(A)R 8(1)(h)(i); B(P)R 19–21). 所有涉及地盤面積的總綱建築圖則。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Max SC and PR depend on site area under the applicant's **title or realistic control**; boundaries also needed | 面積決定上限 |
| 2 | B(A)R **8(1)(h)(i)** diagrammatic plan; [ADV-33](adv-33.md) App. A1 item 3(a)(1.1): substantiate with **lease plan or LSP** | 法定要求 |
| 3 | AP responsible for accuracy; update before certifying completion | 認可人士責任 |
| 4 | Proofs: (a) **DSO lease plan** with coordinates; (b) **LSP** by an Authorized Land Surveyor; (c) lease documents (approx.); (d) **deed polls** of subdivided lots; (e) approved GBP of existing buildings | 證明文件 |
| 5 | (a) or (b) accepted for GBP; (c)–(e) only **provisional**; (a) or (b) must be submitted **before superstructure consent** (condition under B(A)R 10) | 上蓋同意前提交 |
| 6 | Discrepancy with other documents: (a)/(b) prevail; see BO **s.14(2)** | 資料不符 |
| 7 | (a) and (b) differ: AP gives rationale before superstructure consent | 兩者不符 |
| 8 | Later proof changes layout, SC or PR: **amended GBP** before superstructure consent | 修訂圖則 |
| 9 | Land Survey Code rounding: coordinates to **0.001 m**; area to nearest **0.1 m²** if < 2,000 m², **1 m²** if ≥ 2,000 m² (5 up 4 down) | 小數位 |
| 10 | **Two sets** of LSP: one referred to DLO for the DSO | 兩套 LSP |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    LSP area computed as 1,234.56 m² → shown as **1,234.6 m²**; 2,345.5 m² → **2,346 m²**. A 1,234.6 m² site with PR 9 allows 11,111.4 m² GFA (subject to B(P)R 21 and lease). 進位例子。
