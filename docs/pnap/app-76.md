# APP-76 Keeping Buried Services out of Slopes<br><small>地下設施遠離斜坡</small>

## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Leaking water-carrying services (drains, sewers, mains, catchwaters) and even cable ducts or disused pipes can **destabilise slopes and walls** (paras. 1–2).
    - Keep buried services **out of slopes**: as a rule, **not nearer the crest than the slope's vertical height** (GMS s.9.7); re-route existing services when upgrading slopes or relaying services (paras. 3–5).
    - If unavoidable, design the slope to GMS Ch. 5 safety factors **allowing for leakage**, or adopt other protective measures (para. 6 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 輸水設施（雨水渠、污水渠、水管、集水溝）漏水，甚至電纜管道或廢置喉管，均可**影響斜坡及擋土牆穩定**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 地下設施應**遠離斜坡**：一般而言，與坡頂的距離**不應小於斜坡垂直高度**（《斜坡岩土工程手冊》第 9.7 節）；改善斜坡或重鋪設施時應考慮改道（第 3–5 段）。
    - 如無法避免，斜坡須按手冊第 5 章的安全系數設計並**計及漏水影響**，或採用其他保護措施（第 6 段起）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: routing of **water-carrying and other buried services** (drains, sewers, mains, cable ducts, disused pipes) near **slopes and retaining walls**. 斜坡附近的埋地設施。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Guidelines; professional judgement still needed. 指引。 |
| 2 | Leaks from drains, sewers, mains, catchwaters, tunnels, and water carried along **cable ducts or disused pipes**, threaten slope stability. 漏水風險。 |
| 3 | Keep services **out of slopes** where practicable; otherwise make provision. 盡量避開斜坡。 |
| 4 | Rule: not nearer to the **crest** than a distance equal to the slope's **vertical height** (Geotechnical Manual for Slopes s.9.7). 離坡頂不少於坡高。 |
| 5 | **Re-route** existing services when slopes are upgraded or services relaid. 改道現有設施。 |
| 6 | If unavoidable: design the slope to Chapter 5 FoS **allowing for leakage**, or (a) lay services **on the surface** (protected); (b) in **ducts, sealed trenches or sleeves** drained to a monitored outlet with inspection chambers; (c) **stop valves** on both sides of the slope for water mains. 無法避免時的措施。 |
| 7–8 | In **fill slopes**, allow for **differential settlement**: avoid rigid pipelines, use **flexible joints** with short pieces and **non-brittle** pipes. 填土斜坡用柔性接頭。 |
| 9 | Keep accurate **as-built records**, update plans to departments, include in slope record plans, give copies to owners / managers. 竣工記錄。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A 6 m high cut slope above a new block: keep the new 300 mm stormwater drain at least **6 m** back from the crest. If the access road forces a water main within that zone, put it in a **drained sleeve** with an inspection chamber and fit **stop valves** at both ends. 6 米高斜坡例子。
