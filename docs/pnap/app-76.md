# APP-76 Keeping Buried Services out of Slopes<br><small>地下設施遠離斜坡</small>

## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Leaking water-carrying services (drains, sewers, mains, catchwaters) and even cable ducts or disused pipes can **destabilise slopes and walls** (paras. 1–2).
    - Keep buried services **out of slopes**: as a rule, **not nearer the crest than the slope's vertical height** (GMS s.9.7); re-route existing services when upgrading slopes or relaying services (paras. 3–5).
    - If unavoidable, design the slope to GMS Ch. 5 safety factors **allowing for leakage**, or adopt other protective measures (para. 6 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 輸水設施（雨水渠、污水渠、水管、集水溝）漏水，甚至電纜管道或廢置喉管，均可**影響斜坡及擋土牆穩定**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 地下設施應**遠離斜坡**：一般而言，與坡頂的距離**不應小於斜坡垂直高度**（《斜坡岩土工程手冊》第 9.7 節）；改善斜坡或重鋪設施時應考慮改道（第 3–5 段）。
    - 如無法避免，斜坡須按手冊第 5 章的安全系數設計並**計及漏水影響**，或採用其他保護措施（第 6 段起）。
