## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - New streets in **large developments** can split a site into separate sites, each developed to the maximum, fragmenting open space (paras. 1–2).
    - The BO has **no explicit power to transfer PR** (e.g. to keep one site as a park), because the park could later be built on (para. 3).
    - Where **effective planning controls** exist (lease control drawings, **Master Layout Plans**, TPO s.16 permissions), a **higher PR on another site** may be considered by modification, if planning requirements are not impaired; later changes that negate the benefit would be opposed (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - **大型發展**的新街道可能把地盤分成多個獨立地盤，各自盡用發展潛力，令休憩用地分散（第 1–2 段）。
    - 《建築物條例》沒有明確權力**轉移地積比率**（例如保留一個地盤作公園），因公園日後可能被發展（第 3 段）。
    - 如有**有效的規劃管制**（地契管制圖、**總綱發展藍圖**、城規第 16 條許可），可透過變通考慮在**另一地盤給予較高地積比率**，但不得損害規劃要求；日後抵銷有關好處的改變會被反對（第 4–5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **large developments** where new streets split the land into several sites, under **effective planning controls** (lease control drawings, Master Layout Plans, TPO s.16 permissions). 受有效規劃管制的大型發展。

    **Does not apply 不適用**: ordinary single sites; the BO itself has **no power to transfer PR** between sites. 一般地盤；條例本身不容許轉移地積比率。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | New streets in large developments may enclose or isolate land, dividing the original site into **several separate sites**. 新街道把地盤分割。 |
| 2 | Each site is then developed to the maximum and open space is **fragmented**; giving one island site over to a park "loses" its PR, which deters better planning. 休憩用地分散。 |
| 3 | The BO has **no explicit provision** to transfer PR; the BO cannot stop the park being built on later, causing excess building mass and infrastructure overload. 條例無轉移地積比率條文。 |
| 4 | Where **lease control drawings, MLPs or TPO s.16 permissions** exist and a park is proposed on one site, a **higher PR on another site** in the project, with consequential adjustments, may be considered on a modification application (old **Form 29**, now **BA16**), if planning requirements are not significantly impaired. 可透過變通在另一地盤給予較高地積比率。 |
| 5 | Any later change to those controls that would **negate the benefit** granted (old **Form 30**, now **BD 106** permit) would be **opposed**. 日後抵銷好處的修改會被反對。 |

<small>Forms 29 / 30 are the pre-2009 names of today's **BA16** application and **BD 106** permit. 舊表格 29／30 即現時的 BA16 及 BD 106。</small>
