## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - New streets in **large developments** can split a site into separate sites, each developed to the maximum, fragmenting open space (paras. 1–2).
    - The BO has **no explicit power to transfer PR** (e.g. to keep one site as a park), because the park could later be built on (para. 3).
    - Where **effective planning controls** exist (lease control drawings, **Master Layout Plans**, TPO s.16 permissions), a **higher PR on another site** may be considered by modification, if planning requirements are not impaired; later changes that negate the benefit would be opposed (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - **大型發展**的新街道可能把地盤分成多個獨立地盤，各自盡用發展潛力，令休憩用地分散（第 1–2 段）。
    - 《建築物條例》沒有明確權力**轉移地積比率**（例如保留一個地盤作公園），因公園日後可能被發展（第 3 段）。
    - 如有**有效的規劃管制**（地契管制圖、**總綱發展藍圖**、城規第 16 條許可），可透過變通考慮在**另一地盤給予較高地積比率**，但不得損害規劃要求；日後抵銷有關好處的改變會被反對（第 4–5 段）。
