## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - External pipes, windows, paint and finishes need work at height; **permanent access facilities** improve safety (paras. 1–2).
    - Consider these at design stage, e.g. a permanent **suspended access system** (ADV-11); the CIC recommends **gondola systems** at the roof of new buildings (paras. 3–4). See also APP-163.

=== "中文"

    - 外牆喉管、窗、油漆及飾面須在高處工作；**永久通道設施**可提高安全（第 1–2 段）。
    - 應在設計階段考慮，例如永久**吊掛式通道系統**（ADV-11）；建造業議會建議新建築物天台設**吊船系統**（第 3–4 段）；另見 APP-163。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: design of **new buildings**, including residential (strong recommendation). 新建築物設計。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | External maintenance at height; consider permanent safe access at design stage | 設計階段考慮 |
| 3 | Permanent suspended access: see [ADV-11](adv-11.md) | 吊船 |
| 4 | CIC: **gondola** at the roof of new buildings strongly recommended | 屋頂吊船 |
| 5 PlanD | Gondola enclosure as **rooftop utility structure** exempt from height if minimum size; generally ≤ **15 m** above main roof or **10%** of height, whichever less; ridgeline views | 規劃署 |
| 5 LandsD | Exempt from lease height if not excessive | 地政總署 |
| 5 BD | Supports to BO and B(C)R, BA approval; corrosion protection; restraint against sway; safe access | 屋宇署 |
| 6 | Otherwise roof **booms or anchorages** and hooks, bolts, platforms for future scaffolding; corrosion resistant | 吊臂及錨固 |
| 7 | **Cast-in anchors** for fall protection (CIC Technical Guidelines) | 預埋錨固 |
| 8–9 | Access to pipes in **lightwells and re-entrants**; lowest level as **common area** in the DMC with cat-ladder access | 天井及凹位 |
