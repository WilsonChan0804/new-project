## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - External pipes, windows, paint and finishes need work at height; **permanent access facilities** improve safety (paras. 1–2).
    - Consider these at design stage, e.g. a permanent **suspended access system** (ADV-11); the CIC recommends **gondola systems** at the roof of new buildings (paras. 3–4). See also APP-163.

=== "中文"

    - 外牆喉管、窗、油漆及飾面須在高處工作；**永久通道設施**可提高安全（第 1–2 段）。
    - 應在設計階段考慮，例如永久**吊掛式通道系統**（ADV-11）；建造業議會建議新建築物天台設**吊船系統**（第 3–4 段）；另見 APP-163。
