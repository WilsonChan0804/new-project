## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Corruption can lead to works not complying with the BO (para. 1).
    - APs and RSEs should **prohibit site staff** from soliciting or accepting advantages, adopt a **company code of conduct**, and **report bribes to the ICAC immediately** (2526 6366, 24 hours) (para. 2).
    - A bribe may be a loan, gift, favour or offer, not only money (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 貪污可導致工程不符合條例（第 1 段）。
    - 認可人士及註冊結構工程師應**禁止地盤人員**索取或接受利益，制定**公司行為守則**，並在有人行賄時**立即向廉政公署舉報**（2526 6366，24 小時）（第 2 段）。
    - 賄賂不一定是金錢，亦可以是貸款、禮物、恩惠或其他提議（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: AP, RSE and their **site staff** supervising works. 認可人士、結構工程師及其駐地盤人員。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corruption may lead to works not complying with the BO | 背景 |
| 2 | Prohibit staff from soliciting, accepting or offering advantages; adopt a **code of conduct**; report bribes to **ICAC 2526 6366** (24 h) | 須禁止及舉報 |
| 3 | Advantage includes loans, gifts, favours, employment (**Cap. 201**, App. I) | 利益定義 |
| 4 | ICAC **Advisory Services Group** (2526 6363) free advice | 防貪諮詢 |

<small>Appendix I “advantage”: (a) gift, loan, fee, reward, commission; (b) office, employment, contract; (c) discharge of loans or liabilities; (d) other service or favour (not entertainment); (e) exercise or forbearance of a right, power or duty; (f) offer or promise of any of these. 利益的定義。</small>
