## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - A **self-certification** fast track for **simple, lower-risk structural works**, to streamline development while keeping safety (para. 1).
    - Five categories, **A to E**, by extent, complexity, method and risk; eligibility in **App. A** (paras. 2–3).
    - Relies on the **RSE's self-certification**; **Categories C–E** also need an **independent checking engineer (ICE)**. Works outside the criteria go through normal processing (para. 3). Later paras. set procedures and audit.

=== "中文"

    - 為**簡單、風險較低的結構工程**設立**自行認證**快速處理制度，在確保安全下簡化發展程序（第 1 段）。
    - 按規模、複雜程度、施工方法及風險分為 **A 至 E** 五類；資格見**附錄 A**（第 2–3 段）。
    - 倚賴**註冊結構工程師自行認證**；**C 至 E 類**另須委任**獨立審核工程師**；不符準則的工程按一般程序處理（第 3 段）；其後各段訂明程序及抽查。
