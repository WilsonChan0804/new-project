## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - A **self-certification** fast track for **simple, lower-risk structural works**, to streamline development while keeping safety (para. 1).
    - Five categories, **A to E**, by extent, complexity, method and risk; eligibility in **App. A** (paras. 2–3).
    - Relies on the **RSE's self-certification**; **Categories C–E** also need an **independent checking engineer (ICE)**. Works outside the criteria go through normal processing (para. 3). RSE (and ICE) complete the **Appendix B1 / B2 checklists** and **Appendix C certificate** with BA5 / BA6 (para. 4); the ICE is an RSE appointed by the **owner** with **no conflict of interest** (para. 5); BD does a **curtailed check** (para. 7). Processing: **20 days** via ESH (40 on paper) for first submissions, **10 days** (20) for amendments (para. 8); later submissions must stay on self-certification (para. 9); random **audit checks** (para. 12).

=== "中文"

    - 為**簡單、風險較低的結構工程**設立**自行認證**快速處理制度，在確保安全下簡化發展程序（第 1 段）。
    - 按規模、複雜程度、施工方法及風險分為 **A 至 E** 五類；資格見**附錄 A**（第 2–3 段）。
    - 倚賴**註冊結構工程師自行認證**；**C 至 E 類**另須委任**獨立審核工程師**；不符準則的工程按一般程序處理（第 3 段）；註冊結構工程師（及獨立審核工程師）須連同 BA5／BA6 填寫**附錄 B1／B2 核對表**及**附錄 C 證明書**（第 4 段）；獨立審核工程師須為由**業主**委任、**沒有利益衝突**的註冊結構工程師（第 5 段）；屋宇署只作**精簡審查**（第 7 段）。處理時間：首次呈交經電子呈交平台 **20 天**（紙本 40 天），修訂 **10 天**（紙本 20 天）（第 8 段）；其後呈交須沿用自行認證（第 9 段）；屋宇署會**隨機抽查**（第 12 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Self-certification with fast-track processing** for **simple structural works**. 簡單結構工程自行認證。 |
| 2 | Five categories: **A, B, C, D, E**, by extent, complexity, method and risk. 五類。 |
| 3 | Descriptions and eligibility in **Appendix A**; relies on the **RSE's certification**; **C–E** also need an **ICE**. 資格及獨立審核工程師。 |
| 4 | With Forms **BA5 / BA6**: checklists **B1** (RSE) and **B2** (ICE) and the **Appendix C** certificate. 核對表及證明書。 |
| 5 | **ICE**: an RSE on the register, appointed by the **developer/owner**, independent of the RSE and project; notify appointment and declare no conflict. 獨立審核工程師。 |
| 6 | ICE does a **fundamental check** (Appendix B2) and advises eligibility. 基本審核。 |
| 7 | BD's **curtailed check**: completeness, registration status and selected key items. 精簡審查。 |
| 8 | Processing: first submission / resubmission / major revision **20 days** (ESH) or **40** (paper); amendments and fast-track consent **10** or **20 days**. 處理時間。 |
| 9–10 | Once used, later submissions stay on self-certification; may join it at a later submission. 持續採用。 |
| 11 | **Concurrent approval and consent** possible once the building plans are approved (ADM-19). 同時申請。 |
| 12 | Random **audit checks**; misrepresentation or misconduct leads to action under the BO. 抽查。 |
