## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Owners must appoint **registered building professionals**, who have statutory duties to supervise works, notify BD of contraventions and comply with the BO (paras. 1–2).
    - BD only carries out **audit checks**; full compliance is the professional's responsibility (para. 2).
    - BD regulates professionals via registration, prosecution and **discipline**: under **BO s.7(1A)(b)**, negligence or professional misconduct can lead to a disciplinary board (para. 3 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 業主須委任**註冊建築專業人士**，他們負有監督工程、向屋宇署通報違規及遵從條例的法定責任（第 1–2 段）。
    - 屋宇署只作**抽查**；確保完全合規是專業人士的責任（第 2 段）。
    - 屋宇署透過註冊、檢控及**紀律處分**規管專業人士：根據**條例第 7(1A)(b) 條**，疏忽或專業失當可交紀律委員會處理（第 3 段起）。
