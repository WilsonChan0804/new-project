## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Owners must appoint **registered building professionals**, who have statutory duties to supervise works, notify BD of contraventions and comply with the BO (paras. 1–2).
    - BD only carries out **audit checks**; full compliance is the professional's responsibility (para. 2).
    - BD regulates professionals via registration, prosecution and **discipline**: under **BO s.7(1A)(b)**, negligence or professional misconduct can lead to a disciplinary board (para. 3 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 業主須委任**註冊建築專業人士**，他們負有監督工程、向屋宇署通報違規及遵從條例的法定責任（第 1–2 段）。
    - 屋宇署只作**抽查**；確保完全合規是專業人士的責任（第 2 段）。
    - 屋宇署透過註冊、檢控及**紀律處分**規管專業人士：根據**條例第 7(1A)(b) 條**，疏忽或專業失當可交紀律委員會處理（第 3 段起）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **AP, RSE, RGE, Registered Inspectors and Qualified Persons**. 認可人士、註冊結構及岩土工程師、註冊檢驗人員及合資格人士。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Professionals supervise works and notify contraventions; BD's checks are curtailed and audit, **responsibility stays with the professional** | 法定責任 |
| 3 | BA may refer **negligence or misconduct** to a disciplinary board (BO **s.7(1A)(b)**, s.5); considers seriousness | 紀律處分 |
| 4 | Examples in Appendix A | 例子 |
| 5 | Report irregularities in confidence to the **Chief Officer / Technical Services** | 舉報 |

### Appendix A examples 附錄 A 例子 {#app-a-examples}

| Area 範疇 | Examples 例子 |
|---|---|
| 1 Compliance | (a) no supervision and **material deviation**; (b) knowingly **misrepresents** a fact; (c) undertakes work not qualified for |
| 2 Integrity | (a) abets **UBW** or disregard of MWCS; (b) **falsifies** records; (c) advertises UBW skills; (d) undeclared **interests** |
| 3 Co-operation | (a) repeatedly fails to provide information; (b) **obstructs** investigation; (c) ignores written warnings |
| 4 Other laws | (a) repeatedly fails to remind contractor of site safety (Cap. 59); (b) uses BD logo |
