## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BD favourably considers **modifying B(P)R 36** for **internal bathrooms and lavatories** in **domestic units** of reasonable size, without compromising health and safety (paras. 1–2).
    - Conditions (para. 3): artificial lighting and **mechanical ventilation of 5 ACH** to the outside whenever in use (ducts with cleaning access); **permanent ventilation** to open / external air or to a windowed room, via duct or permanently open louvred aperture **≥ 1/20** of floor area; **duct access panels** **150 × 225 mm** (ducts ≥ 150 × 150) or **100 × 225 mm** (smaller) every **4 m** and at bends, vanes and dampers; plus **B(P)R 35A** gas heater provisions and other conditions.

=== "中文"

    - 屋宇署會正面考慮就**住宅單位**內面積合理的**內置浴室及洗手間**變通 **B(P)R 第 36 條**，前提是不影響衞生及安全（第 1–2 段）。
    - 條件（第 3 段）：使用時須有人工照明及通往室外的**機械通風每小時換氣 5 次**（風管須可清潔）；須有通往露天地方／室外或有窗房間的**永久通風**，經風管或永久開啟的百葉開口，面積 **≥ 樓面面積 1/20**；風管每 **4 米**及轉角、導流片、風閘處設**檢修門** **150 × 225 毫米**（風管 ≥ 150 × 150）或 **100 × 225 毫米**（較小風管）；另須符合 **B(P)R 第 35A 條**熱水爐規定等。

## Key table: modification conditions 變通條件 {#key-table-modification-conditions}

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| Mechanical ventilation 機械通風 | **5 ACH** to outside when in use 使用時每小時換氣 5 次 |
| Permanent ventilation 永久通風 | Duct or louvred opening **≥ 1/20** of floor area to open / external air or a windowed room |
| Duct access 風管檢修門 | 150 × 225 mm (ducts ≥ 150 × 150) or 100 × 225 mm, every **4 m** and at bends / dampers |
| Gas water heater 熱水爐 | B(P)R 35A and [APP-27](app-27.md) |
