## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Sewage tunnel routes are gazetted under the **Sewage Tunnels (Statutory Easements) Ordinance**; under **BO s.17A** the BA may refuse or condition works incompatible with them (paras. 1–2).
    - **Scheduled Area No. 5** shows the protection areas; all **GI there counts as building works** (paras. 3–4).
    - Tunnels normally have **≥ 30 m rock cover**, but foundations must still meet DSD's technical notes (Appendix); plans within **100 m of the tunnel centreline** are referred to DSD (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 污水隧道路線根據**《污水隧道（法定地役權）條例》**刊憲；根據**條例第 17A 條**，建築事務監督可拒絕或附加條件於與隧道不相容的工程（第 1–2 段）。
    - **附表所列地區第 5 號**顯示保護範圍；該區**所有土地勘測均屬建築工程**（第 3–4 段）。
    - 隧道一般有 **≥ 30 米岩石覆蓋**，但基礎仍須符合渠務署技術指引（附錄）；位於**隧道中線 100 米內**的工程圖則會轉介渠務署（第 5–6 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: building works and GI **within 100 m** of a **gazetted sewage tunnel** (Sewage Tunnels (Statutory Easements) Ordinance; **Scheduled Area No. 5**) and within the **tunnel protection zones (TPZs)** of other **DSD drainage and sewage tunnels** (DSD Practice Note 2/2017). 污水隧道及渠務署隧道保護區附近的工程。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Paras. 1–3 | Gazetted sewage tunnel routes (ST(SE)O s.4); **BO s.17A** lets the BA refuse or condition incompatible works; **Area No. 5** plans (16 May 2012) in the Land Registry and on BD's website. 法定依據。 |
| Para. 4 | All **GI in Area 5** is building works under BO Part II. 土地勘測屬建築工程。 |
| Para. 5 | Tunnels usually under **≥ 30 m of rock**; DSD technical notes in the Appendix. 一般 30 米岩石覆蓋。 |
| Paras. 6–7 | Plans within **100 m** of the centreline, or GI in Area 5, referred to **DSD and GEO**; compensation via Director of Lands. 轉介渠務署及土力工程處。 |
| Paras. 8–10 | Other DSD tunnels (HK West, Kai Tak Transfer, Lai Chi Kok, Tsuen Wan drainage tunnels; NWNT, Tolo Harbour Effluent, TKO sewage tunnels): follow **DSD PN 2/2017** and its TPZ plans; works in TPZs referred to DSD and GEO. 其他隧道。 |
| App. 2–5 | Tunnel extent per gazetted route and depth; **shaft** = external diameter in soil, internal **+ 5 m** in rock; risks from SF, foundations, caverns, deep boreholes, wells, grouting; **rock** = Grades I–III, **soil** = IV–VI and fills / deposits (Geoguide 3). 定義及風險。 |
| App. 6–7 | Assess **sewage leakage and gas migration** risks (esp. dewatering); works within 100 m get special scrutiny. 污水及氣體。 |
| App. 8(a) | Pressure change on tunnel in soil **≤ 20 kPa** or **5%** of overburden (depth > 20 m); in rock, hydrostatic change **≤ 50 kPa**. 壓力變化限制。 |
| App. 8(b) | Diametric distortion **≤ 0.1%**; total movement **≤ 20 mm**. 變形及位移。 |
| App. 8(c) | Peak particle velocity **≤ 25 mm/s** (blasting), **≤ 15 mm/s** (piling, prolonged vibration). 振動。 |
| App. 8(d)–(f) | No holes or excavation within **3 m** without approval; no pile, foundation, well, soil nail, drain, rock bolt within **3 m**; ground anchors **> 3 m** away. 3 米範圍。 |
| App. 9 | **Monitoring** proposals before works and data during works; within **10 m**: detailed method statements and alignment / depth checks. 監測。 |
| App. 10–11 | GI in Area 5: hole locations, depths, method statements (alignment checks if < 10 m); same pressure and vibration limits; no holes within **3 m**. 土地勘測要求。 |
| App. 12 | Dewatering and wells within 100 m: assess leakage and gas; monitoring programme. 抽水及水井。 |
