## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Sewage tunnel routes are gazetted under the **Sewage Tunnels (Statutory Easements) Ordinance**; under **BO s.17A** the BA may refuse or condition works incompatible with them (paras. 1–2).
    - **Scheduled Area No. 5** shows the protection areas; all **GI there counts as building works** (paras. 3–4).
    - Tunnels normally have **≥ 30 m rock cover**, but foundations must still meet DSD's technical notes (Appendix); plans within **100 m of the tunnel centreline** are referred to DSD (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 污水隧道路線根據**《污水隧道（法定地役權）條例》**刊憲；根據**條例第 17A 條**，建築事務監督可拒絕或附加條件於與隧道不相容的工程（第 1–2 段）。
    - **附表所列地區第 5 號**顯示保護範圍；該區**所有土地勘測均屬建築工程**（第 3–4 段）。
    - 隧道一般有 **≥ 30 米岩石覆蓋**，但基礎仍須符合渠務署技術指引（附錄）；位於**隧道中線 100 米內**的工程圖則會轉介渠務署（第 5–6 段）。
