## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - MTRCL operates all lines (App. A). **Railway protection areas**, roughly **30 m** beyond railway structures (or fence / nearest rail), possibly covering whole lots and more at stations, carry special building guidelines (paras. 1–3).
    - Some are **Scheduled Area No. 3** (BO Schedule 5), adding statutory controls on works and ground investigation (para. 4).
    - Plans in protection areas go to **MTRCL** for comment; protective measures under RO s.27 / Cap. 276 s.15 (para. 6). A **railway monitoring plan** is needed for SF, foundation, ELS and other ground-moving works (para. 7). If a reading reaches the **suspension limit**, BD, EMSD and MTRCL notify each other and works stop; resumption press release **within 48 hours** of agreement (paras. 11–12). **GI in Scheduled Area No. 3 needs approval and consent** (Appendix B guidelines) (para. 14).

=== "中文"

    - 港鐵公司營運所有鐵路（附錄 A）；**鐵路保護範圍**大致為鐵路構築物（或圍欄／最近路軌）以外約 **30 米**，可涵蓋整個地段，車站範圍更大，並有特別的建築指引（第 1–3 段）。
    - 部分範圍屬條例附表 5 的**附表所列地區第 3 號**，工程及土地勘測受額外法定管制（第 4 段）。
    - 保護範圍內的圖則會轉交**港鐵公司**提供意見，並按《鐵路條例》第 27 條或第 276 章第 15 條要求保護措施（第 6 段）；地盤平整、地基、挖掘及其他會引致地面移動的工程須附**鐵路監測計劃**（第 7 段）；監測讀數達**暫停施工限值**時三方互相通知並停工，同意復工後 **48 小時**內發新聞稿（第 11–12 段）；**附表地區第 3 號內的土地勘測須獲批准及同意**（第 14 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | MTRCL operates all railway lines (merger 2 Dec 2007); lines in Appendix A. 港鐵營運所有鐵路。 |
| 2 | Railway protection areas and building/engineering guidelines; plans viewable at MTRCL by appointment. 鐵路保護範圍。 |
| 3 | Boundary about **30 m** outside the railway structure, fence or wall (or nearest rail), possibly the **whole lot**; larger at stations. 約 30 米。 |
| 4 | Some protection areas are **Scheduled Area No. 3** (BO Schedule 5); plans at the Land Registry and BD. 附表地區第 3 號。 |
| 6 | Plans circulated to **MTRCL**; measures required under **RO s.27** or **MTR(LR&RP)O s.15**. 轉交港鐵。 |
| 7 | AP/RSE/RGE monitor and assess movement and vibration of railway structures; a **monitoring plan** with SF, foundation, ELS and similar plans. 監測計劃。 |
| 8 | Keep MTRCL informed of monitoring records (piezometric, ground movement). 通知港鐵。 |
| 9 | MTRCL staff may be authorised to **enter sites** to monitor (Cap. 276 s.12 or RO s.24). 進入地盤。 |
| 10–12 | BD / EMSD / MTRCL **notification mechanism**: reaching the **suspension (Action) limit** stops affected works; joint press releases on suspension and **within 48 hours** of agreed resumption. 通知及公布機制。 |
| 13–14 | GI near tunnels is dangerous; **GI in Scheduled Area No. 3 needs approval and consent** (B(A)R 8(1)(l), Appendix B guidelines, concurrent processing per ADM-16). 土地勘測須批准。 |
| 15 | GI or underground drainage in other protection areas: send to **MTRCL** for comment before starting. 其他範圍須先諮詢港鐵。 |
| 16 | Class I and II minor works with footings, excavation or underground drainage in Area No. 3: BD forwards to MTRCL; get MTRCL's agreement before starting. 小型工程。 |

**Appendix A: lines in operation (12/2022)** — Airport Express, Disneyland Resort, East Rail, High Speed Rail, Island, Kwun Tong, Light Rail, South Island, Tseung Kwan O, Tsuen Wan, Tuen Ma, Tung Chung.

### Appendix B: guidelines for works in railway protection areas (Rev. 12/2022) 鐵路保護範圍工程指引

Every proposal, including utilities, gets special scrutiny by BD and other departments in consultation with MTRCL. Each is assessed individually.

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| (a) **Ground investigation** | Show the locations of holes, pits, trenches, tests and instruments relative to railway structures; depths; a method statement for drilling, digging and backfilling; a **verticality check** for holes within **10 m on plan** of underground railway structures if drilled to **3 m** above their highest point; **depth control** for holes within **3 m on plan**. 鑽孔垂直度及深度控制。 |
| (b)(i) **3 m exclusion** | Without MTRCL's special agreement: **no pile, foundation, borehole, well, soil nail, horizontal drain, rock bolt or other geotechnical installation within 3 m** of any underground railway structure. 3 米內不得打樁或安裝岩土構件。 |
| (b)(ii) Piling near fences | No piling within **3 m on plan** of the railway fence or wall, or **7 m** from the centreline of the nearest track where there is no fence. |
| (b)(iii) Anchors | Every part **> 3 m** from railway structures. The centroid of the fixed length is **> 2 × fixed length** away. |
| (b)(iv) **Pressure change** | Vertical or horizontal pressure change on underground railway structures from excavation, filling, ground improvement, dewatering, field tests (plate load, pressuremeter, packer) or foundation loads, including during construction, **≤ 20 kPa** (footnote: may be reviewed case by case under an engineering approach). 壓力變化不超過 20 千帕。 |
| (c) Demolition | Effective protection when demolishing or removing scaffolding, signs, container offices or buildings above or next to entrances, vent shafts, substations, plant rooms, viaducts or at-grade tracks. |
| (d) **Openings near railway vents** | Windows (openable or fixed), doors, and building ventilation intakes and exhausts **≥ 5 m** from railway ventilation openings. This may drop to **2.5 m** if the railway exhaust is directed away and unlikely to reach the opening by natural convection. 開口距鐵路通風口最少 5 米。 |
| (e) Scaffolding, signs, projections | Not above track level within **6 m on plan** of tracks without MTRCL's agreement; protection needed; may have to be done outside operating hours. |
| (f) **Vertical greening** | **≥ 5 m** from railway ventilation openings. |
| (g) Falling objects | Protect open-section tracks with hoardings, covered walkways, gantries and catch platforms ([APP-102](app-102.md)). |
| (h) Flood protection | Buildings adjoining or integrated with the railway: drainage must stop flooding (blocked drains, burst pipes) from entering railway structures. Sites next to the railway: effective site drainage. Measures agreed with MTRCL. |
| (i) Marine works | Reclamation, dredging and submarine pipes near immersed tunnels and marine structures: submit for approval in consultation with MTRCL. |
| (j) Wells | Sinking wells in protection areas is building works (BO s. 14(1)). Approval and consent are needed. |

**C. Monitoring 監測** — choose an approach considering the railway structures, the works, sequence, site and geology. Predict AAA levels carefully to avoid later changes.

**(a) Empirical approach 經驗法** (without an engineering analysis agreed by MTRCL; footnote 2: stricter values may be needed, e.g. structures with a settlement history):

| Criterion 準則 | Alert | Alarm | Action (limit) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Angular distortion in any plane (incl. plinth / track) | 1:2000 | 1:1350 | **1:1000** |
| Total movement in any plane | 10 mm | 15 mm | **20 mm** |
| Level difference between the rails of a track (perpendicular plane) | 2.5 mm | 3.8 mm | **5 mm** |
| ppv on railway structures from **blasting** (where permitted) | 15 mm/s | 20 mm/s | **25 mm/s** |
| ppv on railway structures from **piling** or other prolonged vibration | 9 mm/s | 12 mm/s | **15 mm/s** |
| ppv on overhead line masts or signalling / telecom furniture | 6 mm/s | 8 mm/s | **10 mm/s** |
| Vibration amplitude on the same | 48 µm | 64 µm | **80 µm** |

Alert / Alarm / Action are about **50% / 75% / 100%** of the limit for movement and **60% / 80% / 100%** for vibration. Vibration is measured with a vibrograph. [APP-137](app-137.md) controls also apply.

**(b) Engineering approach 工程分析法**: site-specific limits per Appendix C. The submission includes preventive and mitigation measures and a full assessment of integrity, stability, serviceability, functionality and operational safety. Use an [ADM-19](adm-19.md) pre-submission enquiry; BD may hold conferences with departments and MTRCL.

**D. Other requirements 其他要求**

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| (a) Road lamp maintenance | Equipment no closer to tracks than the lamp standard where it is higher than the tracks. Above **1 m** over track level and within **6 m**: measures agreed with MTRCL, possibly outside operating hours. |
| (b) Fire / police vehicles | Follow emergency procedures agreed with MTRCL. |
| (c) Tree planting | Send proposals to MTRCL with mature height and spread. Locations agreed so falling trunks or branches won't affect operations (power, signalling). Not within **2 m** of the nearest rail on unfenced sections. |
| (d) Cranes and plant | Tower cranes and hoists next to tracks or above-ground structures, and mobile plant (excavators, mobile cranes, piling rigs) within **6 m on plan** with any part above track level: protection agreed with MTRCL, who may set conditions. The **jib swing** must not come within **6 m** of tracks or above-ground structures on plan without agreement. |
| (e) Storage | No materials, dangerous goods, flammables, containers or huts within **6 m on plan** of railway structures, tracks or vent openings without agreement. |
| (f) Utilities | Trenches or pits in protection areas need departmental approval with MTRCL consulted. Cables, ducts and pipes crossing above or below railway structures, or across at-grade tracks: details and method agreed with MTRCL first. |

### Appendix C: engineering approach (12/2022) 附錄 C：工程分析法

| Para. 段 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Keep effects on the railway within limits set by engineering assessment. (Footnote: the same principles may be applied to other adverse effects.) |
| 2 | Information: (a) ground, groundwater and soil / rock parameters; (b) as-built information, design assumptions and calculations of the railway structures; (c) historical movement records from MTRCL; (d) preventive and mitigation measures with effectiveness, verification and limits; (e) a structural appraisal of the existing railway structures. |
| 3 | Combine measures for the best control. Examples: (a) re-plan (other foundation scheme, setbacks); (b) pre- or post-grouting; (c) better methods (pre-boring, permanent casings for replacement piles, fewer rigs working close together, control of air flushing pressure and drilling rate); (d) stronger ELS (pre-loading, extra strut layers, zone-by-zone excavation and de-propping); (e) observation and recharge wells; (f) better water cut-off (deeper piled wall, toe grouting). |
| 4 | Detailed **ground movement analysis** for each type and stage of works, with justified parameters. If models can't predict it, use experience or trial installation, for MTRCL and BD acceptance. |
| 5–6 | Tolerances differ between structures, so assess case by case: integrity, stability and functionality at each critical stage, using ultimate and serviceability limits (or App. B item C(a)(v)). |
| 7 | Consider combined effects of concurrent works and cumulative effects across stages. Split works into **critical stages**, each **10–50%** of the total estimated movement. **Alert** = 50% of this stage + all previous stages. **Alarm** = 100% of this stage + previous stages; for the last stage, the mean of Alert and Action. **Action** = 100% of the total estimated movement. |
| 8 | Show the AAA breakdown per stage on the monitoring plan (see the example below). |
| 9 | Initial readings by an **independent qualified survey team**, with a photo report of installed checkpoints, to BD and MTRCL **before works start**. Later readings are verified by the team and submitted as the approval letter requires. |
| 10 | Review the assessment regularly against actual movements. The BA may give consent **stage by stage**, so review promptly. |
| 11 | Engineering appraisal of impact on railway equipment (track, power, signalling) in the affected zone, submitted to **MTRCL** for agreement. |

**Example AAA table (para. 8)** 觸發值例子 — a to f are the estimated movements of each stage:

| Stage 階段 | Works 工程 | Estimated 估算 | Alert | Alarm | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grouting + sheet piling | a + b | 0.5(a+b) | a+b | a+b+c+d+e+f (all stages) |
| 2 | Foundation zone 1 | c | a+b+0.5c | a+b+c | ″ |
| 3 | Foundation zone 2 | d | a+b+c+0.5d | a+b+c+d | ″ |
| 4 | ELS stages 1–3 | e | a+b+c+d+0.5e | a+b+c+d+e | ″ |
| 5 | ELS stages 4–6 (last) | f | a+b+c+d+e+0.5f | a+b+c+d+e+0.75f | ″ |
