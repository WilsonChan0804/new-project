## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Under BO s.17(1)6, BA requires **verification tests** on rebars delivered to site or a prefabrication factory, in addition to mill tests; specimens are taken **at random from every batch** (para. 1).
    - BD accepts **CS2:2012**: grade **250** (≤ 12 mm), **500B** and **500C**, covering mass, chemistry, mechanical and bond properties, plus QA stockist and coil processor certification (para. 2).
    - Tests are CS2 **purchaser's tests** by a **HOKLAS** laboratory; reports state the QA class, manufacturer and origin, deformation and mill marks, and batch and delivery date (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 根據條例第 17(1)6 條，建築事務監督要求對運抵地盤或預製廠的鋼筋進行**核證測試**（另加於軋鋼廠測試），樣本須**從每批隨機抽取**（第 1 段）。
    - 屋宇署接受 **CS2:2012**：**250** 級（≤ 12 毫米）、**500B** 及 **500C** 級，涵蓋每米質量、化學成分、機械及黏結性能，以及品質保證供應商及盤條加工商認證（第 2 段）。
    - 測試為 CS2 的**買方測試**，由 **HOKLAS** 認可實驗所進行；報告須列明品質保證類別、製造商及來源地、肋紋及軋製標記、批次及送貨日期（第 3–4 段）。

## Paragraph by paragraph (full) 逐段詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision May 2023 (paras 1, 4, 6, 7, 9 amended; para. 11 added). 已按 2023 年 5 月版本核對。

| Para. 段 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Under BO s. 17(1) item 6, the BA may require rebar tests on approval or consent. They **verify** the properties of bars delivered to site or a prefabrication factory, in addition to mill tests. Specimens are taken **at random from every batch**. BD accepts **CS2:2012** (on CEDD's website). |
| 2 | CS2 covers straight bars, coils and decoiled products (not cold-worked bars): grade **250** (≤ 12 mm), **500B**, **500C**; mass per metre, chemistry, mechanical and bond properties; certification of **QA Stockists** and **QA Coil Processors**; purchaser's testing. |
| 3 | Verification tests are CS2 **purchaser's tests** by a **HOKLAS** laboratory. |
| 4 | Report contents: (a) QA stockist classification and certification for **Class 1 and Class 2** rebars; (b) manufacturer, country of origin, grade and size; (c) sketch of deformation pattern and mill marks; (d) batch and delivery date; (e) measured mass per metre; (f) chemical (product analysis), tensile, bend and, if required, bond test results. |
| 5 | **Coils and decoiled products**: CS2 Amendment No. 2/2018 and Technical Note No. 5 (26 Sep 2018). All coil processors must be QA Coil Processors. Approved Yards under **TC(W) No. 10/2018** are recognised as QA Coil Processors. Transitional delivery documentation rules apply. |
| 6 | **TC(W) 10/2018**: CEDD's **List of Approved Steel Reinforcing Bar Prefabrication Yards**. An **Independent Audit Team (IAT)** engaged by CEDD audits each yard's QMS and supervises centralised batch sampling and testing for public works and housing. |
| 7 | Prefabricated products from Approved Yards within their approved scope (cutting and bending, cages, threading and couplers) are treated as complying with the [Concrete Code 2013 (2020 Ed.)](../codes/concrete-2013.md), provided qualified supervision is given under the BO. |
| 8 | RC structural approvals carry an s. 17(1) item 6 condition for **qualified supervision** by the RSE and RGBC/RSC, including rebar sampling and testing. |
| 9 | **Streamlined option**: batch sampling under the yard's QMS, **witnessed by the IAT**, counts as qualified supervision of sampling (CS2 s. 5). The RSE and RGBC/RSC check the yard's sampling records, IAT witness records and HOKLAS test reports for their project. Copies are kept on site on delivery. A list of products used plus an **RSE compliance statement** goes to BD **within 21 days** of delivery. 簡化安排：21 天內提交清單及聲明。 |
| 10 | **Alternative**: the RSE's QCS and the RGBC/RSC's QCC supervise sampling at the yard themselves, deliver samples to their own HOKLAS laboratory, and test to CS2. |
| 11 | A similar practice note is issued to registered contractors. |

See also [APP-143](app-143.md) (precast), [APP-158](app-158.md) (site supervision of RC).
