## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Using **adjoining walls as permanent shuttering** has caused collapses; not acceptable without a **physical check** of thickness, condition and capacity for wet-concrete and tamping loads (paras. 1–2).
    - If doubtful, use **independent shuttering** or support the wall and minimise the load (para. 3).
    - A wall failure will be taken as **negligence**, an offence under **BO s.40** (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 以**毗鄰建築物牆壁作永久模板**曾導致倒塌；未經**實地檢查**牆身厚度、狀況及承受濕混凝土與搗實荷載的能力，不可接受（第 1–2 段）。
    - 如有疑問，須使用**獨立模板**，或支撐牆身並減低荷載（第 3 段）。
    - 牆身倒塌會被視為**疏忽**，屬**條例第 40 條**罪行（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: using the **wall of an adjoining building as permanent shuttering** when pouring concrete. 以鄰近建築物牆身作永久模板。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | The practice has caused **wall failures**, damage and injury. 曾導致牆身倒塌。 |
| 2 | Not acceptable unless a **physical check** confirms the wall's thickness, condition and capacity for wet concrete and compaction loads. 須實地檢查。 |
| 3 | If doubtful: **independent shuttering**, or support the wall and minimise load. 否則須獨立模板。 |
| 4 | A wall failure will normally be taken as **negligence**, an offence under **BO s.40**. 倒塌可被檢控。 |
