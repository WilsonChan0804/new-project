## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The drainage regulations' fitment standards date from the **late 1950s**; a BD consultancy proposed new standards (paras. 1–2).
    - Since **2005** APs have been strongly urged to adopt the recommendations **voluntarily** to fix the **shortage of female fitments** in offices, malls, department stores, PPEs and cinemas (para. 2).
    - The finalised recommendations will be pursued through **legislative amendment**; meanwhile the revised provision tables in this PNAP are recommended (para. 3 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 排水規例的衞生設備標準訂於 **1950 年代後期**；屋宇署委託顧問研究並建議新標準（第 1–2 段）。
    - 自 **2005 年**起強烈建議認可人士**自願**採用建議，改善辦公室、商場、百貨公司、公眾娛樂場所及戲院**女性衞生設備不足**的問題（第 2 段）。
    - 最終建議將透過**修訂法例**推行；在此之前建議採用本備考的修訂數量表（第 3 段起）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **new** offices, shopping arcades, department stores, places of public entertainment, cinemas, stadiums, religious institutions, funeral parlours; voluntary, via **modification** of B(SFP&W)R where needed. 新建公眾地方（自願採用）。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Regulations date from the 1950s; consultancy recommendations to be adopted voluntarily pending amendment | 背景 |
| 4 | **Offices**: male : female **1 : 1** (modification of s.5(5)(c)) | 辦公室比例 |
| 5 | Shops, department stores, entertainment, cinemas, religious, funeral: **1 : 1.5**; stadiums 1 : 1 | 公眾地方比例 |
| 6–8 | Population: App. A Tables 1–3; fixed seats = seats + staff; offices per s.5(5)(a) | 人口計算 |
| 9–10 | Fitments per App. A **Tables 4–18**; modification if fewer than the Regulations | 設備數目 |
| 11 | **Children's** fitments; child WCs **not counted** | 兒童設備 |
| 12 | **Unisex toilets**: state if additional or counted, with reasons | 共用洗手間 |
| 13 | Persons with a disability: BFA Design Manual | 無障礙 |
| 14–17 | Minimum spaces below; clear spaces must not overlap | 最小空間 |

### Minimum spaces 最小空間 {#minimum-spaces}

| Fitment 設備 | Requirement 要求 |
|---|---|
| WC cubicle | Internal ≥ **700 × 1,200 mm**; clear space 700 × 600 in front of WC; 700 × 600 circulation outside |
| Urinal | Clear 500 × 500; centre ≥ **250 mm** from side wall; ≥ **500 mm** c/c; cubicle ≥ 1,000 × 500; circulation 500 × 600; trough: every 500 mm = 1 urinal |
| Lavatory basin | Clear 600 × 600; centre ≥ **400 mm** from wall; ≥ **600 mm** c/c; circulation 600 × 600 |
