## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Meeting paras. 4–9 is **deemed to satisfy B(C)R 3** for aluminium windows; deviations need justification (para. 1). Large windows per **APP-37** need structural submission; window repair or replacement may be **minor works** (APP-147) (paras. 2–3).
    - Consider imposed loads, barriers, locking devices, corrosion, material QC and **fire / smoke spread between floors** (para. 4).
    - Key dimensions: see table. Hinges and fasteners must resist design wind pressures; **4-bar hinges** per App. A (stainless steel, bars ≥ 2.5 mm); locking devices per APP-37 (paras. 5–9).
    - Use window stays; no corrosive cleaning agents unless washed off immediately; top frame with a **projecting fin and drip** (App. B); **field water penetration tests** advisable (Glass Code 2018 Annex A) (paras. 10–14).

=== "中文"

    - 符合第 4–9 段即**視為符合 B(C)R 第 3 條**；偏離須提出理據（第 1 段）；屬 **APP-37** 範圍的大型窗須呈交結構設計；窗的維修或更換可屬**小型工程**（APP-147）（第 2–3 段）。
    - 須考慮外加荷載、欄障、鎖扣裝置、防銹、物料品質控制及**樓層間火煙蔓延**（第 4 段）。
    - 主要尺寸見下表；鉸及扣件須抵受設計風壓；**四連桿鉸**按附錄 A（不銹鋼，桿厚 ≥ 2.5 毫米）；鎖扣裝置按 APP-37（第 5–9 段）。
    - 使用窗撐；除非即時沖洗，否則不得使用腐蝕性清潔劑；窗框頂部設**凸出鰭片及滴水**（附錄 B）；建議進行**現場滲水測試**（2018 年玻璃守則附件 A）（第 10–14 段）。

## Key table: minimum design requirements 最低設計要求 {#key-table-minimum-design-requirements}

| Item 項目 | Requirement 要求 |
|---|---|
| Fixing lugs 固定耳 | Stainless or HDG steel, **≥ 1.5 mm** thick, at **≤ 300 mm** centres 不銹鋼或熱浸鍍鋅鋼 |
| Structural members 結構構件 | Aluminium **≥ 2 mm** thick 鋁厚 ≥ 2 毫米 |
| Mullion depth 直框深度 | **≥ 38 mm** |
| Side-hung sash 側掛窗扇 | Width **≤ 700 mm** 闊度 |
| Top-hung sash 頂掛窗扇 | Size **≤ 2.5 m²** 面積 |
| 4-bar hinge 四連桿鉸 | Stainless steel, bars **≥ 2.5 mm**, adjustable friction shoe 不銹鋼 |

## Paragraph by paragraph and appendices (full) 逐段及附錄詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision February 2021 (general revision). 已按 2021 年 2 月版本核對。

| Para. 段 | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| 1 | B(C)R s. 3 (suitable materials, properly prepared and fixed) applies. Compliance with paras 4–9 is **accepted as meeting s. 3**; deviations need separate substantiation. 符合第 4–9 段即視為符合 B(C)R 第 3 條。 |
| 2 | Large windows within the [APP-37](app-37.md) criteria need a structural submission. Other windows: AP/RSE ensure the design meets B(C)R; RCs have skilled staff and QA. Standards: APP-37 and [APP-53](app-53.md) Appendix A. |
| 3 | Window repair and replacement may be **minor works** ([APP-147](app-147.md)). |
| 4 | Consider horizontal imposed loads, protection of openings, protective barrier function, **locking devices**, corrosion, material QC, and fire and smoke spread between floors. |
| 5 | Members and glass sized for location, height and orientation. Frames rigidly fixed. **Fixing lugs**: stainless or hot-dip galvanised steel **≥ 1.5 mm** thick at **≤ 300 mm** centres; supervise their fixing. Wider spacing must be justified for stability and waterproofing. |
| 6 | Structural members **≥ 2 mm** aluminium; mullion depth **≥ 38 mm**. Hinges and fasteners resist positive and negative design wind pressure when closed. |
| 7 | Side-hung sash width **≤ 700 mm**; top-hung sash **≤ 2.5 m²**. Hinges carry the sash weight. |
| 8 | 4-bar hinges: Appendix A. |
| 9 | Locking devices: [APP-37](app-37.md) (FOS 1.8, ≤ 8 points per handle). |
| 10 | Window stays reduce wind effects on hinges and casements. |
| 11 | No corrosive cleaning agents on walls and windows after completion unless washed off immediately. |
| 12 | Seepage: poor workmanship is a main cause; see BD's water seepage guidelines (now in [APP-105](app-105.md) Appendix B). |
| 13 | Recommended: a **projecting fin with drip nose** on the top frame member (Appendix B). |
| 14 | Advisable: field **water penetration tests** on installed windows (Glass Code 2018 Annex A lists methods). |

### Appendix A: fixing 4-bar hinges (Rev. 2/2021) 四連桿鉸固定指引

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Stainless steel** hinges with adjustable friction shoe; all bars **≥ 2.5 mm**. Fix with stainless rivets or screws, **not aluminium rivets**. At least **3** stainless rivets **≥ 4.8 mm** dia. or screws **≥ 5 mm** dia. for each top and bottom bar, to both frame and sash. 每支桿最少 3 粒不銹鋼鉚釘或螺絲。 |
| 2 | Prevent contact between dissimilar metals. |
| 3 | Positive anchorage for screws: insert a stainless or HDG steel plate, bar or angle **≥ 3 mm** thick inside the section (Annex I), **or** thicken the section locally to **≥ 5 mm** (Annex II). Otherwise justify the aluminium thickness by calculation. |
| 4 | Hinge length **≥ 60%** of the side-hung sash width. 鉸長度不少於窗扇闊度 60%。 |

Annex I shows a side-hung window (sash ≤ 700 mm) with M5 × 10 stainless screws into stainless plates or bars and an aluminium angle, with holes sealed. Annex II shows local thickening to 5 mm with M5 × 16 mm pan head stainless machine screws (0.8 mm pitch) and a brass plate. Appendix B shows the top frame fin with drip nose. The drawings are in the PDF.
