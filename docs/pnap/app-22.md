## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Dewatering must not impair stability or cause undue settlement of adjoining buildings, streets and land (para. 1).
    - Plans show (para. 2): adjoining **foundations and utilities**; dewatering proposals with any **grouting / recharging**; construction method and sequence; **monitoring instruments** with **alert limits**, actions, reading intervals and record access.
    - Submit a **site investigation report** (groundwater, permeability, compressibility) and a **settlement assessment** of the effects (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 降低地下水位不得影響毗鄰建築物、街道及土地的穩定或造成過度沉降（第 1 段）。
    - 圖則須顯示（第 2 段）：毗鄰**基礎及地下設施**；降水建議（包括**灌漿／回灌**）；施工方法及次序；**監測儀器**、**警戒限值**、應變行動、讀數間隔及紀錄。
    - 須提交**土地勘測報告**（地下水、滲透性、壓縮性）及影響的**沉降評估**（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **dewatering** caused by, or used in, **foundation and basement excavation** works, where it may affect neighbouring buildings, streets and land. 地基及地庫挖掘的抽水。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Dewatering procedures and precautions against instability and **settlement** of neighbours to the BA's satisfaction. 須防止沉降。 |
| 2 | On plans: (a) **foundations of adjoining buildings** and nearby utilities (water, gas, sewers); (b) dewatering proposals incl. **grouting / recharging**; (c) method and sequence; (d) **monitoring instruments** (incl. groundwater) with (i) **limiting criteria** and actions, (ii) reading intervals, (iii) record availability. 圖則資料。 |
| 3 | With plans: (a) **SI report** (groundwater, permeability, compressibility, consolidation, grading); (b) **condition report** of adjoining buildings; (c) **shoring / underpinning** details; (d) **assessment** of excavation and dewatering effects. 附交報告。 |
| 4 | AP / RSE supervise to ensure compliance and **verify design assumptions**; full-time supervisor where needed. 監督及核實假設。 |
| 5 | **Piezometric and settlement** records always available, copies to the BA periodically. 監測記錄。 |
| 6 | Also see CED General Specification (groundwater and drawdown) and **BS 8004:1986**. 其他參考。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A 3-level basement next to old buildings on spread footings: plans show the neighbours' footings, a **cut-off wall plus recharge wells**, excavation sequence, and **piezometers and settlement markers** with **alert / alarm / action** levels (e.g. 10 / 15 / 20 mm settlement), readings **daily** during dewatering; reports include an SI report, a condition survey and a settlement assessment. 地庫抽水例子。
