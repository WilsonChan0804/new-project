## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - An industry-agreed, self-disciplined **floor numbering system** (para. 1).
    - Apart from G/F and roof, number floors in a **logical consecutive series**: G/F, 1/F, 2/F… upward; Basement 1, 2, 3… downward (para. 2).
    - Keep the sequence **regardless of function**: shops, car parks, clubhouses, podium or sky gardens, **refuge floors**, plant floors all get numbers (para. 3). Example in App. A (para. 4). Only **4, 13 and numbers ending in 4** may be omitted; no other omissions, alias names or "also known as" numbers (paras. 5–6). Show floor numbers clearly on plans (para. 7); display them in **Chinese and English** at entrances, lift lobbies and staircase landings (para. 8); the OP follows the approved plans (para. 10).

=== "中文"

    - 業界協定、自律採用的**樓層編號制度**（第 1 段）。
    - 除地下及天台外，各層須按**合乎邏輯的連續序數**編號：向上為地下、1 樓、2 樓……；向下為地庫 1、2、3……（第 2 段）。
    - **不論用途**均須保持連續：商舖、停車場、會所、平台或空中花園、**庇護層**、機房層均須編號（第 3 段）；例子見附錄 A（第 4 段）。只可省略 **4、13 及尾數為 4** 的樓層編號，不得作其他省略、別名或「又名」樓層（第 5–6 段）；圖則須清楚顯示樓層編號（第 7 段）；入口大堂、升降機大堂及樓梯平台須以**中英文**展示（第 8 段）；佔用許可證按核准圖則的樓層編號（第 10 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Code of good practice for **floor numbering**, agreed with stakeholders, adopted on a self-discipline basis. 樓層編號守則。 |
| 2 | Except G/F and roof, all floors numbered **consecutively**: ascending above ground, B1, B2 … below. 連續編號。 |
| 3 | Keep the sequence **regardless of function**: shops, car parks, clubhouses, gardens, **refuge floors**, plant floors, observation decks. 不論用途。 |
| 4 | Example in **Appendix A**. 例子。 |
| 5 | Omitting numbers can delay **emergency services** and confuse users. 省略的影響。 |
| 6 | Only **4, 13 and numbers ending in 4** may be omitted; no other omissions, non-numerical names (unless with the number, e.g. "20/F Sky Garden"), aliases or alternative numbers. 可省略的編號。 |
| 7 | Show floor numbers clearly on plans; BD may ask for amendments if illogical. 圖則顯示。 |
| 8 | Display floor numbers in **Chinese and English** at main entrance, lift lobbies, protected lobbies and staircase landings (also an MOA / FS Code requirement). 中英文標示。 |
| 9 | Logical numbering helps owners use approved plans for repairs and A&A. 方便日後。 |
| 10 | The **OP** and the Rating and Valuation Department use the approved floor numbers. 佔用許可證。 |
| 11 | Not for projects with sales brochures deposited, flats sold, or other specified steps **before 1 Sep 2010**. 不適用項目。 |
