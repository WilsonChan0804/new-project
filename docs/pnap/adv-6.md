## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Consider **lightning protection systems** in new buildings, based on a **risk assessment** to **BS EN 62305**, AS/NZS 1768 or IEC 62305, which also cover design and maintenance (para. 1).
    - Generally needed for buildings that are (para. 2): **very tall or isolated**; for **exhibition or assembly**; for **care or education** (schools, hospitals, child care, RCHEs); for **public utilities or services**; and in **open areas** (outdoor pools, sports fields).

=== "中文"

    - 新建築物應考慮設置**閃電防護系統**，並按 **BS EN 62305**、AS/NZS 1768 或 IEC 62305 進行**風險評估**；該等標準亦涵蓋設計及保養（第 1 段）。
    - 一般須設置於（第 2 段）：**非常高或孤立**的建築物；**展覽或集會**用途；**照顧或教育**用途（學校、醫院、幼兒中心、安老院舍）；**公用設施或公共服務**；以及**空曠地方**（室外泳池、運動場）。
