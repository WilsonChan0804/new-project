## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Excavation on HyD-maintained roads for building works (sheet piling, trial pits, GI, hoardings) needs an **Excavation Permit** under **Cap. 28 s.10**; working without one is an offence (para. 1).
    - To protect underground utilities and co-ordinate disconnections, HyD **rejects** applications without evidence that **advance notices went to all utility undertakers** and their requirements were addressed (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 在路政署維修的道路上為建築工程挖掘（板樁、試坑、土地勘測、圍板）須根據**第 28 章第 10 條**取得**挖掘准許證**；未領准許證屬違法（第 1 段）。
    - 為保護地下公用設施及協調截斷供應，如申請未附證據顯示已**預先通知所有公用設施公司**並回應其要求，路政署會**拒絕**申請（第 2 段）。
