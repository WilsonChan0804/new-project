## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Lift landing doors need **1 hour integrity** and should normally stay **closed** to stop fire spreading via the shaft (para. 1).
    - During maintenance or replacement (Appendix): keep landing doors **closed**; normally **no more than one** open at a time in a shaft, attended by lift workers; extra measures if more must be open (paras. 2–3).

=== "中文"

    - 升降機層門須具 **1 小時完整性**，一般應保持**關閉**，以防火經井道蔓延（第 1 段）。
    - 保養或更換期間（附錄）：層門須保持**關閉**；同一井道一般**只可開啟一道**，並由升降機工人看守；如須同時開啟多道，須採取額外措施（第 2–3 段）。
