## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Lift landing doors need **1 hour integrity** and should normally stay **closed** to stop fire spreading via the shaft (para. 1).
    - During maintenance or replacement (Appendix): keep landing doors **closed**; normally **no more than one** open at a time in a shaft, attended by lift workers; extra measures if more must be open (paras. 2–3).

=== "中文"

    - 升降機層門須具 **1 小時完整性**，一般應保持**關閉**，以防火經井道蔓延（第 1 段）。
    - 保養或更換期間（附錄）：層門須保持**關閉**；同一井道一般**只可開啟一道**，並由升降機工人看守；如須同時開啟多道，須採取額外措施（第 2–3 段）。

!!! warning "Superseded for new works 新工程已不適用"
    Listed in [APP-153 Appendix A](app-153.md#appendix-a) (based on the FRC Code 1996). For FS Code 2011 buildings see **C9.1** (liftwells 120 min, landing doors integrity). The site practice below is still useful for lift maintenance and replacement. 2011 年守則建築物見 C9.1。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Paras. 1–4 | Lift **landing doors** need FRP **≥ 1 h integrity** (FRC Code 1996 para. 11.2) and stay closed unless the car is there; during maintenance or replacement protect the **liftwell's integrity** per the Appendix; similar note to contractors. 升降機門須保持關閉。 |
| App. (a)–(b) | Keep landing doors **closed**; normally **≤ 1 open** per shaft, attended by lift workers; up to **3** open only with **no hot works or welding**. 一般只開一道門。 |
| App. (c)–(d) | Avoid removing more than one door per shaft; if more, protect openings with **1-hour temporary hoarding**. 臨時防火圍板。 |
| App. (e)–(g) | Hoarding openings only for small **vents** (**≤ 5,500 mm²** each, upper part, **≤ 2 per liftway**, max. **4**) and **access doors** (same FRP, self-closing, lockable, openable from inside without key). 圍板開口。 |
| App. (h)–(i) | Hoarding not to block **escape routes**; temporary works in the shaft **non-combustible**. 不得阻擋逃生途徑。 |
| App. (j) | Before leaving works unattended (lunch, end of day), **close** doors or enclose openings with fire-resisting hoarding. 離開前須封閉。 |
