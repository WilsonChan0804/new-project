## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Premature rusting and leaks of cast iron pipes led to requirements (para. 1): pipes and fittings to **BS 416 Pt 1 / BS EN 877** (above ground) or **BS 437 / BS 4622** (underground and > 150 mm); **internal and external coatings** (BS 3416 bitumen, external anti-corrosion inhibitors); able to take extra finishes; **stainless or corrosion-resistant fixings** anchored into solid walls.
    - Mandatory under **s.28(1)** for drainage plans approved after **1 Nov 2004**; list in the materials schedule at OP (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 鑄鐵喉管過早生銹及漏水，因此訂明要求（第 1 段）：喉管及配件符合 **BS 416 第 1 部／BS EN 877**（地面以上）或 **BS 437／BS 4622**（地下及 > 150 毫米）；**內外塗層**（BS 3416 瀝青塗料、外層含防銹劑）；可加外層塗飾；以**不銹鋼或防銹固定件**錨固於實牆。
    - **2004 年 11 月 1 日**後批准的排水圖則根據**第 28(1) 條**強制執行；申請佔用許可證時須列入物料清單（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **cast iron drainage pipes** on drainage plans approved **after 1 Nov 2004** (required under **BO s.28(1)**). 鑄鐵排水管。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Older CI pipes rusted, leaked and had corroded fixings. Guidelines: (a) pipes and fittings to BS or equivalent, e.g. **BS 416-1 / BS EN 877** above ground, **BS 437 / BS 4622** underground and above-ground > 150 mm; (b) **internal and external coatings**; cold-applied bitumen to **BS 3416**; external coat with **anti-corrosion inhibitors**; (c) able to take extra finishing coats; (d) **stainless steel** or similar fixings, anchored into **solid wall**. 四項要求。 |
| 2 | Required under s.28(1) for drainage plans approved after **1 Nov 2004**; list pipes in the **Schedule of Building Materials and Products** at OP stage. 2004 年 11 月起強制。 |
