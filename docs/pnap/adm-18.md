## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BA may enter sites (**BO s.22**) to check compliance; audits support registered persons' supervisory duties and catch structural irregularities early (paras. 1–2).
    - Strategy: **surprise checks**; detailed checks of **foundation and superstructure** works; **performance-based frequency**; unpredictable timing; separating plan-checking from auditing; staff rotation; focus on **structural safety** (para. 3). Audit items in the Appendix (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 建築事務監督可根據**條例第 22 條**進入地盤檢查合規情況；審查協助註冊人士履行監督職責，並及早發現結構問題（第 1–2 段）。
    - 策略：**突擊檢查**；詳細檢查**地基及上蓋**工程；**按表現決定頻率**；時間不可預測；圖則審核與審查分開；人員輪換；集中於**結構安全**（第 3 段）；審查項目見附錄（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: any building site in progress or completed (para. 8); minor works under simplified requirements get selected **audit safety inspections** (para. 10). 所有在建或完成的地盤。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | BA may enter sites under BO **s.22**; audits help AP, RSE, RGE and RCs meet supervisory duties and catch structural irregularities early | 法律權力 |
| 3 | Strategy: (a) detailed foundation and superstructure checks; (b) **performance-based** frequency; (c) unpredictable timing; (d) checking separate from auditing; (e) staff rotation; (f) focus on **structural safety** | 策略 |
| 4 | Scope per **Appendix I** | 範圍 |
| 5 | First visit rated (site, complexity, RC organisation, supervisory staff, documents, quality); poor results bring the next audit **earlier** | 按表現定頻率 |
| 6 | Officer decides proportion inspected; flow chart Appendix II | 抽查比例 |
| 7–8 | **No pre-arrangement** of date or time; no fixed percentage | 不預先通知 |
| 9 | Auditor is **not** the plan-checking officer nor the previous inspector | 人員輪換 |
| 10 | Minor works: audit safety inspections; Appendix I items such as spread footings, RC, canopies apply | 小型工程 |
| 11 | **Two-storey warehouses** (ADM-19 fast track): bearing stratum inspected within **1 working day**; combined superstructure inspection; small warehouses ≤ **1,500 m²** GFA: **one** unscheduled audit | 兩層貨倉 |
| 12 | Investigation focuses on anomalies affecting **structural safety** or frequent ones | 調查異常 |
| 13–14 | Not meant to impede works; similar note to RCs | 對業界影響 |

### Appendix I audit items (extract) 附錄 I 審查項目（摘錄） {#app-i-audit-items}

| Works 工程 | Things audited 審查項目 | Objective 目的 |
|---|---|---|
| Driven piles | Mill certificates, pile type, dimensions, electrode grade; supervisory staff; final set and driving energy; GI and piling records | Yield stress, recognised type, founding stratum, consistency with borelogs |
| Bored piles, mini-piles, socket H-piles | Concrete / grout grade, mill certificates; depth, diameter, bell-out, rake angle; founding material and pre-drill records | Compliance with approved plans |
| Spread footing / raft | Bearing stratum | Suitability of founding material |
| Superstructure | Materials, reinforcement, concrete, canopies (see original) | Compliance with approved plans |
