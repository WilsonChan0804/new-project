## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - BA may enter sites (**BO s.22**) to check compliance; audits support registered persons' supervisory duties and catch structural irregularities early (paras. 1–2).
    - Strategy: **surprise checks**; detailed checks of **foundation and superstructure** works; **performance-based frequency**; unpredictable timing; separating plan-checking from auditing; staff rotation; focus on **structural safety** (para. 3). Audit items in the Appendix (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 建築事務監督可根據**條例第 22 條**進入地盤檢查合規情況；審查協助註冊人士履行監督職責，並及早發現結構問題（第 1–2 段）。
    - 策略：**突擊檢查**；詳細檢查**地基及上蓋**工程；**按表現決定頻率**；時間不可預測；圖則審核與審查分開；人員輪換；集中於**結構安全**（第 3 段）；審查項目見附錄（第 4 段）。
