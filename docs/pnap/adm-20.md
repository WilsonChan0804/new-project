## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The **CDB** (since 2005) lists building materials, components and systems **accepted by government departments**, mainly BD (para. 1), as a web reference for the industry (para. 2).
    - BD listings are based on accredited lab tests or **certificates of conformity** (APP-118, APP-165); entries are **historical data**, so each use must still be certified (para. 3).
    - Structural products may be listed once accepted on a completed project and certified, among other routes (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - **中央資料庫**（2005 年起）列出**政府部門已接納**的建築物料、組件及系統，主要為屋宇署（第 1 段），供業界在網上參考（第 2 段）。
    - 屋宇署的列表以認可實驗所測試或**符合證明書**為基礎（APP-118、APP-165）；資料屬**歷史數據**，每次使用仍須核證（第 3 段）。
    - 結構產品可在已完成項目中獲接納並經核證後列入，亦有其他途徑（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: AP, RSE, RGE and RCs choosing or certifying materials. The data are **historical**; quoting the CDB does **not** shift responsibility. 資料屬歷史紀錄，責任不變。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CDB since **2005**: materials, components and systems accepted by departments, mainly BD | 設立 |
| 2 | Web reference for AP, RSE, RGE, RC; information sharing | 目的 |
| 3 | BD acceptance based on accredited lab tests or **CoC**; certified per APP-118 / APP-165; **historical data** | 範圍 |
| 4 | Structural products listed if (a) accepted in a **completed project** and certified, or (b) on BD's **recognised pile types** list | 結構產品 |
| 5 | Non-structural: from the **Schedule of Building Materials and Products** ([APP-13](app-13.md)), with random audit | 非結構產品 |
| 6 | Design details, test reports and CoC **not shown** (copyright) | 不顯示報告 |
| 7 | **No direct applications** from suppliers or manufacturers | 不接受供應商申請 |
| 8 | Users take **full responsibility**; may quote CDB reference instead of identical test reports; quote the number in the Schedule's Remarks column | 使用責任 |
