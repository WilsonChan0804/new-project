## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Announces the Works Branch **Code of Practice on Inspection and Maintenance of Water Carrying Services Affecting Slopes**: inspection planning, detection and repair options, and permanent measures against leakage (paras. 1–2).
    - Under **BO s.27C** BA may order owners to investigate and repair buried water services in or near slopes; APs, RSEs and RCs should use the code (para. 3). Read with APP-76 (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 公布工務科**《檢驗及維修影響斜坡的帶水設施守則》**：涵蓋檢驗計劃、偵測及維修方案，以及減低漏水影響的永久措施（第 1–2 段）。
    - 根據**條例第 27C 條**，建築事務監督可命令業主調查及修理斜坡內或附近的地下帶水設施；認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊承建商應參考守則（第 3 段）；須與 APP-76 一併閱讀（第 4 段）。
