## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Explains how the BA uses [B(P)R 23A](../regulations/bpr-23-23a.md) to (i) treat a hotel as **non-domestic** for SC and PR, and (ii) disregard **pick-up/set-down areas** and **back-of-house (BOH) supporting facilities** from GFA.
    - **Four tests** (para. 3): a **suitable site**; **basic and ancillary facilities** provided; **central A/C and central hot water**; evidence that it will run as a **licensed hotel** under Cap. 349.
    - **BOH facilities disregarded** must be of reasonable size, **accessible to hotel staff only** and unlikely to be abused. Normally **≤ 5%** of total hotel GFA (para. 8).
    - **Administration and management offices are not** disregarded. Appendix A lists BOH and front-of-house (FOH) facilities, with a **minimum BOH + FOH provision of 3%** of hotel GFA.
    - Applies to **bona fide hotels only**, **not service apartments** (para. 11). Unauthorised change of use is subject to the sanctions in B(P)R 23A(8)–(9).

=== "中文"

    - 解釋建築事務監督如何運用 [B(P)R 第 23A 條](../regulations/bpr-23-23a.md)：(i) 就上蓋面積及地積比率將旅館視作**非住用**；(ii) 不計算**上落客區**及**後勤輔助設施**的總樓面面積。
    - **四項考慮因素**（第 3 段）：**地盤適合**；設有**基本及附屬設施**；**中央空調及中央熱水供應**；有證據顯示將按第 349 章經營**持牌旅館**。
    - 不計算的**後勤設施**須面積合理、**只限旅館員工使用**、不大可能被濫用，一般 **≤ 旅館總樓面面積 5%**（第 8 段）。
    - **行政及管理辦公室不會**獲豁免；附錄 A 列出後勤及前台設施，**後勤及前台設施最少須佔旅館總樓面面積 3%**。
    - 只適用於**真正的旅館**，**不適用於服務式公寓**（第 11 段）；未經許可更改用途須受 B(P)R 第 23A(8)–(9) 條罰則規管。

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "Para. 3 — Hotel concession tests 旅館寬免的考慮因素"
    For hotels to qualify for the treatment under regulation 23A of the B(P)R, the BA will consider the following: (a) whether the site itself or the site relative to its neighbourhood is suitable for hotel development; (b) whether basic facilities such as reception counter, storerooms for linen, administration office, staff toilets for the operation of the hotel and facilities ancillary to the functions of a hotel (e.g. dining, entertainment and shopping facilities commensurate with hotel patronage) are provided; (c) whether the whole building or the portion of the building intended for use as a hotel is provided with a central air-conditioning (A/C) system and a centralised hot water supply system; and (d) whether there is evidence indicating that the hotel building or the hotel part of the building when completed will be operated as a licensed hotel under the Hotel and Guesthouse Accommodation Ordinance (Cap. 349).

    **官方中文本：** 建築事務監督在處理按《規例》第23A條擬用作旅館的申請時，會考慮以下因素：(a) 所涉地盤本身或就其鄰近環境而言是否適合作旅館發展；(b) 是否設有如接待處、布料製品貯存室、行政辦公室、員工洗手間等營運旅館所需的基本設施，以及與旅館營運相關的附屬設施，例如配合旅館顧客需要的餐飲、娛樂及購物設施；(c) 整幢建築物或建築物中擬作旅館用途的部分是否配備中央空調系統和中央熱水供應系統；及 (d) 是否有證據顯示旅館建築物或建築物中的旅館部分於竣工後將按照《旅館業條例》（第349章）經營作持牌旅館。

!!! quote "Para. 8 — Supporting facilities ≤ 5% 輔助設施上限 5%"
    …the BA must be satisfied that the facilities comply with the following criteria: (a) their size is reasonable, their location practical and their design genuine to serve the intended purpose; (b) they are accessible to hotel staff only; and (c) abuse of use is unlikely. Unless there are strong justifications, the BA would not expect the GFA of such facilities to exceed 5% of the total GFA of the hotel. For the avoidance of doubt, offices for the administration and management of the hotel are not considered as unique supporting facilities for the purpose of regulation 23A(3)(b)(iv) of the B(P)R.

    **官方中文本：** ……建築事務監督必須信納該等設施符合以下條件，方可不計算其樓面空間的面積：(a) 其大小合理、位置切實可行，設計亦確實符合擬作用途；(b) 只有旅館員工才能到達該等設施；及 (c) 設施不大可能被濫用。除非有充分理據，建築事務監督認為此等設施的總樓面面積不應超出旅館總樓面面積的5%。為免生疑問，旅館的行政及管理辦公室不會視作《規例》第23A(3)(b)(iv)條所述的獨有輔助設施。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    A hotel can be treated as **non-domestic**, which allows the much higher non-domestic SC and PR in the First Schedule. It may also exclude some back-of-house space. Because this is valuable, the BA wants proof that the building is a **real hotel**: central A/C and hot water, proper hotel facilities, a licence, and a suitable location.

    In return, the owner **cannot later convert** the hotel (or its exempted BOH space) to other uses without BA approval. Service apartments do not qualify.

    Central A/C and hot water plant rooms are disregarded under **reg. 23(3)(b)**, not reg. 23A. They are therefore subject to the **APP-151 pre-requisites and 10% cap** (footnote 1).

=== "中文"

    旅館可被視作**非住用**，從而適用附表 1 中高得多的非住用上蓋面積及地積比率，並可豁免部分後勤面積。由於寬免價值高，建築事務監督要求證明該建築物是**真正的旅館**：中央空調及熱水、合適的旅館設施、牌照及合適位置。

    作為交換，業主日後未經建築事務監督批准，**不得將旅館（或獲豁免的後勤面積）改作其他用途**；服務式公寓不符合資格。

    中央空調及熱水機房是根據**第 23(3)(b) 條**（而非第 23A 條）豁免，因此須符合 **APP-151 先決條件及 10% 上限**（註腳 1）。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! example "Worked example: BOH allowance"
        Hotel GFA **20,000 m²**.

        - Disregarded BOH under 23A(3)(b): normally ≤ 5% × 20,000 = **1,000 m²**
        - Minimum BOH + FOH provision: ≥ 3% × 20,000 = **600 m²**
        - The admin office is **not** part of the 1,000 m². It counts as GFA.

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Treating **service apartments** as hotels: **not eligible** (para. 11).
        - Putting the **management office** into the disregarded BOH area.
        - BOH rooms that guests or the public can reach. They must be **staff-only**.
        - **Extensions or A&A** to existing hotels that add GFA are **re-assessed** under the current rules (para. 12).

=== "中文"

    !!! example "計算例子：後勤面積"
        旅館總樓面面積 **20,000 平方米**。

        - 根據第 23A(3)(b) 條不計算的後勤設施：一般 ≤ 5% × 20,000 = **1,000 平方米**
        - 後勤及前台設施最少：≥ 3% × 20,000 = **600 平方米**
        - 行政辦公室**不屬**該 1,000 平方米，須計入總樓面面積。

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 把**服務式公寓**當作旅館：**不符合資格**（第 11 段）。
        - 把**管理辦公室**計入不計算的後勤面積。
        - 後勤房間可讓客人或公眾進入：必須**只限員工**。
        - 現有旅館的**擴建或改動加建**如增加總樓面面積，須按現行規定**重新評估**（第 12 段）。
