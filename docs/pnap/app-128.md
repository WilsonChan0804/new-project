## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Geotechnical submissions are growing in complexity; keep **focus on key issues** and organise plans, reports and calculations clearly (para. 1).
    - Follow standards and good practice, e.g. **GEO TGN No. 1** (list of de facto standards) (para. 2).
    - Check **geotechnical feasibility at GBP stage**; submit a **geotechnical assessment** under B(A)R 8(1)(ba) where the APP-25 criteria apply (para. 3). Later paras. cover GI, design reports and computer analyses.

=== "中文"

    - 岩土圖則日趨複雜；須**集中於關鍵問題**，並清晰編排圖則、報告及計算（第 1 段）。
    - 須遵從標準及良好作業，例如**土力工程處技術指引第 1 號**（實際標準清單）（第 2 段）。
    - 須在**一般建築圖則階段**審視岩土可行性；符合 APP-25 準則時須根據 B(A)R 第 8(1)(ba) 條呈交**岩土評估**（第 3 段）；其後各段涵蓋土地勘測、設計報告及電腦分析。
