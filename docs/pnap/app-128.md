## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Geotechnical submissions are growing in complexity; keep **focus on key issues** and organise plans, reports and calculations clearly (para. 1).
    - Follow standards and good practice, e.g. **GEO TGN No. 1** (list of de facto standards) (para. 2).
    - Check **geotechnical feasibility at GBP stage**; submit a **geotechnical assessment** under B(A)R 8(1)(ba) where the APP-25 criteria apply (para. 3). Plan **GI** from a desk-study geological model (para. 4); design reports need an **executive summary** and a schedule of assumptions and parameters (para. 6); build in **design reviews** during construction (para. 7); follow B(A)R 8(1)(bb), (bc), (d) and 8(3)–(4) for SF, ELS, foundation and demolition plans (paras. 9–13); computer analyses must state the **program, version and BD reference**, and the RGE completes the **Appendix A proforma** (paras. 14–15).

=== "中文"

    - 岩土圖則日趨複雜；須**集中於關鍵問題**，並清晰編排圖則、報告及計算（第 1 段）。
    - 須遵從標準及良好作業，例如**土力工程處技術指引第 1 號**（實際標準清單）（第 2 段）。
    - 須在**一般建築圖則階段**審視岩土可行性；符合 APP-25 準則時須根據 B(A)R 第 8(1)(ba) 條呈交**岩土評估**（第 3 段）。按案頭研究的地質模型規劃**土地勘測**（第 4 段）；設計報告須有**行政摘要**及假設和參數表（第 6 段）；施工期間應設**設計檢討**（第 7 段）；地盤平整、挖掘、地基及拆卸圖則分別按第 8(1)(bb)、(bc)、(d) 及 8(3)–(4) 條（第 9–13 段）；電腦分析須註明**程式、版本及屋宇署編號**，註冊岩土工程師須填寫**附錄 A 表格**（第 14–15 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Geotechnical submissions are complex; follow standards and good practice (e.g. GEO technical guidance). 岩土呈交。 |
| 3 | Check **geotechnical feasibility at GBP stage**; **geotechnical assessment** under B(A)R 8(1)(ba) where APP-25 criteria apply. 一般建築圖則階段。 |
| 4 | Plan **GI** to refine a preliminary **geological model** from a desk study. 土地勘測。 |
| 5 | Design for an adequate **margin of safety** during and after works, and no damage to adjacent ground. 安全度。 |
| 6 | Reports: **executive summary**, schedule of design assumptions and parameters, with justification (GI, lab tests, groundwater data). 設計報告。 |
| 7 | Provide **design reviews during construction** for complex sites (PNAP 246). 施工期間檢討。 |
| 8 | Show proposed works clearly distinguished from existing (B(A)R 14) and their mutual effects. 新舊工程區分。 |
| 9–10 | Site formation per **B(A)R 8(1)(bb)** (filling: PNAP 55); ELS per **8(1)(bc)** (PNAP 148; dewatering PNAP 74). 地盤平整及挖掘。 |
| 11 | Extra controls in Scheduled Areas 1, 2 and 4 (APP-30, APP-61). 附表地區。 |
| 12 | Foundations near slopes: no adverse effects such as **damming of groundwater** (B(C)R 23; B(A)R 8(1)(d)). 斜坡附近地基。 |
| 13 | Demolishing a building that supports ground: protect stability, no debris stockpiles near slopes (B(A)R 8(3)–(4), PNAP 71). 拆卸。 |
| 14 | Calculations indexed; for pre-accepted programs (PNAP 79) state **name, version, developer, BD reference** and inputs. 電腦程式。 |
| 15 | RGE completes the **Appendix A proforma**. 附錄 A 表格。 |
