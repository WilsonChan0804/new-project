## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The 1995 amendment revised **reg. 41B** so **fireman's lifts** give safe, unobstructed access to the floors served (para. 2).
    - **Reg. 41C**: only fireman's lifts in a **Firefighting and Rescue Stairway (FRS)**, whose stair must serve **all floors** (para. 3).
    - Applies to works not started or consented by **22 May 1995** (BO s.39(2)); the MOA Code was revised to match (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 1995 年修訂**第 41B 條**，使**消防員升降機**能讓消防員安全及暢通無阻地到達所服務的樓層（第 2 段）。
    - **第 41C 條**：**消防及救援樓梯**內只可設消防員升降機，其樓梯須通往**所有樓層**（第 3 段）。
    - 適用於 **1995 年 5 月 22 日**或之前未展開或未獲同意的工程（條例第 39(2) 條）；救援通道守則已相應修訂（第 4–5 段）。
