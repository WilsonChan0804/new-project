## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The 1995 amendment revised **reg. 41B** so **fireman's lifts** give safe, unobstructed access to the floors served (para. 2).
    - **Reg. 41C**: only fireman's lifts in a **Firefighting and Rescue Stairway (FRS)**, whose stair must serve **all floors** (para. 3).
    - Applies to works not started or consented by **22 May 1995** (BO s.39(2)); the MOA Code was revised to match (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 1995 年修訂**第 41B 條**，使**消防員升降機**能讓消防員安全及暢通無阻地到達所服務的樓層（第 2 段）。
    - **第 41C 條**：**消防及救援樓梯**內只可設消防員升降機，其樓梯須通往**所有樓層**（第 3 段）。
    - 適用於 **1995 年 5 月 22 日**或之前未展開或未獲同意的工程（條例第 39(2) 條）；救援通道守則已相應修訂（第 4–5 段）。

!!! warning "Superseded for new works 新工程已不適用"
    Listed in [APP-153 Appendix A](app-153.md#appendix-a): it does **not apply** to buildings designed to the **FS Code 2011**. It still explains the 1995 rule changes for older buildings and for Cap. 502 / 572 upgrading. 按 2011 年消防守則設計的建築物不適用。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | The **B(P)(Amendment) Regulation 1995** amends the firefighting and rescue access rules. 1995 年修訂。 |
| 2 | **Reg. 41B**: fireman's lifts designed and located for **safe, unobstructed access** to the lifts and the floors served. 消防員升降機須安全進出。 |
| 3 | **Reg. 41C**: only **fireman's lifts** in an FRS, and the FRS access stairway serves **all floors**. 消防和救援樓梯間只容消防員升降機，樓梯服務所有樓層。 |
| 4 | Applies under **BO s.39**; not to works being carried out or consented **on or before 22 May 1995** (s.39(2)). 1995 年 5 月 22 日前已動工或獲同意者不適用。 |
| 5 | The **MOA Code** was revised accordingly (later replaced by the 2004 MOA Code and then the FS Code Part D). 守則同步修訂。 |
