## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Under **Cap. 121** s.6(a), the Director of Lands may exempt site formation for exempted NT building works (paras. 1–2).
    - **Criteria** (para. 3): gradient across the lot **≤ 15°**; overall gradient within 10 m outside the lot **< 15°**; no slope within 10 m steeper than **30°** or higher than **1.5 m**; no retaining wall **> 1.5 m** in or within 10 m of the lot.
    - **Conditions** (para. 4): no new retaining wall over 1.5 m (wall plus slope ≤ 1.5 m); height-to-breadth ratio **≤ 2**, masonry or concrete; no slope steeper than 30° or higher than 1.5 m. Otherwise, submit site formation plans to the BA (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 根據**第 121 章**第 6(a) 條，地政總署署長可就新界豁免建築工程的地盤平整發出豁免證明書（第 1–2 段）。
    - **準則**（第 3 段）：地段內坡度 **≤ 15°**；地段外 10 米範圍整體坡度 **< 15°**；10 米內沒有陡於 **30°** 或高於 **1.5 米**的斜坡；地段內或 10 米內沒有高於 **1.5 米**的擋土牆。
    - **條件**（第 4 段）：不得建造高於 1.5 米的擋土牆（牆加斜坡合計 ≤ 1.5 米）；高闊比 **≤ 2**，以磚石或混凝土建造；不得建造陡於 30° 或高於 1.5 米的斜坡；否則須向建築事務監督呈交地盤平整圖則（第 5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **site formation works** for **NT exempted houses** under the **Buildings Ordinance (Application to the New Territories) Ordinance Cap. 121** s.6(a): when the **Director of Lands (DLO)** can issue a certificate of exemption for site formation. 新界豁免屋宇的地盤平整豁免。

    **If criteria are not met 不符合準則**: submit site formation plans to **BD** in the normal way (para. 5). 須向屋宇署正式呈交。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Paras. 1–2 | Cap. 121 exempts certain NT building works from BO ss.4, 9, 14, 21, 30; s.6(a) also allows exemption for **site formation**. 豁免依據。 |
| Para. 3 / App. A criteria | (a) gradient across the lot **≤ 15°**; (b) overall gradient within **10 m** outside the lot **< 15°**; (c) no slope within 10 m outside the lot **steeper than 30° or higher than 1.5 m**; (d) no retaining or terrace wall **> 1.5 m** in the lot or within 10 m. 四項準則。 |
| Para. 4 / App. A conditions | (a) no retaining / terrace wall **> 1.5 m**; wall + slope combined **≤ 1.5 m**; (b) retaining height : breadth **≤ 2**, masonry or concrete; (c) no slope **> 30°** or **> 1.5 m**. 豁免條件。 |
| App. A (building works certificate) | All retaining walls are **site formation works**, not exempt; any structure retaining earth or fill is a retaining wall; slope / wall **stability investigation** may be required. 建築工程豁免證書條件。 |
| Para. 5 | Otherwise submit plans to **BD**. 否則正式呈交。 |
| App. B flow chart | DLO, with **GEO**, decides if investigation is needed (ground features report, joint visit); criteria met → C of E with conditions; investigation needed → AP / engineer report to DLO and GEO; works affecting slopes or needing landslip works → **submit to BD** (AP required). 處理流程。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A NTEH lot with a **12°** cross-fall, surroundings at **10°**, and an existing **1.2 m** terrace wall 6 m away: criteria (a)–(d) met, so the DLO can issue a C of E for site formation; the owner may build a new wall up to **1.5 m** high and at least **0.6 m** thick (1.2 / 0.6 = 2). A **2 m** high new platform wall would need **BD** submission. 新界小屋例子。
