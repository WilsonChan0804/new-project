## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BD's **Site Monitoring Section** inspects sites systematically, focusing on material deviation from plans, defective materials, inadequate precautions, **lack of supervision**, departures from agreed sequences, inexperienced workers, non-compliance with conditions or orders, and BO contraventions (para. 1).
    - It advises the AP / RSE / RGE on corrective action; sanctions include **orders (s.23, 24A)**, prosecution and discipline (para. 2). Inspection frequency rises for hazardous works and stages (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 屋宇署**地盤監察組**有系統地巡查地盤，重點包括與圖則有重大偏差、物料有缺陷、預防措施不足、**欠缺監督**、偏離協定工序、工人經驗不足、不遵從條件或命令及違反條例（第 1 段）。
    - 會建議認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師採取糾正措施；制裁包括**命令（第 23、24A 條）**、檢控及紀律處分（第 2 段）；危險工程及階段的巡查較頻密（第 3 段）。
