## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Applies to **residential (domestic) buildings**. It does **not** cover hotels, guesthouses or residential care homes, whose A/C profile is different (para. 2).
    - Compliance is a **pre-requisite for GFA concessions** under [APP-151](app-151.md) para. 6(b), for domestic and composite developments.
    - Four requirements (para. 6): **RTTV<sub>Wall</sub> ≤ 12.5 W/m²**, **RTTV<sub>Roof</sub> ≤ 3.5 W/m²**; **OTTV of residents' recreational facilities ≤ 20 W/m² (tower) / 40 W/m² (podium)**; submit **NVTC** (natural ventilation) compliance; glass **VLT ≥ 50%** and **external reflectance ≤ 20%**.
    - Timing: a compliance statement on the first plans → **summary sheets before consent** → **Energy Efficiency Report at OP**.
    - **Sunshades** that reduce RTTV/OTTV are **not GFA or SC**. Those over **750 mm** need a quantitative assessment, and none should exceed **1.5 m** (para. 12).

=== "中文"

    - 適用於**住宅（住用）建築物**；**不包括**冷氣使用模式不同的酒店、賓館及安老院舍等（第 2 段）。
    - 符合本備考是住用及綜合用途發展根據 [APP-151](app-151.md) 第 6(b) 段取得**總樓面面積寬免的先決條件**。
    - 四項規定（第 6 段）：**牆身住宅熱傳送值 ≤ 12.5 瓦／平方米**、**屋頂 ≤ 3.5 瓦／平方米**；**住客康樂設施整體熱傳送值 ≤ 20 瓦／平方米（大廈）／40 瓦／平方米（平台）**；提交**自然通風**符合程度；玻璃**可見光透射率 ≥ 50%**、**外反光率 ≤ 20%**。
    - 時序：首次圖則附符合聲明 → **申請動工同意書前提交摘要表** → **申請佔用許可證時提交能源效益報告**。
    - 有助降低熱傳送值的**遮陽篷不計總樓面面積及上蓋面積**；伸出超過 **750 毫米**須提交量化評估，且不應超過 **1.5 米**（第 12 段）。

!!! tip "Applies / does not apply 適用／不適用"
    **Applies**: new **residential (domestic) buildings** seeking **GFA concessions** under [APP-151](app-151.md) (it is a pre-requisite), and their **residents' recreational facilities (RRF)**. Revised OTTV<sub>RRF</sub> limits for plans submitted **on or after 31 Dec 2025** (para. 17). 適用於申請總樓面面積寬免的新住宅建築物及住客會所。
    **Does not apply**: **hotels, guesthouses, care homes** and other premises with a non-household A/C profile (para. 2); **A&A or change of use** not creating a new residential building (para. 18). Commercial buildings and hotels are under the **OTTV** control of [B(EE)R](../regulations/beer-123m.md) and [APP-67](app-67.md). 酒店、院舍不適用；商業及酒店另受 OTTV 管制。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Buildings use over **90 %** of Hong Kong's electricity, residential about **27 %**; measures from a 2010 consultancy study. 背景。 |
| 2 | "Residential building" = **domestic building** (BO s.2(1)), excluding hotels, guesthouses, care homes. 定義。 |
| 3 | Measures in the **Guidelines** (2014): (a) **RTTV** of the envelope with **VLT** and **ER** of glazing; (b) **natural ventilation for thermal comfort (NVTC)**. 主要措施。 |
| 4 | **RRF** are like commercial buildings, so their **OTTV** is controlled like hotels and offices. 會所 OTTV。 |
| 5 | A **Technical Committee** reviews RTTV and OTTV standards under the Energy Saving Plan 2015–2025+. 技術委員會。 |

| 7 | **First GBP** may omit calculations but must carry a **statement** of compliance. 首次呈交。 |
| 8 | After GBP approval and **before consent**: **Summary Sheets** (Appendices A and B). 同意前。 |

| 10 | Sample calculations (Guidelines App. IV) and BD **spreadsheets**. 計算範例。 |
| 11 | Other materials: properties from **reliable sources**, with background. 其他物料。 |
| 12 | **Genuine sunshades** not counted for GFA or SC; projection **> 750 mm** needs a quantitative assessment; not expected **> 1.5 m**. 遮陽篷。 |
| 13 | Sunshades **≤ 750 mm** do not obstruct **prescribed windows**. 不阻擋訂明窗。 |
| 14 | No sunshades over **streets** (BO s.31(1)) unless exempted. 不得伸出街道。 |
| 15 | BD publishes RTTV and OTTV<sub>RRF</sub> after the **OP**. 公開資料。 |
| 16 | NVTC data collected for research, **not published**. 自然通風資料。 |
| 17 | Revised 6(b) limits for plans submitted on or after **31 Dec 2025**. 生效日期。 |
| 18 | Not for A&A or change of use without a new residential building. 不適用情況。 |
| 19 | Limits reviewed periodically. 定期檢討。 |

## Natural ventilation and the Guidelines 自然通風及指引 {#nvtc}

The design rules behind 6(a) and 6(c) are in the BD [Guidelines on Energy Efficiency of Residential Buildings 2014](../codes/bd-guidelines.md). 第 6(a) 及 6(c) 段的設計規則載於屋宇署 2014 年指引：

| Item 項目 | Guideline rule 指引規定 |
|---|---|
| RTTV method 計算方法 | Wall: heat conduction through opaque wall and window + solar radiation through glass, by **orientation**, with **external shading** (overhangs, fins) and self-shading; **deemed-to-satisfy** route for walls |
| Glass in prescribed windows 訂明窗玻璃 | **VLT ≥ 50 %** (para. 6(d)); control of **glare** |
| **Cross ventilation** 對流通風 | Primary and secondary openings on different sides; total room depth between them **≤ 12 m** |
| **Single-sided ventilation** 單面通風 | Room depth **≤ 4.5 m** from the centre of the openable window |
| **A/C heat rejection** 冷氣機散熱 | At least **1.5 m** from openable windows of adjacent units |

## Key quotes 原文摘錄 {#key-clauses}

!!! quote "Para. 6 — Pre-requisite for GFA concessions 寬免先決條件"
    …the compliance with the following design and construction requirements is included as one of the pre-requisites for the granting of gross floor area (GFA) concessions for green/amenity features and non-mandatory/non-essential plant rooms and services in a residential building under… APP-151: (a) the RTTV of wall (RTTVWall) and roof (RTTVRoof) should not exceed 12.5 Watt/m² … and 3.5 Watt/m² … respectively; (b) the OTTVRRF should not exceed 20 Watt/m² … (in case of a building tower) or 40 Watt/m² … (in case of a podium)…; (c) the extent of compliance with NVTC requirements … should be submitted to the BD; and (d) glass forming part of the building envelope … should have a VLTGlass of not less than 50% and an ERGlass of not more than 20%.

    **中譯（非官方）：** 符合以下設計及建造規定，是住宅建築物根據 APP-151 取得綠化／適意設施及非強制性／非必要機房總樓面面積寬免的先決條件之一：(a) 牆身及屋頂的住宅熱傳送值分別不超過 12.5 及 3.5 瓦／平方米；(b) 住客康樂設施的整體熱傳送值不超過 20 瓦／平方米（大廈）或 40 瓦／平方米（平台）；(c) 須向屋宇署提交符合自然通風規定的程度；及 (d) 構成建築物外殼的玻璃（例如幕牆、覆蓋層、天窗、窗及門）的可見光透射率不少於 50%，外反光率不多於 20%。

!!! quote "Paras. 12–13 — Sunshades 遮陽篷"
    Genuine sunshades that are conducive to the reduction of RTTV and OTTV are not accountable for GFA and shall not be included in site coverage calculations. In case the sunshades project more than 750 mm from the external walls, quantitative assessment should be submitted to the BA for consideration. It is not envisaged that sunshades would project more than 1.5 m from the external walls. … sunshades with a projection of not more than 750 mm are regarded as not causing obstructions to prescribed windows.

    **中譯（非官方）：** 有助降低住宅熱傳送值及整體熱傳送值的真正遮陽篷，不計入總樓面面積及上蓋面積。如遮陽篷伸出外牆超過 750 毫米，須向建築事務監督提交量化評估以供考慮；預期遮陽篷不會伸出外牆超過 1.5 米。……伸出不超過 750 毫米的遮陽篷不會被視為阻擋訂明窗。

## Explanation 淺白解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    **RTTV** measures how much heat enters the flats through the walls (and windows) or the roof, per m² of envelope. A lower value means cooler flats and less A/C use. It depends on wall insulation, **window-to-wall ratio**, glass **shading coefficient**, orientation and external shading.

    **OTTV<sub>RRF</sub>** is the same idea for the **clubhouse**. Clubhouses are air-conditioned like commercial buildings, so they use the commercial OTTV method.

    **Why it matters:** if you fail APP-156, you fail **APP-151 pre-requisite 6(b)**, and the development can lose **all** its green/amenity GFA concessions (balconies, utility platforms, clubhouse, etc.). Check the façade design (glass type, window ratio, sunshades) **before** freezing the elevations.

=== "中文"

    **住宅熱傳送值（RTTV）**量度每平方米外殼經牆身（及窗戶）或屋頂進入單位的熱量；數值越低，單位越涼快，冷氣耗電越少。影響因素包括牆身隔熱、**窗牆比**、玻璃**遮陽系數**、方向及外部遮陽。

    **住客康樂設施整體熱傳送值（OTTV<sub>RRF</sub>）**是**會所**的同類指標；會所的冷氣使用與商業建築物相似，故採用商業的 OTTV 計算方法。

    **重要性**：若未能符合 APP-156，即未能符合 **APP-151 第 6(b) 項先決條件**，發展項目可能失去**全部**環保及適意設施寬免（露台、工作平台、會所等）。應在**定案立面前**核對外牆設計（玻璃類型、窗牆比、遮陽篷）。

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Design levers to reduce RTTV"
        - Lower **window-to-wall ratio** on east/west façades.
        - Glass with a lower **shading coefficient** (but keep **VLT ≥ 50%** for prescribed windows, and **ER ≤ 20%**).
        - **External shading**: overhangs and side fins. Sunshades ≤ 750 mm are simplest: no obstruction to prescribed windows and no quantitative assessment.
        - Light-coloured façades (lower **absorptivity**).

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - **Reflective glass** that pushes **ER above 20%**. This fails 6(d), and APP-2's curtain-wall criterion as well.
        - Forgetting the **podium clubhouse**: it has its own OTTV limit (40 W/m²).
        - Not including the **compliance statement** on the first GBP submission (para. 7).
        - Sunshades **cannot project over streets** without exemption under BO s.31(1) (para. 14).

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "降低住宅熱傳送值的設計手法"
        - 減少東／西立面的**窗牆比**。
        - 選用**遮陽系數**較低的玻璃（但訂明窗須保持**可見光透射率 ≥ 50%**，以及**外反光率 ≤ 20%**）。
        - **外部遮陽**：簷篷及側鰭；≤ 750 毫米的遮陽篷最簡單：不視為阻擋訂明窗，亦毋須量化評估。
        - 淺色外牆（較低**吸收率**）。

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 使用**反光玻璃**令**外反光率超過 20%**：不符合 6(d)，亦不符合 APP-2 的幕牆準則。
        - 忘記**平台會所**有獨立的整體熱傳送值上限（40 瓦／平方米）。
        - 首次提交一般建築圖則時沒有附上**符合聲明**（第 7 段）。
        - 遮陽篷如無《建築物條例》第 31(1) 條豁免，**不得伸出街道**（第 14 段）。
