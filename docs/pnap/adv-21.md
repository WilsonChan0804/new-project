## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Framework for marine disposal of dredged or excavated sediment under the **Dumping at Sea Ordinance (Cap. 466)**: justify dredging, obtain sediment data for the contractor's permit, classify sediment into **three categories** by contaminant level, and the disposal arrangements for each (para. 1).
    - Applies to private projects with marine disposal not yet agreed by the **Marine Fill Committee**; re-test if more than **3 years** passes between sampling and works, unless DEP waives (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 根據**《海上傾倒物料條例》（第 466 章）**處理疏浚或挖掘沉積物的架構：說明疏浚需要、取得沉積物資料以支持承建商的許可證申請、按污染程度把沉積物分為**三類**，以及各類的傾卸安排（第 1 段）。
    - 適用於尚未獲**海上填料委員會**同意的私人海上傾卸項目；如抽樣與動工相隔超過 **3 年**須重新測試，除非環保署署長豁免（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: private projects needing **marine disposal** of dredged / excavated sediment (reclamation, marinas, deep basements) whose proposals the MFC has not yet agreed. Re-test if more than **3 years** pass between sampling and works. 需要海上棄置沉積物的私人項目。

### Appendix A: sediment quality criteria 沉積物質素準則

| Contaminant 污染物 | LCEL 下限 | UCEL 上限 |
|---|---|---|
| Cadmium Cd (mg/kg dry wt.) | 1.5 | 4 |
| Chromium Cr | 80 | 160 |
| Copper Cu | 65 | 110 |
| Mercury Hg | 0.5 | 1 |
| Nickel Ni* | 40 | 40 |
| Lead Pb | 75 | 110 |
| Silver Ag | 1 | 2 |
| Zinc Zn | 200 | 270 |
| Arsenic As (metalloid) | 12 | 42 |
| Low molecular weight PAHs (µg/kg dry wt.) | 550 | 3160 |
| High molecular weight PAHs | 1700 | 9600 |
| Total PCBs | 23 | 180 |
| Tributyltin* (µg TBT/L in interstitial water) | 0.15 | 0.15 |

\* Exceeds the UCEL if greater than the value shown.

| Category 類別 | Definition 定義 | Handling 處理 |
|---|---|---|
| **L** | All levels **≤ LCEL** | Dredge, transport and dispose so as to minimise contaminant loss |
| **M** | Any level **> LCEL**, none > UCEL | Handle with care; **isolate** on disposal unless biological tests show no harm to the marine environment |
| **H** | Any level **> UCEL** | Handle with great care; **isolate** on disposal |

### Appendix B: sediment assessment guidelines 沉積物評估指引

Close supervision by the AP/RSE/RGE is needed. Sampling, testing and the Sediment Quality Report normally take about **8 months**. DEP keeps a list of accredited laboratories for biological tests.

| Tier 層級 | What happens 內容 |
|---|---|
| **I** Desk study | Proposal to DEP, copied to the MFC Secretary (CEDD). It covers: project name; site plan and boundary; volume; timetable and disposal space needed; dredging history; previous site use; other data (grain size, TOC, geotechnical data, past tests). DEP either accepts it as a formal SQR (Category L, open sea disposal) or requires Tier II. The AP may go straight to Tier II. |
| **II** Chemical screening | Test proposal to DEP (copy MFC). It covers: timetable; sample locations with HK metric grid coordinates; sample types (grab, gravity core, piston, vibrocore); tests on all Table 1 contaminants except TBT. DEP may also ask for TBT, DDT, other organochlorines or spill-related chemicals. Composite samples for biological tests are also tested for moisture, grain size (% < 63 µm), TOC, porewater ammonia and salinity. |
| | **Grid**: low contamination **200 × 200 m**, surface only; high **100 × 100 m**, vertical profile; very high (near outfalls, nullahs) **50 × 50 m**, vertical profile. |
| | **Vertical sub-samples** start at the seabed, then 0.9 m, 1.9 m, 2.9 m, then every 3 m to the bottom of the dredged layer. Sample sizes: metals 0.5 L, organics 0.5 L, biological 6 L (laboratory to confirm). Take **reference sediment** if biological tests are expected. |
| | **Storage**: metals in HDPE bottles; organics in wide-mouth borosilicate glass with Teflon-lined lids; pretreatment to USEPA SW-846 Ch. 3 / 4. Keep at **4 °C** in the dark, **not frozen**. Maximum holding time: chemical **2 weeks**, biological **8 weeks**. |
| | **Laboratories**: HOKLAS-accredited, or an equivalent national scheme overseas. |
| | **To Tier III**: Category L stops here. **Category M** goes to Tier III; **Category H** goes only if any level is **> 10 × LCEL**. |
| **III** Biological screening | Test proposal (number of tests, compositing, species, conditions). Composite of up to **5** continuous samples of the same category. Three toxicity tests form one set: **10-day burrowing amphipod**, **20-day burrowing polychaete**, **48–96 hour larvae** (bivalve or echinoderm). Category H > 10 × LCEL: **dilution test**, 1 part sample to **9 parts reference sediment**. If overlying water ammonia **≥ 20 mg/L**, purge (6 volume changes per 24 h for 24 h, repeat once if still above 20 mg/L; amphipod test only). **Failing any one** of the three tests is a fail. Include negative and positive controls. |
| Reporting | After each stage, a report to DEP (copy MFC): plans showing the three categories and the disposal type for each; laboratory; sample collection, handling and storage details; methods and detection limits; results with reporting basis; QA/QC; departures from protocol; for biology, organism details, control water and sediment, preparation, test conditions, water quality and statistics. |

**Test species (Tier III)** 測試物種

| Test 測試 | Species 物種 |
|---|---|
| 10-day amphipod | *Ampelisca abdita*, *Eohaustorius estuarius*, *Leptocheirus plumulosus* (US EPA 1994 / PSEP 1995) |
| 20-day polychaete | *Neanthes arenaceodentata* (PSEP 1995) |
| 48–96 h larvae | Bivalve: *Mytilus* spp., *Crassostrea gigas*; echinoderm: *Dendraster excentricus*, *Strongylocentrotus* spp. (PSEP 1995) |

**Table 2: failure criteria (all at p ≤ 0.05 against reference sediment, two-sample test such as a t-test)** 不合格準則

| Test 測試 | Endpoint 指標 | Fails if 不合格條件 |
|---|---|---|
| Amphipod | Survival | Significantly different **and** < **80%** of reference |
| Polychaete | Dry weight (excluding dead and missing worms) | Significantly different **and** < **90%** of reference |
| Larvae | Normality survival (normal larvae surviving relative to the starting number) | Significantly different **and** < **80%** of reference |

Table 1 (analytical methods and reporting limits) lists US EPA preparation and determination methods (e.g. 3050B digestion; 6010C / 6020A / 7000A series; 8260B / 8270C for PAHs; 8082 for PCBs; Krone et al. 1989 for TBT). The PDF layout of the limits column does not extract cleanly, so the individual reporting limits are **not reproduced here**; use the PDF and consult DEP.

### Appendix C: management framework 處置流程

| Result 結果 | Disposal 處置方式 |
|---|---|
| Tier I shows little or no contamination, or Tier II **Category L** | **Type 1 open sea disposal** |
| **Category M**, passes Tier III | Type 1 open sea disposal (**dedicated sites**, monitored) |
| **Category M** fails, **or Category H ≤ 10 × LCEL**, or Category H > 10 × LCEL that passes the dilution test | **Type 2 confined marine disposal** (East Sha Chau mud pits at the time of issue) |
| **Category H > 10 × LCEL** failing the dilution test | **Type 3 special treatment / disposal**, agreed with DEP case by case (treatment to make it fit for confined disposal is not excluded) |

The cell for "Category H ≤ 10 × LCEL" is read from the flowchart's branches, so check the PDF diagram.

Notes 註:

1. Most open sea sites are shared, so contracts should allow switching between sites.
2. Dedicated sites are monitored.
3. For Type 2 and 3, the contract states the MFC and DEP allocation conditions.
4. For Type 3, the AP/RSE agrees treatment or disposal with DEP.
5. An allocation may require chemical tests (plus biological tests for M and some H) on sediment from **5% of vessels** en route. Five samples are mixed into one composite per vessel. Disposal sites are monitored under CEDD, and very large allocations may need extra monitoring.
6. Trailer suction hopper dredgers at East Sha Chau must use a **down-a-pipe** method approved by DCE.
