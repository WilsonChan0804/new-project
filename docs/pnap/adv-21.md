## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Framework for marine disposal of dredged or excavated sediment under the **Dumping at Sea Ordinance (Cap. 466)**: justify dredging, obtain sediment data for the contractor's permit, classify sediment into **three categories** by contaminant level, and the disposal arrangements for each (para. 1).
    - Applies to private projects with marine disposal not yet agreed by the **Marine Fill Committee**; re-test if more than **3 years** passes between sampling and works, unless DEP waives (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 根據**《海上傾倒物料條例》（第 466 章）**處理疏浚或挖掘沉積物的架構：說明疏浚需要、取得沉積物資料以支持承建商的許可證申請、按污染程度把沉積物分為**三類**，以及各類的傾卸安排（第 1 段）。
    - 適用於尚未獲**海上填料委員會**同意的私人海上傾卸項目；如抽樣與動工相隔超過 **3 年**須重新測試，除非環保署署長豁免（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: private projects needing **marine disposal** of dredged / excavated sediment (reclamation, marinas, deep basements) whose proposals the MFC has not yet agreed. Re-test if more than **3 years** pass between sampling and works. 需要海上棄置沉積物的私人項目。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Framework under **DASO Cap. 466**; scope as above | 範圍 |
| 3 | Rationale for removal to the **MFC Secretary** early (EIA stage); Category L **< 50,000 m³** exempt | 說明疏浚理由 |
| 4 | No justification needed: emergency dredging; fairway / berth maintenance; maintenance of watercourses | 免說明 |
| 5 | Otherwise plan to **keep mud in place**; consolidation time is not a reason to dredge; full cost comparison | 原則保留淤泥 |
| 6 | DEP classifies sediment by **Chemical Exceedance Levels** (App. A) | 分類 |
| 7 | Sampling and testing per App. B; waived for emergencies and maintenance dredging **< 5,000 m³** | 抽樣測試 |
| 8–9 | Sampling plan to DEP; **Preliminary Sediment Quality Report (PSQR)** | 初步報告 |
| 10 | Category M / some H: **biological screening** and formal **SQR** ≥ 3 months before tender | 生物篩選 |
| 11–12 | SQR reliability period ≥ 3 years; contract clause: contractor must keep SQR valid for the **dumping permit** | 有效期 |
| 13–14 | MFC (or DEP for < 50,000 m³ Category L) allocates open sea or **confined** disposal site; charge per m³ in situ | 分配棄置地點 |
