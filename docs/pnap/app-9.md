## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - After a draft map of a **proposed country park** is published, **no new development** may be carried out there without the prior approval of the **Country and Marine Parks Authority** (Cap. 208 s.10(1)) (para. 2).
    - The BA may **reject plans under BO s.16(1)(d)** (contravening another enactment) until that approval is obtained, or if its conditions are breached (para. 3).
    - Consult the Authority **early**, and check country park and special area boundaries (AFCD plans) (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - **擬設郊野公園**的草圖公布後，未經**郊野公園及海岸公園管理局**事先批准，不得在該處進行**新發展**（第 208 章第 10(1) 條）（第 2 段）。
    - 在取得批准前，或違反批准條件時，建築事務監督可根據**《建築物條例》第 16(1)(d) 條拒批圖則**（違反其他成文法則）（第 3 段）。
    - 應**及早**諮詢管理局，並核對郊野公園及特別地區的界線（漁護署圖則）（第 3–4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: new development on land within a **designated or proposed country park or special area** under the **Country Parks Ordinance Cap. 208**. 位於指定或擬議郊野公園或特別地區的新發展。

    **Not here 不在此列**: country park **enclaves** controlled only by OZP / DPA plans (Town Planning Ordinance); declared monuments ([APP-69](app-69.md)). 只受分區計劃大綱圖管制的「不包括土地」；古蹟。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Country Parks Authority has greatly increased the designated and proposed **country parks and special areas** under Cap. 208 s.10(1). 郊野公園範圍擴大。 |
| 2 | **Cap. 208 s.10(1)**: after a s.9(1) notice, **no new development** in a proposed country park on the draft map without the Authority's **prior approval**. 須事先獲批准。 |
| 3 | The BA may **disapprove** plans under **BO s.16(1)(d)** until that approval is obtained, or if its **conditions** are not met; consult the Authority **early**. 未獲批准可按第 16(1)(d) 條拒批。 |
| 4 | APs and RSEs should know the **locations and boundaries** of country parks (plans from AFCD, formerly the Agriculture and Fisheries Department). 須熟悉範圍。 |

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Submitting GBP for sites inside or partly inside a **country park enclave** without Cap. 208 approval.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 位於**郊野公園「不包括的土地」**或部分位於郊野公園的地盤，未取得第 208 章批准便呈交圖則。
