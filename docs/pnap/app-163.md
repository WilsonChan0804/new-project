## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Since **1 Feb 2021**, the B(C)R requires **adequate means of access for maintenance and repair (M&R access)** of **external walls, curtain walls, claddings, roofs and their projections**. The **Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance 2021** shows how to comply, replacing the 2019 Design for Safety code (para. 1).
    - **Transitional**: not applicable to works in progress or with consent by **31 Jan 2021** (generally foundations). For A&A, APs should **explore** providing M&R access (paras. 2–3).
    - APs submit **M&R access plans** with details, supporting documents and the **developer / owner's undertaking** (para. 4).
    - **2024 amendments** (Appendix A): at least **one anchor for a personal fall arrest system** at **every balcony and utility platform**, readily reachable, at **1.5–1.8 m** above the adjoining floor (new para. 3.2.4; for new or major-revision GBP submitted **from 1 Dec 2024**); outer surfaces of balconies / UPs reached by **powered elevating or suspended platforms**; guard-rails with toe-boards where stepping onto inaccessible roofs, with alternatives where impracticable.

=== "中文"

    - 自 **2021 年 2 月 1 日**起，B(C)R 規定須為**外牆、幕牆、覆蓋層、屋頂及其伸出物**提供**足夠的維修通道**；《2021 年外部維修通道作業守則》說明如何符合，取代 2019 年的守則（第 1 段）。
    - **過渡安排**：在 **2021 年 1 月 31 日**已進行或已獲同意的工程（一般指地基）不受規限；改動加建工程應**研究**提供維修通道（第 2–3 段）。
    - 認可人士須呈交**維修通道圖則**，連同細節、證明文件及**發展商／業主承諾書**（第 4 段）。
    - **2024 年修訂**（附錄 A）：**每個露台及工作平台**最少設**一個個人防墮系統錨固點**，方便到達，高度距相鄰樓面 **1.5–1.8 米**（新第 3.2.4 段；適用於 **2024 年 12 月 1 日起**呈交的新圖則或重大修訂）；露台／工作平台外表面以**機動升降台或吊船**維修；如須踏上非開放屋頂，須在邊緣設連踢腳板的護欄，不切實可行時可用替代方案。

## Key table: what needs M&R access 須提供維修通道的部分 {#key-table-what-needs-mr-access}

| Element 構件 | B(C)R section 條文 |
|---|---|
| External walls and their projections 外牆及伸出物 | 27(2) |
| Claddings exposed to weather 外露覆蓋層 | 28(5) |
| Curtain walls and projections 幕牆及伸出物 | 31(3) |
| Roofs and projections 屋頂及伸出物 | 34(3) |

## Key table: 2024 amendments 2024 年修訂 {#key-table-2024-amendments-2024}

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 | Applies 適用 |
|---|---|---|
| Fall arrest anchor 防墮錨固點 | **≥ 1** per balcony / UP; reachable from the floor; **1.5–1.8 m** high; structural details submitted | GBP / major revision from **1 Dec 2024**; advised for ongoing projects |
| Balcony / UP outer face 露台／工作平台外表面 | Powered elevating work platform or suspended working platform 機動升降台或吊船 | Immediate 即時 |
| Inaccessible roof edge 非開放屋頂邊緣 | Guard-rails with toe-boards if stepping on is needed; alternatives where impracticable 護欄連踢腳板 | Immediate 即時 |

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Façade features (fins, sunshades, curtain wall) with **no feasible access** shown for cleaning and repair.
        - No **owner's undertaking** for the maintenance system.
        - Balconies and UPs on new projects without the **fall arrest anchor**.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 外牆構件（鰭板、遮陽板、幕牆）沒有顯示可行的清潔及維修通道。
        - 沒有提交維修系統的**業主承諾書**。
        - 新項目的露台及工作平台沒有**防墮錨固點**。
