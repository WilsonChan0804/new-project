## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Amendments to the **Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004**: **App. A (Sep 2016)** and **App. B (Oct 2023)** (para. 1).
    - Sep 2016 changes: the **RGE** role where geotechnical concerns exist and the Minor Works Control System; scaffold and **catchfan stability in typhoons**; an extra **heavy-duty net**; guidance on machine demolition and **aged buildings** to old design codes; waste disposal and debris procedures (App. A).

=== "中文"

    - **2004 年拆卸建築物作業守則**的修訂：**附錄 A（2016 年 9 月）**及**附錄 B（2023 年 10 月）**（第 1 段）。
    - 2016 年 9 月修訂：有岩土問題時**註冊岩土工程師**的角色及小型工程監管制度；**颱風期間棚架及防護網穩定**；加設**重型安全網**；機械拆卸及按舊設計守則建造的**舊樓**拆卸指引；廢料處置及碎料處理程序（附錄 A）。

## Amendments summary 修訂摘要 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, first issue October 2023. The full clause-by-clause content of the 2016 amendments is on the [Demolition Code page](../codes/demolition-2004.md). 已按 2023 年 10 月版本核對。

**Appendix A (September 2016)** 二〇一六年九月:
- (a) **RGE** role for demolition with geotechnical concerns, and the **MWCS** (Code not for Type G minor works; the old UBW removal guidelines are obsolete).
- (b) Stabilising critical scaffold parts, including **catchfans**, in strong wind and typhoons.
- (c) An extra layer of **heavy-duty net** on scaffolds against falling debris.
- (d) Guidance on **machine demolition** and on aged buildings designed to **LCC** codes.
- (e) Updated waste disposal facilities and debris handling.

**Appendix B (October 2023)** 二〇二三年十月:

| Item 項目 | Clause 條文 | Amendment 修訂 |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Table 3.4 | **Propping** requirements added for light-weight mechanical plant up to **5,800 kg**. |
| (b) | 3.8.1 | Training now by the **Hong Kong Institute of Construction** (replacing the CITA reference). |
| (c) | 3.8.8 (new) | Precautions at the **interface between two adjacent** demolition / construction sites. |
| (d) | 3.8.9 (new) | Stronger precautions at **floor openings and free edges**. |
| (e) | 3.8.10 (new) | Tighter control of **conveying debris through floor openings**. |
| (f) | 3.10.7 | Clarified C&D material disposal requirements. |
| (g) | 4.2.4(C)(1), (3), Fig. 4.5 | Revised method and procedure for demolishing **exterior columns**. |
| (h) | App. E 6(D) | Clarified personal information in **Form BA20** posted near the site entrance. |
| (i) | Apps F and G figures | **Temporary platforms** are required for cantilevered structures **unless** they are removed by **cut and lift** or similar methods (cl. 3.5.1(B)). |

The detailed wording of these clauses (including the Table 3.4 propping values) is in the PDF amendment tables. Check there before design.
