## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Adopts **GEOSPEC 3 – Model Specification for Soil Testing** (GEO): **32** classification and compaction tests (App. A) and **7** shear strength and compressibility tests (App. B) (para. 1).
    - Developed from a GEO review of **BS 1377:1990** (GEO Report No. 36 for Phase I) (para. 2).
    - AP, RSE and RGE should **specify GEOSPEC 3** for soil testing in investigation, design and construction (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 採用土力工程處的 **GEOSPEC 3 土壤測試標準規格**：**32** 項分類及壓實測試（附錄 A）及 **7** 項剪力強度及壓縮性測試（附錄 B）（第 1 段）。
    - 由土力工程處檢討 **BS 1377:1990** 發展而成（第一階段為 GEO 報告第 36 號）（第 2 段）。
    - 認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊岩土工程師在勘測、設計及施工中應**指定採用 GEOSPEC 3**（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: all **soil tests** supporting plans, approval / consent conditions (s.17(1) item 6(b)), GI in Scheduled Areas, site formation plans and fill compaction records: use **Geospec 3**, by **HOKLAS**-accredited labs. 土壤測試須按 Geospec 3 及由認可實驗室進行。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Geospec 3 – Model Specification for Soil Testing** (GEO): **32 Expanded Phase I** tests (classification, compaction; App. A) and **7 Phase II** tests (shear strength, compressibility; App. B). 32 + 7 項測試。 |
| 2 | Background: GEO review of **BS 1377:1990**; Phase I first in **GEO Report No. 36** (1994). 背景。 |
| 3 | Adopt Geospec 3; plans or consent may be **refused** if soil tests are not to it; fill completion certificates may not be accepted otherwise ([APP-15](app-15.md)). 不符合可拒批。 |
| 4 | Only **HOKLAS-accredited** labs; only **HOKLAS-endorsed** certificates accepted (HOKLAS 009 directory; Geospec 3 clause 2.3). 認可實驗室。 |
| 5 | **Credibility** of data matters (disapproval and sanctions for misrepresentation): test schedule fits the geology; supervise sampling, storage, transport; careful testing and vetting; reproduce reports only **in full**. 數據可信性。 |
| 6 | Tests on or after **1 Jul 2004**: only labs accredited **to Geospec 3**. 2004 年 7 月 1 日後。 |
| 7 | Tests before 1 Jul 2004: GEO Report 36 Phase I certificates (App. C) still accepted. 之前的測試。 |
| App. A–C | Lists of test methods (moisture content, Atterberg limits, particle density and size, compaction, etc.; Phase II strength and consolidation). 測試方法表。 |
