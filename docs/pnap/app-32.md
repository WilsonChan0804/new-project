## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The **Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance (Cap. 301)** sets **Airport Height Restrictions (AHRs)** shown on plans deposited in the Land Registry (para. 1).
    - **Temporary exemption** from the **Director-General of Civil Aviation**, normally **≤ 2 months** each time; contractors must get prior approval for **cranes and plant** projecting above the limit (paras. 2–3).
    - **Third runway**: new AHRs from **31 May 2022** (stricter near the airport, more relaxed elsewhere); not applicable to buildings with plans approved, or minor works completed, before that date (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - **《香港機場（障礙管制）條例》（第 301 章）**訂定**機場高度限制**，載於存放在土地註冊處的圖則（第 1 段）。
    - 可向**民航處處長**申請**臨時豁免**，一般每次 **≤ 2 個月**；承建商須事先取得批准才可使用超出限制的**吊機及機械**（第 2–3 段）。
    - **第三條跑道**：新高度限制由 **2022 年 5 月 31 日**起生效（機場附近較嚴、其他地區較寬鬆）；在該日前已獲批圖則的建築物或已完成的小型工程不受影響（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: buildings, and **cranes and temporary plant** during construction, in areas under **Airport Height Restrictions (AHRs)** set by Cap. 301 Orders. New third-runway AHRs from **31 May 2022**. 受機場高度限制地區的建築物及施工機械。

    **Not affected by the new AHRs 不受新限制影響**: buildings on plans **approved before 31 May 2022**, and minor works and designated exempted works **completed** before that date (para. 4). 2022 年 5 月 31 日前批准的圖則。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Cap. 301** restricts building heights for aviation safety; limits vary by location and are on **AHR Plans** (Land Registry; LandsD Map Publications Centres; CAD website). 高度限制圖。 |
| 2 | **Temporary exemption** from the **DGCA**, normally **≤ 2 months**, extendable by further 2-month periods. 臨時豁免最多 2 個月，可續期。 |
| 3 | AP / RSE must ensure the RC gets DGCA **prior approval** for any **temporary plant** (e.g. cranes) likely to exceed the limits (Practice Note for Registered Contractors 7). 施工機械須事先批准。 |
| 4 | **Third runway**: the 2021 Amendment Order brings **new AHRs** into force on **31 May 2022**: stricter near HKIA (sea and hills), less strict for most of Hong Kong; transitional exemption as above. 第三跑道新限制。 |

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Tower cranes exceeding the AHR without **CAD approval**.
        - Using pre-2022 AHR plans for new schemes.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 塔式吊機超出高度限制而沒有**民航處批准**。
        - 新項目仍使用 2022 年前的高度限制圖。
