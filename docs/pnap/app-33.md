## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - PFA may partly replace OPC if curing is effective (early strength is lower in cold weather) (para. 1).
    - Conditions (para. 2): PFA only with OPC to **BS 3892 Pt 1**; blended cement to **BS 6588** with **≤ 25%** PFA (not combined with added PFA); total PFA **≤ 35%** of cementitious content, with **> 25%** only for special uses such as marine works and mass pours under expert control.
    - AP / RSE specify PFA content in structural submissions; RC ensures the supplier's quality control (para. 2(d)).

=== "中文"

    - 如能有效養護，煤灰可部分代替普通硅酸鹽水泥（寒冷天氣早期強度較低）（第 1 段）。
    - 條件（第 2 段）：煤灰只可與普通水泥合用並符合 **BS 3892 第 1 部**；混合水泥須符合 **BS 6588**，煤灰含量 **≤ 25%**（不可再另加煤灰）；煤灰總量 **≤ 膠凝材料的 35%**，**> 25%** 只限海事工程及大體積澆灌等特殊用途，並須專家指導。
    - 認可人士／註冊結構工程師須在結構圖則訂明煤灰含量；註冊承建商須確保供應商有品質控制（第 2(d) 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: concrete using **pulverised fuel ash (PFA)** as partial cement replacement, or **PFA blended cement**. 使用粉煤灰的混凝土。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | PFA has technical and environmental benefits but needs **good curing**; early strength lower in cold weather. 須妥善養護。 |
| 2(a) | PFA as a separate constituent only with **OPC**, to **BS 3892-1:1982** (max. water requirement may not apply). 只可與普通水泥混合。 |
| 2(b) | **Blended cement** to **BS 6588:1985**, nominal PFA **≤ 25%**; no extra PFA on top of blended cement. 混合水泥 ≤ 25%。 |
| 2(c) | PFA **≤ 35%** of cementitious content; **25%** normal; **> 25%** only for special uses (marine, mass pours) with expert advice and strict control. 上限 35%。 |
| 2(d) | AP / RSE specify PFA content and extent in structural submissions; ensure the supplier's **quality control**; be told of deviations. 須列明用量。 |
| 2(e) | Over 25%: check effects on **formwork striking, creep, long-term deflection**. 超過 25% 須評估。 |
