## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Poorly designed pump rooms and ventilation systems disturb residents; separate pump rooms from flats with **buffer spaces** (stairs, switch rooms) and treat both airborne and structure-borne noise (paras. 1–2).
    - **Airborne**: quieter pumps (if total sound power > **100 dB(A)**, insulate more); walls and slabs next to sensitive receivers **≥ STC 50**; doors facing sensitive areas **≥ STC 25**; acoustic louvres or silencers on openings (para. 2(a)).
    - **Structure-borne**: **anti-vibration mounts** and flexible connections for pumps and motors (para. 2(b)); ventilation guidelines follow.

=== "中文"

    - 設計不當的泵房及通風系統會滋擾住戶；泵房應以**緩衝空間**（樓梯、電掣房）與住宅分隔，並處理空氣傳聲及結構傳聲（第 1–2 段）。
    - **空氣傳聲**：選用較寧靜的泵（總聲功率 > **100 dB(A)** 時須加強隔音）；毗鄰易受影響地方的牆及樓板 **≥ STC 50**；面向該等地方的門 **≥ STC 25**；開口加隔音百葉或消音器（第 2(a) 段）。
    - **結構傳聲**：泵及摩打裝**防震座**及柔性接駁（第 2(b) 段）；其後為通風系統指引。
