## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Poorly designed pump rooms and ventilation systems disturb residents; separate pump rooms from flats with **buffer spaces** (stairs, switch rooms) and treat both airborne and structure-borne noise (paras. 1–2).
    - **Airborne**: quieter pumps (if total sound power > **100 dB(A)**, insulate more); walls and slabs next to sensitive receivers **≥ STC 50**; doors facing sensitive areas **≥ STC 25**; acoustic louvres or silencers on openings (para. 2(a)).
    - **Structure-borne**: **anti-vibration mounts** and flexible connections for pumps and motors (para. 2(b)); ventilation guidelines follow.

=== "中文"

    - 設計不當的泵房及通風系統會滋擾住戶；泵房應以**緩衝空間**（樓梯、電掣房）與住宅分隔，並處理空氣傳聲及結構傳聲（第 1–2 段）。
    - **空氣傳聲**：選用較寧靜的泵（總聲功率 > **100 dB(A)** 時須加強隔音）；毗鄰易受影響地方的牆及樓板 **≥ STC 50**；面向該等地方的門 **≥ STC 25**；開口加隔音百葉或消音器（第 2(a) 段）。
    - **結構傳聲**：泵及摩打裝**防震座**及柔性接駁（第 2(b) 段）；其後為通風系統指引。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: design of **pump rooms** and **ventilation / air-conditioning plant** near noise sensitive receivers (flats, schools). 泵房及通風系統設計。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Poor design causes noise annoyance | 目的 |
| 2 | Pump rooms: buffer with stairs or switch rooms | 泵房位置 |
| 2(a) | Airborne: quieter pumps (> **100 dB(A)** total needs more insulation); walls and slabs **STC 50**; doors **STC 25**; acoustic louvres | 空氣傳聲 |
| 2(b) | Structure-borne: **anti-vibration mounts**, flexible joints, isolated pipe hangers, rubber sleeves; size pipes to limit velocity | 結構傳聲 |
| 3 | Ventilation: aim **5 dB(A) below** the NCO Technical Memorandum acceptable levels; quieter plant, isolation; near receivers: relocate, enclosures, silencers | 通風系統 |
