## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - **Amenity features** are not statutory requirements but **improve building quality and management**. The BA may grant **modification / exemption** (mostly from GFA and SC) to encourage them.
    - A proposal must meet at least one **objective** (para. 2): better building management, better quality of life, **avoiding UBW temptation**, or better compatibility with the neighbourhood.
    - Typical features and limits: A/C plant rooms ≤ **1%** of total GFA, AHUs ≤ **4%** of each floor; management offices and guard rooms ≤ **0.2%** of GFA (domestic: or **5 m² per 50 flats**; non-domestic: or **120 m²**, whichever is smaller).
    - Covered walkways ≤ **3.5 m** wide × **4.5 m** high. Trellis ≤ **5%** of roof area. Outdoor seat shelters ≤ **10%** of the open space and ≤ **20 m²** each. Podium gardens under a tower ≤ **5%** of domestic GFA.
    - **New in July 2025**: shelters for outdoor seats, covered areas at public open space, covered pick-up/set-down areas and **accessible lift extensions at existing buildings**, all to promote an **elderly-friendly** environment.

=== "中文"

    - **適意設施**並非法定要求，但可**提升建築物質素及管理**；建築事務監督可給予**變通／豁免**（主要是總樓面面積及上蓋面積）以作鼓勵。
    - 申請須符合最少一項**目標**（第 2 段）：有效的建築物管理、提升生活質素、**避免僭建誘因**，或改善與鄰近環境的協調。
    - 主要設施及限制：冷氣機房 ≤ 總樓面面積 **1%**，空氣處理機 ≤ 每層 **4%**；管理處及看更室 ≤ 總樓面面積 **0.2%**（住用：或每 50 個單位 **5 平方米**；非住用：或 **120 平方米**，以較小者為準）。
    - 有蓋走道 ≤ 闊 **3.5 米** × 高 **4.5 米**；花棚架 ≤ 天台面積 **5%**；戶外座椅上蓋 ≤ 休憩空間 **10%**、每個 ≤ **20 平方米**；住宅大廈下的平台花園 ≤ 住用總樓面面積 **5%**。
    - **2025 年 7 月新增**：戶外座椅上蓋、公眾休憩用地的有蓋地方、有蓋上落客區，以及**現有建築物加建暢通易達升降機**，以推廣**長者友善**環境。

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "Para. 8 — A/C plant rooms 冷氣機房"
    The following provisions would be considered reasonable in the absence of the detailed design layout of the plants: (a) air-conditioning plant rooms not exceeding 1% of the total GFA of a building; and/or (b) air handling units, not exceeding 4% of the total GFA of each floor on which they are situated.

    **中譯（非官方）：** 在未有機房詳細設計佈局時，以下面積會被視為合理：(a) 冷氣機房不超過建築物總樓面面積的 1%；及／或 (b) 空氣處理機不超過其所在樓層總樓面面積的 4%。

!!! quote "Para. 14(c)–(d) — Management facilities 管理設施"
    (c) the maximum GFA in a domestic building or domestic part of a composite building is 0.2% of the total domestic GFA or 5m² for every 50 flats, whichever is smaller; and (d) the maximum GFA that may be allowed for exemption in a non-domestic building or non-domestic part of a composite building is 0.2% of the total non-domestic GFA or 120m², whichever is smaller.

    **中譯（非官方）：** (c) 住用建築物或綜合用途建築物住用部分的上限為住用總樓面面積的 0.2% 或每 50 個單位 5 平方米，以較小者為準；及 (d) 非住用建築物或綜合用途建築物非住用部分可獲豁免的上限為非住用總樓面面積的 0.2% 或 120 平方米，以較小者為準。

!!! quote "Para. 46 — Implementation 實施"
    The amenity features in paragraphs 20, 24, 30 and 42 which aim to promote elderly friendly built environment are applicable to residential and composite buildings, as well as commercial buildings often patronised by the elderly. If such features already exist in an existing building and have been included in GFA/SC calculations, application for exemption of such features from GFA/SC calculations and transferring the GFA/SC for other purposes will not be accepted.

    **中譯（非官方）：** 第 20、24、30 及 42 段旨在推廣長者友善建築環境的適意設施，適用於住宅及綜合用途建築物，以及長者經常光顧的商業建築物。如該等設施已存在於現有建築物並已計入總樓面面積／上蓋面積，則不接受申請將其豁免並把該面積轉作其他用途。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    APP-42 is a **menu of "nice-to-have" features** that the BA will exempt from GFA (and sometimes SC), because they make buildings better managed and more pleasant. There are **no hard and fast rules** (para. 3). Each application is judged on its merits, so explain **why** the feature is needed and **why its size** is right.

    Three things recur throughout:

    1. **Size limits**, often a % of GFA or a fixed m², to stop abuse.
    2. **Common parts**: the area must be designated as common parts in the DMC, so no owner can take it over.
    3. **APP-151**: most items need the pre-requisites (SBD, BEAM Plus, energy). Some also count towards the **10% cap**.

=== "中文"

    APP-42 是一份**「錦上添花」設施清單**：因為這些設施有助建築物管理及提升舒適度，建築事務監督會豁免其總樓面面積（有時包括上蓋面積）。**沒有硬性規則**（第 3 段），每宗申請按個別情況考慮，因此須說明設施**為何需要**及**面積為何合理**。

    三個重複出現的要點：

    1. **面積限制**：通常以總樓面面積百分比或固定平方米計，以防濫用。
    2. **公用部分**：須在公契中訂為公用部分，避免個別業主據為己有。
    3. **APP-151**：大部分項目須符合先決條件（可持續建築設計、綠建環評、能源），部分亦計入 **10% 上限**。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! example "Worked example: management office (domestic)"
        Residential development: **600 flats**, total domestic GFA **36,000 m²**.

        - 0.2% × 36,000 = **72 m²**
        - 5 m² × (600 ÷ 50) = **60 m²**
        - Max. exempted = smaller = **60 m²** (also counts towards the APP-151 10% cap)

    !!! example "Worked example: trellis on a common roof"
        Common roof area **400 m²**: total trellis ≤ 5% × 400 = **20 m²**, and each trellis ≤ 20 m².

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - **In-flat A/C plant rooms** need justification and must meet the 500 mm level drop and open-side rules (para. 12).
        - **Trellis and seat shelters cannot be on a refuge roof**.
        - Features already counted as GFA in an existing building **cannot be re-exempted** to free up GFA for other uses (para. 46).
        - Radio base station **rooms** are **GFA accountable** and treated as new buildings needing an OP (para. 40).

=== "中文"

    !!! example "計算例子：住宅管理處"
        住宅發展：**600 個單位**，住用總樓面面積 **36,000 平方米**。

        - 0.2% × 36,000 = **72 平方米**
        - 5 平方米 × (600 ÷ 50) = **60 平方米**
        - 豁免上限 = 較小者 = **60 平方米**（並計入 APP-151 的 10% 上限）

    !!! example "計算例子：公用天台花棚架"
        公用天台面積 **400 平方米**：花棚架總面積 ≤ 5% × 400 = **20 平方米**，每個 ≤ 20 平方米。

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - **單位內冷氣機房**須論證，並符合低 500 毫米及一邊開敞的規定（第 12 段）。
        - **花棚架及座椅上蓋不得設於庇護天台**。
        - 現有建築物已計入總樓面面積的設施，**不可再申請豁免**以騰出面積作其他用途（第 46 段）。
        - 無線電基站**房間**須**計入總樓面面積**，並被視為新建築物，須取得佔用許可證（第 40 段）。
