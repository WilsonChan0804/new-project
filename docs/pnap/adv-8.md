## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - GEO keeps a **Catalogue of Slopes** of sizeable man-made slopes and retaining walls, each with a **registration number**; definitions and criteria in Apps A and B (para. 1).
    - Data are in the **Slope Information System**, also online at the HK Slope Safety website (para. 2).
    - APs / RSEs and their geotechnical consultants should give GEO information on completed permanent features **formed or modified** within or next to the development that the owners must maintain (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 土力工程處備存**斜坡名冊**，記錄具規模的人造斜坡及擋土牆，每項均有**登記編號**；定義及準則見附錄 A、B（第 1 段）。
    - 資料存於**斜坡資料系統**，亦可在香港斜坡安全網站查閱（第 2 段）。
    - 認可人士／註冊結構工程師及其岩土顧問須向土力工程處提供發展項目內或毗鄰、業主須負責維修並已**新建或改動**的永久斜坡資料（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: AP / RSE and geotechnical consultants for site formation or **slope upgrading** in private developments, for features the owners must maintain. 私人發展的斜坡及擋土牆。

### Registration criteria 註冊準則 {#registration-criteria}

| Type 類型 | Definition 定義 | Registrable if 註冊準則 |
|---|---|---|
| F | Fill slope or platform ≥ **15°**, fill ≥ 1 m, no wall | Fill slopes **> 5 m**; or < 5 m with Consequence-to-life Category 1 or 2 |
| C / CR | Cut slope (with wall) | Cut slopes and walls **> 3 m** |
| R | Retaining wall; walls on a > 45° slope combined | **> 3 m** |
| FR | Fill slope with wall | as F |
| DT | Disturbed terrain (landslide repairs, terraces), natural gradient > 15° | Combined height meets the criteria |

## Appendices A–D in full 附錄 A–D 詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision February 2002 (not revised since). The office addresses, the website links and the old PNAP number quoted in the PNAP (PNAP 234 = [APP-109](app-109.md)) date from 2002. Check GEO's current requirements before submitting. 已按 2002 年版本核對，地址及網址或已過時。

**Extra points in the main text 正文補充**
- **Para. 2**: GEO's Slope Information System (SIS) is online on the Hong Kong Slope Safety website.
- **Para. 3**: maintenance responsibility comes from LandsD's Slope Maintenance Responsibility Information System (SMRIS) and from lease clauses (cutting away clauses, green hatched black areas).
- **Para. 4**: submit with the **certificate of completion** of site formation or feature upgrading works:
  - a **1:1000 site plan** with location plan showing each feature boundary;
  - the record sheets (Appendix C) and photos;
  - a soft copy of the data in the Appendix D structure (the standard database file was downloadable from the slope safety website).
- **Para. 5**: GEO returns the plan marked with the **registration numbers**.
- **Para. 6**: tell owners the feature numbers for their maintenance records, and advise them to put the numbers in the **DMC**.

### Appendix A: feature types (6/2001) 斜坡類別

| Code 代號 | Definition 定義 |
|---|---|
| **F** | Fill slope or platform with a surface **≥ 15°** and fill at least **1 m** thick, with no retaining wall |
| **C** | Cut slope with no retaining wall |
| **R** | Retaining wall supporting a slope or platform sloping **< 15°** (fill or natural ground). A series of walls each below registrable height on an overall slope **> 45°** is judged on **combined height** |
| **CR** | Cut slope with retaining wall(s) at crest, middle or toe |
| **FR** | Fill slope with retaining wall(s) at crest, middle or toe |
| **DT** | **Disturbed terrain**: landslide scar repairs or composite cut / fill on natural ground **> 15°**, where individual slopes are below the height criteria but the **total** height meets them |

Boundaries are marked on the plan for each type (wall plan boundary, fill / cut boundary, combined boundary, disturbed terrain boundary).

### Appendix B: registration criteria (Rev. 2/2002) 登記準則

Registrable features 須登記:
- (a) **Cut slopes** (with any walls) and **retaining walls** higher than **3 m**.
- (b) **Fill slopes** (with any walls) higher than **5 m**.
- (c) Fill slopes **below 5 m** that pose a **direct risk to life** (consequence-to-life Category **1 or 2**, see [APP-109](app-109.md)).
- (d) Disturbed terrain as defined in Appendix A. This covers landslide repairs, cemeteries, cleared squatter land and agricultural terraces (existing or abandoned).

**Not registrable 不須登記**

| Type 類別 | Note 備註 |
|---|---|
| 1 Seawalls / marine structures | Water-facing side of coastal soil- or rock-retaining structures |
| 2 Dams and water-retaining structures | Including private service reservoir walls; **fill embankments in front of them are registered** |
| 3 Basement / screen walls | Part of a building, with the free side facing into the building |
| 4 Tunnel / shaft linings | **Free-standing portal slopes and walls are registered** |
| 5 Culverts / covered nullahs | **Free-standing sidewalls of open nullahs, catchwaters and river banks meeting the criteria are registered** |
| 6 Bridge abutments | **Fill embankments and reinforced fill structures are registered** |
| 7 Temporary features | Stockpiles, working quarry faces, active tips; **abandoned quarry faces and completed tips are registered** as cut / fill slopes |

Walls built **monolithic with a building** are not registered; tell GEO if one is already in the catalogue.

### Appendix C: record sheets (Rev. 6/2001) 紀錄表

| Sheet 頁 | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Reference no. (GEO can supply one), address, 1980-datum coordinates, toe level (mPD). Slope and wall data: material, wall type, height, length, angle, berms, surface cover, weepholes / horizontal drains, channels (crest, berm, toe, slope), down pipes, special measures (soil nails, anchors, reinforced earth, long horizontal drains, rock bolts, buttress walls). Services on the slope and at the crest |
| 2 | **Consequence-to-life**: facilities at crest and toe and their distances, category per APP-109. General: dates of GI and construction, contractors, designer, as-built drawing nos., nearest raingauge. Prescriptive measures Types 1–3, evidence of GEO checking, stability assessment |
| 3 | Location plan and **1:1000 site plan** |
| 4 | Record photographs with comments, dates and reference numbers |

### Appendix D: SIS data structure (version 1.2) 數據結構

Field-by-field database format matching Appendix C, for example:
- FEATURE, LOCATION, EAST / NORTH (6-digit, 800000–900000), TOE_ELV;
- slope fields SF_MAT (soil, rock, soil & rock), SF_GEOLOGY, SF_HEIGHT, SF_ANGLE (0–90), surface cover percentages, weephole and drain size / spacing bands, channel size bands, special measures Y/N;
- wall fields WF_TYPE (with level platform, at crest, at toe, mid-slope) and WF_MATERIAL (random rubble, masonry, gabion, concrete, others);
- service sizes, CONSEQ_CAT (1, 2, 3), and crest and toe facility distances and types.

Use the GEO's current template rather than copying field lengths from the 2002 PNAP.
