## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - GEO keeps a **Catalogue of Slopes** of sizeable man-made slopes and retaining walls, each with a **registration number**; definitions and criteria in Apps A and B (para. 1).
    - Data are in the **Slope Information System**, also online at the HK Slope Safety website (para. 2).
    - APs / RSEs and their geotechnical consultants should give GEO information on completed permanent features **formed or modified** within or next to the development that the owners must maintain (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 土力工程處備存**斜坡名冊**，記錄具規模的人造斜坡及擋土牆，每項均有**登記編號**；定義及準則見附錄 A、B（第 1 段）。
    - 資料存於**斜坡資料系統**，亦可在香港斜坡安全網站查閱（第 2 段）。
    - 認可人士／註冊結構工程師及其岩土顧問須向土力工程處提供發展項目內或毗鄰、業主須負責維修並已**新建或改動**的永久斜坡資料（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: AP / RSE and geotechnical consultants for site formation or **slope upgrading** in private developments, for features the owners must maintain. 私人發展的斜坡及擋土牆。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | GEO **Catalogue of Slopes**; each feature numbered; definitions App. A, criteria App. B | 斜坡目錄 |
| 2 | **Slope Information System**: GEO Homantin and hkss.ced.gov.hk | 資料系統 |
| 3 | Report completed **permanent features** formed or modified within or next to the site that owners maintain; maintenance responsibility from LandsD **SMRIC** and lease | 須提交資料 |
| 4 | **1:1000 plan**, record sheet (App. C), photos, soft copy (App. D) with the **completion certificate** | 提交內容 |
| 5 | GEO returns the plan with **feature numbers** | 編號 |
| 6 | Tell owners the numbers; include in the **DMC** | 公契 |

### Registration criteria 註冊準則 {#registration-criteria}

| Type 類型 | Definition 定義 | Registrable if 註冊準則 |
|---|---|---|
| F | Fill slope or platform ≥ **15°**, fill ≥ 1 m, no wall | Fill slopes **> 5 m**; or < 5 m with Consequence-to-life Category 1 or 2 |
| C / CR | Cut slope (with wall) | Cut slopes and walls **> 3 m** |
| R | Retaining wall; walls on a > 45° slope combined | **> 3 m** |
| FR | Fill slope with wall | as F |
| DT | Disturbed terrain (landslide repairs, terraces), natural gradient > 15° | Combined height meets the criteria |
