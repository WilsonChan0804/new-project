## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - **Fire engineering** offers a flexible alternative to the prescriptive MOE, MOA and FRC codes, especially for special, large or complex buildings and A&A, achieving **equivalent overall safety** (paras. 1–2).
    - Understand the performance requirements and prescriptive minimums; small deviations may need only **like-for-like equivalence** (possibly with tests) rather than a full fire safety strategy (para. 3).
    - Larger deviations need a full fire engineering study with compensating measures (later paras.). Current guidance: FS Code **Part F / G**.

=== "中文"

    - **消防工程**為走火、救援通道及耐火結構守則的規定提供靈活的替代方案，特別適用於特殊、大型或複雜的建築物及改動加建，須達致**同等的整體安全**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須了解性能要求及最低規定；輕微偏差可能只需證明**同等替代**（或須測試），毋須制定全面消防策略（第 3 段）。
    - 較大偏差須進行全面消防工程研究並提供補償措施（其後各段）；現行指引見消防守則 **F／G 部**。
