## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Fire engineering** offers a flexible alternative to the prescriptive MOE, MOA and FRC codes, especially for special, large or complex buildings and A&A, achieving **equivalent overall safety** (paras. 1–2).
    - Understand the performance requirements and prescriptive minimums; small deviations may need only **like-for-like equivalence** (possibly with tests) rather than a full fire safety strategy (para. 3).
    - Objectives to meet (para. 4): no unacceptable risk of fire spread; occupants reach safety **without being endangered**; firefighters can operate; no fire spread to other buildings. Methods: **risk assessment**, **hazard analysis** and deterministic **equivalence** (paras. 5–8). Follow the flow chart (Appendix) and prepare a **fire safety strategy report** (paras. 9–11); consult **BD and FSD before submission**; the **AP** leads (paras. 12–13). For FS Code buildings, use **Part G** instead ([APP-153](app-153.md)).

=== "中文"

    - **消防工程**為走火、救援通道及耐火結構守則的規定提供靈活的替代方案，特別適用於特殊、大型或複雜的建築物及改動加建，須達致**同等的整體安全**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須了解性能要求及最低規定；輕微偏差可能只需證明**同等替代**（或須測試），毋須制定全面消防策略（第 3 段）。
    - 須達到的目標（第 4 段）：火警發展及蔓延風險可接受；住用人可在**不受危害**下到達安全地方；消防員可進行救援；火勢不會蔓延至其他建築物。方法包括**風險評估**、**危害分析**及確定性的**等效**比較（第 5–8 段）；按附錄流程圖進行並擬備**消防安全策略報告**（第 9–11 段）；呈交前須諮詢**屋宇署及消防處**，由**認可人士**主導（第 12–13 段）。按消防守則設計的建築物改用 **G 部**（[APP-153](app-153.md)）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fire engineering as an alternative to the **MOE, MOA and FRC Codes** to meet B(C)R 90 and B(P)R 41–41C. 消防工程方法。 |
| 2 | For impracticable cases, special, large or complex buildings and A&A; aim for safety **equivalent** to full prescriptive compliance. 適用情況。 |
| 3 | Small departures: show like-for-like **equivalence** with conservative assumptions. 小偏差。 |
| 4 | Objectives: limit fire development and spread; occupants reach safety unharmed; firefighting access; no spread to adjacent buildings. 性能目標。 |
| 5–7 | Deterministic design combining **risk** and **hazard**; Hong Kong lacks risk statistics, so **hazard analysis** with fire models is preferred. 方法。 |
| 8 | Equivalence: the alternative performs at least as well as the complying design in the same fire scenario. 等效。 |
| 9–10 | Design procedure flow chart (Appendix): scenarios, fire loads, occupants, protection; revise until criteria are met. 設計程序。 |
| 11 | **Fire safety strategy report** contents (introduction, building, objectives, strategy, analysis, management). 策略報告。 |
| 12 | Consult **BD and FSD** before submitting building plans, to agree objectives. 及早諮詢。 |
| 13 | No accreditation of fire engineers yet: the **AP** leads, with specialist input. 認可人士主導。 |
| 14–15 | Keep up to date with overseas codes; this note is an initial framework. 參考資料。 |
