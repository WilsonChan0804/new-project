## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Reminder to provide **protective and precautionary measures** to protect adjoining buildings and the public (paras. 1–2).
    - Pay attention to **demolition, dewatering, ELS, pouring concrete against adjoining walls, and structural works** (para. 3); see APP-21, APP-22, APP-26, APP-57 and the Demolition Code, and ADV-4 on environmental nuisance (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 提醒須提供**保護及預防措施**，以保障毗鄰建築物及公眾（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須特別注意**拆卸、降低地下水位、挖掘及側向承托、靠鄰牆澆混凝土及結構工程**（第 3 段）；參考 APP-21、APP-22、APP-26、APP-57 及拆卸守則，環境滋擾見 ADV-4（第 4–5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: all construction sites: reminder to provide **protective and precautionary measures** for adjoining buildings and the public. 所有地盤的預防措施。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Construction can damage neighbours and cause nuisance; provide protective measures. 須保護鄰近物業及公眾。 |
| 3 | Watch especially: (a) **demolition**; (b) **dewatering**; (c) **lateral support** for excavation; (d) **pouring concrete against adjoining walls**; (e) structural works. 重點工程。 |
| 4 | Guidance in old PNAPs 71, 74, 81, 148, 224 and the Demolition Code (now [APP-21](app-21.md), [APP-22](app-22.md), [APP-26](app-26.md), [APP-57](app-57.md), etc.). 參考作業備考。 |
| 5 | Also old PNAP 144 on **environmental nuisance**. 環境滋擾。 |
