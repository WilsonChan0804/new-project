## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Reminder to provide **protective and precautionary measures** to protect adjoining buildings and the public (paras. 1–2).
    - Pay attention to **demolition, dewatering, ELS, pouring concrete against adjoining walls, and structural works** (para. 3); see APP-21, APP-22, APP-26, APP-57 and the Demolition Code, and ADV-4 on environmental nuisance (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 提醒須提供**保護及預防措施**，以保障毗鄰建築物及公眾（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須特別注意**拆卸、降低地下水位、挖掘及側向承托、靠鄰牆澆混凝土及結構工程**（第 3 段）；參考 APP-21、APP-22、APP-26、APP-57 及拆卸守則，環境滋擾見 ADV-4（第 4–5 段）。
