## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BD staff are on **24-hour call** for emergencies on sites; reaching the AP, RSE or RGE quickly can avoid road closures or the cost of a Government contractor (paras. 1–2).
    - Give BD your **phone, fax and mobile numbers** (Appendix A letter to the Registration Unit) and keep them updated; used only for emergencies (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 屋宇署人員**24 小時候命**處理地盤緊急事故；如能及時聯絡認可人士、註冊結構工程師或註冊岩土工程師，可避免封路或由政府承建商處理而向業主收費（第 1–2 段）。
    - 須向屋宇署提供**電話、傳真及手提電話號碼**（以附錄 A 信件寄註冊組），並及時更新；只作緊急用途（第 3–4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: every AP, RSE and RGE responsible for building works in progress. Give BD your emergency contact numbers and keep them updated. 每位負責工程的認可人士、結構及岩土工程師。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | BD staff are on **24-hour call** for emergencies, some on sites with works in progress | 24 小時候命 |
| 2 | Contacting the AP / RSE / RGE in time avoids delay (e.g. clearing roads after collapsed **scaffolding or hoarding**) and use of the **Government Contractor at the owner's expense** | 及時聯絡 |
| 3 | AP / RSE / RGE have a **duty** to ensure no danger to the public; give BD telephone, fax, mobile numbers and report changes with the **Appendix A** letter | 提供及更新號碼 |
| 4 | Numbers used by BD staff **only for emergencies** | 只供緊急用途 |
| App. A | Letter to the Registration Unit (fax 2625 0437): telephone, mobile, fax, signature, registration no.; personal data statement (Rev. 4/2019) | 通知信 |
