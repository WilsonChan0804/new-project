## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Gives **design guidelines** (Appendix A) for car parks, loading/unloading bays, access roads, driveways and ramps. They are based on **HKPSG Chapter 8, Table 11** and Transport Department requirements.
    - Three levels of standard: **desirable**, **minimum**, and **absolute minimum**. The absolute minimum is accepted only in **exceptional circumstances with full justification** and swept-path evidence (para. 3).
    - The BA checks **stall and bay dimensions, run-ins/outs, gradients, curve radii, swept paths and turning circles** (para. 4).
    - A car park must be **properly designed** before it can be disregarded from GFA under [APP-2](app-2.md) (para. 5). Deviations are not by themselves a reason to disapprove the plans.
    - Key numbers: private car stall **5 × 2.5 m**, headroom **2.4 m**; 90° aisle **6 m** desirable (abs. min. 5.0 m); straight ramp for cars **1:10** desirable, **1:7** max., **1:6** absolute max.; vertical clearance over driveways **5.1 m**.

=== "中文"

    - 提供停車場、上落客貨位、通路、車道及斜路的**設計指引**（附錄 A），以 **《香港規劃標準與準則》第 8 章表 11** 及運輸署規定為基礎。
    - 三級標準：**理想**、**最低**及**絕對最低**；絕對最低標準只會在**特殊情況並有充分理據**及車輛迴轉範圍證明下才接納（第 3 段）。
    - 建築事務監督會審核**車位及上落客貨位尺寸、進出口、坡度、彎道半徑、車輛迴轉範圍及迴轉圓環**（第 4 段）。
    - 停車場須**設計妥當**，才可按 [APP-2](app-2.md) 不計入總樓面面積（第 5 段）；未符合標準本身不會構成拒批圖則的理由。
    - 主要數字：私家車位 **5 × 2.5 米**，淨高 **2.4 米**；90° 車道理想闊 **6 米**（絕對最低 5.0 米）；私家車直斜路理想 **1:10**、最陡 **1:7**、絕對最陡 **1:6**；車道上的垂直淨空 **5.1 米**。

!!! tip "Applies / does not apply 適用／不適用"
    **Applies**: car parks, loading and unloading bays, access roads, driveways and ramps shown on building plans. The Appendix A figures are **guidelines**: use the desirable standard, the minimum where constrained, or a justified alternative with **swept paths** (para. 3). 適用於圖則上的停車場、上落貨區、通路、車道及斜道；附錄 A 為指引。
    **Separately controlled**: **private streets and access roads** ([B(P)SAR Cap. 123G](../regulations/bpsar-123g.md)); **run-ins and run-outs** on public roads ([APP-144](app-144.md), Transport Department); **parking numbers** (HKPSG, lease); **accessible spaces** ([DMUA 2026](../codes/dmua-2026.md)). 私家街道、出入口、車位數目及無障礙車位另有規定。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Car park plans may also go to the **Director of Lands** and the **Commissioner for Transport (C for T)**. 亦須送地政總署及運輸署。 |
| 2 | Car parks and ancillary works must give an **acceptable standard of convenience and safety**. 便利及安全。 |
| 3 | Follow **Appendix A**; where constrained use the **minimum** standard or a justified alternative shown by **swept paths**; **absolute minimum** only in exceptional cases with full justification. 三級標準。 |
| 4 | BA checks (a) **stall, bay and run-in/out dimensions**; (b) **gradients, ramp radii, swept paths, turning circles**; (c) general notes on standards used; flexible where safety is kept; highlight and justify deviations. 審核項目。 |
| 5 | To disregard car parks from GFA under **B(P)R 23(3)(b)** ([APP-2](app-2.md)), the BA must be satisfied with the **design**; deviations are **not** a fundamental ground for disapproval but must be fixed in later submissions. 豁免總樓面面積。 |
| 6 | **C for T** advises on road safety, traffic management and run-ins/outs; the **BA** checks car park standards. 運輸署及屋宇署分工。 |

## Key clause 主要條文 {#key-clause}

!!! quote "Para. 3 — Desirable, minimum and absolute minimum standards 理想、最低及絕對最低標準"
    To assist Authorized Persons (APs) in meeting the above objective, a set of general guidelines is given at Appendix A. In case where full compliance with the desirable standard is difficult due to physical constraints or other relevant considerations, the APs may use the minimum standard shown in Appendix A or may propose alternative acceptable standards. In such circumstances, APs are advised to demonstrate with swept paths and other design data that safety of and convenience to users of the car parks will not be compromised. Absolute minimum standard will only be accepted under exceptional circumstances and with full justifications.

    **官方中文本：** 為協助認可人士達到上述目標，附錄A載述了一般指引。如果由於實際限制或其他相關考慮因素而難以完全符合理想標準的要求，認可人士可以採用附錄A的最低標準，或提出其他可接受的標準。在此情況下，建議認可人士利用車輛迴轉範圍及其他設計數據，證明停車場使用者的安全及便利將不會受到損害。只有在特殊情況，並有充分理據支持下，才會接納絕對最低標準。

## Design guidelines 設計指引 (Appendix A) {#design-guidelines}

=== "English"

    **Car parks and loading bays** (Appendix A para. 1):

    1. **Separate** run-ins for cycles and motor vehicles where possible; cycle parks at G/F near the entrance, grouped and segregated.
    2. G/F shops, workshops and offices should reach the **loading areas** easily, without disturbing traffic inside or outside.
    3. Good **visibility** so vehicles leaving a ramp do not conflict with cars reversing into or out of spaces.
    4. Goods vehicles load from the **rear**: perpendicular bays with about **one vehicle length** clear in front for reversing; **hammerhead** layout recommended; show **swept paths**; a **turntable** where severely constrained.
    5. Park in **one manoeuvre** without reversing the steering lock.
    6. Traffic **signs and road markings**.
    7. **Swept path analysis** wherever needed.
    8. **Motorcycle** spaces grouped, away from the run-in.
    9. In non-commercial buildings, **bicycle** parks near the residential lobby, fully segregated.
    10. **Accessible** spaces to the BFA / DMUA standard (**3.5 m** wide).

    **Access roads** (para. 2): follow [Cap. 123G](../regulations/bpsar-123g.md); the **5.1 m** clearance may be lowered where the road serves only certain vehicles.

    **Ramps in multi-storey car parks** (para. 3): two-way ramps **not recommended**; **no conflict points** at either end (no crossings or junctions); clear visibility at the top; car standards adjusted for other vehicles.

=== "中文"

    **停車場及上落貨位**（附錄 A 第 1 段）：

    1. 單車及汽車出入口盡量**分開**；單車位設於地面近入口，集中並分隔。
    2. 地面商舖、工場及辦公室應方便到達**上落貨區**，不影響內外交通。
    3. 確保**視線**良好，避免駛離斜道的車輛與倒車入位或離開的車輛衝突。
    4. 貨車由**車尾**上落貨：垂直貨位前預留約**一個車身長度**倒車；建議**錘頭式**佈局；圖則須顯示**行車軌跡**；嚴重受限時設**轉盤**。
    5. 一次駛入車位，毋須反向扭軑。
    6. 設**交通標誌及路面標記**。
    7. 需要時進行**行車軌跡分析**。
    8. **電單車**位集中並遠離出入口。
    9. 非商業建築物的**單車**位設於住宅大堂附近並完全分隔。
    10. **無障礙**車位按無障礙手冊（闊 **3.5 米**）。

    **通路**（第 2 段）：按[第 123G 章](../regulations/bpsar-123g.md)；只供某類車輛使用時 **5.1 米**淨空可降低。

    **多層停車場斜道**（第 3 段）：**不建議**雙程斜道；斜道兩端**不設衝突點**（無行人過路處或路口）；頂端視線清晰；其他車輛須調整標準。

## Explanation 淺白解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    APP-111 is the **geometry handbook** for car parks. The BA checks that vehicles can actually park and move **safely**, mostly by looking at dimensions and **swept paths**. Transport Department still comments on traffic matters, especially run-ins and run-outs (para. 6).

    Design to the **desirable** standard where possible. Use the **minimum** only with swept-path justification, and the **absolute minimum** only in exceptional cases. Deviations will not normally cause plans to be disapproved, but they **must be fixed** in later submissions. A poorly designed car park may also **not be disregarded from GFA**.

    General design tips from Appendix A: park in **one manoeuvre** without reversing the steering lock; reserve **one vehicle length** in front of loading bays for reversing (a **hammerhead** layout is recommended); keep bicycles and motorcycles **grouped and segregated**; design accessible parking to the **BFA Manual**.

=== "中文"

    APP-111 是停車場的**幾何設計手冊**。建築事務監督主要透過尺寸及**車輛迴轉範圍**核對車輛能否**安全**停泊及行駛；運輸署仍會就交通事宜（特別是進出口通道）提供意見（第 6 段）。

    應盡量採用**理想**標準；只有在提供迴轉範圍理據時才用**最低**標準，**絕對最低**標準只限特殊情況。未符合標準一般不會導致拒批圖則，但**必須在其後的呈交中改正**；設計欠佳的停車場亦可能**不獲豁免總樓面面積**。

    附錄 A 的一般設計要點：泊車時應可**一次過**完成而毋須反向扭軚；上落貨位前預留**一個車身長度**作倒車（建議採用**錘頭式**佈局）；單車及電單車位應**集中並分隔**；殘疾人士車位按 **BFA 手冊**設計。

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! example "Worked example: ramp length"
        Level difference between car park floors **3.5 m**, straight ramp for private cars.

        - At **1:10** (desirable): horizontal length = 3.5 × 10 = **35 m**
        - At **1:7** (maximum): 3.5 × 7 = **24.5 m**
        - At **1:6** (absolute max.): 3.5 × 6 = 21 m, only with full justification.

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Using **absolute minimum** aisle widths (5.0 m) without swept-path justification.
        - Forgetting **headroom** for goods vehicles (light goods **3.6 m**, heavy **4.7 m**) along the whole route, not only at the bay.
        - Missing **widening on bends** for narrow driveways.
        - Accessible spaces at **3.5 m** wide. Check the BFA Manual for numbers and location.

=== "中文"

    !!! example "計算例子：斜路長度"
        停車場樓層高低差 **3.5 米**，私家車直斜路。

        - 以 **1:10**（理想）：水平長度 = 3.5 × 10 = **35 米**
        - 以 **1:7**（最陡）：3.5 × 7 = **24.5 米**
        - 以 **1:6**（絕對最陡）：3.5 × 6 = 21 米，須有充分理據。

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 沒有迴轉範圍理據便採用**絕對最低**車道闊度（5.0 米）。
        - 忘記貨車沿整條路線的**淨高**（輕型貨車 **3.6 米**、重型 **4.7 米**），而不只是車位處。
        - 狹窄車道遺漏**彎道加闊**。
        - 殘疾人士車位闊 **3.5 米**，數目及位置按 BFA 手冊核對。
