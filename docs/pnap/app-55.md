## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Fees under **B(A)R 42** are payable on first submissions; **resubmissions are free** (para. 1).
    - **New buildings**: fee on **GFA**, covering all associated site formation, structural and drainage plans. **No accountable GFA** (transformer stations, petrol stations, oil depots, jetties) and **A&A**: fee per **plan sheet (A1 or smaller)** (paras. 2–3).
    - Pay by **crossed cheque** to the HKSAR Government with **Form BD24**; no cash or post-dated cheques; **non-refundable** on withdrawal; discrepancies settled by supplementary fee or refund (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - **B(A)R 第 42 條**的費用在首次呈交時繳付；**再呈交免費**（第 1 段）。
    - **新建築物**：按**總樓面面積**收費，已包括相關地盤平整、結構及排水圖則；**沒有須計算樓面面積**的建築物（變電站、油站、貯油庫、碼頭）及**改動加建**：按**圖則張數（A1 或以下）**收費（第 2–3 段）。
    - 以**劃線支票**付予香港特別行政區政府，連同**表格 BD24**；不收現金或期票；撤回後**不退款**；如有差異會補收或退還（第 4–5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **plan fees** under **B(A)R 42** for **first submissions and major revisions**; **resubmissions are free**. Not charged for proposals responding to **statutory orders / notices** or safety advisory letters (para. 12); exemptions for non-profit community buildings by **BA16** (para. 7). 呈交圖則費用。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fees per **B(A)R 42** on submission (regs. 29, 33); **resubmissions free**. 再呈交免費。 |
| 2 | New buildings (permanent or temporary): first submission fee covers **all associated plans** (SF, structural, drainage), charged on **GFA**. 新建築物按總樓面面積。 |
| 3 | No accountable GFA (transformer stations, petrol stations, oil installations, jetties) and **A&A**: charged per **plan (A1 or smaller)**, all categories. 按圖則張數。 |
| 4 | Pay on application by **crossed cheque** to "The Government of the HKSAR" (no cash or post-dated); AP calculates on **Form BD 24**; **non-refundable** even if withdrawn. 劃線支票。 |
| 5 | Supplementary fee or refund if miscalculated. 補交或退款。 |
| 6 | No fee or dishonoured cheque: disapproval under **s.16(1)(f)**; supplementary fees within **14 days**. 未付費可拒批。 |
| 7 | **Exemption** for non-profit schools, hospitals, community buildings via **BA16**; no cheque needed. 豁免。 |
| 8 | Receipt at the counter or by post; keep it for refunds. 收據。 |
| 9 | **Major revision** (item 7A): needs fundamental reassessment and repeat circulation ([ADM-2](adm-2.md)), e.g. (a) block layout or number; (b) number of storeys, podium, basements; (c) floor plans changing PR, SC, lighting, MoE; (d) **change of principal use** (office ↔ residential, hotel → office); (e) site area / shape; (f) access; (g) substantial modifications or bonuses; (h) wrong ground assumptions for SF works; (i) review under new law or a new OZP. 重大修訂例子。 |
| 10 | In doubt ask the **CBS / NB1** (answer in **7 days**); a new **BA5** with every major revision. 查詢及 BA5。 |
| 11 | **Localised** major revision: fee on the affected GFA (e.g. per podium or affected floors). 局部重大修訂。 |
| 12 | No fee for proposals under **statutory orders / notices** or advisory letters on building, fire or slope safety. 法定命令免費。 |
| 13 | **ESH**: pay by **FPS** or cheque with the acknowledgement receipt within **14 days**. 電子呈交付款。 |

## Fee table (Form BD 24) 收費表 {#fee-table}

| Category 類別 | Rate per 100 m² (GFA ÷ 100, rounded up) | Minimum charge |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial, GFA **≤ 20,000 m²** | **$2,740** | $10,400 |
| Industrial, GFA **> 20,000 m²** | **$2,200** | $547,100 |
| Non-industrial, GFA **≤ 10,000 m²** | **$4,340** | $10,350 |
| Non-industrial, GFA **> 10,000 m²** | **$3,480** | $434,400 |
| No GFA / A&A: per plan **≤ A1** (larger = multiples of A1) | **$14,200** per plan | — |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A **non-industrial** building of **15,000 m²** GFA: 15,000 ÷ 100 = 150 × **$3,480** = **$522,000**, above the $434,400 minimum, so **$522,000**. An A&A submission of **6 A1 plans**: 6 × $14,200 = **$85,200**. A resubmission after disapproval: **free**. 收費例子。
