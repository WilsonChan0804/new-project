## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Fees under **B(A)R 42** are payable on first submissions; **resubmissions are free** (para. 1).
    - **New buildings**: fee on **GFA**, covering all associated site formation, structural and drainage plans. **No accountable GFA** (transformer stations, petrol stations, oil depots, jetties) and **A&A**: fee per **plan sheet (A1 or smaller)** (paras. 2–3).
    - Pay by **crossed cheque** to the HKSAR Government with **Form BD24**; no cash or post-dated cheques; **non-refundable** on withdrawal; discrepancies settled by supplementary fee or refund (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - **B(A)R 第 42 條**的費用在首次呈交時繳付；**再呈交免費**（第 1 段）。
    - **新建築物**：按**總樓面面積**收費，已包括相關地盤平整、結構及排水圖則；**沒有須計算樓面面積**的建築物（變電站、油站、貯油庫、碼頭）及**改動加建**：按**圖則張數（A1 或以下）**收費（第 2–3 段）。
    - 以**劃線支票**付予香港特別行政區政府，連同**表格 BD24**；不收現金或期票；撤回後**不退款**；如有差異會補收或退還（第 4–5 段）。
