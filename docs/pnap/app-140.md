## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The **Buildings (Amendment) Ordinance 2004** created the **RGE register**; RGEs become statutorily responsible for geotechnical works instead of the AP (paras. 1–2).
    - Basic qualification: RPE (geotechnical). Transitionally, an AP (engineer list) or RSE could apply under **s.53I** within 12 months of **31 Dec 2004** (by **30 Dec 2005**) with **7 years** of relevant site formation experience (para. 3). This route has now closed.

=== "中文"

    - **2004 年《建築物（修訂）條例》**設立**岩土工程師名冊**，由註冊岩土工程師取代認可人士承擔岩土工程的法定責任（第 1–2 段）。
    - 基本資格為註冊專業工程師（岩土）；過渡期內，認可人士（工程師名單）或註冊結構工程師可在 **2004 年 12 月 31 日**後 12 個月內（**2005 年 12 月 30 日**前）根據**第 53I 條**申請，須具 **7 年**相關地盤平整經驗（第 3 段）；此途徑已結束。

!!! warning "Historic transitional arrangement 歷史過渡安排"
    The window for APs and RSEs to apply under **BO s.53I** closed on **30 Dec 2005**. Today registration as an **RGE** is through the normal route (RPE (Geotechnical) and registration committee interview). 申請期已於 2005 年 12 月 30 日屆滿。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hillside developments need geotechnical expertise; previously the **AP** was statutorily responsible, with geotechnical sub-consultants. 背景。 |
| 2 | **Buildings (Amendment) Ordinance 2004** created the **RGE register**; the RGE investigates, designs, supervises and is **statutorily responsible** for geotechnical works. 設立註冊岩土工程師。 |
| 3 | Basic qualification: **RPE (Geotechnical)**. From **31 Dec 2004**, APs (engineers list) and RSEs could apply within **12 months** (by **30 Dec 2005**) under **s.53I** if, in the past **7 years**: (a) involved in site formation completed under the BO; (b) were the appointed AP / RSE; (c) have suitable experience and competence. 過渡申請條件。 |
| 4 | Competence shown by, within 7 years: (a) full involvement (plans, approval, supervision, completion) in **5 Category I** or **2 Category II** projects, or **3 Cat. I + 1 Cat. II** ([APP-28](app-28.md) categories), not by sub-consultants or claimed by others; (b) certified (AP) or reported completion (RSE); (c) no contravention record. 經驗要求。 |
| 5 | BA may ask for more documents; registration valid **5 years**, renewable while on the AP / RSE register. 有效期 5 年。 |
| 6 | Read with old PNAP 43. 相關作業備考。 |
