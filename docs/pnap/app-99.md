## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Reg. 19 requires WC cisterns to discharge **9–14 L**, but BA and WSD accept **lower volumes** if compatible with the bowl for **single-flush clearance** and meeting WSD requirements (paras. 1–2).
    - With **mains (waterworks) flushing water**, reg. 91 disapplies regs 19–23, so **no modification is needed**; comply with the Waterworks Ordinance. With **other flushing water**, BA will grant a **modification of reg. 19** on the same conditions (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 第 19 條規定水廁水箱沖水量為 **9–14 公升**，但如沖水量與廁盆配合，可**一次沖清**並符合水務署要求，建築事務監督及水務署接受**較低沖水量**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 使用**水務設施沖廁水**時，第 91 條豁免第 19–23 條，**毋須變通**，但須符合《水務設施條例》；使用**其他沖廁水**時，建築事務監督會按相同條件**變通第 19 條**（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: WC **flushing cisterns** discharging **less than 9 litres** (Drainage Regulations reg. 19 requires **9–14 L**). Flushed with **water from the waterworks**: no modification needed (reg. 91); with **other water** (e.g. sea water): apply for **modification of reg. 19**. 低流量沖廁水箱。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Reg. 19**: cisterns discharge **9–14 litres**. 9 至 14 公升。 |
| 2 | BA and Water Authority accept **lower volumes** if compatible with the bowl for **effective single-flush** clearance and the apparatus meets **WSD** requirements. 可接受較低水量。 |
| 3 | **Reg. 91**: regs. 19–23 do not apply to fitments flushed from the **waterworks**, so no modification is needed; comply with the **Waterworks Ordinance** and WSD circulars. For **non-waterworks** flushing water, BA will grant a **modification of reg. 19** if para. 2 is met. 水務供水毋須變通。 |
