## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Licensing authorities refer plans of **child care centres, kindergartens and restaurants** to BD for comments on **structural safety and means of escape** (para. 1).
    - **Above G/F in non-purpose-built buildings**: acceptable in composite buildings with ≤ 2 commercial floors above G/F, or where the centre occupies whole commercial floors with ≤ 2 commercial floors above G/F, and in institutional buildings, if premises meet the **MOE Code** and the **height limits**: child care centres **12 m / 24 m** (Child Care Centres Regulation 19), kindergartens **24 m** (Education Regulation 7) (para. 2).
    - In high-rise domestic, wholly commercial or other composite buildings: also **two independent exits** for the exclusive use of the centre; in **single staircase** buildings: **two extra staircases** and the common stair sealed off; single-family houses ≤ 3 storeys and NTEHs are **unconditional** (para. 2).
    - Otherwise the **change of use** (BO s.25) will be prohibited. **Not in industrial buildings** (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 發牌當局會把**幼兒中心、幼稚園及食肆**的圖則轉交屋宇署，就**結構安全及走火通道**提供意見（第 1 段）。
    - **在非專用建築物地面層以上**：如屬地面以上不多於 2 層商業樓層的綜合用途建築物，或中心佔用整層商業樓層而地面以上不多於 2 層仍屬商業，以及機構建築物，須符合**走火通道守則**及**高度限制**：幼兒中心 **12／24 米**（《幼兒中心規例》第 19 條）、幼稚園 **24 米**（《教育規例》第 7 條）（第 2 段）。
    - 在高層住用、全商業或其他綜合用途建築物：另須設**兩個獨立出口**供中心專用；**單樓梯**建築物：須另設**兩條樓梯**並把公用樓梯隔開；不多於 3 層的單一家庭住宅及新界豁免屋宇**沒有條件**（第 2 段）。
    - 不符合者，**更改用途**（第 25 條）會被禁止；**不得設於工業大廈**（第 3 段）。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Kindergartens proposed above **24 m**.
        - Relying on the common stair in a single-staircase building.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 幼稚園設於 **24 米**以上。
        - 在單樓梯建築物依賴公用樓梯。
