## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Priority for new building submissions may be granted to (para. 1): urgently needed **public utility / institutional** buildings and **Housing Society** projects; **HKSTP** land; **URA** projects; projects likely to **significantly boost the economy** (with department support).
    - Other high public interest projects only exceptionally (para. 2). Apply **with the first submission**; A&A projects normally not, except **anti-pollution** works (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 新建築物圖則可獲優先處理的項目（第 1 段）：急需的**公用設施／機構**建築物及**房屋協會**項目；**香港科技園公司**土地；**市區重建局**項目；可**顯著促進經濟**的項目（須有部門支持）。
    - 其他高度公眾利益項目只在特殊情況下考慮（第 2 段）；須**隨首次呈交**申請；改動加建項目一般不獲批，但**防治污染**工程除外（第 3 段）。
