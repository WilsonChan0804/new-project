## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Priority for new building submissions may be granted to (para. 1): urgently needed **public utility / institutional** buildings and **Housing Society** projects; **HKSTP** land; **URA** projects; projects likely to **significantly boost the economy** (with department support).
    - Other high public interest projects only exceptionally (para. 2). Apply **with the first submission**; A&A projects normally not, except **anti-pollution** works (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 新建築物圖則可獲優先處理的項目（第 1 段）：急需的**公用設施／機構**建築物及**房屋協會**項目；**香港科技園公司**土地；**市區重建局**項目；可**顯著促進經濟**的項目（須有部門支持）。
    - 其他高度公眾利益項目只在特殊情況下考慮（第 2 段）；須**隨首次呈交**申請；改動加建項目一般不獲批，但**防治污染**工程除外（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **new building** submissions in the listed categories, applying with the **first submission**. A&A works normally **not**, except anti-pollution projects. Priority covers BD matters only, not land. 新建築物首次呈交時申請。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1(a) | (i) urgently required **public utility and institutional** buildings; (ii) **HK Housing Society** projects | 公用事業及房協 |
| 1(b) | Projects on land of the **HK Science and Technology Parks Corporation** | 科技園 |
| 1(c) | Projects for the **URA** by a wholly owned subsidiary or joint venture | 市建局 |
| 1(d) | Projects likely to contribute **significantly to economic growth** | 經濟增長 |
| 2 | (a)(i) covers schools, substations and similar if urgently needed and in the public interest; (d) needs **positive support** from departments; other high-interest projects only in exceptional cases after consultation | 準則說明 |
| 3 | Apply **with the first submission**; A&A normally not granted, but **anti-pollution** projects (air, noise, water, effluent) may be | 申請時間 |
| 4 | Kept under **review**; withdrawn if the project does not proceed expeditiously | 可撤回 |
| 5 | Priority applies to **BD matters only**, not land transactions | 只限屋宇署 |
