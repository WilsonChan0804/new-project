## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Guidance on planning, design and installation of **greening** in buildings (para. 1).
    - Whether greening is **building works** depends on fixation, permanence, use, size, associated works and ease of removal; e.g. **fixed planter boxes are building works**, but laying soil and planting are not (para. 2).
    - Some greening installations are **minor works** (APP-147) (para. 3); later paras. cover structural loads, drainage, fire safety and maintenance.

=== "中文"

    - 就樓宇**綠化**的規劃、設計及安裝提供指引（第 1 段）。
    - 綠化是否屬**建築工程**視乎固定程度、永久性、用途、大小、相關工程及拆除難易；例如**固定花槽屬建築工程**，鋪泥及種植則不是（第 2 段）。
    - 部分綠化工程屬**小型工程**（APP-147）（第 3 段）；其後各段涵蓋結構荷載、排水、消防及維修。
