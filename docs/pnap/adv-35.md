## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Is it building works?** It depends on fixation, permanence, use, size, associated works and ease of removal, considered together. **Fixed planter boxes are building works**; laying soil and planting in them are not (para. 2).
    - **Approval route**: no new building works means **no submission**, but the AP/RSE must still check structure, drainage, fire safety and change in use (para. 4). New works or **strengthening** need an **A&A submission** (para. 5). Some items can go through **minor works** instead ([APP-147](app-147.md)) (para. 3).
    - **Loads**: soil, waterproofing, drainage layers and plants are **dead load**, taken **fully saturated**; add irrigation, construction load **and** the normal **imposed load** for the use (paras. 6–7).
    - **Drainage**: surface fall **not shallower than 1:80**; existing drains may need upgrading to avoid ponding and overloading; sound waterproofing **before** greening (paras. 9–11).
    - **Fire and safety**: greenery must not reduce or block **refuge** areas (para. 12); **protective barriers** to B(C)R and safe **maintenance access** (para. 13); owners told how to inspect and maintain (para. 14).

=== "中文"

    - **是否屬建築工程？** 須綜合考慮固定程度、永久性、用途、大小、相關工程及拆除難易。**固定花槽屬建築工程**；在花槽內鋪泥及種植則不屬（第 2 段）。
    - **審批途徑**：不涉及新建築工程**毋須呈交**，但認可人士／註冊結構工程師仍須檢查結構、排水、消防及用途改變（第 4 段）；涉及新工程或**加固**須呈交**改動及加建**圖則（第 5 段）；部分項目可按**小型工程**進行（[APP-147](app-147.md)）（第 3 段）。
    - **荷載**：泥土、防水層、排水層及植物屬**恒載**，須以**完全飽和**重量計算；另加灌溉系統、施工荷載**及**該用途的一般**外加荷載**（第 6–7 段）。
    - **排水**：表面斜度**不可淺於 1:80**；現有排水系統或須改善以免積水及超載；鋪設綠化**前**須確保防水良好（第 9–11 段）。
    - **消防及安全**：綠化不得減少或阻塞**庇護**範圍（第 12 段）；按規例設**防護欄障**及安全**維修通道**（第 13 段）；須告知業主如何檢查及保養（第 14 段）。

!!! tip "Applies / does not apply 適用／不適用"
    **Applies**: green roofs, podium and sky gardens, planters, vertical greening, water ponds, in **new and existing** buildings. 適用於新舊建築物的綠化天台、平台、空中花園、花槽、垂直綠化及水池。
    **Does not decide**: whether greenery gets **site coverage or GFA relief** (see [JPN 3](jpn-3.md), [APP-152](app-152.md) site coverage of greenery), or the **minor works** item limits ([TG](../regulations/mw-technical-guidelines.md)). 是否獲上蓋面積或總樓面面積寬免另見 JPN 3 及 APP-152。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Scope: considerations for AP/RSE in **planning, designing and installing** greening. 範圍。 |
| 2 | Building works or not: judged on **fixation, permanence, nature and use, size, associated works and facilities, ease of assembly and removal**, in totality. Fixed planter boxes = building works; soil and plants = not. 是否屬建築工程的考慮因素。 |
| 3 | Some greening works are **minor works** (B(MW)R Schedule 1) under the simplified requirements of [APP-147](app-147.md). 小型工程。 |
| 4 | **No new building works → no plan submission**, but check structural capacity, drainage, fire safety and **change in use**. 毋須呈交但仍須檢查。 |
| 5 | New building works, including **strengthening**, → submit **A&A** proposals for approval. 須呈交改動加建。 |
| 6 | Structural: consider every layer (**drainage, substrate, growing medium, plants**), **fully saturated** weight, **irrigation** and **construction** loads. Higher risk: **inaccessible roofs, long-span roofs/podiums, water ponds, cantilever slabs**. 結構考慮及高風險情況。 |
| 7 | Per the [Loading Code](../codes/dil-2011.md): greening weight is **dead load**; **imposed load is added on top**; where people may congregate use that use's minimum imposed load. 恒載加外加荷載。 |
| 8 | Existing buildings: check **approved records and actual site conditions** against the greening load (from green carpets to deep ponds); follow [APP-117](app-117.md). 現有建築物結構評估。 |
| 9 | Drainage designed for **heavy rain**; surface fall **≥ 1:80**; drainage to suit the chosen greening system. 排水斜度。 |
| 10 | Existing drains may be **inadequate**: provide extra drainage to avoid **ponding and overloading**. 加設排水。 |
| 11 | Ensure **sound waterproofing first**; inspect and maintain drains; fix leaks and blockages at once. 防水及保養。 |
| 12 | Greening must not affect **means of escape**; **refuge** areas not reduced or obstructed. 不得影響逃生及庇護層。 |
| 13 | **Protective barriers** (balustrades, railings) to B(C)R; access and safety provisions for **maintenance**. 欄障及維修通道。 |
| 14 | Advise owners on **safe use, regular inspection and maintenance** of structure and drainage. 告知業主保養。 |

## Explanation 解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    The note is a checklist rather than a set of numbers: the only fixed figure is the **1:80 fall**. The real design work is the **load**: a saturated growing medium weighs roughly **16–20 kN/m³** (check the supplier's data), so 300 mm of soil alone is about **5–6 kPa**, more than a typical office imposed load. Because the greening is **dead load**, the roof must also carry its normal imposed load (2.0 kPa for an inaccessible flat roof, or the use load where people gather).

    For **existing buildings**, start from the approved structural plans: an old roof designed for 2.0 kPa imposed load often cannot take deep planting without strengthening, and strengthening turns it into an **A&A submission** (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    本作業備考以檢查清單為主，唯一固定數字是 **1:80 斜度**。設計重點在於**荷載**：飽和的種植土約重**每立方米 16–20 千牛**（須參考供應商資料），單是 300 毫米泥土已約 **5–6 千帕**，比一般辦公室外加荷載還大。由於綠化屬**恒載**，天台仍須另外承受正常外加荷載（不可到達平天台 2.0 千帕，或人羣聚集用途的荷載）。

    **現有建築物**須先查核核准結構圖：舊天台若只按 2.0 千帕設計，種植較深的綠化往往須加固，而加固即須**呈交改動加建圖則**（第 5 段）。

## Worked example 例子 {#worked-example}

A roof garden on an existing office building, open to staff 現有辦公樓的員工天台花園：

| Item 項目 | Load 荷載 |
|---|---|
| Growing medium 300 mm × 18 kN/m³ (saturated) 種植土 | 5.4 kPa (dead) |
| Drainage and filter layers 排水及過濾層 | 0.3 kPa (dead) |
| Plants and paving allowance 植物及鋪面 | 0.5 kPa (dead) |
| **Imposed load**: people may congregate → assembly without fixed seats 外加荷載 | **5.0 kPa** ([DIL Table 3.2](../codes/dil-2011.md)) |
| **Total characteristic** 合計 | **≈ 11.2 kPa** |

If the roof was approved for 2.0 kPa imposed load with light finishes, the extra load needs strengthening → **A&A submission**. Provide a 1:80 fall to outlets, overflow, 1.1 m barriers and a maintenance route. 如天台原設計只承受 2.0 千帕，須加固並呈交改動加建；並須設 1:80 斜度、溢流口、1.1 米欄障及維修通道。

<small>Densities and layer weights are typical values for illustration; use the supplier's saturated data. 數值只供說明，須採用供應商的飽和重量。</small>

## Common pitfalls 常見錯誤 {#common-pitfalls}

- Using **dry** soil weight instead of **saturated** weight (para. 6). 以乾泥重量計算。
- Treating the greening as the imposed load and **not adding** the use load (para. 7). 把綠化當作外加荷載而沒有另加使用荷載。
- Placing planters on a **refuge floor** in a way that reduces the refuge area (para. 12). 花槽減少庇護層範圍。
- Relying on existing rainwater outlets that are sized for a bare roof (para. 10); no overflow. 沿用原有雨水口、沒有溢流。
- Forgetting **maintenance access** and anchors at planter edges ([External Maintenance Code](../codes/external-maintenance-2021.md)). 忽略維修通道。
