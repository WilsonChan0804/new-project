## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BA keeps the **AP, SE, GE and Inspectors registers** (BO s.3). Entry needs the **prescribed qualifications** plus a **Registration Committee recommendation**; an AP or RSE with the required experience may join the RI register without one (para. 2).
    - **AP**: Registered Architect, RPE (structural / civil) or Registered Professional Surveyor. **RSE**: RPE (structural, or civil with structural experience). **RGE**: RPE (geotechnical). Plus relevant post-qualification **local experience** (B(A)R 3) (para. 3).
    - Registration needs the **prescribed qualification** plus the **Registration Committee's recommendation** (BO s.3(7)) (para. 2). AP: RA, RPE (structural / civil) or RPS; RSE: RPE (structural, or civil with structural experience); RGE: RPE (geotechnical); plus **1 continuous year of Hong Kong experience in the last 3 years** (para. 3). Apply on **Form BA 1**; registration lasts **5 years**; renew on **BA 1A** between **4 months and 28 days** before expiry; restore on **BA 1B** within **2 years** (paras. 8–11).

=== "中文"

    - 建築事務監督備存**認可人士、結構工程師、岩土工程師及檢驗人員名冊**（第 3 條）；列入名冊須具**訂明資格**並獲**註冊事務委員會推薦**；具所需經驗的認可人士或註冊結構工程師可毋須推薦而列入檢驗人員名冊（第 2 段）。
    - **認可人士**：註冊建築師、註冊專業工程師（結構／土木）或註冊專業測量師；**註冊結構工程師**：註冊專業工程師（結構，或具結構經驗的土木）；**註冊岩土工程師**：註冊專業工程師（岩土）；另須具相關**本地經驗**（B(A)R 第 3 條）（第 3 段）。
    - 註冊須具**訂明資格**並獲**註冊事務委員會推薦**（第 3(7) 條）（第 2 段）。認可人士：註冊建築師、註冊專業工程師（結構／土木）或註冊專業測量師；結構工程師：結構（或具結構經驗的土木）註冊專業工程師；岩土工程師：岩土註冊專業工程師；另須在**過去 3 年內有連續 1 年香港經驗**（第 3 段）。以 **BA 1** 申請；註冊有效 **5 年**；須於屆滿前 **4 個月至 28 天**內以 **BA 1A** 續期；除名後 **2 年**內可以 **BA 1B** 申請恢復（第 8–11 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | BA keeps the **AP, SE, GE and Inspectors' registers** (BO s.3). 四個名冊。 |
| 2 | Inclusion needs the prescribed qualifications **and** the Registration Committee's recommendation (s.3(7)); some RI cases without IRC recommendation (s.3(7AA)). 資格及推薦。 |
| 3 | AP: **RA, RPE (structural/civil) or RPS**; RSE: **RPE structural** (or civil with approved structural experience); RGE: **RPE geotechnical**; plus **1 continuous year in the 3 years** before applying of relevant Hong Kong experience. 認可人士等資格。 |
| 4 | RI: an AP, RSE, RA, RPE (building, structural, civil, building services, materials) or RPS (building surveying, QS), with repair and maintenance experience in Hong Kong (e.g. AP/RSE within **7 years**; others **1 year in 3**). 註冊檢驗人員資格。 |
| 5–6 | Four panels for **APRC, SERC, GERC and IRC**; committees examine and interview and recommend. 註冊事務委員會。 |
| 7 | Scope of **professional interviews** in Appendix A. 面試範圍。 |
| 8 | Apply on **Form BA 1** with the fee and advisory notes. 申請表格。 |
| 9 | Registration valid **5 years**. 有效期 5 年。 |
| 10 | Renew on **Form BA 1A** **not earlier than 4 months and not later than 28 days** before expiry; late applications not accepted. 續期時限。 |
| 11 | Removed names may be **restored within 2 years** of expiry on **Form BA 1B**. 恢復註冊。 |
