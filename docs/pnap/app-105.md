## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Seepage arises from defective fabric or water-borne pipes; hard to fix because the affected (lower) flat is not the cause (paras. 1–2).
    - New buildings must meet **B(C)R 3** (suitable materials), **27** (impervious external walls), **32–33** (damp protection, watertight wet-room floors), **34** (weatherproof roofs) (para. 3).
    - Pay attention to roofs, external walls, curtain walls, windows, balconies, UPs, bathrooms, kitchens, pipes, car park floors and basements; specify waterproofing, supervise and **test** (para. 5).
    - **No water-borne pipes embedded in structural elements** (columns, slabs, walls, beams, transfer plates, caps, footings) except as App. A allows; state this on structural and drainage plans or justify, otherwise disapproval under s.16(1)(i) (paras. 6–7). Genuine **pipe ducts** (APP-93) may be GFA-exempt (para. 8).
    - **Enhanced guidelines (App. B)** apply to new or major-revision GBP submitted **after 31 Mar 2025** (paras. 9–10).

=== "中文"

    - 滲水源於建築物料損壞或輸水喉管；受影響的（下層）單位並非肇事者，因此難以解決（第 1–2 段）。
    - 新建築物須符合 **B(C)R 第 3 條**（合適物料）、**第 27 條**（不透水外牆）、**第 32–33 條**（防潮、濕區樓面防水）及**第 34 條**（屋頂防風雨）（第 3 段）。
    - 注意屋頂、外牆、幕牆、窗、露台、工作平台、浴室、廚房、喉管、停車場樓面及地庫；須訂明防水措施、加強監督並作**測試**（第 5 段）。
    - 除附錄 A 准許者外，**輸水喉管不得埋於結構構件**（柱、樓板、牆、樑、轉換層、樁帽、基腳）；須在結構及排水圖則註明，或提出理據，否則根據第 16(1)(i) 條拒批（第 6–7 段）；真正的**管道槽**（APP-93）可獲豁免樓面面積（第 8 段）。
    - **加強指引（附錄 B）**適用於 **2025 年 3 月 31 日後**呈交的新一般建築圖則或重大修訂（第 9–10 段）。

## Paragraph by paragraph and appendices (full) 逐段及附錄詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

| Para. 段 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Seepage in existing buildings is mainly a **management and maintenance** matter for owners; hard to resolve because the affected (lower) owner is not the cause and leak paths are hard to trace. |
| 3 | B(C)R performance requirements: **s.3** suitable materials and workmanship; **s.27** external walls impervious; **s.32–33** walls in contact with damp and floors of rooms with water supply must prevent penetration; **s.34** roofs weatherproof ([B(C)R](../regulations/bcr-123q.md)). |
| 4–5 | Special care with materials, design, workmanship and supervision for roofs, external walls, curtain walls, windows, balconies, utility platforms, bathrooms, kitchens, pipes, car parks and basements; specify waterproofing and **compliance tests**. |
| 6–7 | **No embedded water-borne pipes** in structural elements except as in **Appendix A**; state this explicitly on **structural and drainage plans**; otherwise submit routing and justification — omission leads to **disapproval** (BO s.16(1)(i); B(C)R s.14(4)(a)). |
| 8 | Genuine **pipe ducts** per [APP-93](app-93.md) may be excluded from GFA. |
| 9–10 | **Appendix B** replaces the **2005** guidelines; applies to new GBP / major revisions and **A&A** submitted **after 31 Mar 2025**. |
| 11 | A similar note issued to registered contractors. |

### Appendix A – embedment of water-borne pipes 水管嵌入結構

| Element 構件 | Allowed only if 只在以下情況容許 |
|---|---|
| General 一般 | **Not** permitted in columns, slabs, structural walls, beams, transfer plates, pile caps, footings — except piercing below, accessible for maintenance, with **sleeves** preferred |
| **Slab / transfer plate** (vertical pipe) 樓板 | Hole **≤ 150 mm** dia. or the minimum bar spacing (lesser); no main bar severed; trimming bars ≥ main bar size |
| **Beam** (horizontal pipe) 樑 | Hole **≤ 150 mm** or **⅓ beam depth** (lesser), at the **neutral axis**; trimming bars **≥ T16** vertical and horizontal each side; no shear links severed |
| **Structural wall** (horizontal pipe) 結構牆 | Hole **≤ 150 mm** or min. vertical bar spacing (lesser); trimming bars ≥ vertical bar size both sides |

### Appendix B – Guidelines on Prevention of Water Seepage in New Buildings (Sep 2024) 預防新建樓宇滲水指引（2024）

Deemed-to-comply provisions for the B(C)R performance requirements; equivalent alternatives acceptable.

| Area 位置 | Key requirements 主要要求 | Para. |
|---|---|---|
| **Roof** (incl. any top-most slab exposed to weather) 天台 | Durable waterproofing over the **entire roof**, turned up **≥ 300 mm** above finished level; **continuous angle fillets**; protective finishes on accessible roofs (avoid tile expansion damage); plant on dampers, membrane to plinth first; waterproof enclosures for E&M, avoid embedded conduits; falls and outlets; **overflows** to drains; **water flood test** | 2–4 |
| Balcony and utility platform 露台及工作平台 | Treated as **roof** (para. 3 criteria) | 5 |
| External wall / precast façade 外牆 | Impervious (B(C)R 27); thickness, details, rendering; junctions between buildings; precast: **slotted joint with water bar and monolithic kicker**; concreting and tie-hole grouting; finishes keyed with waterproof additives and movement joints; tests after finishes ([ADV-31](adv-31.md)); **full-scale mock-up water penetration test** and on-site tests for precast façades ([Precast Code](../codes/precast-2016.md) §4.3) | 6–13 |
| Curtain wall 幕牆 | Collect and discharge seepage / condensation (B(C)R 29(3)); **mock-up and on-site water penetration tests** ([Glass Code](../codes/glass-2018.md) Annex A) | 14–15 |
| Window (incl. window / glass walls) 窗 | Hood with fall and **drip**; trimming bars at opening corners; frame joints, gaskets, sealant, waterproof grout; avoid oversized openings (metal formwork); **non-shrink waterproof grout**; supervision of lugs / anchors; **on-site water test** of windows and joints | 16–19 |
| **Bathroom and toilet** 浴室及洗手間 | Waterproofing over the **entire floor**, up every wall **≥ 300 mm**; round bathtub / shower: **≥ 2 200 mm** high; behind basin / sink: **≥ 1 100 mm** or **300 mm above the fixture** (higher); angle fillets; under-tray for non-proprietary shower trays; minimise wall / floor installations; floor drains placed for short falls and access; access under bathtubs; galvanised reinforcement advisable; sealant round fixtures, protective cement plaster, careful tiling; **water flood test**; sample drawings Annex 2 | 20–22 |
| **Sunken slabs** 下沉樓板 | Cast **monolithically** with the floor (no construction joint); waterproof the **entire sunken trough** plus floor and walls, preferably in one homogeneous layer; seal access panel edges; **access points / inspection chambers** with sealed removable covers; prefabrication advisable (see APSEC 2023–24 on cleaning eyes) | 20(g) |
| **Kitchen (incl. open kitchen)** 廚房 | Waterproofing over the **entire floor incl. open kitchen area**, up walls **≥ 300 mm**; behind sink / countertop **≥ 1 100 mm** or **300 mm above the countertop**; angle fillets; monolithic countertop and splashback, sealed joints; sunken slab rules apply; flood test for enclosed kitchens | 23–24 |
| Plumbing and drainage pipes 水管及排水管 | Durable pressure-rated pipes; **independent flushing valves per flat** advisable; avoid excessive bends / joints; no embedment except App. A; brackets, pressure-reducing valves, expansion collars, insulation; **rust-free sleeves cast in**, sealed and fire-stopped; membrane dressed **≥ 20 mm** up the pipe at the platform and **≥ 50 mm** into the floor outlet, **100 mm** around the pipe, overlapped; **licensed plumbers**; pressure test | 25–29 |
| Car park floor 停車場樓面 | Crack control (low w/c, low permeability, waterproof cement, joints); wheel loads as dynamic; waterproof **car-wash areas** with falls and channels; falls, no ponding; **intercepting channels** at top and bottom of ramps; water-stops at movement joints (not at low points); proprietary coating advisable | 30 |
| Basement 地庫 | Suitable waterproofing; design as **water-retaining structure** (except pile caps, thick footings, diaphragm walls) with diversion; construction sequence; water stops at joints and temporary supports; **cavity wall with channels**; hardcore / filter; external tanking maintenance-free, **water test before backfilling** | 31–33 |
| **Supervision and testing** 監督及測試 | Quality supervision by AP / RSE and TCPs and the RC's AS and TCPs; formwork, reinforcement, spacers, compaction, curing and waterproofing closely supervised; manufacturer's specs; **water flood tests**; **infra-red scanning** helpful | 34–36 |
| **Smart technology** 智能科技 | Leak sensors on plumbing / drainage; AI defect detection (CCTV, drones), cable robots in ducts; access points; GFA modification for genuine inspectable **pipe ducts** ([APP-2](app-2.md)) | 37–39 |

**Annex 1 – reinforced concrete roofs** (not for roof slabs ≥ 1 m thick) 鋼筋混凝土天台

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| Design 設計 | Span / depth per [Concrete Code](../codes/concrete-2013.md) s.7; loads incl. finishes, parapets, waterproofing, maintenance and **ponding**; cover for **exposure condition 2** or higher; **waterproof concrete ≥ C35**, same grade for slabs and beams; hot-dip galvanised bars advisable |
| Construction 施工 | Cast **monolithically**, avoid construction joints; bar spacers; placing / compaction / curing / formwork removal per Concrete Code s.10; supervision to the [Site Supervision Code](../codes/site-supervision-2009.md) Table 9.1; **water ponding test** |
| Pipes 管道 | Avoid embedding pipes, esp. in slabs |
| Roof beams 天台樑 | Cantilever top bars anchored (cl. 9.4.3) with stirrups; exposure 2 |
| **Roof slabs** 天台板 | Min. **150 mm** thick; high-yield bars **both faces, both directions**, **≥ T10 @ ≤ 150 mm**, **≥ 0.25 %**; cantilevers per [APP-68](app-68.md); crack-control bar table (e.g. 150 mm slab, 4 m span, SDL 3 kPa, LL 5 kPa → T12-125) |
