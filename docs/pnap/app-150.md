## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Supports the Government's **revitalisation** of vacant or under-used industrial buildings by **wholesale conversion** (LandsD practice notes for lease matters) (para. 1).
    - BD takes a **pragmatic approach** to modifications for A&A conversions, and **green / energy-efficient features** support the case (paras. 2–3):
        - **Refuge floor** difficult to provide: refuge floor with **greenery** and enhanced FSI favourably considered (FSD no objection; APP-122);
        - **Office conversion** short of natural light and ventilation: modification of **B(P)R 30–31** with artificial lighting, mechanical ventilation and **BEAM Plus 40%** in **EU and IEQ** (APP-130);
        - **Larger A/C boxes / platforms** or projections over streets with efficient A/C units (APP-19);
        - **New curtain walls** projecting over streets (BO s.31(1)) with low-energy glazing and BEAM Plus 40% EU / IEQ (APP-2).
    - **PPE** in converted non-domestic buildings: FS Code **B19.2 and B20.9** (para. 4).
    - **Data centres** etc.: redundant car parking spaces (excluded from GFA under B(P)R 23(3)(b)) may be converted to **transformer rooms / essential plant rooms** by modification of B(P)R 23(3)(a), with an **undertaking to reinstate** the car parks if the use ceases (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 配合政府**活化**空置或未盡其用工業大廈的**整幢改裝**政策（地契事宜見地政總署作業備考）（第 1 段）。
    - 屋宇署對改裝的改動加建工程採取**務實態度**處理變通申請，**環保／節能設施**有助支持申請（第 2–3 段）：
        - 難以提供**庇護層**：設**綠化**及加強消防裝置的庇護層會獲正面考慮（消防處無異議；APP-122）；
        - **改作辦公室**而天然照明及通風不足：如有人工照明、機械通風並在 **BEAM Plus「能源使用」及「室內環境質素」達 40%**，可變通 **B(P)R 第 30–31 條**（APP-130）；
        - 使用節能冷氣機的**較大冷氣機箱／平台**或伸出街道（APP-19）；
        - 伸出街道的**新幕牆**（《建築物條例》第 31(1) 條），須採用低能耗玻璃並達 BEAM Plus 40%（APP-2）。
    - 改裝後非住用建築物的**公眾娛樂場所**：見消防安全守則 **B19.2 及 B20.9**（第 4 段）。
    - **數據中心**等：原本根據 B(P)R 第 23(3)(b) 條不計總樓面面積而變得多餘的停車位，可透過變通第 23(3)(a) 條改作**變壓房／必要機房**，須承諾在停止使用時**還原**停車位（第 5 段）。

## Key table: favourable modifications 會正面考慮的變通

| Difficulty 困難 | Modification 變通 | Condition 條件 | Ref. 參考 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refuge floor 庇護層 | FS Code refuge floor requirements | Greenery design + enhanced FSI; no FSD objection 綠化及加強消防 | APP-122 |
| Natural light / ventilation (office) 照明通風 | B(P)R 30, 31 | Artificial light, mechanical ventilation, **BEAM Plus 40% EU + IEQ** 達 40% | APP-130 |
| A/C boxes / platforms 冷氣機平台 | Size / projection over street 尺寸及伸出 | Energy-efficient A/C units 節能冷氣機 | APP-19 |
| Curtain wall over street 幕牆伸出街道 | BO s.31(1) | Low-energy glazing, BEAM Plus 40% EU + IEQ 低能耗玻璃 | APP-2 |
| Plant rooms for data centres 數據中心機房 | B(P)R 23(3)(a) | Redundant car parks; owner and operator **undertaking to reinstate** 承諾還原 | APP-151 App. A |

## Key clause 主要條文

!!! quote "Para. 3(ii) — Office conversion 改作辦公室"
    In the case of conversion to office use, if there are difficulties in providing the required natural lighting and ventilation due to constraints posed by the original design as industrial building, application for modification of Regulations 30 and 31 of the Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R) will be favourably considered subject to adequate artificial lighting and mechanical ventilation and energy efficient design that could achieve 40% in the categories of Energy Use (EU) and Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ) under the BEAM Plus Certification conferred by the Hong Kong Green Building Council being incorporated in the proposal.

    **官方中文本：** 對於改裝作辦公室用途的方案，如因工業大廈原有設計所限而難以提供所需的天然照明和通風，若方案具備足夠的人工照明和機械通風以及具能源效益設計，並能在香港綠色建築議會授予的"BEAM Plus"認證中就"能源使用"和"室內環境質素"類別達至40%水平，當局會正面考慮要求就《建築物（規劃）規例》第30和31條給予變通的申請。
