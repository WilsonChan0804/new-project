## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BD stores approved plans electronically (Building Record Management System); prepare plans so **images stay clear** (paras. 1–3).
    - Follow **App. A drawing standards**; non-conformity is **not a ground for disapproval** but adoption is appreciated (paras. 4–5). "Plans" covers building, drainage, site formation and structural plans and calculations (para. 6).

=== "中文"

    - 屋宇署以電子方式儲存核准圖則（建築紀錄管理系統）；擬備圖則時須確保**影像清晰**（第 1–3 段）。
    - 採用**附錄 A 繪圖標準**；不符合**不構成拒批理由**，但希望採用（第 4–5 段）；「圖則」包括建築、排水、地盤平整及結構圖則與計算（第 6 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: building, drainage, site formation and structural plans and ADM-8 calculations submitted for approval. Recommended practice: **not a ground for disapproval**. 建議做法，不構成不批准理由。

### Appendix A drawing standards 附錄 A 繪圖標準 {#app-a-standards}

| Item 項目 | Standard 標準 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| (1) Sizes | A0 1189×841, A1 841×594, A2 594×420, A3 420×297, A4 297×210 mm; avoid larger than A0 | 圖紙尺寸 |
| (2) Borders | A0, A1 **20 mm**; A2–A4 **10 mm** unused border | 邊界 |
| (3)(a)–(e) | Centre marks; matt surface, good contrast; **unique drawing number**; revisions **(Rev.A), (Rev.B)**; clear title with location and type of works | 一般標準 |
| (3)(f)–(j) | Notes read one direction; scale; **legend** (right side); colours do not image, so also **hatch or annotate** (rc, br, ms); no heavy hatching over text | 一般標準 |
| (4) | Unused space **90 mm wide × 150 mm high** at the **lower right** for BA stamps | 官方蓋印位置 |
| (5) | **Plan Index** with the OP application, A size, one per category (building, structural, drainage); **asterisk** marks the latest approved plans representing the completed building | 圖則索引 |
