## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Bridges over streets / lanes on or supported by a **private lot** need **exemption under BO s.31(1)** (paras. 1–2).
    - Exemption may be given if the bridge is (a) required by **lease, planning condition** or consistent with OZP / ODP / layout plans; or **functionally necessary** linking developments; or of **public benefit** (pedestrian segregation, links to transport, public footbridges); or other special circumstances, acceptable to LandsD, PlanD, TD and HyD (para. 2(a)).
    - For **pedestrian or vehicular links only**; commercial activity generally not allowed, and **GFA-accountable** under the OZP if permitted; width and storeys justified by flows; **visual and air ventilation** studies may be needed for bridges > **1 storey** or > **6 m** wide (para. 2(b)–(c)).
    - Aesthetics acceptable to **ACABAS** (Advisory Committee on the Appearance of Bridges and Associated Structures): submit **10 sets** of A3 proposals per ETWB TC(W) 36/2004 (paras. 2(d), 3–4).
    - Free-standing bridges **wholly on Government land** are outside the BO (HyD); settle **maintenance responsibility** with HyD first (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 在**私人地段**上或由地段支承、橫跨街道／巷的天橋，須取得**《建築物條例》第 31(1) 條豁免**（第 1–2 段）。
    - 如天橋：(a) 為**地契、規劃條件**所規定或符合分區計劃大綱圖等；或連接兩邊發展屬**功能上必需**；或具**公眾利益**（人車分隔、連接交通設施或公共天橋網絡）；或有其他特殊情況，並獲地政總署、規劃署、運輸署及路政署接受，可獲豁免（第 2(a) 段）。
    - 只作**行人或車輛通道**；一般不准商業活動，如獲准須按大綱圖**計入總樓面面積**；闊度及層數須以人流支持；多於 **1 層**或闊逾 **6 米**的天橋或須進行**視覺及空氣流通**評估（第 2(b)–(c) 段）。
    - 外觀須獲**橋樑及有關建築物外觀諮詢委員會**接受：按 ETWB TC(W) 36/2004 呈交 **10 套** A3 建議（第 2(d)、3–4 段）。
    - **完全位於政府土地**的獨立天橋不受《建築物條例》管制（由路政署負責）；施工前須與路政署議定**保養責任**（第 5–6 段）。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Shops or kiosks on a link bridge without planning permission and GFA counted.
        - Late ACABAS submission delaying the s.31(1) exemption.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 天橋上設商店或攤位而沒有規劃許可及計入總樓面面積。
        - 太遲呈交外觀委員會，延誤第 31(1) 條豁免。
