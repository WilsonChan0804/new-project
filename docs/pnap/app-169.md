## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - For **drilled-in anchors** and **grouted bolts, dowels or rebars**, BA may impose s.17(1) item 6 conditions for **strength tests** on a representative number, with a method statement (para. 1).
    - Pre-accepted method statements: **App. A** anchors for cantilevers, hangers and curtain wall remedial works; **App. B** anchors for other works; **App. C** grouted bolts / dowels / rebars or T-bolts in cast-in channels for curtain walls and cladding (para. 1).
    - Quote the relevant appendix on the plans and no method statement is needed; alternatives need BD agreement (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 就**鑽入式錨栓**及**灌漿螺栓、銷釘或鋼筋**，建築事務監督可根據第 17(1) 條第 6 項施加條件，要求對具代表性數量進行**強度測試**並提交施工方法說明（第 1 段）。
    - 預先接受的方法說明：**附錄 A** 用於懸臂構築物、吊架及幕牆修葺的錨栓；**附錄 B** 其他工程的錨栓；**附錄 C** 幕牆及覆蓋層的灌漿螺栓／銷釘／鋼筋或預埋槽 T 形螺栓（第 1 段）。
    - 在圖則註明所採用的附錄即毋須另交方法說明；其他方法須取得屋宇署同意（第 2 段）。

## Appendices A–C in full 附錄 A–C 詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, first issue October 2023. Para. 2: if the RSE **quotes Appendix A, B or C** on the plans, no separate method statement is needed. Any other method must be agreed by BD. 已按 2023 年 10 月版本核對。

### Comparison of the three pre-accepted methods 三套預先接納方法比較

| Item 項目 | **App. A** Drilled-in anchors: cantilevers, hangers, curtain wall remedial 懸臂／吊架／幕牆修葺 | **App. B** Drilled-in anchors: other works 其他工程 | **App. C** Grouted bolts / dowels / rebars, or T-bolts in cast-in channels (curtain wall / cladding) 灌漿螺栓／T 型螺栓 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sampling 抽樣 | **≥ 5% or 5 nos.**, whichever is more, per type and size | **≥ 1% or 5 nos.** per type and size | **≥ 1% or 5 nos.** per type, size and embedment (or per assembly type and size) |
| Test 測試 | Tension and/or shear proof load, as appropriate | Same | Same |
| Test load 測試荷載 | **≥ 1.5 ×** manufacturer's recommended load | Same | Same |
| Tension frame supports 拉力架支座 | Equal distance, **≥ 8A** from the axis | ≥ 8A | ≥ 8A, or **≥ 2h<sub>ef</sub>** for channel assemblies |
| Displacement gauge 位移量度 | On the base material, independent of the frame, **≥ 12A** (tension) / **≥ 6A** (shear), accuracy **0.02 mm** | Not required | Not required |
| Loading 加載 | Continuous at **9–11 N/(mm²·s)**, or **≥ 10 equal increments**, with load and displacement readings | Constant rate | Constant rate |
| Hold 持荷 | **≥ 1 hour** at maximum test load; read at start and end to check creep and relaxation | **≥ 2 minutes** | **≥ 2 minutes** |
| Unloading 卸載 | **≥ 5 decrements** (incremental), or a constant rate (continuous); record recovered displacement | Constant rate | Constant rate |
| Acceptance 合格準則 | No separation, plastic deformation or harmful effect, **and** recovery **≥ 80%** of total deformation at maximum load | No separation, plastic deformation or harmful effect | Same as B |
| Results 結果 | **Load–displacement graph for each test**, plus a description of behaviour | Description of behaviour | Description of behaviour (also failure of rebar / anchor legs on the channel) |

A = hole diameter of the anchor (App. C: maximum bar diameter), or ¼ of the embedded length, whichever is greater. h<sub>ef</sub> = effective embedment depth of the cast-in channel. A 為鑽孔直徑或埋入長度的四分之一，取較大者。

### Common details (all three appendices) 共通細節

- **Load measurement** accurate to **2%**. Tension is applied axially and concentrically through a suitable attachment.
- **Shear steel block**:
  - Diameter **5d**, thickness **d + 0.8 mm** (d = outside diameter of the part projecting from the base).
  - Quenched steel, hardness at least **HV700**, edges radiused **0.4 mm** where they touch the fixing.
  - Placed directly on the base material. A PTFE sheet **≤ 2 mm** thick goes between the base and the rig. Shear is applied parallel to the surface.
  - Reaction is **≥ 8A** each side, at right angles to the load (App. C: or 2h<sub>ef</sub>).
- **Clearance hole in the block**:

| Nominal dia. d<sub>nom</sub> (mm) | Hole dia. (mm) |
|---|---|
| 6, 8 | d<sub>nom</sub> + 1 |
| 10–24 | d<sub>nom</sub> + 2 |
| 27, 30 | d<sub>nom</sub> + 3 |

- **Procedure** 程序:
  1. Visually check the fixing and the area around it for defects or cracks.
  2. Apply an initial force of **≤ 1%** of the test load to take up slack. For an App. A/B shear test, hand-tighten first, then tighten to the manufacturer's torque while that force is held, then release it.
  3. After the test, inspect again and record any separation, plastic deformation or harmful effect.
- **Behaviour to report** 須報告的表現:
  - slip in the hole;
  - onset of cracking;
  - rupture of the base: a **cone pull-out** or **splitting** through the plane of the fixing, stated separately;
  - tension or shear fracture of the fixing;
  - deformation of parts, e.g. thread stripping.
- If failure happens before the maximum load or the full hold time, record the failure load or time and the type of failure.
