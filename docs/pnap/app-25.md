## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - On sites with geotechnical risk, the **GBP submission must include a geotechnical assessment (GA)**, so that fundamental constraints are found **early** (para. 1).
    - **Triggers** (new buildings, para. 2(a)): site gradient > **15°**; a slope > **30°** and > **7.5 m** high on or within **7.5 m** of the site; excavation > **10 m** long and > **7.5 m** deep, or below a **45° plane** from a retaining wall toe, or below **HKPD −1 m** on reclaimed land; a retaining wall > **6 m** high on or within **6 m**; or **special geology** (landslides, debris flows, boulders, rockfall).
    - **A&A works** need a GA if they may significantly affect slopes, retaining walls or adjacent foundations, or involve such excavation (para. 2(b)).
    - **Contents** (para. 3): topography, geology, groundwater; effect on and of existing features; feasibility, construction method and sequence; schematic site formation / foundation plans; **ground investigation scope** (GEOGUIDE 2 s.15.3 supervision); planned slope / wall investigations and remedial works.
    - Missing GA where required → GBP may be **rejected** under BO s.16(1)(a) (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 有岩土風險的地盤，**一般建築圖則須附岩土評估**，以便**及早**發現根本性限制（第 1 段）。
    - **觸發條件**（新建築物，第 2(a) 段）：地盤傾斜度 > **15 度**；地盤內或 **7.5 米**範圍內有 > **30 度**及 > **7.5 米**高的斜坡；挖掘長 > **10 米**並深 > **7.5 米**，或低於擋土牆底 **45 度**斜面，或在填海土地低於**主水平基準 −1 米**；地盤內或 **6 米**範圍內有 > **6 米**高擋土牆；或**特殊地質**（山泥傾瀉、泥石流、礫石、墜石）。
    - **改動加建工程**如可能對斜坡、擋土牆或相鄰基礎造成顯著影響，或涉及上述挖掘，亦須提交（第 2(b) 段）。
    - **內容**（第 3 段）：地形、地質、地下水；與現存結構的相互影響；可行性、施工方法及次序；地盤平整／地基示意圖；**土地勘測範圍**（GEOGUIDE 2 第 15.3 節的監督水平）；斜坡／擋土牆勘察及補救工程。
    - 須附而沒有附岩土評估 → 可根據《建築物條例》第 16(1)(a) 條**拒批**（第 4 段）。

## Key table: when a GA is required (para. 2) 何時須提交岩土評估（第 2 段）

| # | Trigger 觸發條件 | Threshold 門檻 |
|---|---|---|
| (a)(i) | Site gradient (boundary to boundary, or any 50 m strip for large sites) 地盤傾斜度 | **> 15°** |
| (a)(ii) | Slope on site or within **7.5 m** 地盤內或 7.5 米內的斜坡 | **> 30°** and **> 7.5 m** high (incl. walls at toe/crest) |
| (a)(iii) | Excavation face **> 10 m** long, and any of: 挖掘面長 > 10 米並 | (1) deeper than **7.5 m**; (2) below a **45°** plane from a retaining wall toe; (3) below **HKPD −1 m** on reclaimed coastal land 深 > 7.5 米；低於 45° 斜面；填海地低於 −1 mPD |
| (a)(iv) | Retaining wall on site, or ≥ 6 m length of it within **6 m** 擋土牆 | Retained height **> 6 m** |
| (a)(v) | Special geology 特殊地質 | Previous landslides, debris flows, threatening boulders, potential rockfall 山泥傾瀉、泥石流、礫石、墜石 |
| (b) | A&A works (not a new building) 改動加建 | Significant impact on slopes, retaining walls, adjacent foundations, or excavation per (a)(iii) 顯著影響或涉及上述挖掘 |

!!! quote "Para. 2(a)(i)–(ii) — Triggers 觸發條件"
    (i) where the maximum gradient across a site from boundary to boundary or, for a large site, across any 50 m long strip, is greater than 15°; (ii) where a slope inclined at more than 30° and greater than 7.5 m high (including the height of any retaining wall at its toe or crest) exists on the site or within 7.5 m of the site…

    **官方中文本：** (i) 橫跨地盤邊界（或對於大的地盤，橫跨任何50米直線長度的範圍）的最大傾斜度超過15度；(ii) 在地盤內或在距地盤邊界7.5米範圍以內有傾斜度超過30度及高度超過7.5米的斜坡存在（包括斜坡底部或斜坡頂部所有擋土牆的高度）……

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    Hong Kong has many steep, slope-affected and reclaimed sites. APP-25 makes sure the **geotechnical feasibility** is thought through **before** the layout is fixed. A building that cannot be founded or excavated safely as drawn is a fundamental problem, and BD will reject the plans rather than find out later.

=== "中文"

    香港有大量陡峭、受斜坡影響及填海地盤。APP-25 確保在定案佈局**之前**已考慮**岩土可行性**；若按圖則無法安全建造地基或挖掘，便屬根本性問題，屋宇署會拒批圖則，而不是日後才發現。
