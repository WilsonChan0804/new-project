## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - B(C)R sets **performance requirements**; **App. A** lists the standards and technical criteria the BA accepts as complying (paras. 1–2).
    - Other versions or standards may be approved if shown to achieve the performance, with full background and local suitability; seek BD **agreement in principle early** (paras. 2–3).

=== "中文"

    - B(C)R 訂明**性能要求**；**附錄 A** 列出建築事務監督接受為符合要求的標準及技術準則（第 1–2 段）。
    - 如能證明達到性能要求，並詳述背景及本地適用性，其他版本或標準亦可獲批；應**及早**向屋宇署取得原則上同意（第 2–3 段）。

^ **BS 8118 note**: BS 8118-1 has been replaced by parts of BS EN 1999, but it is still accepted locally. From **1 January 2017** use a partial load factor of **1.4 for wind** instead of the BS 8118 Table 3.1 value. The exception is amendments to structural plans already approved with the original **1.2** factor. 鋁結構風荷載分項系數 1.4。
