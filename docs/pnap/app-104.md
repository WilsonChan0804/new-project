# APP-104 Exclusion of Floor Areas for Recreational Use<br><small>豁免計算入總樓面面積的康樂設施</small>

## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - **Residents' recreational facilities** (clubhouses) in residential developments may be exempted from GFA by **modification of B(P)R 23(3)(a)** under BO s.42, case by case ([APP-42](app-42.md) para. 32).
    - The total exempted area, **including voids, plant rooms (e.g. pool filtration) and covered courts**, is limited by a **sliding scale** (Table 1): **5%** of domestic GFA for developments up to 25,000 m², falling to **2.5%** above 125,000 m², with minimum allowances.
    - Subject to the APP-151 **pre-requisites and 10% cap**. Covered landscaped and play areas under the tower are outside Table 1 (≤ **5%** of domestic GFA, pre-requisites only).
    - **New from 31 Aug 2025**: at least **one elderly-friendly toilet and elderly fitness equipment** must be provided (para. 5).
    - **Not for** luxury membership clubs or commercial undertakings. A kitchen ≤ **15 m²** is allowed only where the site has no commercial floor space.
    - Must be **common area** in the DMC. The undertaking is registered before OP, and use is for **owners, residents and bona fide visitors only**.

=== "中文"

    - 住宅發展的**住客康樂設施**（會所）可根據《建築物條例》第 42 條**變通 B(P)R 第 23(3)(a) 條**，按個別情況豁免總樓面面積（[APP-42](app-42.md) 第 32 段）。
    - 豁免總面積（**包括中空位置、機房（如泳池過濾機房）及有蓋球場**）受**遞減比例**限制（表 1）：住用總樓面面積 25,000 平方米或以下為 **5%**，超過 125,000 平方米為 **2.5%**，並設最低面積。
    - 須符合 APP-151 **先決條件及 10% 上限**；住宅大廈下的有蓋園景及遊樂場地不受表 1 限制（≤ 住用總樓面面積 **5%**，只須符合先決條件）。
    - **2025 年 8 月 31 日起**：須最少提供**一個長者友善洗手間及長者健體設施**（第 5 段）。
    - **不適用於**豪華會員會所或商業經營；只有在地盤沒有商業樓面時，才可設 ≤ **15 平方米**的廚房。
    - 須在公契中訂為**公用地方**；承諾書須在佔用許可證申請前登記，並只供**業主、住客及其真正訪客**使用。

## Key table: sliding scale (Table 1) 遞減比例（表 1）

| Total domestic GFA 住用總樓面面積 | Max. concession 豁免上限 (% of domestic GFA **or** fixed area, whichever is **greater** 取較大者) |
|---|---|
| ≤ 25,000 m² | **5%** |
| > 25,000 – 50,000 m² | **4.5%** / 1,250 m² |
| > 50,000 – 75,000 m² | **4.0%** / 2,250 m² |
| > 75,000 – 100,000 m² | **3.5%** / 3,000 m² |
| > 100,000 – 125,000 m² | **3.0%** / 3,500 m² |
| > 125,000 m² | **2.5%** / 3,750 m² |

<small>Includes all voids, plant rooms (incl. pool filtration plant), covered courts, etc. serving solely the recreational facilities. 包括只服務康樂設施的中空位置、機房（包括泳池過濾機房）、有蓋球場等。</small>

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "Para. 4 — Justification and limit 理據及上限"
    All applications for exclusion of floor areas for recreational use from GFA calculations must be accompanied by information substantiating the need for the areas with justification on overall size of the facilities, the headroom requirements and the mix of the various facilities as well as how the recreational facilities will be operated and controlled to ensure their exclusive use by the owners and residents including their bona fide visitors. The Building Authority (BA) would not, however, allow the GFA of such facilities including all voids, plant rooms (including filtration plant room for swimming pool), covered courts, etc. serving solely for the recreational facilities to exceed the limitation in a sliding scale as stated in Table 1…

    **中譯（非官方）：** 所有申請豁免康樂設施樓面面積的個案，須附上資料證明需要該等面積，並就設施的整體大小、淨高要求、各類設施的組合，以及如何營運和管制設施以確保只供業主、住客及其真正訪客使用提供理據。然而，建築事務監督不會容許該等設施的總樓面面積（包括只服務康樂設施的所有中空位置、機房（包括泳池過濾機房）、有蓋球場等）超出表 1 的遞減比例上限……

!!! quote "Para. 6 — Not for luxury clubs 不適用於豪華會所"
    This concession does not apply to substantially luxury clubs with restaurants and other facilities obviously meant for an exclusive membership and commercial undertakings rather than for the general benefit of owners and residents.

    **中譯（非官方）：** 本寬免不適用於設有餐廳及其他設施、明顯供專屬會員及商業經營，而非為業主及住客一般利益而設的豪華會所。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    Clubhouses make housing estates better places to live, so the BA exempts them from GFA. But clubhouses can be very large, so the **sliding scale** keeps the exemption proportionate. Larger estates get a lower **percentage**, but never less than a **fixed minimum area**.

    Everything that **only** serves the clubhouse counts towards the limit: pool plant rooms, voids over sports halls, covered courts. The facilities must stay **for residents**. They cannot become a commercial club or restaurant, and the developer must register an undertaking so that future owners are bound.

=== "中文"

    會所能提升屋苑的居住質素，因此建築事務監督會豁免其總樓面面積；但會所可以非常龐大，所以以**遞減比例**確保豁免與規模相稱：屋苑越大，**百分比**越低，但不會少於**固定最低面積**。

    **只服務**會所的一切面積均計入上限：泳池機房、體育館上的中空位置、有蓋球場等。設施必須**供住客使用**，不得變成商業會所或餐廳；發展商須登記承諾書，以約束日後的業主。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! example "Worked example: clubhouse allowance"
        **(a)** Domestic GFA **60,000 m²** → 4.0% × 60,000 = 2,400 m² vs 2,250 m² → max. **2,400 m²**
        **(b)** Domestic GFA **52,000 m²** → 4.0% × 52,000 = 2,080 m² vs 2,250 m² → max. **2,250 m²** (the fixed minimum governs)

        The clubhouse area also counts towards the **APP-151 10% cap**, together with balconies, utility platforms, etc.

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Forgetting to include **voids and pool plant rooms** in the Table 1 total.
        - A **restaurant or commercial café** in the clubhouse is not exempted (para. 7).
        - No **elderly-friendly toilet and fitness equipment** on plans submitted after 31 Aug 2025 (para. 5).
        - OP is held back until the **undertaking and layout plan** are registered and the GFA concession information is shown on the approved plans per PNAP ADM-2 (para. 11).

=== "中文"

    !!! example "計算例子：會所面積"
        **(a)** 住用總樓面面積 **60,000 平方米** → 4.0% × 60,000 = 2,400 平方米，與 2,250 平方米比較 → 上限 **2,400 平方米**
        **(b)** 住用總樓面面積 **52,000 平方米** → 4.0% × 52,000 = 2,080 平方米，與 2,250 平方米比較 → 上限 **2,250 平方米**（以固定最低面積為準）

        會所面積亦與露台、工作平台等一同計入 **APP-151 的 10% 上限**。

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 忘記把**中空位置及泳池機房**計入表 1 總數。
        - 會所內的**餐廳或商業咖啡店**不獲豁免（第 7 段）。
        - 2025 年 8 月 31 日後提交的圖則沒有**長者友善洗手間及健體設施**（第 5 段）。
        - 須待**承諾書及佈局圖**登記，並按 PNAP ADM-2 在核准圖則上顯示寬免資料後，才會發出佔用許可證（第 11 段）。
