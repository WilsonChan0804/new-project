## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The **Concrete Code 2013 (2020 Edition)** includes the 2017 and 2020 amendments; later amendments: **App. A (Feb 2022)**, **App. B (Jun 2023)**, **App. C (Apr 2024)** (para. 1).
    - Feb 2022 items include **plain concrete linings** (cl. 6.2.3), use of **100 / 150 mm cubes** (Table 10.2), and early strength by the **maturity method** (cl. 11.7.5.4, ASTM C1074) (App. A).

=== "中文"

    - **2013 年混凝土結構作業守則（2020 年版）**已納入 2017 及 2020 年修訂；其後修訂：**附錄 A（2022 年 2 月）**、**附錄 B（2023 年 6 月）**、**附錄 C（2024 年 4 月）**（第 1 段）。
    - 2022 年 2 月修訂包括**素混凝土襯砌**（第 6.2.3 條）、**100／150 毫米立方體**的使用（表 10.2），以及以**成熟度法**監測早期強度（第 11.7.5.4 條、ASTM C1074）（附錄 A）。

## Amendments in full 修訂詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision April 2024 (Appendix C added). The **2020 Edition** (Dec 2020) includes the amendments of 13 June 2017 and 24 November 2020. Later amendments (from BD's Technical Committee) are in Appendices A–C below. See also the [Concrete Code page](../codes/concrete-2013.md). 已按 2024 年 4 月版本核對。

### Appendix A: February 2022 二〇二二年二月

| Clause 條文 | Amendment 修訂 |
|---|---|
| **6.2.3, Fig. 6.18b** (new) | **Plain concrete linings** (tunnels, caverns). Use where: (a) curvature suits axial distribution of loads; (b) the lining is in relatively good rock and **always in compression**; (c) imperfections are considered by rigorous analysis; (d) a plain arch may join an RC invert if the junction meets 6.2.3.2. Capacity from the interaction curve: e<sub>x</sub> ≤ 0.1h: **n<sub>LT</sub> ≤ 0.32 h f<sub>cu</sub>** (6.63a); 0.1h < e<sub>x</sub> ≤ 0.3h: **n<sub>LT</sub> ≤ 0.4 (h − 2e<sub>x</sub>) f<sub>cu</sub>** (6.63b); beyond **0.3h** a straight line to zero (cracking limit). Shear per 6.1.2.5(k) and 6.2.2.3(r). |
| **10.3.4.2(a)** | Test **100 mm cubes**, or **150 mm cubes if maximum aggregate > 20 mm**, at 28 days. At least one sample per grade per day. |
| **Table 10.2** | Re-tabulated with 100 mm first (150 mm in brackets) — see the table below. |
| **10.3.4.2(b)(i)–(iii)** | Switching between C1 and C2 uses an SD of **5.5 MPa (100 mm)** / **5 MPa (150 mm)** for 40 results. C2 may be used before 40 results if earlier data show SD below that. Switch C2 → C1 (or C1 → C2) on the **35th day** after the last pair of cubes in the set of 40. |
| 10.3.4.2(b)(iv) | Grades ≤ C60: SD exceeds **8.5 MPa (100 mm)** / **8 MPa (150 mm)**. |
| 10.3.4.2(b)(vi) | Average of the latest 40 results exceeds grade strength by **12 MPa (100 mm)** / **10 MPa (150 mm)**, and every result by **5 MPa** / **4 MPa**. |
| **11.7.5.4, Table 11.2** (new) | **Maturity method** for early in-situ strength (temperature–time history, up to **14 days**) to justify **striking formwork and falsework** instead of the 10.3.8.2 minimum periods. **Not** for striking times **< 24 h**. The proposal covers: maturity function and constants; apparatus and calibration; strength–maturity relationship; estimating and validating in-situ strength; re-calibration; QA and supervision. Use the **same mix** as the calibration. Correction factor (Table 11.2): pfa or ggbs mixes **0.7** (≤ 48 h) / **0.8** (> 48 h); other mixes **0.8**. Standard: **ASTM C1074-19ε1** (added to Annex A). |

**Table 10.2: compressive strength compliance criteria** 抗壓強度合格準則

| Grade 等級 | Criterion | Column A: mean of 4 consecutive results exceeds grade by at least 100 mm (150 mm) | Column B: no individual result below grade minus 100 mm (150 mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ C20 | C1 | 7 MPa (5 MPa) | 2 MPa (3 MPa) |
| ≥ C20 | C2 | 5 MPa (3 MPa) | 2 MPa (3 MPa) |
| < C20 | C1 or C2 | 3 MPa (2 MPa) | 2 MPa (2 MPa) |
