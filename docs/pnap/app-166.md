## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Grille: an openwork metal barrier covering an opening. Louvre: sloping blades admitting air or light and excluding rain (para. 1).
    - Types (para. 2): **Type 1** external cladding; **Type 2** forming all or part of an external wall; **Type 3** protective barrier. Interior decorative grilles need no structural submission.
    - Structural submission (para. 3): see table. Plans show framing, anchors, loads, materials, workmanship and calculations; typical details may go in the **superstructure plans** (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 柵檔：覆蓋開口的金屬鏤空屏障；百葉：傾斜葉片，可通風透光並擋雨（第 1 段）。
    - 類別（第 2 段）：**第 1 類**外部覆蓋層；**第 2 類**構成全部或部分外牆；**第 3 類**防護欄障；固定於室內的裝飾柵檔毋須呈交結構圖則。
    - 結構圖則要求（第 3 段）見下表；圖則須顯示框架、錨固、荷載、物料、施工及計算；典型細節可納入**上蓋結構圖則**（第 4–5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **metal grilles and louvres** fixed to or in the exterior of buildings, by function: **Type 1** external cladding, **Type 2** part or whole of an external wall, **Type 3** protective barrier. Not for internal decorative facings (fn. 1). 金屬格柵及百葉。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Definitions: **grille** = grating or openwork barrier; **louvre** = sloping overlapping blades admitting air / light and excluding rain. 定義。 |
| 2 | Types 1, 2, 3 (may combine). 三種功能。 |
| 3 | Structural submission (table below). 結構呈交要求。 |
| 4 | Plans: framing and elements; anchors and supports; loads, standards, codes; materials; workmanship (connections, protection); calcs incl. **parent structure** check, stability, element design, deflection. 圖則內容。 |
| 5 | Streamlined: put **typical details** in the **superstructure plans** instead of separate submission; location and elevations on GBP; ADV-33 App. B7 checklist and B10 samples. 簡化做法。 |
| 6 | Where no submission is needed, AP and RSE still ensure safety. 毋須呈交仍須安全。 |
| 7 | Metal **window security grilles** and wind guards = **designated exempted works** (B(MW)R Sch. 2 Part 2 item 27). 指定豁免工程。 |
| 8 | No specific MW items, but may use cladding (**3.31**), window / window wall (**2.8, 3.6**) or barrier (**1.6**) items ([APP-147](app-147.md)). 小型工程項目。 |

| Type 類型 | Structural submission required if 須呈交結構圖 | Design reference |
|---|---|---|
| **1** Cladding 外牆覆面 | Any part **> 6 m** above adjoining ground or floor | ADV-33 App. B6, B9 |
| **2** External wall 外牆 | Span **> 6 m**, or design wind pressure **≥ 2.86 kPa** with opening **> 6 m²** or least dimension **> 1.8 m** | ADV-33 App. B7, B10 |
| **3** Protective barrier 護欄 | **Always**, unless also Type 2 and below its thresholds | [APP-110](app-110.md) para. 5 |
