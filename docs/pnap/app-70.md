## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Screens, fans and catch platforms are required by law (Construction Sites (Safety) Regs 49(1), B(DW)R 3(2)(a), Summary Offences Ord. s.4B(2)) (para. 1).
    - Full **PVC sheeting** spreads fire (FSD), destabilises **bamboo scaffolds** in wind (Labour), and blocks **light and ventilation** of occupied buildings (paras. 2–3).
    - Use **flame-retardant** sheeting, **strengthen** scaffolds for wind, and avoid blocking L&V of occupied buildings (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 法例規定須設保護幕、防護網及接料平台（《建築地盤（安全）規例》第 49(1) 條、B(DW)R 第 3(2)(a) 條、《簡易程序治罪條例》第 4B(2) 條）（第 1 段）。
    - 全面以 **PVC 膠布**包圍會助長火勢蔓延（消防處）、在強風下影響**竹棚**穩定（勞工處），並阻擋有人居住建築物的**照明及通風**（第 2–3 段）。
    - 須使用**阻燃**膠布、**加固**棚架以抵受強風，並盡量避免阻擋有人居住建築物的照明通風（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **plastic (PVC) sheeting** covering scaffolding around buildings under construction, demolition, repair or renovation. 棚架外圍的膠布。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Screens, fans and catch platforms are required by **Cap. 59I reg. 49(1)**, **B(DW)R reg. 3(2)(a)** and **Summary Offences Ordinance s.4B(2)**. 法定保護設施。 |
| 2 | **FSD**: full PVC sheeting worsens **fire spread**. 加劇火勢蔓延。 |
| 3 | **Labour Department**: poorly fixed sheeting destabilises **bamboo scaffolds** in wind; may block **light and ventilation** of occupied buildings. 影響棚架穩定及通風。 |
| 4 | Use (a) **flame-retardant** sheeting; (b) scaffolds **strengthened for wind**; (c) avoid blocking light and ventilation of occupied buildings. 三項措施。 |
