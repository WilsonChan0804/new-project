## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - A **prescribed registered contractor (PRC)** is appointed for minor works (B(MW)R 28); except RMWC (Individual), each appoints at least one **Authorized Signatory**, and gives **continuous supervision** (BO s.9AA(6)) (para. 1).
    - The AS signing **MW01–07, 10–12** must be responsible for the works; **one AS per submission**, deemed to be the MW01 / MW03 signatory (para. 2).
    - Forms without the AS name are incomplete; mismatches with the supervision plan lead to rejection (para. 3). If the PRC cannot act, **stop the minor works** (para. 5). **Temporary AS** (PNRC 72 Appendix B): notify **≥ 7 days** before (sudden illness: proof **within 3 days**), **14 days** each time, **30 days** in total; the stand-in signs site inspection records only (paras. 6–9). Same approach as [APP-138](app-138.md).

=== "中文"

    - 小型工程須委任**訂明註冊承建商**（B(MW)R 第 28 條）；除註冊小型工程承建商（個人）外，均須委任最少一名**獲授權簽署人**，並提供**持續監督**（條例第 9AA(6) 條）（第 1 段）。
    - 簽署 **MW01–07、10–12** 表格的獲授權簽署人須為負責人；**每份呈交只接受一名**，以簽署 MW01／MW03 者為準（第 2 段）。
    - 沒有獲授權簽署人姓名的表格視為不完整；與監工計劃書不符會被拒絕（第 3 段）。承建商未能行事須**停止小型工程**（第 5 段）。**暫代獲授權簽署人**（PNRC 72 附錄 B）：最少 **7 天前**通知（突然患病須於 **3 天內**提交證明），每次 **14 天**、合共 **30 天**；暫代人只簽署地盤巡查紀錄（第 6–9 段）；做法與 [APP-138](app-138.md) 相同。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | The **prescribed registered contractor (PRC)** appoints an AS and gives continuous supervision (BO s.9AA(6), B(MW)R Part 7). 訂明註冊承建商。 |
| 2 | The AS signing **MW01–07, MW10–12** is responsible; **one AS per submission**, deemed the one signing **MW01** (or the first form). 每份呈交一名。 |
| 3 | AS name on every form; missing = incomplete; supervision plan and MW01 names must match. 須註明姓名。 |
| 4 | Changing the AS: add to registration (PNRC 38 / 69) or notify per PNRC 72 Appendix A. 更換。 |
| 5 | PRC unable to act: **cease the minor works**. 停工。 |
| 6 | Temporary AS: PNRC 72 Appendix B (AP countersigns for Class I); **≥ 7 days** notice or proof in **3 days**; same register; **14 days**, up to **30 days** in total. 暫代條件。 |
| 7–8 | Nominee not eligible: not accepted, works may stop; absence over **30 days**: change the AS. 不符合及超過 30 天。 |
| 9 | Temporary AS signs site inspection records only, not supervision plans or MW forms. 暫代職責。 |
