## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - A **prescribed registered contractor (PRC)** is appointed for minor works (B(MW)R 28); except RMWC (Individual), each appoints at least one **Authorized Signatory**, and gives **continuous supervision** (BO s.9AA(6)) (para. 1).
    - The AS signing **MW01–07, 10–12** must be responsible for the works; **one AS per submission**, deemed to be the MW01 / MW03 signatory (para. 2).
    - Forms without the AS name are incomplete; mismatches with the supervision plan lead to rejection (para. 3). Later paras. cover temporary absence. Same approach as APP-138.

=== "中文"

    - 小型工程須委任**訂明註冊承建商**（B(MW)R 第 28 條）；除註冊小型工程承建商（個人）外，均須委任最少一名**獲授權簽署人**，並提供**持續監督**（條例第 9AA(6) 條）（第 1 段）。
    - 簽署 **MW01–07、10–12** 表格的獲授權簽署人須為負責人；**每份呈交只接受一名**，以簽署 MW01／MW03 者為準（第 2 段）。
    - 沒有獲授權簽署人姓名的表格視為不完整；與監工計劃書不符會被拒絕（第 3 段）；其後各段處理暫時缺勤；做法與 APP-138 相同。
