## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The GEO **Geotechnical Information Unit (GIU)**, in the Civil Engineering Library, Homantin, is the largest collection of site-specific geotechnical data (para. 1).
    - Holds **GI reports**, lab test reports, slope and wall records, **landslip cards**, piezometric and rain gauge data, and Mid-levels Study data (para. 2); retrieval by a **cell referencing system** on 1:5 000 / 1:20 000 sheets; plus the library's literature (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 土力工程處的**岩土工程資料庫**設於何文田土木工程圖書館，是最大的地盤岩土資料庫（第 1 段）。
    - 藏有**土地勘測報告**、化驗報告、斜坡及擋土牆紀錄、**山泥傾瀉紀錄卡**、地下水壓及雨量資料，以及半山區研究資料（第 2 段）；以 1:5 000／1:20 000 地圖上的**方格參照系統**檢索，並可參閱圖書館文獻（第 3–4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: AP, RSE, RGE and consultants looking for existing **site-specific geotechnical data** before ground investigation or design. Note: the note dates from **1995**; check current GEO arrangements. 本備考為 1995 年版本。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | GIU operated by **GEO** since 1983 in the Civil Engineering Library, Civil Engineering Building, Homantin; largest collection of site-specific geotechnical data | 資料庫簡介 |
| 2 | Holds: (a) **GI reports** incl. geophysical, (b) lab test reports, (c) card index of slopes and retaining walls, (d) **landslip records**, (e) **piezometric data**, (f) rain gauge records; Mid-levels Study data 1979–81 | 館藏 |
| 3 | Retrieval by the **GIU Cell Referencing System** on 1:5 000 (urban) and 1:20 000 survey sheets | 檢索方法 |
| 4 | Library also holds textbooks, codes, journals and papers on HK geology; computer catalogue | 參考書籍 |
| 5 | Reference only, not to be taken out; photocopying at nominal charge | 只供參閱 |
| 6 | Plans of registerable slopes and retaining walls (Catalogue of Slopes) with data sheets and photos | 斜坡目錄 |
| 7 | Catalogue information by application form; reply in **10 days** | 申請資料 |
