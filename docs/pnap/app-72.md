## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Blasting is controlled by the **BO** and **Dangerous Goods Ordinance (Cap. 295)**; AP / RSE / RGE ensure no harm to services, slopes, walls and buildings from vibration, and protect workers, traffic and the public (paras. 1–2).
    - GBP-stage geotechnical assessment states whether blasting is needed. A **blasting assessment** by a competent person (**≥ 4 years of experience**), signed by the RGE / RSE, goes with the site formation, ELS or foundation plans (para. 3; contents in App. A).
    - Plans show blasting areas, ground conditions, **sensitive receivers**, control criteria, monitoring, precautions (road closures, evacuation, restricted hours) and reporting (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 爆破受**條例**及**《危險品條例》（第 295 章）**規管；認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師須確保震動不會損害設施、斜坡、擋土牆及建築物，並保護工人、交通及公眾（第 1–2 段）。
    - 一般建築圖則階段的岩土評估須說明是否需要爆破；由合資格人士（**≥ 4 年經驗**）擬備、註冊岩土／結構工程師簽署的**爆破評估**，須連同地盤平整、挖掘及側向承托或基礎圖則呈交（第 3 段；內容見附錄 A）。
    - 圖則須顯示爆破範圍、地質狀況、**易受影響的地方**、控制準則、監測、預防措施（封路、疏散、限制時間）及報告要求（第 3 段）。
