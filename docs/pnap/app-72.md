## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Blasting is controlled by the **BO** and **Dangerous Goods Ordinance (Cap. 295)**; AP / RSE / RGE ensure no harm to services, slopes, walls and buildings from vibration, and protect workers, traffic and the public (paras. 1–2).
    - GBP-stage geotechnical assessment states whether blasting is needed. A **blasting assessment** by a competent person (**≥ 4 years of experience**), signed by the RGE / RSE, goes with the site formation, ELS or foundation plans (para. 3; contents in App. A).
    - Plans show blasting areas, ground conditions, **sensitive receivers**, control criteria, monitoring, precautions (road closures, evacuation, restricted hours) and reporting (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 爆破受**條例**及**《危險品條例》（第 295 章）**規管；認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師須確保震動不會損害設施、斜坡、擋土牆及建築物，並保護工人、交通及公眾（第 1–2 段）。
    - 一般建築圖則階段的岩土評估須說明是否需要爆破；由合資格人士（**≥ 4 年經驗**）擬備、註冊岩土／結構工程師簽署的**爆破評估**，須連同地盤平整、挖掘及側向承托或基礎圖則呈交（第 3 段；內容見附錄 A）。
    - 圖則須顯示爆破範圍、地質狀況、**易受影響的地方**、控制準則、監測、預防措施（封路、疏散、限制時間）及報告要求（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **blasting** for rock excavation in site formation, ELS and foundation works, and exceptionally in **demolition**; under the **BO** and the **Dangerous Goods Ordinance Cap. 295** (blasting permit from the **Commissioner of Mines**). 爆破工程。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Para. 1 | Controlled by **Cap. 123** and **Cap. 295**. 法例。 |
| Para. 2 | No adverse effect on services, slopes, walls, buildings; protective measures against injury, traffic disruption, vibration and nuisance. 須防止損害。 |
| Para. 3 | The **geotechnical assessment** at GBP stage identifies the need for blasting; a **blasting assessment** by a competent person (**≥ 4 years'** experience), signed by the RGE / RSE, with SF / ELS / foundation plans. Plans show: (a) blast areas; (b) geology and groundwater; (c) **sensitive receivers**; (d) control criteria; (e) monitoring; (f) precautions (road closure, evacuation, hours); (g) reporting; (h) a note to read with the assessment. Same if the need arises later. 爆破評估。 |
| Para. 4 | Include the competent person's **CV**; they supervise to verify assumptions. 履歷。 |
| Paras. 5–7 | Qualified supervision (s.17): a **competent supervisor**, independent of the RC, with ≥ 4 years' experience, assists the **TCP T5**; one person may do both if qualified; CVs before blasting; blasting items in the **TCP checklists** (App. B). 監督要求。 |
| Para. 8 | Demolition by explosives only exceptionally, with a blasting assessment and the **Demolition Code**. 拆卸爆破。 |
| Para. 9 | **DG (General) Regulation 46**: RC applies for a **blasting permit**; conditions on flyrock, vibration, air-overpressure, design, limits and monitoring. 爆破許可證。 |
| Para. 10 | Railway guidelines (former PNAPs 77, 279) and the General Specification for Civil Engineering Works. 鐵路指引。 |

## Appendix A: contents of a blasting assessment 附錄 A：爆破評估內容 {#appendix-a}

| Item | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| (a) | Site plans: blast areas and **all sensitive receivers** (streets, structures, foundations, railways, utilities, mains, drains, sewers, gas, slopes, walls, boulders, tunnels, caverns) |
| (b)–(c) | Study of topography, geology, ground and surface water, constraints, site history; **condition survey** of receivers |
| (d)–(e) | Assessment showing no injury or damage; **preventive works** on receivers if needed |
| (f) | **Action limits**: vibration, air-overpressure etc., based on receivers' condition, with sources and stakeholder agreement |
| (g)–(h) | Outline **blast design**; **safety management system** and procedures |
| (i) | Inspections, surveys and **monitoring** stations, criteria and limits |
| (j) | Protective measures: **evacuation**, road / area closure, warnings |
| (k)–(l) | Explosives delivery arrangements; on-site **explosive store** feasibility if needed |

## Appendix B: TCP checklist items 附錄 B：監督人員工作 {#appendix-b}

| TCP T5 (with competent supervisor) | TCP T3 |
|---|---|
| Check the RC's **method statement**; check each **blast design** and precautions against approved plans and permit; verify ground conditions; ensure preventive and protective measures before each blast; monitor receivers before and after; **regular reports** | Inspect preventive works; inspect protective measures before each blast; monitor operations against the permit; inspect receivers before / after; ensure monitoring quality |
