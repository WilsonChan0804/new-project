## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Basic sanitary fitments should normally be installed **before the OP**: deferral causes fit-out waste, disorder and difficulty testing plumbing (paras. 1–3).
    - BA will favourably consider **modifying B(SSF,P,D&L)R** where (para. 3): (a) **restaurants and hotels** whose operators will refit after OP; (b) the developer offers **purchaser-chosen fittings**.
    - Application details the deferred items, with an **undertaking** to install them before actual occupation, to the regulations and WSD requirements, under AP supervision by the RGBC with a licensed plumber, and to notify WSD (form **WW046**) (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 基本衞生設備一般須在**佔用許可證發出前**安裝：延後安裝會產生裝修廢物、造成混亂並難以測試喉管（第 1–3 段）。
    - 以下情況建築事務監督會正面考慮**變通 B(SSF,P,D&L)R**（第 3 段）：(a) 營運者會在佔用許可證發出後重新裝修的**食肆及酒店**；(b) 發展商提供**由買家選擇的設備**。
    - 申請須列明延後安裝的項目，並**承諾**在實際入住前安裝，符合規例及水務署要求，由認可人士監督、註冊一般建築承建商聘用持牌水喉匠安裝，並以表格 **WW046** 通知水務署（第 4 段）。
