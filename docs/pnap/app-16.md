## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Cladding (metal, stone, GRC…) must meet **B(C)R 3 and 28**: materials, fixings, strength, durability, **non-combustibility** (FS Code Part E: E10.1, E12.1 external; E13.1 internal), maintenance access (paras. 1, 3).
    - Permanent flexible **movement joints**; **stainless steel** or corrosion-resistant fixings; for stone, **mortar or epoxy bonding alone is not a permanent fixing** (paras. 2, 4).
    - Show cladding on **GBP**; above **6 m** from adjoining ground or floor, also submit **structural plans** (framing, joints, anchors, loads, materials, workmanship, calculations). Metal cladding typical details may go in the **superstructure plans** (paras. 4–6).
    - A **separate RSE** may be appointed (BA4 / BA5), with an assessment of effect on the parent structure endorsed by the project RSE (paras. 7–9).
    - **Tests**: drilled anchors above 6 m pull-out tested to **≥ 1.5 × recommended tensile load**; stone flexural (ASTM C880 / EN 12372) and anchorage (ASTM C1354) tests, **≥ 5 specimens** each; **limestone** aged tests retain **≥ 80%**; T3 / T1 TCPs check stone; independent lab reports before OP (paras. 10–16). Some works are minor works (para. 17).

=== "中文"

    - 覆蓋層（金屬、石材、玻璃纖維強化混凝土等）須符合 **B(C)R 第 3 及 28 條**：物料、固定件、強度、耐用性、**不燃性**（消防守則 E 部：外部 E10.1、E12.1；內部 E13.1）及維修通道（第 1、3 段）。
    - 設永久可伸縮**活動縫**；固定件用**不銹鋼**或防腐物料；石材**只用砂漿或環氧樹脂黏合不算永久固定**（第 2、4 段）。
    - 在**一般建築圖則**顯示覆蓋層；離毗鄰地面或樓面 **6 米**以上者，另須呈交**結構圖則**（框架、伸縮縫、錨固、荷載、物料、施工、計算）；金屬覆蓋層的典型細節可納入**上蓋結構圖則**（第 4–6 段）。
    - 可委任**另一註冊結構工程師**（BA4／BA5），並附對主結構影響的評估，由項目結構工程師簽署確認（第 7–9 段）。
    - **測試**：6 米以上鑽入式錨栓拉拔測試 **≥ 1.5 倍建議拉力**；石材抗彎（ASTM C880／EN 12372）及錨固（ASTM C1354）測試，每項 **≥ 5 個樣本**；**石灰石**老化測試後須保留 **≥ 80%**；由 T3／T1 適任技術人員檢查石材；佔用許可證前提交獨立測試報告（第 10–16 段）；部分工程屬小型工程（第 17 段）。

## Paragraph by paragraph (full) 逐段詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision January 2026 (paras 1–5 amended, para. 6 added). 已按 2026 年 1 月版本核對。

| Para. 段 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cladding is a facing or decoration additional to the structure (metal, stone, GRC, etc.). It must meet **B(C)R ss. 3 and 28**: materials, fixings, strength, durability, non-combustibility, and access for maintenance or repair. |
| 2 | Enough permanent flexible horizontal and vertical joints for differential movement. Anchors, dowels and fixings in **stainless steel** or other corrosion-resistant material, permanent and protected. |
| 3 | Standards: Appendix A. Non-combustibility: [FS Code](../codes/fire-safety-code-2011.md) Part E section 5. External cladding cl. **E10.1, E12.1**; internal cladding deemed-to-satisfy cl. **E13.1**. |
| 4 | **GBP**: location, layout, elevation and material. Above **6 m** from adjoining ground or floor: also **structural plans** (thickness, strength, durability, type, fixing material, support layout), or approval / consent may be delayed or refused. For stone, **sand/cement bedding and/or epoxy alone is not a permanent fixing**. 石材只靠砂漿或環氧樹脂不算永久固定。 |
| 5 | Structural plan contents: (a) framing and elements (no unnecessary shop details); (b) elevations with expansion joints; (c) anchors and supports; (d) loads, standards, codes; (e) material specs (steel, aluminium, granite / marble / limestone, GRC, anchors); (f) welding, corrosion protection, bi-metallic measures; (g) calculations: parent structure check, stability of the system, element design, deflection of main members. |
| 6 | **New (2026): streamlined metal cladding**. The RSE may put general notes, typical layout and typical details in the **superstructure plans** instead of a separate cladding submission. Location, layout and elevation still go on the GBP. AP and RSE ensure compatibility with the GBP. See checklist App. B6 and sample drawing App. B9 of [ADV-33](adv-33.md). 金屬覆蓋層可納入上蓋結構圖則。 |
| 7 | A **separate RSE** may be appointed: Forms **BA4 / BA5** with the plans; certifies completion under **B(A)R 25(3)**. |
| 8 | Separate RSE's **assessment report** on the parent structure, with a statement signed by the project RSE. |
| 9 | Separate RSE supervises the works including cast-in anchorage (anchor plates, embeds, through bolts), unless pre-installed before appointment; then the project RSE shows them on superstructure plans and supervises; the two RSEs coordinate any changes. |
| 10 | **Drilled-in anchors** for cladding above 6 m: on-site strength tests on a representative number of each type and size, directed by the RSE or AP. |
| 11 | **Pull-out capacity ≥ 1.5 ×** manufacturer's recommended tensile load; pass if no separation, plastic deformation or harmful effect. |
| 12 | Stone properties vary, so tests verify the design average and characteristic strengths and form part of QA/QC. RSE appoints a **T3** TCP and the RC a **T1** TCP on site to spot harmful constituents (clay-like minerals, iron minerals, veins) in delivered panels. |
| 13 | Per stone type: (a) flexural strength to **ASTM C880** or **BS EN 12372**; (b) anchorage strength to **ASTM C1354**; checked against Appendix B. |
| 14 | **Limestone** (sensitive to acid rain): also **aged strength tests** (temperature and moisture cycling); residual flexural and anchorage capacity **≥ 80%** of the standard test results. |
| 15 | **≥ 5 specimens** chosen at random from site deliveries for each test in 13(a), 13(b) and 14. |
| 16 | Tests by or under an agency **independent of the stone supplier**, certified by it and endorsed by the AP/RSE; reports to the BA **before the OP application**. |
| 17 | Some cladding erection, repair, replacement or removal is **minor works**: B(MW)R Schedule 1 and [APP-147](app-147.md). |

### Appendix B: stone panel acceptance criteria (Rev. 3/2021) 附錄 B：石材合格準則

| Criterion 準則 | Requirement 要求 |
|---|---|
| (a) Characteristic flexural strength | **> 3 ×** design allowable flexural strength |
| (b) Average flexural strength | **> FSF ×** design allowable flexural strength |
| (c) Characteristic anchorage strength | **> 4.2 ×** design allowable anchorage strength |
| (d) Average anchorage strength | **> ASF ×** design allowable anchorage strength |

Characteristic strength = average − **K × σ**, K = **3.41** for at least 5 specimens; σ = standard deviation. **FSF = VF × DF**, **ASF = FSF × 1.4**.

**Variation factor VF 變異系數**

| Coefficient of variance | 0–5% | 5–10% | 10–20% | > 20% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Granite 花崗岩 | 2.0 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 3.5 |
| Limestone 石灰石 | 3.0 | 3.5 | 4.0 | 4.5 |
| Marble 大理石 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 3.5 | 4.0 |

**Durability factor DF 耐用系數**

| Fraction of initial flexural strength | 100% | 95–75% | 75–60% | < 60% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DF | 1.0 | 1.2 | 1.5 | 1.8 |

Default values where not substantiated: coefficient of variance **10–20%**; strength fraction **< 60%** for limestone and marble, **75–60%** for granite. Resulting factors:

| Stone 石材 | FSF | ASF |
|---|---|---|
| Granite | 3.0 × 1.5 = **4.5** | 4.5 × 1.4 = **6.3** |
| Limestone | 4.0 × 1.8 = **7.2** | 7.2 × 1.4 = **10.08** |
| Marble | 3.5 × 1.8 = **6.3** | 6.3 × 1.4 = **8.82** |

**Aged strength test for limestone (para. 3)** 石灰石老化測試. Allowed if the strength fraction is taken as < 60%:

- (a) **50 thermal cycles**: dry 4 h at **77 °C ± 2 °C**, cool 4 h at **0 °C ± 2 °C**, then inspect.
- (b) **50 wet/dry cycles**: immerse 4 h in water at **23 °C ± 2 °C**, forced-air or oven dry 4 h at 23 °C ± 2 °C, then inspect.
- (c) Strength tests after both sets of cycles show residual strength meets para. 13 (≥ 80%, para. 14).
- Para. 4: the RSE may propose another procedure for BA acceptance before testing.
