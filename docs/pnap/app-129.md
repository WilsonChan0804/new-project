## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - To save landfill and fill capacity, recycled rock, glass cullet and concrete may be used as aggregates in **C20–C35** concrete (para. 1).
    - **C20 with 100% recycled aggregate** (App. A): only **minor or non-structural** works such as on-grade slabs, blinding, U-channels, pipe bedding, post and fence footings, and mass fill (para. 5).
    - **C25–C35 with ≤ 20% recycled aggregate** (App. B): works without major structural concern, e.g. landscape features and planters (para. 6). Based on SCCT / HyD specs (WBTC 12/2002) (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 為節省堆填及公眾填料空間，再生岩石、碎玻璃及混凝土可用作 **C20–C35** 混凝土的骨料（第 1 段）。
    - **C20、100% 再生骨料**（附錄 A）：只限**小型或非結構**工程，例如地台板、墊層、U 形渠、喉管墊層、柱及圍欄基腳及素混凝土填料（第 5 段）。
    - **C25–C35、再生骨料 ≤ 20%**（附錄 B）：沒有重大結構問題的工程，例如園景設施及花槽（第 6 段）；以混凝土技術常務委員會及路政署規格為基礎（工務局技術通告 12/2002）（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: concrete with **recycled coarse aggregates** (crushed old concrete): **C20 with 100%** (minor and non-structural works, App. A) or **C25–C35 with up to 20%** (App. B). **Not** for liquid-retaining, prestressed, **transfer** or **hanger** structures (para. 8). 再造骨料混凝土。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Recycling inert C&D material saves fill and landfill; guidelines for C20–C35, based on SCCT / HyD specs (WBTC 12/2002). 背景。 |
| 5 | **C20, 100%**: on-grade slabs, blinding, U- / stepped channels, pipe bedding and haunching, post and fence footings, unloaded mass fill. C20 用途。 |
| 6 | **C25–C35, ≤ 20%**: (a) landscape planters, fence walls, mass walls and footings; (b) **stormwater manholes and sand traps**; (c) footways, cycle tracks, **RC infill walls**, mass concrete under footings or rafts. C25–C35 用途。 |
| 7 | Precast paving units: HyD guidance notes. 鋪路磚。 |
| 8 | **Not** in liquid-retaining, prestressed, transfer or hanger structures. 禁止用途。 |
| 9 | State on the **general structural specifications** plan and **Part I** calcs ([ADM-8](adm-8.md)); note compliance with App. A / B; show the scope on plans. 圖則註明。 |
| 10 | Other concrete requirements still apply. 其他要求。 |

## Appendices A–B: technical guidelines 附錄 A、B {#appendices}

| Item | App. A: C20, 100% recycled | App. B: C25–C35, 20% recycled |
|---|---|---|
| Code 守則 | Concrete Code 2013 | Concrete Code 2013; **OPC to BS EN 197-1** only |
| Coarse aggregate 粗骨料 | 100% recycled (Table 1) | **80% natural**, 20% recycled (Table 1), tested **weekly** per source |
| Fine aggregate 細骨料 | CS3 grading M; **no recycled fines** | CS3; **no recycled fines** |
| Grading | CS3 single-sized 20 mm and 10 mm | Same |
| Mix 配比 | **OPC 100 : fine 180 : 20 mm 180 : 10 mm 90** (kg) | Designed mix with trials |
| Workability 坍落度 | Pre-wet aggregates; slump **75 mm** | Pre-wet; slump **≥ 75 mm** |
| Strength 強度 | Cubes per CS1; min. **14 MPa (7 d)**, **20 MPa (28 d)**; lab trial each of 3 cubes **≥ 26 MPa** | Per CS1 and Concrete Code grade compliance |
| Production 生產 | — | **Separate stockpiles / silos** and batching compartment |

| Table 1: recycled coarse aggregate 再造粗骨料要求 | Limit |
|---|---|
| Min. dry particle density | **2,000 kg/m³** |
| Max. water absorption | **10%** |
| Wood and material lighter than water | **0.5%** |
| Other foreign materials (metal, plastic, clay, asphalt, glass) | **1%** |
| Fines / sand (< 4 mm) | **4% / 5%** |
| Sulphate | **1%** |
| Flakiness index | **40%** |
| 10% fines value | **100 kN** |
| Chloride | **0.05%** of combined aggregate |

| Annex to App. B: trials 試驗 | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Lab trials | 3 batches; 2 slumps and 6 cubes each. Mean slump within **20 mm or 25%**. Plant SD > 5.5 / 5 MPa (or no data): mean of 18 cubes > grade **+14 / +12 MPa** (100 / 150 mm), each **+7 / +6**; SD within limits: mean **+10 / +8**, each **+3 / +2** |
| Plant trials | 1 batch per day for 3 days per plant (≥ 60% mixer capacity; 3 truck mixers); samples at **⅙, ½, ⅚** of discharge; mean of 9 slumps within 20 mm or 25%, batch range ≤ **20%**; mean of 18 cubes > grade **+12 / +10**, each **+5 / +4**, batch range ≤ **20%** |
