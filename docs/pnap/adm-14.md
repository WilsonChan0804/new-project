## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Plans approvable subject to **minor amendments** may be amended with BD's prior agreement (para. 1).
    - (a) **Amend in BD** after an interview with the case officer, authenticated by signature; bring specialist contractors (curtain wall, foundation, hoarding) where useful; (b) hand in **substitute sheets** with amendments clearly annotated, individually authenticated and agreed with the case officer (para. 2). If amended plans or forms are **not returned by the agreed date**, the submission may be **disapproved** (BO s.16(1)(i), 16(1)(c)) (para. 3). Amendments affecting the **lease** (e.g. GFA exclusions) need an **extra copy** and a development schedule for **LandsD** (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 如圖則只須**輕微修訂**即可批准，可在屋宇署事先同意下修訂（第 1 段）。
    - (a) 與個案主任面談後**在屋宇署內修訂**並簽署確認，如有需要可帶同專門承建商（幕牆、地基、圍板）出席；(b) 遞交清楚註明並逐項簽署、已獲個案主任同意的**替換圖紙**（第 2 段）。修訂圖則或表格**未在協定日期前交回**，可被**拒絕**（第 16(1)(i)、16(1)(c) 條）（第 3 段）；影響**地契**的修訂（例如不計總樓面面積）須**多交一份**連發展附表轉交**地政總署**（第 4 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Plans approvable **subject to minor amendments** may be amended with BD's prior agreement. 小修訂。 |
| 2 | Ways to amend: (a) **in BD** after an interview, authenticated by signature; (b) **substitute sheets** with amendments annotated and authenticated; (c)–(d) other agreed routes, including **Revised Submission** in the ESH. 修訂方式。 |
| 3 | Not returned or not submitted via ESH by the **agreed date**: may be **disapproved** (BO s.16(1)(i), (c)). 逾期可被拒絕。 |
| 4 | Lease implications (paper submissions): extra annotated copy and a **Development Schedule** for LandsD. 地契影響。 |
| 5 | A facility that may be modified or withdrawn if abused. 可修改或取消。 |
