## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Plans approvable subject to **minor amendments** may be amended with BD's prior agreement (para. 1).
    - (a) **Amend in BD** after an interview with the case officer, authenticated by signature; bring specialist contractors (curtain wall, foundation, hoarding) where useful; (b) hand in **substitute sheets** with amendments clearly annotated, individually authenticated and agreed with the case officer (para. 2). Later paras. cover forms and timing.

=== "中文"

    - 如圖則只須**輕微修訂**即可批准，可在屋宇署事先同意下修訂（第 1 段）。
    - (a) 與個案主任面談後**在屋宇署內修訂**並簽署確認，如有需要可帶同專門承建商（幕牆、地基、圍板）出席；(b) 遞交清楚註明並逐項簽署、已獲個案主任同意的**替換圖紙**（第 2 段）；其後各段涵蓋表格及時限。
