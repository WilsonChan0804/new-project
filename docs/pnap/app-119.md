## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - After a fatal fall through a school stairwell, the Coroner recommended **avoiding open-well staircases** in new schools (para. 1).
    - In schools and premises for **children and youngsters**, evaluate hazards from proper and improper use of accessible open wells; add **safety nets or higher railings** where appropriate (para. 2).
    - Open stairwells: handrails to the MOE Code and B(P)R Third Schedule, and **balustrades ≥ 1100 mm** high (B(C)R 8) (para. 3).
    - Provide protective barriers wherever a level difference **exceeds 600 mm** (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 一名學生從學校樓梯井墮下死亡後，死因裁判官建議新校舍**避免採用開敞豎井樓梯**（第 1 段）。
    - 在學校及供**兒童及青少年**使用的處所，須評估可接觸的開敞豎井在正常及不當使用下的危險，並在適當處加設**安全網或較高欄杆**（第 2 段）。
    - 開敞樓梯井：扶手須符合走火守則及 B(P)R 附表三，**欄杆高度 ≥ 1100 毫米**（B(C)R 第 8 條）（第 3 段）。
    - 高低差**超過 600 毫米**之處須設防護欄障（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: new **school buildings** and other premises used by **children and youngsters** with open wells or stair-wells. 學校及兒童使用處所的樓梯井及中空位。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | After a student fell six floors through a stairwell, the Coroner recommended **avoiding open-well staircases** in new schools. 死因庭建議。 |
| 2 | APs should follow this **as far as practicable**; where wells are accessible, assess hazard from proper or improper use and add **safety nets or higher railings**. 加安全網或較高欄杆。 |
| 3 | Open stairwells need protection: handrails (MOE Code 17.6, B(P)R Third Schedule para. 8) and balustrades **≥ 1,100 mm** (**B(C)R 8**). 欄杆 ≥ 1.1 米。 |
| 4 | Protective barriers wherever a level difference exceeds **600 mm**. 高差 600 毫米須設護欄。 |
