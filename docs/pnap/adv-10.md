## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Lift shaft platforms are **temporary wall-supported** platforms in lift shafts (rain shelters, formwork, mid-shaft protection, debris catching, crash decks) (para. 1).
    - Governed by the Construction Sites (Safety) Regs, **OSHO (Cap. 509)** and Cap. 59; collapses have killed workers (paras. 2–3).
    - Labour Department may serve **suspension notices** if requirements are not met, e.g. each platform **designed by the contractor's AP / RSE** (para. 4 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 升降機槽平台是升降機槽內**由牆身支承的臨時平台**（擋雨、模板、井道中段保護、接載碎料、防撞平台）（第 1 段）。
    - 受《建築地盤（安全）規例》、**《職業安全及健康條例》（第 509 章）**及第 59 章規管；曾有平台倒塌導致工人死亡（第 2–3 段）。
    - 如未符合要求，勞工處可發出**暫時停工通知書**，例如每個平台須**由承建商聘用的認可人士／註冊結構工程師設計**（第 4 段起）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: temporary wall-supported platforms in **lift shafts** during construction (rain shelter, formwork, mid-shaft, debris-catching, crash deck). Enforced by **Labour Department**. 施工期間的升降機槽平台。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purposes of lift shaft platforms | 用途 |
| 2 | Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations; general duties under **OSHO Cap. 509** and FIUO Cap. 59 | 法例 |
| 3 | Fatal collapses in the past | 事故 |
| 4(a) | Design by the **contractor's AP / RSE** with full details | 設計 |
| 4(b) | Method statements by a **TCP T4** engineer, signed off by the AP / RSE | 施工計劃 |
| 4(c) | Erection under full-time supervision of a **TCP T1** competent person; checked on completion | 監督 |
| 4(d) | Alterations: same design and sign-off route | 改動 |
| 4(e) | **Risk assessment** by a planning team (≥ one T4) before erection, maintenance, dismantling | 風險評估 |
| 4(f)–(h) | Maintain; remove accumulated materials; T1 supervision of dismantling; nothing thrown onto platforms unless designed for it | 維修及拆卸 |
| 4(i) | **Fall-arresting systems** | 防墮 |
| 4 (lead) | Non-compliance: LD may serve **suspension notices** (OSHO s.10) | 暫時停工 |
| 5 | On site: design plan, construction plan, erection / dismantling records, risk assessment | 工地文件 |
| 6 | **TOP** cases: fire separation and safety measures kept until the full OP | 臨時佔用許可證 |
| 7 | References: OSHO, FIUO, CS(S)R Part VA, Guidelines on Safety of Lift Shaft Works | 參考 |
