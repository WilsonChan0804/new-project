## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Lift shaft platforms are **temporary wall-supported** platforms in lift shafts (rain shelters, formwork, mid-shaft protection, debris catching, crash decks) (para. 1).
    - Governed by the Construction Sites (Safety) Regs, **OSHO (Cap. 509)** and Cap. 59; collapses have killed workers (paras. 2–3).
    - Labour Department may serve **suspension notices** if requirements are not met, e.g. each platform **designed by the contractor's AP / RSE** (para. 4 onward).

=== "中文"

    - 升降機槽平台是升降機槽內**由牆身支承的臨時平台**（擋雨、模板、井道中段保護、接載碎料、防撞平台）（第 1 段）。
    - 受《建築地盤（安全）規例》、**《職業安全及健康條例》（第 509 章）**及第 59 章規管；曾有平台倒塌導致工人死亡（第 2–3 段）。
    - 如未符合要求，勞工處可發出**暫時停工通知書**，例如每個平台須**由承建商聘用的認可人士／註冊結構工程師設計**（第 4 段起）。
