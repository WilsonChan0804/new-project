## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - A proper **condensate drainage system** for air-conditioners must form part of the **surface water drainage** and be shown in the drainage submission, otherwise plans are **disapproved under BO s.28(1)** (para. 1).
    - Existing buildings without one: advise owners to install a **communal system** during comprehensive maintenance (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 冷氣機的**冷凝水排放系統**須屬建築物**地面水排水系統**的一部分，並在排水圖則顯示，否則根據**條例第 28(1) 條拒批**（第 1 段）。
    - 沒有此系統的現有建築物：建議業主在全面維修時加裝**公用系統**（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: drainage submissions for new buildings: show an **A/C condensate disposal system** as part of the **surface water** drainage, or plans are **disapproved** under **BO s.28(1)**. Existing buildings: recommended during major maintenance. 冷氣機冷凝水排放。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Provide a **condensate disposal system** within the **surface water** drainage and show it in the drainage submission, else disapproval under **s.28(1)**. 須設冷凝水排放系統。 |
| 2 | Existing buildings without one: advise owners to install a **communal system** during comprehensive maintenance. 現有建築物建議加裝。 |
