## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **BO s.18(1)** authorises necessary shoring; **s.18(6)** lets BA authorise **entry** into buildings for it (para. 1).
    - Apply only when shoring inside the neighbour is the **only** option and **consent was refused** despite reasonable efforts; give applicant details, address and lot, purpose, justification, any agent and registration number (paras. 3–4).
    - BA normally issues written authority within **2 weeks**, to be **posted** on the building; damage must be repaired and compensation may be claimed (s.18(4), (5)) (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - **條例第 18(1) 條**准許搭建必要的支撐；**第 18(6) 條**容許建築事務監督授權為此**進入**建築物（第 1 段）。
    - 只有在鄰樓內支撐是**唯一**方法，並已採取合理步驟仍**未獲同意**時才可申請；須提供申請人資料、地址及地段、目的、理據、代理人及註冊編號（第 3–4 段）。
    - 建築事務監督一般在 **2 星期**內發出書面授權，並須**張貼**於有關建築物；造成的損壞須修復，並可申索賠償（第 18(4)、(5) 條）（第 5–6 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: entering **adjacent buildings** to erect **shoring** under **BO s.18** when the owners' consent cannot be obtained and there is **no other way** to provide precautions. 進入鄰近建築物搭建支撐。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | **s.18(1)** authorises necessary shoring; **s.18(6)** lets the BA authorise **entry**; procedures and affected parties' rights. 法定依據。 |
| 3 | Before applying: shoring inside the neighbour is the **only** option, and **consent was refused** despite all reasonable steps. 申請前提。 |
| 4 | Written application: (a) applicant; (b) HKID / passport / BR no.; (c) address and lot of the building to enter; (d) purposes; (e) **justification** that it is the only means; (f) any **agent** (s.18(6)(b)); (g) RC registration no. 申請資料。 |
| 5 | If satisfied, written authorisation normally within **2 weeks**; post a copy **conspicuously** on the building. 兩星期內發出。 |
| 6 | **s.18(4)–(5)**: repair damage and **compensate** occupiers; disputes to the **Lands Tribunal** (**s.18A**). 賠償。 |
| 7 | Demolition shoring: old PNAP 71 ([APP-21](app-21.md)). 拆卸支撐。 |
