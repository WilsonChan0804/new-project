## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - After SARS, BD promotes ventilation of **common corridors and lift lobbies** (including entrance lobbies) beyond the statutory rooms (para. 1).
    - Consider it at design stage; if natural ventilation is not feasible, provide **mechanical ventilation** (para. 2): sufficient **fresh air** for the population, intakes and exhausts placed to avoid **contamination and short-circuiting**, and **accessible** ducts for maintenance (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 沙士後，屋宇署推廣在法定房間以外為**公用走廊及升降機大堂**（包括入口大堂）提供通風（第 1 段）。
    - 應在設計階段考慮；如自然通風不可行，應提供**機械通風**（第 2 段）：按人數提供足夠**新鮮空氣**、進氣及排氣口須避免**污染及短路**，風管須**方便維修**（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: common corridors, lift and entrance lobbies (recommendation at design stage); not a statutory requirement. 公用走廊及大堂（建議）。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Promote ventilation of common areas beyond the B(P)R requirements for habitable rooms, offices, kitchens | 目的 |
| 2 | Provide ventilation; if natural is not feasible, **mechanical** | 自然或機械通風 |
| 3(a) | Enough **fresh air** for the expected population | 足夠鮮風 |
| 3(b) | Intake and exhaust designed against **contamination and short-circuiting** | 進排氣位置 |
| 3(c) | System and ducts **accessible** for maintenance | 方便維修 |
| 4 | Kept under review | 檢討 |
