## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - After SARS, BD promotes ventilation of **common corridors and lift lobbies** (including entrance lobbies) beyond the statutory rooms (para. 1).
    - Consider it at design stage; if natural ventilation is not feasible, provide **mechanical ventilation** (para. 2): sufficient **fresh air** for the population, intakes and exhausts placed to avoid **contamination and short-circuiting**, and **accessible** ducts for maintenance (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 沙士後，屋宇署推廣在法定房間以外為**公用走廊及升降機大堂**（包括入口大堂）提供通風（第 1 段）。
    - 應在設計階段考慮；如自然通風不可行，應提供**機械通風**（第 2 段）：按人數提供足夠**新鮮空氣**、進氣及排氣口須避免**污染及短路**，風管須**方便維修**（第 3 段）。
