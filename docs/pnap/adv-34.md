## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BD encourages **BIM** for projects under the BO; this PNAP guides BIM-based statutory submissions (para. 1). Example BIM applications in App. A (para. 2).
    - **GBP**: follow BD's guidelines on **BIM in GBP submissions** and sample drawings and models (para. 3). **Other statutory plans**: follow the **CIC–BD guidelines**, BIM objects and templates (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 屋宇署鼓勵在條例下的項目採用**建築信息模擬**；本備考為以此呈交法定圖則提供指引（第 1 段）；應用例子見附錄 A（第 2 段）。
    - **一般建築圖則**：按屋宇署**一般建築圖則採用建築信息模擬**指引及樣本圖則和模型（第 3 段）；**其他法定圖則**：按**建造業議會與屋宇署聯合指引**、建築信息模擬物件及範本（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: optional use of BIM to prepare GBP and other statutory plans (new buildings and A&A). Approval is still on the **prescribed plans**. 選擇以 BIM 準備圖則。

### Appendix A uses (summary) 附錄 A 應用（摘要） {#app-a-uses}

| Plans 圖則 | BIM shows 顯示 | Simulation 模擬 |
|---|---|---|
| General building | Irregular forms, projections, site levels, **area calculations**, MOE and compartmentation, A&A relationship | Phasing of construction and A&A |
| Drainage | Complex systems, relation to foundations | Phasing |
| Superstructure | Materials, FRR and cover, steel connections, **transfer structures and load path**, façades, A&A temporary supports | Phasing |
| Foundation / ELS | Types, levels, bearing stratum, relation to ground and adjoining structures | Top-down construction |
| Site formation | Profiles, geology, adjoining features | Phasing |
| Demolition | Complicated sequence, partial demolition | Method and precautions |
