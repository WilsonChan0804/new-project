## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - If an AP, RSE or RGE is ill or temporarily away from HK, nominate a **temporary replacement** within **7 days** on **Form BA21** (or **MW09** for minor works) (para. 1).
    - One BA21 can list **all active projects**; an RSE / RGE under several APs submits one form per AP, signed by that AP; the nominee cites the earlier BA21 when writing to BD (para. 2).
    - Only when **temporary** and due to **illness or absence from Hong Kong** (para. 3); acting for **more than 3 months**, or at frequent intervals, is not temporary and may be refused, so the owner should appoint a new AP/RSE/RGE (para. 4). The nominee **cannot nominate** someone else (para. 2(e)).

=== "中文"

    - 認可人士、註冊結構工程師或註冊岩土工程師如患病或暫時離港，須在 **7 天**內以**表格 BA21**（小型工程用 **MW09**）提名**臨時代替人**（第 1 段）。
    - 一份 BA21 可列出**所有進行中項目**；在不同認可人士下工作的結構／岩土工程師須按每位認可人士各交一份並由其簽署；代行人與屋宇署通訊時須註明根據較早的 BA21 行事（第 2 段）。
    - 只限**暫時性**且因**患病或離港**（第 3 段）；代行**超過 3 個月**或頻密代行不屬暫時性，可不獲接納，業主應另行委任（第 4 段）；代行人**不得再提名**他人（第 2(e) 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | AP / RSE / RGE ill or temporarily away: nominate a replacement on **Form BA 21** (or **MW09** for minor works) **within 7 days** (BO ss.4(2), 4A(5); B(A)R 23(2); B(MW)R s.49). 7 天內呈交 BA 21 / MW09。 |
| 2(a)–(c) | **One BA 21** listing all active projects (in duplicate); an RSE/RGE under different APs: one form per AP, signed by that AP; Class I minor works: **one MW09**. 每份表格列出所有項目。 |
| 2(d) | The nominee refers to the BA 21 / MW09 each time they write to the BD. 代行人須註明。 |
| 2(e) | The nominee **cannot nominate** another person. 不得再提名。 |
| 2(f) | Tell the BD by letter **within 7 days** if the arrangement ends early. 提早結束須 7 天內通知。 |
| 2(g) | Class I minor works: nominee files **MW31 within 7 days** after the nomination ends (B(MW)R s.50). MW31。 |
| 3 | Allowed **only** if **temporary** and due to **illness or absence from Hong Kong**. 只限暫時及患病或離港。 |
| 4 | More than **3 months**, or frequent, is not temporary and may be refused; the owner should appoint another person. 超過 3 個月不接納。 |
| 5 | BD acknowledges, copying the owner with the period and reason. 屋宇署確認並通知業主。 |
