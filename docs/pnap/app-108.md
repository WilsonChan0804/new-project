## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - In exchange for **dedicating land or floor area for public passage**, the BA may grant (i) **GFA exemption** of the dedicated area, and/or (ii) **bonus PR and SC**.
    - **Two categories** (para. 2): **(i) setback at street level**, bonus under [B(P)R 22](../regulations/bpr-18a-22.md) (PR bonus = lesser of **5 × dedicated area** and **20%** of permissible PR); **(ii) passage within or through a building**, bonus by modification: **5 ×** the area at **ground level**, **2 ×** at **other levels**, total ≤ **20%** of permissible PR.
    - **GFA exemption** may be given whenever a dedication is accepted as desirable. **Bonus PR/SC** only if the government considers the dedication **essential** (para. 7).
    - Acceptable reasons: relieving **congestion**, linking to **footbridges/subways**, **road safety / public convenience**, or suitable **greenery** (para. 6).
    - The result must not exceed the **OZP** restrictions (para. 5). A **Deed of Dedication** must be executed **before OP/TOP** (para. 10).

=== "中文"

    - 作為**撥出土地或樓面作公眾通道**的補償，建築事務監督可給予 (i) 撥出面積的**總樓面面積豁免**，及／或 (ii) **額外地積比率及上蓋面積**。
    - **兩類**（第 2 段）：**(i) 地面臨街後移**，按 [B(P)R 第 22 條](../regulations/bpr-18a-22.md)計算（額外地積比率為**撥出面積 5 倍**與准許地積比率 **20%** 的較小者）；**(ii) 建築物內或穿過建築物的通道**，以變通方式給予：**地面層 5 倍**、**其他樓層 2 倍**，總額 ≤ 准許地積比率 **20%**。
    - 只要撥出被接納為合適，便可給予**總樓面面積豁免**；只有政府認為撥出**必需**時才給予**額外地積比率及上蓋面積**（第 7 段）。
    - 可接受的理由：紓緩**擠塞**、連接**行人天橋／隧道**、**道路安全／公眾便利**，或合適的**綠化**（第 6 段）。
    - 所得發展密度不得超過**分區計劃大綱圖**限制（第 5 段）；**發出佔用許可證或臨時佔用許可證前**須簽立**撥地契約**（第 10 段）。

## Key table: concessions for dedication (para. 2) 撥地寬免（第 2 段） {#key-table-concessions-for-dedication-para-2-2}

| Category 類別 | GFA exemption 總樓面面積豁免 | Bonus SC 額外上蓋面積 | Bonus PR (as additional GFA) 額外地積比率 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(i)** Setback at **street level** for public passage 地面臨街後移作公眾通道 | Dedicated area may be exempted 可豁免 | Per **B(P)R 22(1)** formula 按第 22(1) 條公式 | **Lesser** of **5 × dedicated area** and **20%** of permissible PR 撥出面積 5 倍或准許地積比率 20%，取較小者 |
| **(ii)** Passage **within / through a building** 建築物內／穿過建築物的通道 | Dedicated area exempted 豁免 | Normally equivalent to B(P)R 22(1) 一般相當於第 22(1) 條 | **5 ×** area at **ground level**; **2 ×** area at **other levels**; total ≤ **20%** of permissible PR 地面 5 倍；其他樓層 2 倍；總額 ≤ 20% |

<small>Category (ii) is granted by **modification** of B(P)R on individual merits. Street widening shown on town plans is dealt with under **B(P)R 22(2)** (para. 4). 第 (ii) 類以**變通**方式按個別情況批出；城市規劃圖則上的街道擴闊按第 22(2) 條處理。</small>

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

!!! quote "Para. 2(ii) — Dedication within a building 建築物內撥出地方"
    …The maximum bonus plot ratio, in terms of additional GFA, generally will be at multiples of the dedicated area (five times for dedicated area at ground level and two times for areas at other floor levels) subject to the total bonus in return for the dedication not to exceed 20% of the permissible plot ratio with the dedicated area being exempted from accountable GFA.

    **官方中文本：** ……在批出額外地積比率時，其最大額外樓面面積通常是撥出地方的若干倍數（額外樓面面積是地面層撥出地方面積的5倍，或是其他樓層撥出地方面積的2倍），但可寬免的總額外樓面面積不得超過准許地積比率的20%，並可豁免撥出地方面積計算入總樓面面積。

!!! quote "Para. 7 — Justification 理據"
    All applications for dedication should be accompanied by suitable justification to demonstrate that there is a genuine need by the public for such dedication and the dedicated areas are reasonable in the circumstances to cater for the need. In general, upon acceptance of a proposed dedication of land or area within a building for public passage as desirable, the BA may exempt such land or area from GFA calculations. However, bonus plot ratio and site coverage for the development will only be allowed if such dedication is considered to be essential by government, taking into account alternative public passages available in the vicinity.

    **官方中文本：** 所有撥出土地的申請都應該附有適當的理由，證明公眾對所撥出的地方有真正的需求，且撥出的地方在該情況下可合理地應付所需。一般而言，當建築事務監督接受撥出土地或建築物內樓面用作公眾通道的建議是合適的，他或可豁免將這部分土地或樓面計入總樓面面積。但是，在給予額外地積比率和上蓋面積時，政府會考慮到鄰近地方的替代公眾通道並只有認為撥出土地是必需時，才會准許。

## Explanation 淺白解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    This is the classic **"bonus plot ratio"** mechanism. If a private developer gives the public a **walkway**, by setting back at street level or by opening a passage through the podium, the BA can let the building have **extra floor area** as compensation.

    There are two levels of reward:

    1. **GFA exemption only.** The passage itself is not counted. This is given when the passage is merely *desirable*.
    2. **Bonus PR/SC.** Extra floor area is given on top. This is only given when the government considers the passage *essential*, for example a key link to a footbridge or MTR station.

    Remember that the bonus **cannot break the OZP**. The **Deed of Dedication** binds the owners to keep the passage open, maintained and signposted. The owners remain responsible for its upkeep (para. 9).

=== "中文"

    這是典型的**「額外地積比率」**機制：私人發展商若為公眾提供**通道**（地面臨街後移或在平台開設穿過建築物的通道），建築事務監督可給予**額外樓面面積**作補償。

    補償分兩個層次：

    1. **只豁免總樓面面積**：通道本身不計算，適用於通道屬*合適*的情況。
    2. **額外地積比率／上蓋面積**：在豁免以外另加面積，只在政府認為通道*必需*時給予，例如連接行人天橋或港鐵站的重要通道。

    緊記額外面積**不得超出分區計劃大綱圖**；**撥地契約**約束業主保持通道開放、妥善維修及展示告示，維修責任由業主承擔（第 9 段）。

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! example "Worked example: passage through a building"
        Site **1,000 m²**, permissible PR **8** (max. GFA 8,000 m²). Passage dedicated through the podium: **60 m²** at G/F and **40 m²** at 1/F (linking to a footbridge), accepted as essential.

        - Bonus GFA = 5 × 60 + 2 × 40 = 300 + 80 = **380 m²**
        - 20% cap = 20% × 8 × 1,000 = 1,600 m² → bonus **380 m²** OK
        - The 100 m² of dedicated passage is also **exempted** from GFA.
        - Check that 8,380 m² does not exceed the OZP limit.

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Assuming a bonus is automatic. Without government support that the passage is **essential**, only **GFA exemption** is given.
        - Passages also used by the building's own occupants: the concession is **pro-rated** by the share of public traffic (para. 8).
        - Leaving the **Deed of Dedication** too late. No OP or TOP is issued until it is executed and its terms are fulfilled (para. 10).
        - Missing **notices** showing the public passage, opening hours and the management party (para. 9).

=== "中文"

    !!! example "計算例子：穿過建築物的通道"
        地盤 **1,000 平方米**，准許地積比率 **8**（最大總樓面面積 8,000 平方米）。在平台撥出通道：地面層 **60 平方米**、1 樓 **40 平方米**（連接行人天橋），獲接納為必需。

        - 額外樓面面積 = 5 × 60 + 2 × 40 = 300 + 80 = **380 平方米**
        - 20% 上限 = 20% × 8 × 1,000 = 1,600 平方米 → 額外 **380 平方米**符合
        - 撥出的 100 平方米通道亦**豁免**總樓面面積。
        - 須核對 8,380 平方米沒有超出分區計劃大綱圖的限制。

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 以為必定獲得額外面積：若政府不認為通道**必需**，只會給予**總樓面面積豁免**。
        - 通道同時供建築物使用者使用：寬免按公眾使用比例**折算**（第 8 段）。
        - 太遲處理**撥地契約**：簽立契約並履行條款前，不會發出佔用許可證或臨時佔用許可證（第 10 段）。
        - 沒有展示註明公眾通道、開放時間及管理人的**告示**（第 9 段）。
