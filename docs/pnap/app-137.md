## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Driving, boring and extracting piles (including temporary pile walls in ELS) cause **vibration and settlement** that can damage adjacent buildings, especially non-structural elements (para. 1).
    - AP / RSE / RGE must ensure works do not impair stability or damage neighbours; choose safe methods and supervise vigilantly (para. 2).
    - Plans follow the **Foundations Code** and APP-18, especially **clause 7.2 monitoring and assessment** of adjacent property (para. 3); ELS pile walls need the same monitoring proposal (para. 5); method confirmed and a **PR plan** before starting (paras. 6–7); **test or trial piles** verify vibration and settlement, measured as **ppv on 3 axes** at every metre (paras. 8–12); a **five-tier** Alert / Alarm / Action 1–3 system is advised (para. 14).

=== "中文"

    - 打樁、鑽樁及拔樁（包括挖掘及側向承托工程的臨時樁牆）會引致**震動及沉降**，可損壞鄰近建築物，特別是非結構構件（第 1 段）。
    - 認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師須確保工程不影響穩定或損壞鄰近物業；須選用安全方法並嚴密監督（第 2 段）。
    - 圖則須按**基礎守則**及 APP-18，特別是**第 7.2 條對鄰近物業的監測及評估**（第 3 段）；臨時樁牆亦須同樣的監測建議（第 5 段）；動工前確認施工方法並制定**公共關係計劃**（第 6–7 段）；以**測試樁或試樁**核實震動及沉降，按每米貫入深度量度**三軸峰值質點速度**（第 8–12 段）；建議採用警戒／警報／行動 1–3 的**五級**制度（第 14 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Pile installation (driving, boring, extraction, temporary pile walls) causes **vibration and settlement**; AP/RSE/RGE must ensure no damage to adjacent property. 打樁震動及沉降。 |
| 3 | Plans follow the **Foundation Code** and APP-18, especially **clause 7.2**; the BA may require (a)–(e): monitoring proposal, vibration and settlement assessments, precautions and **trial piles**. 監測及評估要求。 |
| 5 | **ELS** with temporary pile walls (sheet piles, pipe piles, channel planking): same monitoring proposal; trial piles if neighbours are vulnerable. 挖掘及側向承托工程。 |
| 6 | Before starting: RSE confirms method, **maximum concurrent piles** and plant with the contractor; changes need an **amendment plan** and re-assessment. 動工前確認。 |
| 7 | Prepare a **Public Relations Plan** (Foundation Code 7.2.7) and submit to the BA for agreement before piling. 公共關係計劃。 |
| 8 | Neighbours **not vulnerable**: verify vibration and settlement on **test piles**. 測試樁。 |
| 9 | **Vulnerable** neighbours (monuments, masonry): submit a **trial pile** proposal at each critical ground condition. 試樁。 |
| 10 | **Quality supervision** of test and trial piles under BO s.17 and the Site Supervision Code. 合格監督。 |
| 11 | Record maximum **ppv** at every metre of penetration, at final set and at obstructions, on **3 orthogonal axes**, with a calibrated device. 震動量度。 |
| 12 | Condition survey after trial piles; **trial pile report** to the BA before consent for working piles. 試樁報告。 |
| 13 | Review on site; **suspend** works and revise measures if needed (Foundation Code 7.2). 暫停工程。 |
| 14 | Enhanced **five-tier** triggers: Alert, Alarm, **Action 1, 2, 3** (provisional values in Appendix A). 五級觸發水平。 |
| 15 | Site-specific limits by an engineering approach (APP-24 Appendix C), subject to authorities' and owners' acceptance. 按地盤訂定限值。 |
