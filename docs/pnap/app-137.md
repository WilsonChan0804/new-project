## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Driving, boring and extracting piles (including temporary pile walls in ELS) cause **vibration and settlement** that can damage adjacent buildings, especially non-structural elements (para. 1).
    - AP / RSE / RGE must ensure works do not impair stability or damage neighbours; choose safe methods and supervise vigilantly (para. 2).
    - Plans follow the **Foundations Code** and APP-18, especially **clause 7.2 monitoring and assessment** of adjacent property (para. 3); ELS pile walls need the same monitoring proposal (para. 5); method confirmed and a **PR plan** before starting (paras. 6–7); **test or trial piles** verify vibration and settlement, measured as **ppv on 3 axes** at every metre (paras. 8–12); a **five-tier** Alert / Alarm / Action 1–3 system is advised (para. 14).

=== "中文"

    - 打樁、鑽樁及拔樁（包括挖掘及側向承托工程的臨時樁牆）會引致**震動及沉降**，可損壞鄰近建築物，特別是非結構構件（第 1 段）。
    - 認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師須確保工程不影響穩定或損壞鄰近物業；須選用安全方法並嚴密監督（第 2 段）。
    - 圖則須按**基礎守則**及 APP-18，特別是**第 7.2 條對鄰近物業的監測及評估**（第 3 段）；臨時樁牆亦須同樣的監測建議（第 5 段）；動工前確認施工方法並制定**公共關係計劃**（第 6–7 段）；以**測試樁或試樁**核實震動及沉降，按每米貫入深度量度**三軸峰值質點速度**（第 8–12 段）；建議採用警戒／警報／行動 1–3 的**五級**制度（第 14 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Pile installation (driving, boring, extraction, temporary pile walls) causes **vibration and settlement**; AP/RSE/RGE must ensure no damage to adjacent property. 打樁震動及沉降。 |
| 3 | Plans follow the **Foundation Code** and APP-18, especially **clause 7.2**; the BA may require (a)–(e): monitoring proposal, vibration and settlement assessments, precautions and **trial piles**. 監測及評估要求。 |
| 5 | **ELS** with temporary pile walls (sheet piles, pipe piles, channel planking): same monitoring proposal; trial piles if neighbours are vulnerable. 挖掘及側向承托工程。 |
| 6 | Before starting: RSE confirms method, **maximum concurrent piles** and plant with the contractor; changes need an **amendment plan** and re-assessment. 動工前確認。 |
| 7 | Prepare a **Public Relations Plan** (Foundation Code 7.2.7) and submit to the BA for agreement before piling. 公共關係計劃。 |
| 8 | Neighbours **not vulnerable**: verify vibration and settlement on **test piles**. 測試樁。 |
| 9 | **Vulnerable** neighbours (monuments, masonry): submit a **trial pile** proposal at each critical ground condition. 試樁。 |
| 10 | **Quality supervision** of test and trial piles under BO s.17 and the Site Supervision Code. 合格監督。 |
| 11 | Record maximum **ppv** at every metre of penetration, at final set and at obstructions, on **3 orthogonal axes**, with a calibrated device. 震動量度。 |
| 12 | Condition survey after trial piles; **trial pile report** to the BA before consent for working piles. 試樁報告。 |
| 13 | Review on site; **suspend** works and revise measures if needed (Foundation Code 7.2). 暫停工程。 |
| 14 | Enhanced **five-tier** triggers: Alert, Alarm, **Action 1, 2, 3** (provisional values in Appendix A). 五級觸發水平。 |
| 15 | Site-specific limits by an engineering approach (APP-24 Appendix C), subject to authorities' and owners' acceptance. 按地盤訂定限值。 |

## Paragraph by paragraph (full) 逐段詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision November 2024 (general revision). 已按 2024 年 11 月版本核對。

### Assessment report contents, para. 3(a)–(e) 評估報告內容

| Item 項目 | Content 內容 |
|---|---|
| (a) | **Pre-construction condition survey** with a full photo record of external and common areas of vulnerable neighbours, and internal areas where access is possible. 施工前狀況勘察。 |
| (b) | **Preliminary assessment** of vibration and settlement on structural and non-structural elements, covering the whole works and stating the types and duration of the main vibration-generating activities. |
| (c) | Proposed **vibration and settlement limits** (considering Appendix A) and a **monitoring proposal**, with critical monitoring points identified by the RSE/RGE. |
| (d) | If vibration limits **above Appendix A** are proposed: detailed assessment of vibration magnitude (Foundation Code, **BS ISO 4866**, **CIRIA Technical Note 142**, or other references the BA accepts), including the **cumulative effect** of all piles, and detailed engineering analysis of neighbours' stability. Railway protection areas: [APP-24](app-24.md) Appendix B. |
| (e) | Near vulnerable neighbours: a **trial pile proposal** to validate the assessments (paras 9, 11, 12). |

### Other points 其他要點

| Para. 段 | Detail 細節 |
|---|---|
| 4 | Percussive / vibratory installation near vibration-sensitive structures (Foundation Code cl. 7.2.6) is **not normally accepted** unless trial piles show it is safe. Shoring of cracked members of neighbours may be needed before trial piles. 對震動敏感建築物一般不接受衝擊／振動打樁。 |
| 6 | The RSE also meets other departments' requirements. Changes to method, concurrent piles or plant: amendment plan with re-assessment and, if needed, revised items 3(b) and (c). |
| 9 | Trial piles at each **critical ground condition** where the largest vibration and settlement are expected: usually the highest founding level, shallow obstructions, or interbedded rock and soil. The number depends on site conditions, especially on very large sites. |
| 11 | Readings taken under the RSE/RGE's direction, with the BA's agreement, supervised by **TCPs** of the AP/RSE/RGE/RGBC/RSC streams. If limits are exceeded or damage is seen: **stop all pile installation**, RSE/RGE reviews the cl. 7.2 measures, and works resume only after the review report and revised measures satisfy the BA. 超出限值須停工。 |
| 12 | **Two sets** of the trial pile report (one set if submitted through BD's **Electronic Submission Hub**), before applying for consent for working piles. |
| 14 | Foundation Code cl. 7.2.4 and 7.2.6 give three levels (Alert, Alarm, Action); this PNAP advises splitting Action into **Action Levels 1, 2 and 3** ("5A" system) so actions can step up earlier. |
| 15 | Site-specific limits by engineering approach ([APP-24](app-24.md) App. C) depend on available information, and need acceptance by the relevant authorities and/or permission of the affected owners **before plan approval**, case by case. |

### Appendix A: recommended limits (11/2024) 附錄 A：建議限值

Ground movement depends on installation / extraction method, sequence, geology, groundwater, layout and workmanship. It can harm neighbours with piles of low lateral resistance or foundations with low factors of safety, especially **shallow foundations**. Tolerable settlement is judged case by case (para. 2).

**Settlement and tilting (para. 3)** — provisional 5A trigger values where there are no particularly sensitive neighbours 沉降及傾斜限值:

| Instrument 儀器 | Criterion 準則 | Alert | Alarm | Action 1 | Action 2 | Action 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground settlement marker | Total settlement | 12 mm | 18 mm | 20 mm | 22 mm | 25 mm |
| Services settlement marker | Total settlement **or** angular distortion | 12 mm or 1:600 | 18 mm or 1:450 | 20 mm or 1:400 | 22 mm or 1:350 | 25 mm or 1:300 |
| Building tilting marker | Angular distortion | 1:1000 | 1:750 | 1:600 | 1:550 | 1:500 |

**Ground-borne vibration (para. 4)** — guide values of maximum **ppv (mm/s)**, without an engineering analysis of building response 震動限值（最大質點速度）:

| Building condition 建築物狀況 | Vibration type 震動類別 | Alert | Alarm | Action 1 | Action 2 | Action 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Robust and stable buildings in general | Transient (e.g. drop hammer) | 9 | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 |
| | Continuous (e.g. vibratory hammer) | 4.5 | 6 | 6.5 | 7 | 7.5 |
| Vibration-sensitive / dilapidated buildings | Transient | 4.5 | 6 | 6.5 | 7 | 7.5 |
| | Continuous | 1.8 | 2.4 | 2.6 | 2.8 | 3 |

**Contingency measures (para. 5) 應變措施**

| Level 級別 | Action 行動 |
|---|---|
| Alert | Monitor more often and at more check points. 加密監測。 |
| Alarm | Review the pile installation method to reduce vibration or settlement. 檢討打樁方法。 |
| Action 1 | Joint site inspection by AP/RSE/RGE/RGBC/RSC and stakeholders; RSE/RGE checks stability and serviceability of affected property; AP/RSE/RGE proposes and carries out remedial works after consulting stakeholders. |
| Action 2 | RGBC/RSC **immediately notifies** the AP/RSE/RGE, authorities and stakeholders; RGBC/RSC carries out a detailed investigation and stability / serviceability check; joint site inspection; RSE/RGE reviews and if needed revises the design and method statements. |
| Action 3 | **Suspend** the related works; resume only after remedial and preventive measures are completed. 暫停工程。 |

Para. 6: take **Action Level 3** measures if (a) any station reaches the site-specific trigger based on the **serviceability limit** (the maximum calculated or allowable movement or response of ground, groundwater, structures and services), or otherwise the provisional Action 3 value; (b) **undue settlement**, e.g. an increase of **5 mm between two consecutive daily readings**; or (c) signs of distress or damage in any neighbouring property.
