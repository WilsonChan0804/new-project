## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Driving, boring and extracting piles (including temporary pile walls in ELS) cause **vibration and settlement** that can damage adjacent buildings, especially non-structural elements (para. 1).
    - AP / RSE / RGE must ensure works do not impair stability or damage neighbours; choose safe methods and supervise vigilantly (para. 2).
    - Plans follow the **Foundations Code** and APP-18, especially **clause 7.2 monitoring and assessment** of adjacent property (para. 3); later paras. give vibration and settlement limits and monitoring.

=== "中文"

    - 打樁、鑽樁及拔樁（包括挖掘及側向承托工程的臨時樁牆）會引致**震動及沉降**，可損壞鄰近建築物，特別是非結構構件（第 1 段）。
    - 認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師須確保工程不影響穩定或損壞鄰近物業；須選用安全方法並嚴密監督（第 2 段）。
    - 圖則須按**基礎守則**及 APP-18，特別是**第 7.2 條對鄰近物業的監測及評估**（第 3 段）；其後各段訂明震動及沉降限值及監測。
