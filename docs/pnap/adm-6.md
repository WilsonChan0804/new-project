## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Computer programs used for design calculations must be **acceptable to the BA** before submissions (para. 1).
    - Apply for **prior acceptance**: an RSE for structural (ref. **"S"**), an RGE for geotechnical (ref. **"G"**) programs; quote the BD number (para. 2).
    - Use the App. A pro forma with documentation: modelling, flow charts, codes complied with, theory and algorithms, **limitations**, input / output and interpretation (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 用作設計計算的電腦程式須在呈交前**獲建築事務監督接納**（第 1 段）。
    - 申請**預先接納**：結構程式由註冊結構工程師申請（編號**「S」**），岩土程式由註冊岩土工程師申請（編號**「G」**）；呈交時須註明屋宇署編號（第 2 段）。
    - 以附錄 A 表格申請並附文件：模型、流程圖、所符合的守則、理論及演算法、**限制**、輸入／輸出及結果詮釋（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: any AP, RSE or RGE using computer programs to produce calculations or designs for submission. Structural programs: **RSE** applies; geotechnical: **RGE**. 使用電腦程式作結構或岩土設計。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ensure the program is **acceptable to the BA** before submitting plans | 須先獲接納 |
| 2 | Apply for prior acceptance: structural by **RSE**, geotechnical by **RGE**; BD reference number prefixed **S** or **G**, quoted in submissions | S / G 編號 |
| 3 | Pro forma **Appendix A** with user's manual: (a) modelling, (b) flow charts, (c) codes complied with, (d) theory, (e) **limitations**, (f) input/output, (g) interpretation, (h) **verified examples** for every part | 申請文件 |
| 4 | In submissions give the Part I calculation information of [ADM-8](adm-8.md) para. 9(b)/10(b) | Part I 資料 |
| 5 | RSE / RGE responsible for **program execution**; complete **Appendix B** (structural) and/or **C** (geotechnical) statements | 執行責任 |
| 6 | Program without a valid BD number: BA may require all para. 3 information | 無編號程式 |
| 7 | Acceptance valid up to **3 years** from last acceptance | 有效期三年 |
| 8 | Renewal on expiry, **not earlier than 4 months** before expiry | 續期 |
| 9 | Changes to an accepted program: submit amendments and updated manual **before use** | 修訂須先接納 |
| 10 | Lists of accepted programs and limitations: NBD counter or BD website | 接納清單 |
