## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The **Steel Code 2011** was amended in Nov 2016, May 2021 and Mar 2023 (**2023 Edition**), and again by **App. A (Apr 2026)**, with immediate effect (paras. 1–2).
    - Apr 2026 items include definitions of Ys / Us (cl. 3.1.3), welding of **different steel grades** (cl. 3.4), **building acceleration limits** (cl. 5.3.4), lap joint and net area equations (9.9, 9.10), short-term concrete modulus (Table 10.1), composite column classification (Fig. 10.17) and allowable stresses (cl. 10.5.1).

=== "中文"

    - **2011 年鋼結構守則**於 2016 年 11 月、2021 年 5 月及 2023 年 3 月修訂（**2023 年版**），並以**附錄 A（2026 年 4 月）**再修訂，即時生效（第 1–2 段）。
    - 2026 年 4 月修訂包括 Ys／Us 定義（第 3.1.3 條）、**不同鋼材等級的焊接**（第 3.4 條）、**建築物加速度限值**（第 5.3.4 條）、搭接及淨截面面積公式（9.9、9.10）、混凝土短期彈性模量（表 10.1）、組合柱分類（圖 10.17）及容許應力（第 10.5.1 條）。

## April 2026 amendments in detail 二〇二六年四月修訂詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision **April 2026**, effective **immediately**. Earlier amendments (Nov 2016, May 2021, Mar 2023) are in the **2023 Edition**; BD's website has a summary of old versions. See also the [Steel Code page](../codes/steel-2011.md). 已按 2026 年 4 月版本核對，即時生效。

| Item 項目 | Clause 條文 | Amendment 修訂 |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | 3.1.3 | Ultra high strength steel (p<sub>y</sub> > 690 N/mm²): p<sub>y</sub> = Y<sub>s</sub>/γ<sub>m1</sub> ≤ U<sub>s</sub>/γ<sub>m2</sub>. Y<sub>s</sub> and U<sub>s</sub> are now **determined per cl. 3.1.2** (previously defined directly as min. R<sub>eH</sub> and R<sub>m</sub>). |
| (b) | 3.1.5 | Duplicated paragraph on through-thickness properties deleted (no technical change). |
| (c) | 3.4 | **Welding different grades**: consumables are specified to the **lower** grade of steel (was "the most onerous grade governs"). For p<sub>y</sub> ≤ 690 N/mm² consumables match or exceed the parent steel. Ultra high strength steel may use lower-strength consumables with matching elongation and Charpy, with the weld designed on weld metal strength. 不同等級鋼材焊接以較低等級為準。 |
| (d) | 5.3.4(b) | **Tall building acceleration**: the old fixed limits (10-year return, worst 10 minutes: **15 milli-g residential**, **25 milli-g office / hotel**) are **replaced**. Comfort is acceptable if the **1-year and 10-year peak accelerations** are within the limits in the **Wind Code (HKWC 2019)**. 改為按風力守則的 1 年及 10 年加速度限值。 |
| (e) | Eq. 9.9 | Typo corrected in the long-joint reduction factor for lap joints (β<sub>Lw</sub> = 1.2 − 0.002 L<sub>j</sub>/a ≤ 1.0). |
| (f) | Eq. 9.10 | Effective net area a<sub>e</sub> = K<sub>e</sub> a<sub>n</sub> ≤ a<sub>g</sub>, with K<sub>e</sub> = 1.2 (S275), 1.1 (S355), 1.0 (S460), 0.84 (S550), 0.80 (S690). a<sub>n</sub> is clarified as the net area of the leg after deducting holes. |
| (g) | 10.1.2, Table 10.1 | Information on the **short-term elastic modulus of concrete** added. |
| (h)–(n) | 10.5, Fig. 10.17, Tables 10.11, 10.13 | **Composite columns**: classification figure; allowable grades — for **concrete-filled hollow sections**, steel **235–690 N/mm²** and normal-weight concrete **C25–C80**; new cl. 10.5.2(5): special attention to **QA, temperature control and curing** for concrete above **C60** in filled hollow sections; updated geometric ratio limits, design strengths, buckling curves, imperfections and buckling checks. The detailed tables are not reproduced here; use the 2026 amendment PDF. |
| (o) | 13.6.4 | **Footbridges**: **vertical** natural frequency **≥ 3 Hz**, otherwise limit vertical acceleration per Annex A2.5 guidance. Lateral crowd response need not be considered if there are **no significant lateral modes below 1.5 Hz**. 行人天橋振動。 |
| (p) | 14.3.2, 14.3.3 | **Mechanised and automatic welding**: welding operators and weld setters must pass qualification tests witnessed by a qualified welding inspector, with certificates endorsed by an **independent testing body** (Annex A1.4.3), as for manual welders. WPS per Annex A1.4.2. |
| (q) | Table 14.2b | Definition of **carbon equivalent value** revised. |
| (r) | Annex A1.4.2.2 | Acceptable standards for mechanised and automatic welding added. |
| (s) | Annex A2.5 | General reference on **vibration of steel-framed systems** added. |
