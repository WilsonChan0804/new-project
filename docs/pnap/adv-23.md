## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - **Shotcrete and chunam** slopes look harsh and reflect heat; vegetation helps absorb CO₂ and mitigate the **urban heat island** (para. 1).
    - Make engineered slopes as **natural** as possible and minimise shotcrete and chunam (para. 2).
    - BA strongly recommends these **landscape and bio-engineering** guidelines when forming, upgrading or maintaining slopes and retaining walls, with landscape input throughout (paras. 3–4 onward).

=== "中文"

    - **噴漿及灰泥**斜坡外觀生硬且反射熱力；植物有助吸收二氧化碳及紓緩**市區熱島效應**（第 1 段）。
    - 應使人造斜坡盡量**自然**，並減少使用噴漿及灰泥（第 2 段）。
    - 建築事務監督強烈建議在新建、改善或維修斜坡及擋土牆時採用本**園景及生物工程**指引，並全程納入園景設計（第 3–4 段起）。
