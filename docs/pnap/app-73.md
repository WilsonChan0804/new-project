## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Service (scavenging) lanes give **light, ventilation, access and escape**, and act as **fire breaks** (para. 1).
    - Using a private lane for BO purposes needs proof of **permanent rights-of-way** for all users; exits may discharge into it only with **unfettered rights**; obstructed or decaying lanes are not acceptable for MOE / access (paras. 3–5).
    - **Site area**: an abutting private lane owned by the developer and not needed under the BO may be **counted in SC / PR**; where rights-of-way exist, apply to modify **B(P)R 23(2)(a)**; the lane must be **free of structures** on completion (paras. 6–7).
    - Diverted lanes only needed as a neighbourhood path: favourable consideration to count the old lane area (para. 8). Lane areas are **excluded from open space** under B(P)R 25 (para. 9).
    - Aim for direct lanes **≥ 3 m** wide; **PR / SC concessions** for free **surrender** of land for lane improvement, with a plan and undertaking to pay for paving, drainage and lighting (paras. 10–13).

=== "中文"

    - 後巷（清理垃圾巷）提供**照明、通風、通道及逃生途徑**，並起**防火間隔**作用（第 1 段）。
    - 以私家後巷符合條例要求，須證明所有使用者享有**永久通行權**；出口只可在享有**不受限制的通行權**時通往後巷；受阻或破舊的後巷不可作逃生或通道（第 3–5 段）。
    - **地盤面積**：發展商擁有並毋須作條例用途的毗鄰私家後巷，可**計入上蓋面積／地積比率**；如有通行權，須申請變通 **B(P)R 第 23(2)(a) 條**；完工時後巷須**沒有構築物**（第 6–7 段）。
    - 只作鄰里通道的改道後巷，可正面考慮計算原有後巷面積（第 8 段）；後巷面積**不計入第 25 條的空地**（第 9 段）。
    - 目標為直通及闊 **≥ 3 米**的後巷；免費**交回**土地以改善後巷可獲**地積比率／上蓋面積寬免**，須附圖則及承諾負擔鋪路、排水及照明費用（第 10–13 段）。
