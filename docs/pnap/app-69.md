## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Guidance for **adaptive re-use, A&A works and preservation-cum-development** of historic buildings (para. 1).
    - **Declared / proposed monuments** (Cap. 53): no works without a **permit from the Antiquities Authority**; otherwise plans may be **disapproved under BO s.16(1)(d)** (para. 2).
    - **Graded buildings** (AAB **Grade 1** outstanding, **Grade 2** special, **Grade 3** some merit) have no statutory protection, but A&A and **demolition plans are referred** to the **Commissioner for Heritage's Office** and **AMO**, who may explore conservation options (paras. 4–5).
    - Contact **AMO early** to identify **character-defining elements**; BD takes a **pragmatic, performance-based** approach to modifications, e.g. performance-based imposed loads (CoP Dead and Imposed Loads 2011, s.4.2) to avoid heavy strengthening (paras. 7–8).
    - Use the **Practice Guidebook for Adaptive Re-use of Heritage Buildings 2012** (alternative designs for structure, fire safety, barriers, BFA, sanitary fitments); Appendix A lists justifications for typical modifications; BD **Heritage Units** handle enquiries (paras. 9–10).

=== "中文"

    - 為歷史建築的**活化再用、改動加建及保育結合發展**提供指引（第 1 段）。
    - **法定古蹟／暫定古蹟**（第 53 章）：未取得**古物事務監督許可證**不得進行工程，否則圖則可根據**第 16(1)(d) 條拒批**（第 2 段）。
    - **已評級建築物**（古諮會**一級**價值極高、**二級**有特別價值、**三級**有若干價值）沒有法定保護，但改動加建及**拆卸圖則會轉介**發展局**文物保育專員辦事處**及**古蹟辦**，以研究保育方案（第 4–5 段）。
    - 應**及早聯絡古蹟辦**確定**具特色元素**；屋宇署對變通採取**務實、以表現為本**的態度，例如按《2011 年恆載及外加荷載作業守則》第 4.2 節採用以表現為本的外加荷載，以免大規模加固（第 7–8 段）。
    - 參考《2012 年活化再用及改動加建文物建築實用指南》（結構、消防、欄障、無障礙、衞生設備的替代設計）；附錄 A 列出常見變通所需理據；屋宇署**文物組**處理查詢（第 9–10 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: adaptive re-use, A&A works and preservation-cum-development of **historic buildings**: declared or proposed **monuments** (Cap. 53) and buildings **graded or proposed to be graded** by the AAB (fn. 1). 法定古蹟、暫定古蹟及評級歷史建築。

    **Note 注意**: graded buildings have **no statutory protection** under Cap. 53, but plans are referred to the **CHO and AMO** (para. 5). Works to a monument without an Antiquities Authority permit may be disapproved (para. 2). 評級建築沒有法定保護，但會轉介文物保育專員辦事處及古物古蹟辦事處。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Guidelines for adaptive re-use, A&A and preservation-cum-development of historic buildings. 目的。 |
| 2–3 | **Monuments / proposed monuments** (Cap. 53, gazetted): **Cap. 53 s.6** bans works without a **permit** from the Antiquities Authority (SDEV); otherwise disapproval under **BO s.16(1)(d)**. List on the AMO website. 古蹟須有許可證。 |
| 4 | AAB grading: **Grade 1** outstanding merit, preserve if possible; **Grade 2** special merit, selectively preserve; **Grade 3** some merit, some form of preservation desirable. 一、二、三級定義。 |
| 5–6 | Graded buildings: no statutory protection, but A&A and **demolition** plans are referred to **CHO and AMO**, who may approach the AP / owner on conservation options. 轉介保育部門。 |
| 7 | Contact the **AMO early** to identify heritage value and **character-defining elements**; balance conservation, constraints, re-use, A&A scope and redevelopment. 及早聯絡古物古蹟辦事處。 |
| 8 | **Pragmatic approach** to modifications / exemptions; **performance-based** alternatives if equivalent safety and health; e.g. design imposed loads by **Loading Code 2011 s.4.2** instead of heavy strengthening. 務實處理，接受表現為本方案。 |
| 9 | **Practice Guidebook for Adaptive Re-use of and A&A Works to Heritage Buildings 2012**: alternative designs (Section 5) for structure, fire safety, barriers, BFA, sanitary fitments; justifications in **Appendix A**. 實務指南及附錄 A。 |
| 10 | **Heritage Units** in BD's New Buildings Divisions process these and support the **Revitalising Historic Buildings Through Partnership Scheme**; pre-submission enquiries per [ADM-19](adm-19.md). 屋宇署文物建築組。 |

| Table A: BO and regulations | Key justification 主要理據 |

| A3 B(C)R 8(2)(b), (c), 8(3): low barriers | Existing **900–1,100 mm** high, gaps slightly > 100 mm, curb < 150 mm; not for premises mainly for children or elderly; Guidebook 5.30(d)–(f) |
| A4 B(C)R 17(1): weak floors | Layouts and fixtures; greatest likely load (Loading Code 4.2, Guidebook 5.8); structural assessment; **occupant control** (actual counting, FS Code Table B1 note 3) |
