## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Cap. 572** (in force **1 Jul 2007**) upgrades fire safety in old **composite** and **domestic (> 3 storeys)** buildings whose plans were first submitted (or which were built) **on or before 1 Mar 1987** (paras. 1–2). NT exempted houses (Cap. 121) excluded (para. 6).
    - "Domestic" excludes hotels, guesthouses, elderly homes, child care centres; "composite" excludes buildings whose non-domestic part is wholly industrial or godown (paras. 3–5).
    - Measures (para. 7): **fire safety construction** (MOE, MOA, fire spread / structural integrity) per **Schedules 1 and 2** and the 1996 MOE and FRC Codes and 1995 MOA Code; and **FSI** (sprinklers, ventilation cut-off, emergency lighting, alarms, hydrant / hose reel).
    - **Director of Buildings** enforces construction, **Director of Fire Services** enforces FSI; BD Fire Safety Section serves **Fire Safety Directions** (s.5); non-compliance can lead to a **compliance order** (s.6); alternatives where full compliance is unreasonable (paras. 9–13).

=== "中文"

    - **第 572 章**（**2007 年 7 月 1 日**生效）提升舊**綜合用途**及**住用（多於 3 層）**建築物的消防安全，適用於圖則在 **1987 年 3 月 1 日或之前**首次呈交（或在該日或之前建成）的建築物（第 1–2 段）；新界豁免屋宇（第 121 章）除外（第 6 段）。
    - 「住用」不包括酒店、賓館、安老院、幼兒中心；「綜合用途」不包括非住用部分全屬工廠或貨倉的建築物（第 3–5 段）。
    - 措施（第 7 段）：**消防安全建造**（逃生途徑、救援通道、防火蔓延及結構完整）按**附表 1 及 2** 及 1996 年走火及耐火守則、1995 年救援通道守則；以及**消防裝置**（花灑、通風截斷、應急照明、警鐘、消防栓／喉轆）。
    - **屋宇署署長**執行建造要求，**消防處處長**執行消防裝置要求；屋宇署消防安全組根據第 5 條發出**消防安全指示**；不遵從可導致**遵從令**（第 6 條）；全面遵從不合理時可採用替代方案（第 9–13 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **composite** buildings and **domestic** buildings of **more than 3 storeys** with plans first submitted (or built) **on or before 1 Mar 1987** (FS(B)O Cap. 572, in force **1 Jul 2007**). 1987 年 3 月 1 日前的綜合用途及多於三層的住宅建築物。

    **Does not apply 不適用**: NT exempted houses (Cap. 121); composite buildings whose non-domestic part is wholly **factory, godown, warehouse or bulk storage**; hotels, guesthouses, elderly homes, child care centres are not "domestic purposes" (paras. 4–6). Commercial buildings: [APP-94](app-94.md) (Cap. 502). 新界豁免屋宇等不適用。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cap. 572 upgrades **target buildings** to current fire safety standards: FSI and fire safety construction. 目的。 |
| 2 | Target: composite, or domestic **> 3 storeys**, with plans first submitted or built on or before **1 Mar 1987**. 目標樓宇。 |
| 3 | "Domestic building" includes residents' **club house, car park and recreation** facilities. 住用建築物包括會所等。 |
| 4 | "Domestic purposes" excludes **hotel, guesthouse, elderly home, child care centre, nursery**. 住用用途不包括酒店等。 |
| 5 | "Composite building" excludes buildings whose non-domestic part is wholly industrial or storage. 綜合用途定義。 |
| 6 | Not for buildings under **Cap. 121** (NT exempted houses). 不適用於新界豁免屋宇。 |
| 7 | Measures: (a) construction: **MoE, MoA, fire spread / structural integrity**; (b) FSI: sprinklers, ventilation cut-offs, emergency lighting, manual alarms, hydrant / hose reel, FSI Code 1994. 措施。 |
| 8 | Scope in **Schedule 1** (non-domestic parts) and **Schedule 2** (domestic); detail in the **MOE 1996, FRC 1996, MOA 1995** codes. 附表及守則。 |
| 9–10 | **Director of Buildings** (construction) and **Director of Fire Services** (FSI); BD **Fire Safety Section**. 執法機關。 |
| 11–12 | **Fire safety direction** under s.5(1) / 5(2); on conviction, a **compliance order** (s.6(1)). 指示及命令。 |
| 13 | Where compliance is unreasonable (structure, technology), **alternative requirements** or measures on fire engineering principles. 替代要求。 |
| 14 | **ACFSO** advises (s.5(10)); Appendix A. 諮詢委員會。 |
| 15–16 | BO approval for non-exempted works, **3 sets** of FSI plans; **no fee** (B(A)R 42) if works only meet a direction. 3 套圖則；免費。 |
