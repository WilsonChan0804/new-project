## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Displaced reinforcement causes corrosion and spalling and has led to collapses (para. 1).
    - Do not fix a batch until **tested** (APP-45); **wire-brush** loose rust and mill scale; do not use badly corroded steel (paras. 2–3).
    - **Spacers and chairs at ≤ 1.5 m** (closer for bars ≤ 20 mm) to keep position and **cover**; concrete or proprietary spacers, as small as practicable, strong and durable as the surrounding concrete (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 鋼筋移位會引致銹蝕及剝落，甚至導致倒塌（第 1 段）。
    - 每批鋼筋須在**測試**完成後才可紮放（APP-45）；以**鋼絲刷**清除鬆銹及軋鱗；嚴重銹蝕的鋼筋不得使用（第 2–3 段）。
    - **墊塊及鋼筋凳間距 ≤ 1.5 米**（≤ 20 毫米鋼筋須較密），以保持位置及**保護層**；墊塊用混凝土或專利產品，盡量細小，強度及耐用性不低於周圍混凝土（第 4–5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: all reinforced concrete works; AP / RSE (quality supervision) and RCs (continuous supervision). 所有鋼筋混凝土工程。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Displaced reinforcement causes corrosion, spalling and collapse | 目的 |
| 2 | Fix each batch only after **testing** is complete | 先測試 |
| 3 | Wire-brush loose rust and mill-scale; no badly corroded steel | 除鏽 |
| 4 | Spacers and chairs at **≤ 1.5 m**, closer for bars **≤ 20 mm** | 墊塊間距 |
| 5 | Spacers of concrete or proprietary type, strength and durability ≥ surrounding concrete | 墊塊物料 |
| 6 | Tie laps and intersections; ties **not in the cover** | 紮綁 |
| 7 | No walking on bars; independent planks; protect during later trades; **extra chairs** for **cantilever top bars** | 防止移位 |
| 8 | Laps only at specified positions; couplers per manufacturer | 搭接 |
| 9 | Brace free-standing bars and cages | 支撐 |
| 10 | RC **continuous supervision**; AP / RSE quality supervision | 監督 |
| 11 | AP / RSE **inspect before concreting**; walls and columns before formwork closed | 澆注前檢查 |
| 12 | Similar note to RCs | 承建商 |
