## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Displaced reinforcement causes corrosion and spalling and has led to collapses (para. 1).
    - Do not fix a batch until **tested** (APP-45); **wire-brush** loose rust and mill scale; do not use badly corroded steel (paras. 2–3).
    - **Spacers and chairs at ≤ 1.5 m** (closer for bars ≤ 20 mm) to keep position and **cover**; concrete or proprietary spacers, as small as practicable, strong and durable as the surrounding concrete (paras. 4–5).

=== "中文"

    - 鋼筋移位會引致銹蝕及剝落，甚至導致倒塌（第 1 段）。
    - 每批鋼筋須在**測試**完成後才可紮放（APP-45）；以**鋼絲刷**清除鬆銹及軋鱗；嚴重銹蝕的鋼筋不得使用（第 2–3 段）。
    - **墊塊及鋼筋凳間距 ≤ 1.5 米**（≤ 20 毫米鋼筋須較密），以保持位置及**保護層**；墊塊用混凝土或專利產品，盡量細小，強度及耐用性不低於周圍混凝土（第 4–5 段）。
