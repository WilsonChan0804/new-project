## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - BD aims to **facilitate**, not find faults (para. 1). The **GBP prevails** over all other plan types, and all plans must **tally** before OP or A&A completion (paras. 2–3).
    - **Pre-submission enquiry**: a written enquiry gets a **"letter of assurance"** normally within **45 days**, with pre-submission conferences with departments (para. 4). Enquiries during the project are answered within **4 weeks** (para. 21).
    - **Curtailed check** (paras. 6–13): BD checks **fundamental issues only** (Appendices A–D): density, safety, health and environment, and major allied-legislation issues. **Non-fundamental issues are not checked or raised**, but the AP/RSE/RGE remain **responsible** for them. BD runs **random audit checks**.
    - **Staged GBP submission** (paras. 14–20; first GBP after **31 Mar 2023**): **Stage I** (first approval), **Stage II** (before superstructure consent), **Stage III** (before OP/TOP), with deferred items listed in PNAP ADV-33 App. A1.
    - **Minor amendments** after first consent need no prior approval if **B(A)R 33(1) is modified**, except the items in **Appendix G**. All must be approved before OP (paras. 28–31).
    - **Concurrent approval + consent** for A&A, drainage and secondary structural works. **30-day fast track** for simple A&A, two-storey warehouses, and curtain wall / cladding repairs (paras. 33–39). **Concept drawings** and **digital files / BIM** are required for floor-area calculations (paras. 22–27).

=== "中文"

    - 屋宇署的宗旨是**協助**而非挑錯（第 1 段）。**一般建築圖則凌駕**其他類別圖則，各類圖則在申請佔用許可證或改動加建完工前須**互相吻合**（第 2–3 段）。
    - **呈交前查詢**：書面查詢一般在 **45 天**內獲發**「保證函」**，並可與各部門舉行呈交前會議（第 4 段）；項目期間的查詢一般 **4 星期**內回覆（第 21 段）。
    - **精簡審核**（第 6–13 段）：屋宇署**只審核根本性事項**（附錄 A–D）：密度、安全、衞生及環境，以及相關法例的主要事項；**非根本性事項不會審核或列為拒批理由**，但認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師仍須**負責**；屋宇署會**隨機抽查**。
    - **分階段呈交一般建築圖則**（第 14–20 段；2023 年 3 月 31 日後首次呈交）：**第 I 階段**（首次批准）、**第 II 階段**（申請上蓋工程動工同意書前）、**第 III 階段**（申請佔用許可證前），押後項目見 PNAP ADV-33 附錄 A1。
    - 取得首次動工同意書後的**輕微修訂**，如獲**變通 B(A)R 第 33(1) 條**，毋須事先批准（**附錄 G** 所列項目除外），但須在申請佔用許可證前取得批准（第 28–31 段）。
    - 改動加建、排水及次要結構工程可**同時申請批准及動工同意書**；簡單改動加建、兩層倉庫及幕牆／覆蓋層維修可**在 30 天內快速處理**（第 33–39 段）；須提交**概念圖則**及計算樓面面積的**電子檔／BIM**（第 22–27 段）。

|---|---|
| **1. Density** 密度 | Site area claimed; **site classification** (B(P)R 19(2)); private street / service lane deducted; **GFA and SC diagrams** (accountable, non-accountable, exempted); dedication / surrender for bonus; overall **PR and SC** vs permissible (incl. bonus, mean street level, podium/building height) 地盤面積、地盤分類、總樓面面積及上蓋面積圖、額外面積、整體地積比率及上蓋面積 |
| **2(a). Means of access** 消防進出途徑 | Number and disposition of **fireman's lifts / FRS**; initial access; **EVA** 消防員升降機、消防和救援樓梯間、緊急車輛通道 |
| **2(b). Means of escape** 逃生途徑 | Single-staircase rules; **MoE table** (occupancy factor, exits number/width); **discharge value**; G/F discharge; staircase relationships; **travel distance**; independent staircases for basements, kindergartens, child care; **refuge floors**; PPE premises 逃生途徑表、疏散量、行走距離、庇護層 |
| **2(c). Fire resisting construction** 耐火結構 | **FRC table** (FRR vs compartment and use); protection of adjoining buildings; staircase/lobby external walls; smoke vents; bridges and tunnels 耐火結構表、鄰近建築物保護、排煙口 |

| **4. Allied legislation** 相關法例 | **BFA** (initial access, accessible route, accessible toilet); fire certificate; **OZP**; vehicular run-in/out (TD, HyD); geotechnical assessment (APP-25); **airport height**; railway protection; road works (Cap. 370 s.22); sewage tunnels (BO s.17A); **antiquities** (Cap. 53 s.6) 無障礙、大綱圖、車輛出入、岩土、機場高度、鐵路、古蹟 |

## Key clause 主要條文

!!! quote "Para. 12 — Non-fundamental issues remain the AP's responsibility 非根本性事項仍由認可人士負責"
    Under the curtailed check system, non-fundamental issues will not be checked and will not be raised as disapproval items. These relate generally to matters that do not affect the basic design of the proposed building. It is the responsibility of the AP/RSE/RGE to ensure that all such non-fundamental issues fully comply with the relevant regulations and codes of practice before commencement of the works. After commencement of works, any contravention of the Buildings Ordinance (BO) and its subsidiary regulations should be rectified as and when they are discovered and in any event, before certification of completion of works.

    **中譯（非官方）：** 在精簡審核制度下，非根本性事項不會被審核，亦不會列為拒批事項；這些一般是不影響擬建建築物基本設計的事項。認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師有責任確保所有非根本性事項在動工前完全符合相關規例及作業守則。動工後如發現任何違反《建築物條例》及其附屬規例之處，須盡快糾正，無論如何須在證明工程完成前糾正。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    ADM-19 explains **what BD actually checks**. BD does **not** check every detail. It checks the things that would change the **basic design**: density, fire safety, light and ventilation, open space, BFA and other major legal constraints. Everything else is the AP's **professional responsibility** (self-regulation), backed by random audits and disciplinary action for repeat offenders.

    For big projects, **staged submissions** let you get the first GBP approved early with the essentials, and add details before superstructure consent and before OP. During construction, **minor amendments** can proceed without waiting for approval, as long as they are not on the Appendix G list and are approved before OP.

=== "中文"

    ADM-19 解釋**屋宇署實際審核甚麼**。屋宇署**不會**審核每個細節，只審核會改變**基本設計**的事項：密度、消防安全、採光通風、空地、無障礙及其他主要法例限制；其餘屬認可人士的**專業責任**（自我規管），並由隨機抽查及對屢犯者的紀律處分配合。

    大型項目可**分階段呈交**：先以主要資料取得首次一般建築圖則批准，再在申請上蓋工程動工同意書前及佔用許可證前補充細節。施工期間，只要不屬附錄 G 所列項目並在佔用許可證前取得批准，**輕微修訂**可先行進行而毋須等待批准。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Treating "not raised by BD" as "approved". Non-fundamental issues are **still the AP's responsibility** (para. 12).
        - Structural, drainage or site formation plans that **don't match the latest GBP**. The GBP prevails (para. 2).
        - Forgetting to mark amendment plans **"This set of plans is submitted for Stage II Curtailed Check"** (para. 17).
        - Carrying out amendments on the **Appendix G list** (not minor) without prior approval.
        - No **digital file / BIM** for computer-calculated floor areas (paras. 23–25).

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 以為「屋宇署沒有提出」即等於「已批准」：非根本性事項**仍由認可人士負責**（第 12 段）。
        - 結構、排水或地盤平整圖則**與最新一般建築圖則不符**：以一般建築圖則為準（第 2 段）。
        - 修訂圖則忘記註明**「This set of plans is submitted for Stage II Curtailed Check」**（第 17 段）。
        - 未經事先批准進行屬**附錄 G**（不屬輕微）的修訂。
        - 以電腦計算樓面面積卻沒有提交**電子檔／BIM**（第 23–25 段）。
