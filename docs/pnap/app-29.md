## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Building works for lifts and escalators follow **B(C)R 9A**, with standards in the **2011 Lift Code** (BA); equivalents accepted if justified (paras. 1–2).
    - Check **fixing details**: guide-rail brackets, lift machine, deflector sheave brackets; machine rooms and liftwells must withstand normal loads (para. 3).
    - **Amendments** to the 2011 Lift Code: App. A (**Aug 2015**), App. B (**Sep 2019**, plans submitted on or after **1 Dec 2019**) (para. 4).
    - E&M and operation per **EMSD** codes under **Cap. 618**; complete all **associated works** (App. C / D checklists) before applying to EMSD to put lifts into service (paras. 5–6).

=== "中文"

    - 升降機及自動梯的建築工程按 **B(C)R 第 9A 條**，詳細標準見建築事務監督的 **2011 年升降機守則**；有理據時可接受等效標準（第 1–2 段）。
    - 注意**固定細節**：導軌支架、升降機機器、導向輪支架；機房及升降機槽須能承受正常荷載（第 3 段）。
    - 2011 年守則的**修訂**：附錄 A（**2015 年 8 月**）、附錄 B（**2019 年 9 月**，適用於 **2019 年 12 月 1 日**或之後呈交的圖則）（第 4 段）。
    - 機電及操作要求按**機電署**根據**第 618 章**發出的守則；向機電署申請使用許可前，須完成所有**相關工程**（附錄 C／D 清單）（第 5–6 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **building works** for lifts and escalators (liftwells, pits, machine and pulley rooms, fixings, access, ventilation) under **B(C)R 9A** and the **2011 Lift Code** (BD), amended **Aug 2015** (App. A) and **Sep 2019** (App. B, for new plans or major revisions submitted **on or after 1 Dec 2019**). The lift equipment itself is under **EMSD** and Cap. 618. 升降機及自動梯的建築工程。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **B(C)R 9A** sets performance requirements; detail in the **2011 Lift Code**. 法定依據。 |
| 2 | Other standards accepted if **equivalent**; explain background and suitability. 可用其他標準。 |
| 3 | Adequate strength for operation, maintenance and inspection; check **fixing details** (layout, materials, anchors, load distribution) for (a) **guide rail brackets**, (b) **lift machine**, (c) **deflector sheave brackets**. 固定細節。 |
| 4 | Code amendments: **App. A (Aug 2015)**; **App. B (Sep 2019)** from 1 Dec 2019. 守則修訂。 |
| 5 | Electrical, mechanical and operational rules: EMSD Codes under the **Lifts and Escalators Ordinance Cap. 618**. 機電署守則。 |
| 6 | Finish all **associated works** before applying to DEMS for permission to use (App. C lifts, App. D escalators). 完成相關工程。 |
| 7 | Safety references in **App. E**. 安全指引。 |

## Appendices A–B: Lift Code amendments 附錄 A、B：守則修訂 {#code-amendments}

| Code para. | Amendment 修訂 |
|---|---|
| 3.2.7 (2015) | **Inspection traps**: key lock, relockable without key |
| 3.2.8 (2015) | Inspection and emergency doors: **dual key-operated locks** (two keys simultaneously from outside), openable from inside without a key, figure-type **warning sign ≥ 100 mm** near keyholes |
| 3.2.9 (2015) | Single designated-key lock acceptable where entry is unlikely: landing doors used as inspection / emergency doors, or sills **≥ 1 m** above floor |
| 3.2.3(b), 3.8.3(b), 3.10.3, 3.15.3(b), 4.10.2, 4.11.7(e), 5.1.5 (2019) | Notices **incised or embossed**, durable, letters ≥ **25 mm** (15 mm for 3.10.3) |
| 3.3.2 (2019) | Top vent **≥ 1%** of well area, **≥ 0.3 m²** per well (0.4 / 0.5 / 0.6 m² for 4 / 5 / 6 lifts in a common well); **ferrules ≥ 50 mm** above slab |
| 3.7.1 (2019) | Partition between moving parts of adjacent lifts for the **full height** of the well (was 2.5 m); separate wells need their own vents; 3.7.2 deleted |
| 3.8.2 (2019) | Pit access door **required if pit > 2 m** (and if layout permits for 1.6–2 m), leading to stairs per 3.12.4, imperforate, not opening inwards, lockable, openable from inside |
| 3.8.5 (2019) | **Fall-arrest anchorages** next to the lowest landing door at **1.5–1.8 m**, unless a pit access door exists |
| 3.12.2, 3.12.4 (2019) | Machine room access by **stairs** if level difference **> 0.6 m** (ladders only up to 0.6 m with platform and hand holds); stairs **≥ 900 mm** wide, tread **≥ 225**, riser **≤ 175 mm**, **≤ 16 steps** per flight, handrails both sides, barriers **≥ 1.1 m** |
| 3.17.2 (2019) | **Lifting hooks** with SWL notice also at the **liftwell ceiling** for car and rope replacement |

## Appendices C–D: associated works often incomplete 附錄 C、D：常見未完成工程 {#appendices-c-d}

| Lifts (App. C, 24 items) 升降機 | Escalators (App. D, 13 items) 自動梯 |
|---|---|
| Outward-opening, self-closing, lockable machine room doors with warning notices; safe access; platform railings and steps; wells and rooms **fully enclosed**, holes sealed; permanent **lighting** of wells, pits, rooms, lobbies; fan guards, cross-ventilation, wind guards; permanent power cables; labelled **isolation switches** and MCBs; protruding bars removed; inspection / emergency doors with locks; **partitions** in common wells; pits enclosed and **waterproofed**; cat ladders; mesh over top vents; well ventilation to open air; debris cleared; **earth bonding**; landing entrance works; bilingual notices; max. load on lifting beams | Self-closing lockable machine room doors with notices; safe access; holes filled; permanent cables; labelled switches; **guards** where balustrade gap to walls > **100 mm**; **obstruction guards** at floor intersections and criss-cross escalators; handrail clearances; clear height **≥ 2.3 m**; unrestricted landing areas; permanent lighting; smooth continuous surfaces facing handrails and under false ceilings |

<small>App. E: Labour Department Code of Practice for Safety at Work (Lift and Escalator); safety belt guidance; CIC Guidelines on Safety of Lift Shaft Works Vols 2–3. 附錄 E 安全指引。</small>
