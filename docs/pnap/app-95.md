## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Sale offices and show flats on construction sites are open to the public, so (para. 1): no part of a new building may be used for them **without a TOP**; temporary buildings on site need a **temporary building permit and a TOP**.
    - BA must be satisfied that the premises are **structurally suitable**, **fire-separated** from the works, have **independent protected access and escape** to a street, and adequate **FSI** (para. 2).
    - Conditions may limit the **period**, the **number of persons**, and may require **partial or total suspension** of building works during use (para. 3). Breaches: TOP revoked and prosecution under BO s.40(1), (5) (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 地盤內的售樓處及示範單位向公眾開放，因此（第 1 段）：新建築物任何部分**未有臨時佔用許可證**不得作此用途；地盤內的臨時建築物須取得**臨時建築物准許證及臨時佔用許可證**。
    - 建築事務監督須信納處所**結構適合**、與工程**防火分隔**、有**獨立受保護的通道及逃生途徑**通往街道，並有足夠**消防裝置**（第 2 段）。
    - 條件可限制**使用期限**及**人數**，並可要求使用期間**部分或全部暫停**建築工程（第 3 段）；違反者臨時佔用許可證會被撤銷，並可根據第 40(1)、(5) 條檢控（第 4 段）。
