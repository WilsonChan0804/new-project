## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Sale offices and show flats on construction sites are open to the public, so (para. 1): no part of a new building may be used for them **without a TOP**; temporary buildings on site need a **temporary building permit and a TOP**.
    - BA must be satisfied that the premises are **structurally suitable**, **fire-separated** from the works, have **independent protected access and escape** to a street, and adequate **FSI** (para. 2).
    - Conditions may limit the **period**, the **number of persons**, and may require **partial or total suspension** of building works during use (para. 3). Breaches: TOP revoked and prosecution under BO s.40(1), (5) (para. 4).

=== "中文"

    - 地盤內的售樓處及示範單位向公眾開放，因此（第 1 段）：新建築物任何部分**未有臨時佔用許可證**不得作此用途；地盤內的臨時建築物須取得**臨時建築物准許證及臨時佔用許可證**。
    - 建築事務監督須信納處所**結構適合**、與工程**防火分隔**、有**獨立受保護的通道及逃生途徑**通往街道，並有足夠**消防裝置**（第 2 段）。
    - 條件可限制**使用期限**及**人數**，並可要求使用期間**部分或全部暫停**建築工程（第 3 段）；違反者臨時佔用許可證會被撤銷，並可根據第 40(1)、(5) 條檢控（第 4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **sale offices and show flats** opened to the public on a **construction site**, either in the new building (needs a **TOP**) or in a **temporary building** on site (needs a **temporary building permit and a TOP**). 地盤內的售樓處及示範單位。

    **Not here 不在此列**: show flats **off site** in an existing building (normal A&A / change of use rules). 地盤外的示範單位。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1(a) | No part of a new building, finished or not, used as a sale office or show flat **without a TOP**. 須有臨時佔用許可證。 |
| 1(b) | No temporary building on site so used **without a temporary building permit and a TOP**. 臨時建築物須有許可證及佔用許可證。 |
| 2 | The BA must be satisfied of: (a) **structural suitability**; (b) **fire separation** from the works; (c) **independent, protected access and escape** directly to a street; (d) **FSI** to FSD's satisfaction. 結構、防火分隔、獨立逃生、消防裝置。 |
| 3 | Conditions may: (a) **limit the period**; (b) **cap the number of persons**; (c) require **partial or total suspension** of works during use. 可限制期限、人數及暫停工程。 |
| 4 | Breach may lead to **TOP revocation** and prosecution under **BO s.40(1) and (5)**. 違反可撤銷許可證及檢控。 |
| 5 | The AP's application must justify para. 2 and state the **period** and **expected number of persons**. 申請須列明期限及人數。 |
