## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - BO s.17 lets the BA require **qualified supervision** of works with significant geotechnical content (para. 1).
    - **Category I**: periodic inspections by the geotechnical engineer who prepared the design, where stability or movement results are **sensitive to design assumptions**, which must be checked on site (B(A)R 8(1)(bb), (bc)) (para. 2).
    - **Category II**: periodic inspections by a **senior** (partner / director) geotechnical engineer for highly sensitive sites, e.g. next to old shallow-founded buildings. **Category III**: **full-time** supervision by an experienced person for workmanship-critical works (anchors, fill compaction, complex ELS). Combinations allowed; nominees agreed by BA (paras. 2–4).
    - Not normally imposed where geotechnical problems are minor and failure consequences insignificant (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 條例第 17 條容許建築事務監督要求對涉及重要岩土內容的工程進行**合格監督**（第 1 段）。
    - **第 I 類**：如穩定或位移計算結果**對設計假設敏感**，須由負責設計的岩土工程師定期巡查，在現場核實假設（B(A)R 第 8(1)(bb)、(bc) 條）（第 2 段）。
    - **第 II 類**：高度敏感地盤（例如毗鄰淺基礎舊樓）由**資深**（合夥人／董事級）岩土工程師定期巡查；**第 III 類**：工藝要求高的工程（地錨、填土壓實、複雜的挖掘及側向承托）由有經驗人員**全職**監督；可組合使用，人選須經建築事務監督同意（第 2–4 段）。
    - 岩土問題輕微且失敗後果不大的地盤一般不會施加（第 5 段）。
