## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - A **suspended working platform** (gondola) includes the platform and all lifting appliances, gear, counterweights, outriggers and supports (para. 1). Rope failures and poor maintenance have caused fatal accidents (para. 2).
    - Controlled by the **Factories and Industrial Undertakings (Suspended Working Platforms) Regulation** (from 1 Jul 1995) (para. 3).
    - Sound construction, adequate support, proper maintenance and protected electrical parts (para. 5); see Labour Department guidance.

=== "中文"

    - **吊船**包括工作台及所有起重裝置、索具、平衡重、伸出支架及支承（第 1 段）；曾有吊索斷裂及保養不善引致致命意外（第 2 段）。
    - 受**《工廠及工業經營（吊船）規例》**（1995 年 7 月 1 日起）規管（第 3 段）。
    - 須構造穩固、支承足夠、妥善保養並保護電器部件（第 5 段）；參考勞工處指引。
