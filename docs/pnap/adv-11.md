## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - A **suspended working platform** (gondola) includes the platform and all lifting appliances, gear, counterweights, outriggers and supports (para. 1). Rope failures and poor maintenance have caused fatal accidents (para. 2).
    - Controlled by the **Factories and Industrial Undertakings (Suspended Working Platforms) Regulation** (from 1 Jul 1995) (para. 3).
    - Sound construction, adequate support, proper maintenance and protected electrical parts (para. 5); see Labour Department guidance.

=== "中文"

    - **吊船**包括工作台及所有起重裝置、索具、平衡重、伸出支架及支承（第 1 段）；曾有吊索斷裂及保養不善引致致命意外（第 2 段）。
    - 受**《工廠及工業經營（吊船）規例》**（1995 年 7 月 1 日起）規管（第 3 段）。
    - 須構造穩固、支承足夠、妥善保養並保護電器部件（第 5 段）；參考勞工處指引。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: any suspended working platform carrying persons in an industrial undertaking (Regulation in force **1 Jul 1995**, s.17 from 1 Jan 1996). 載人吊船。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Definition: platform suspended by lifting gear, incl. appliances, counterweights, outriggers, electrical apparatus | 定義 |
| 2–4 | Fatal accidents from rope failure; LD **FIU (SWP) Regulation** | 法例 |
| 5 | Sound construction; protect electrical parts | 構造 |
| 6 | Width ≥ **440 mm**; guard-rails **900–1,150 mm**; toe boards ≥ **200 mm**; gap ≤ **700 mm**; safe access | 尺寸 |
| 7 | **Wire ropes** only; clear of the façade; counterweights ≥ **3×** balancing weight; a **safety rope** with automatic device at each suspension point | 鋼纜及配重 |
| 8 | Erect, dismantle, alter under a **competent person** | 合資格人士 |
| 9 | Workers ≥ **18**, trained and certified; **safety harness** on an independent lifeline | 工人 |
| 10–11 | Inspection before use; **load test and thorough examination** by a competent examiner before use and after repair or weather; periodic tests; certificate displayed | 測試及檢驗 |
| 12 | Mark **safe working load** and max persons; identification mark | 安全負荷 |
| 13 | References: FIUO, the Regulation, LD Code of Practice and Guidance Notes | 參考 |
