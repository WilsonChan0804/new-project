## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - External chutes made of **old metal barrels** are noisy and unsafe: weather and debris impact can destabilise them, endangering workers and passers-by (paras. 1–3).
    - Use **safer ways to lower debris**; if chutes are used, assemble and secure them properly, consider **stronger, quieter materials** and **internal** locations (para. 4). APs and RSEs should ensure site safety (para. 5).

=== "中文"

    - 以**舊金屬桶**組成的外置垃圾槽嘈吵且不安全：受天氣及廢料撞擊影響可能不穩，危及工人及途人（第 1–3 段）。
    - 應採用**較安全的方法運送廢料**；如使用垃圾槽，須妥善組裝及固定，並考慮使用**較堅固、較寧靜的物料**及設於**室內**範圍（第 4 段）；認可人士及註冊結構工程師須確保地盤安全（第 5 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **temporary refuse chutes** for lowering debris on construction sites, especially chutes made of **old metal barrels** on external walls. 地盤臨時垃圾槽。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | External metal-barrel chutes are **noisy** and **unsafe** (weather, debris impact); collapse endangers workers and **passers-by**. 不安全。 |
| 4 | Use **safer means** of removing debris; if chutes are used, assemble, secure and install them properly; prefer other **stronger materials** and **internal** locations to cut noise. 改用較安全方法。 |
| 5 | AP / RSE ensure site safety measures and pass on the safety message. 監督責任。 |
| 6 | Similar note to contractors. 承建商另有作業備考。 |
