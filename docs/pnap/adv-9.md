## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Government collects property data to forecast completions for policy and planning (para. 1).
    - APs are asked to return **App. A** in **early March and early September** each year with details and progress of all private building projects in hand (para. 2).

=== "中文"

    - 政府收集物業資料以預測落成量，供政策及規劃之用（第 1 段）。
    - 認可人士須在每年 **3 月初及 9 月初**交回**附錄 A**，列出所有進行中私人建築項目的資料及進度（第 2 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: APs with private building projects in hand (voluntary statistical return, early **March and September**). 每年三月及九月。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | What it says 內容 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Government collates property market data for completion forecasts | 目的 |
| 2 | Return **Appendix A** in early March and September for every private project in hand | 每半年 |
| 3 | Request for co-operation | 合作 |

### Form definitions 表格定義 {#form-definitions}

| Column 欄 | Meaning 定義 |
|---|---|
| (1) | Anticipated date of temporary or full OP |
| (2) | Progress, e.g. plans approved, foundation completed, up to 4/F |
| (4) | Self-contained domestic units: separate cooking and bathroom / lavatory |
| (5) | **Saleable area**: incl. balconies, verandahs; to outside of external walls and middle of party walls; **excl.** bay windows, yards, gardens, terraces, flat roofs, carports |
| (7) | Non-domestic units; unpartitioned floor = one unit (e.g. 5 units × 3 floors + 7 floors = **22**) |
| (8) | **Internal floor area**: to internal face of enclosing walls |
