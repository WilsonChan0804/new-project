## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - The **PPE (Amendment) Regulation 1996** (from **28 Jun 1996**) focuses on **licensing**, moving design and construction rules elsewhere (paras. 1–2):
    - **B(C)(A)R 1996**: defines "cinema" and controls **barriers at upper-tier edges**; **B(P)(A)R 1996**: new **Part VIA** on PPE planning and design; **MOE Code 1996 Part III**: PPE means of escape.
    - Applies to works not started or consented by 28 Jun 1996 (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - **1996 年公眾娛樂場所（修訂）規例**（**1996 年 6 月 28 日**起）集中於**發牌**，設計及建造規定移至其他法例（第 1–2 段）：
    - **1996 年建築物（建造）（修訂）規例**：界定「戲院」並規管**上層座位邊緣的欄障**；**1996 年建築物（規劃）（修訂）規例**：新增 **第 VIA 部**規管公眾娛樂場所的規劃及設計；**1996 年走火守則第 III 部**：公眾娛樂場所的逃生途徑。
    - 適用於 1996 年 6 月 28 日或之前未展開或未獲同意的工程（第 3 段）。

!!! warning "Superseded for new works 新工程已不適用"
    Listed in [APP-153 Appendix A](app-153.md#appendix-a): it does **not apply** to buildings designed to the **FS Code 2011**. Current rule: [FS Code Part B](../codes/fsc-part-b-means-of-escape.md#clause-by-clause) Section 3 (B19–B28) and [B(P)R 49A–49E](../regulations/bpr-39-49e.md#clause-by-clause). Still relevant to older buildings and Cap. 502 / 572 upgrading. 按 2011 年消防守則設計的建築物不適用。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | The **PPE (Amendment) Regulation 1996** (from **28 Jun 1996**) leaves mainly **licensing** in the PPE Regulation. 公眾娛樂場所規例只保留發牌。 |
| 2(a) | **B(C)(Amendment) Regulation 1996**: defines **cinema**; protective barriers at the edge of **upper tiers**. 電影院定義及上層邊緣護欄。 |
| 2(b) | **B(P)(Amendment) Regulation 1996**: new **Part VIA** on situation, planning and design of PPE. 新增第 VIA 部。 |
| 2(c) | **MOE Code 1996 Part III**: PPE means of escape, moved from the repealed regulations (with part of old PNAP 54). 逃生途徑第 III 部。 |
| 3 | Apply under **BO s.39(2)**; not to works carried out or consented **on or before 28 Jun 1996**. 過渡安排。 |
| 4 | A separate note covers the revised MOE Code ([APP-82](app-82.md)). 另有作業備考。 |
