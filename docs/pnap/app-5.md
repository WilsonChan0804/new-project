## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - **Storey height** (B(P)R 24) and **balcony clear height** (B(P)R 3(3)) are measured **between structural members**, ignoring finishes (false ceilings, screeding, plaster, flooring) (para. 2).
    - **Minimum heights**: rooms for habitation / offices **2.5 m** (health); underside of beams **2.3 m**, but **2 m** for peripheral beams projecting from external walls; **2 m** in staircases; beams over doorways or narrow corridors at **door height** ("notional doorway") (paras. 3–5).
    - **Maximum storey heights** for domestic buildings (para. 5): **flats**: typical floor **3.5 m**, topmost floor **4 m** (one storey only of a duplex/triplex); **houses**: **4.5 m**. Higher storeys may be regarded as GFA abuse.
    - **Transfer plates are not GFA** if (para. 6): thickness ≤ **typical storey height** (domestic ≤ 40 storeys) or ≤ **4.5 m** (domestic > 40 storeys and non-domestic); area ≤ **2 × the GFA of the lowest floor** above. The building services zone inside is not counted in the thickness but needs safe maintenance access (para. 7).

=== "中文"

    - **樓層高度**（B(P)R 第 24 條）及**露台淨高**（第 3(3) 條）以**結構構件之間**量度，不計飾面（假天花、批盪、地台）（第 2 段）。
    - **最低高度**：居住／辦公室房間 **2.5 米**（衞生理由）；樑底 **2.3 米**，但外牆向內突出的周邊樑可為 **2 米**；樓梯 **2 米**；門口或狹窄走廊上的樑可為**門高**（「假想門口」）（第 3–5 段）。
    - 住用建築物的**最高樓層高度**（第 5 段）：**單位**：標準樓層 **3.5 米**、頂層 **4 米**（複式／三層複式只限其中一層）；**屋宇**：**4.5 米**。
    - **轉換層不計總樓面面積**，條件（第 6 段）：厚度 ≤ **標準樓層高度**（≤ 40 層住用）或 ≤ **4.5 米**（> 40 層住用及非住用）；面積 ≤ 其上建築物**最低樓層總樓面面積的 2 倍**；轉換層內的屋宇裝備區不計入厚度，但須有安全維修通道（第 7 段）。

## Key table 主要表格

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 | Para. 段 |
|---|---|---|
| Measurement 量度 | Between **structural members**, not finishes 結構構件之間 | 2 |
| Rooms for habitation / office (min.) 居住／辦公室房間最低高度 | **2.5 m** | 5 |
| Underside of beams (min.) 樑底最低高度 | **2.3 m**; peripheral beams in walls **2 m**; beams over doorways / narrow corridors at door height 周邊樑 2 米；門口樑可為門高 | 3–4 |
| Staircases (min. clear) 樓梯最低淨高 | **2 m** | 4 |
| **Flat: typical floor (max.)** 單位標準樓層最高 | **3.5 m** | 5 |
| **Flat: topmost floor (max.)** 單位頂層最高 | **4 m** (only one storey of a duplex/triplex) 只限複式其中一層 | 5 |
| **House (max.)** 屋宇最高 | **4.5 m** | 5 |
| **Transfer plate thickness** 轉換層厚度 | Domestic ≤ 40 storeys: ≤ **typical storey height**; domestic > 40 storeys and non-domestic: ≤ **4.5 m** (lowest block where several share a plate) ≤ 40 層住用：≤ 標準層高；其他 ≤ 4.5 米 | 6(a)–(b) |
| **Transfer plate area** 轉換層面積 | ≤ **2 ×** total GFA of the **lowest floor** of the blocks above ≤ 其上最低層總樓面面積 2 倍 | 6(c) |

!!! quote "Para. 6 — Transfer plate not GFA-accountable 轉換層不計總樓面面積"
    To allow for flexibility in designing transfer plates for supporting building blocks, a transfer plate which satisfies the following design parameters would normally not be accountable for gross floor area (GFA): (a) For domestic blocks of not more than 40 storeys, the thickness of the transfer plate should not be greater than the storey height of the typical floor of the building blocks; (b) For domestic blocks of more than 40 storeys and non-domestic blocks, the thickness of the transfer plate should not be greater than 4.5 m; and (c) The total area of the transfer plate should not be more than twice that of the total GFA of the lowest floor of building blocks atop.

    **官方中文本：** 為使支撐建築物的轉移板在設計上更為靈活，符合以下設計參數的轉移板一般無須計入總樓面面積：(a) 不超過40層高的住用建築物，其轉移板的厚度不應超過該建築物標準樓層的高度；(b) 超過40層高的住用建築物及所有非住用建築物，其轉移板的厚度不應超過4.5米；以及 (c) 轉移板的總面積不應超過其上方建築物最低樓層的總樓面面積2倍。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    Very tall storeys can later be split with a **mezzanine** (illegal extra floor area). So BD caps domestic storey heights for GFA purposes. Very thick transfer plates can hide usable space. APP-5 gives clear **maximums** so the designer knows what is accepted without argument, while keeping **minimum** heights for health. These rules also matter for the overall building height under [JPN 5](jpn-5.md).

=== "中文"

    過高的樓層日後可能加建**閣樓**（非法增加樓面面積），因此屋宇署為計算總樓面面積而限制住宅層高；過厚的轉換層亦可能隱藏可用空間。APP-5 訂明清晰的**上限**，讓設計師知道哪些會被接納，同時保留基於衞生的**最低**高度。這些規則亦影響 [JPN 5](jpn-5.md) 下的建築物整體高度。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! example "Worked example: transfer plate"
        Residential tower of **45 storeys**, typical storey **3.15 m**, lowest tower floor GFA **700 m²**.

        - > 40 storeys → max. thickness **4.5 m** (not 3.15 m)
        - Max. plate area = 2 × 700 = **1,400 m²**
        - Exceeding either → the plate may be **GFA accountable**.

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Measuring height to the **false ceiling** instead of the structural soffit.
        - Applying the **4 m topmost floor** to both levels of a duplex.
        - A transfer plate **larger than 2 × the tower's lowest floor** (e.g. extending over the podium).

=== "中文"

    !!! example "計算例子：轉換層"
        **45 層**住宅大廈，標準層高 **3.15 米**，大廈最低層總樓面面積 **700 平方米**。

        - > 40 層 → 最大厚度 **4.5 米**（而非 3.15 米）
        - 最大轉換層面積 = 2 × 700 = **1,400 平方米**
        - 超出任何一項 → 轉換層或須**計入總樓面面積**。

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 量度至**假天花**而非結構板底。
        - 把**頂層 4 米**套用於複式單位的兩層。
        - 轉換層**大於大廈最低層的 2 倍**（例如延伸至平台上方）。
