## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - The 1988 **SPUN** study found rock caverns viable, with environmental benefits, for uses such as container facilities, oil and gas storage, sewage treatment, refuse transfer, warehousing and community uses (para. 1).
    - Guidance (para. 2): **Geoguide 4** (cavern engineering, 1992); **HKPSG Chapter 11** (planning); **Guide to Fire Safety Design for Caverns 1994**.

=== "中文"

    - 1988 年**地下空間用途研究**確認岩洞可行並有環境效益，可用作貨櫃設施、油氣儲存、污水處理、垃圾轉運、貨倉及社區用途等（第 1 段）。
    - 指引（第 2 段）：**岩土指南第 4 冊**（岩洞工程，1992 年）；**《香港規劃標準與準則》第 11 章**（規劃）；**《1994 年岩洞消防安全設計指南》**。
