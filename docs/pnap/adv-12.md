## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Display prominently: site **address / lot number**, names of the **AP, RSE and registered contractor**, and **contact numbers**, so the public can report unsafe conditions (para. 1).
    - Advertisements are not allowed on hoardings, but **basic development information** (works, use, height, blocks, completion date) is acceptable under B(P)R 66(4); other displays stay within the lot (paras. 2–3).

=== "中文"

    - 須在顯眼位置展示：地盤**地址／地段編號**、**認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊承建商**名稱及**聯絡電話**，方便市民舉報不安全情況（第 1 段）。
    - 圍板上不准展示廣告，但根據 B(P)R 第 66(4) 條可展示**基本發展資料**（工程、用途、高度、座數、落成日期）；其他展示須在地段範圍內（第 2–3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: building sites; display is **desirable** to let the public report unsafe conditions. 建築地盤。
