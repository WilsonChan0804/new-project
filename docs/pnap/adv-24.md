## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Floor drain **traps can dry out** by evaporation (para. 1).
    - Consider **diverting waste water from a fitment** to the floor drain U-trap, complying with the Drainage Regulations, with smooth flow and **no backflow** (para. 2); modifications considered case by case (para. 3). Now mandatory in many cases under **APP-164**.

=== "中文"

    - 地台去水的**水封可能因蒸發而乾涸**（第 1 段）。
    - 可考慮**把衞生設備的廢水引入**地台去水 U 形隔氣彎管，須符合排水規例，排水暢順且**不會回流**（第 2 段）；變通按個別情況考慮（第 3 段）；根據 **APP-164**，很多情況下現已屬強制規定。
