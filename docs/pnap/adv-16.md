## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Under **BO s.32(2)**, the **Commissioner of Rating and Valuation** allocates building numbers and prescribes how they are marked; the CRV or BA may order owners to comply (paras. 1–2).
    - Follow **RVD Practice Note 1/2007**: display above or beside the entrance in **contrasting colour**, digits at least **50 mm high and 40 mm wide** (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 根據**條例第 32(2) 條**，**差餉物業估價署署長**編配建築物門牌並訂明標示方式；署長或建築事務監督可命令業主遵從（第 1–2 段）。
    - 按**差估署作業備考 1/2007**：在入口上方或旁邊以**對比顏色**展示，每個數字最少**高 50 毫米、闊 40 毫米**（第 3–4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: new buildings (apply after consent) and A&A affecting street names or numbers. 新建築物及影響門牌的工程。
