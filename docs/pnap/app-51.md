## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Encloses a guidance note (App. A) on **monitoring and maintenance** of horizontal drains, amplifying the Geotechnical Manual for Slopes (para. 1).
    - In **Scheduled Area No. 1** (Mid-levels), maintenance is controlled by **BO s.28A–28D** (para. 2).
    - Elsewhere, drains needing long-term maintenance where risk to life is significant are approved only with a **positive maintenance undertaking**, which is realistic only for owners that will not change, e.g. a major body building for its own use (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 附上排水斜管**監測及保養**指引（附錄 A），補充《斜坡岩土工程手冊》（第 1 段）。
    - **附表所列地區第 1 號**（半山區）的保養受**條例第 28A–28D 條**規管（第 2 段）。
    - 其他地區，如危及生命的風險顯著，而排水管須長期保養，只會在有**明確保養承諾**時批准；一般只適用於業權不會改變的情況，例如大型機構自用的發展（第 3–4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **horizontal drains** installed with building works to lower groundwater in slopes. In **Scheduled Area No. 1 (Mid-levels)** maintenance is also controlled by **BO s.28A–28D**; elsewhere, drains needing long-term maintenance where there is a **significant risk to life** are approved only with a **positive maintenance undertaking** (paras. 2–4). 斜坡水平排水管。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Ref. | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| Para. 1 | Guidance (Appendix A) amplifying Chapters 4 and 11 of the **Geotechnical Manual for Slopes**. 指引。 |
| Para. 2 | **Mid-levels (Scheduled Area 1)**: extra control under **s.28A–28D**. 半山區另受管制。 |
| Paras. 3–4 | Elsewhere, only with a **positive undertaking** to maintain; realistic only where ownership will **not change** (e.g. a major body developing for its own use). 須承諾保養。 |
| App. 1–4 | Short drains in rock for joints; longer ones in soil to cut groundwater; weigh **monitoring and maintenance cost** at preliminary design; procedures apply where drains are needed for the **factor of safety**; the designer stays involved; the owner appoints a **maintenance officer**. 背景及責任。 |
| App. 5 | Instrumentation scheme: **piezometers** installed early (with the GI), **Halcrow buckets** for peak levels, drain flow collection. 儀器。 |
| App. 6 | Monitor piezometers and flows **every 2 months** (dry season), **weekly** (wet season) and **within 2 days** of heavy rain (> **100 mm / 24 h**); automatic systems possible. 監測頻率。 |
| App. 7–8 | Preferably **two wet seasons** before final design; establish **base groundwater levels**; monitor during and after installation; **observational approach**; watch for nearby pumping. 兩個雨季。 |
| App. 9–11 | Effectiveness, in order: (a) levels stay **below design level** in storms; (b) **lower base level**; (c) smaller storm response; (d) faster recession; also flow trends, data adequacy and the drains' share of the FoS. 成效評估。 |
| App. 12–13 | Assess before the end of **Contract Maintenance**; continue or remediate as needed; reactivate if seepage or flows change markedly. 終止監測。 |
| App. 14 | Clogging by roots, fungi, algae, fines, precipitates: **flush with a water jet** from the deep end outwards (brush if dried deposits). 清洗方法。 |
| App. 15 | Designer hands over the programme and **as-built** details; maintain **at least once** in Contract Maintenance, then **annually**: inspect, weed, clear outlets, flush, replace liners; keep records; test large flow increases for **leaking services**. 每年保養。 |
| App. 16–17 | Hard-to-reach systems may rely on piezometer monitoring and flush only when needed; an experienced officer examines the system **annually**. 特殊情況。 |
