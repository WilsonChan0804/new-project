## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Encloses a guidance note (App. A) on **monitoring and maintenance** of horizontal drains, amplifying the Geotechnical Manual for Slopes (para. 1).
    - In **Scheduled Area No. 1** (Mid-levels), maintenance is controlled by **BO s.28A–28D** (para. 2).
    - Elsewhere, drains needing long-term maintenance where risk to life is significant are approved only with a **positive maintenance undertaking**, which is realistic only for owners that will not change, e.g. a major body building for its own use (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 附上排水斜管**監測及保養**指引（附錄 A），補充《斜坡岩土工程手冊》（第 1 段）。
    - **附表所列地區第 1 號**（半山區）的保養受**條例第 28A–28D 條**規管（第 2 段）。
    - 其他地區，如危及生命的風險顯著，而排水管須長期保養，只會在有**明確保養承諾**時批准；一般只適用於業權不會改變的情況，例如大型機構自用的發展（第 3–4 段）。
