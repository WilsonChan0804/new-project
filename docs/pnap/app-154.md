## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **MOE**: FS Code Part B, occupancy **2 m² / person** (Table B1 item 5d); usable floor area includes exit routes, balcony corridors and lift lobbies, excluding niches and staircases (para. 2).
    - **FRC**: treat as **"Other Assembly Premises"** (Table C1); **offering burners** are special hazard areas separated per C13 (para. 3). **MOA**: B(P)R 41A–41D and Part D; EVA modification on **Form BA16** (APP-136) (para. 4).
    - **Sanitary fitments**: see table (para. 5). **BFA**: B(P)R 72; allow worship space in front of niches **in addition to** accessible and MOE clear widths (para. 6). Loads per the 2011 Loads Code (para. 7).
    - **Conversion**: a **material change in use** (s.25); only in **non-domestic** buildings; in industrial buildings only **whole-building** conversion (FSD); other laws and the lease apply (paras. 8–9).

=== "中文"

    - **逃生途徑**：消防守則 B 部，人數按 **2 平方米／人**（表 B1 第 5d 項）；可用樓面面積包括出口路線、露台式走廊及升降機大堂，但不包括龕位及樓梯（第 2 段）。
    - **耐火結構**：視為**「其他集會處所」**（表 C1）；**化寶爐**屬特殊危險地方，須按 C13 分隔（第 3 段）。**救援通道**：B(P)R 第 41A–41D 條及 D 部；緊急車輛通道變通以**表格 BA16** 申請（APP-136）（第 4 段）。
    - **衞生設備**見下表（第 5 段）。**無障礙**：B(P)R 第 72 條；龕位前須預留拜祭空間，**另加**無障礙及逃生所需淨闊度（第 6 段）；荷載按 2011 年荷載守則（第 7 段）。
    - **改裝**：屬**重大用途更改**（第 25 條）；只可在**非住用**建築物；工業大廈只接受**整幢**改裝（消防處）；亦須符合其他法例及地契（第 8–9 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **new buildings** for use as a columbarium (paras. 2–7) and **conversion** of existing premises to columbarium use (para. 8). 新建或改作靈灰安置所。

    **Also needed 另須**: town planning, FEHD, traffic, fire safety licensing and **lease** approvals, and a licence under the Private Columbaria Ordinance where applicable (para. 9). 另須規劃、地契及發牌等批准。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Design and construction requirements for columbaria under the BO. 目的。 |
| 2 | **MoE** per FS Code Part B; occupancy factor **2 m² per person** (Table B1, UC 5d); usable area **includes** exit route widths (incl. "balcony approach" corridors) and lift lobbies, **excludes** niche areas and staircases. 每人 2 平方米。 |
| 3 | **FRC** per FS Code Part C as **"Other Assembly Premises"** (Table C1); **offering burners** are a **special hazard** (C13). 其他集會處所；化寶爐屬特殊危險。 |
| 4 | **MoA** per B(P)R 41A–41D and FS Code Part D; no / deficient EVA: **BA16** with justification ([APP-136](app-136.md)). 救援進出途徑。 |
| 5 | **Sanitary fitments** per Table 1 (below), separate male and female rooms. 衞生設備。 |
| 6 | **BFA** per B(P)R 72 and the BFA Manual; aisles between niche rows need space for **worship** in addition to the accessible and escape width. 無障礙及拜祭空間。 |
| 7 | Dead and imposed loads per the **Loading Code 2011**. 荷載。 |
| 8 | **Conversion** is likely a **material change in use** (BO s.25(1)); meet paras. 2–7, only in **non-domestic** buildings; in **industrial buildings** only **whole-building** conversion (FSD). 改變用途；工業大廈只可全幢改裝。 |
| 9 | Comply with other legislation and **lease** restrictions; obtain other approvals and licences. 其他法例及地契。 |

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    A floor of **600 m²** usable area (niche areas excluded): population 600 / 2 = **300**, male : female 1 : 1.5 → **120 male, 180 female**. Male: 1 WC + 1 urinal per 150 → **1 + 1**, 1 basin per 300 → **1**. Female: 1 WC per 75 → **3** (180 / 75 = 2.4, round up), 1 basin per 300 → **1**. Exit capacity per Table B2 for 300 persons: **2 exits**, total width 2,500 mm. 衞生設備及出口計算。
