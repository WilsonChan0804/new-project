## At a glance (last version) 重點摘要（最後版本） {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Policy: no unnecessary felling or damage; protect preserved trees (e.g. **protective fences**) during works (para. 1).
    - Trees on lease-controlled private land or government land: **prior Government consent** via the **District Lands Officer**, following **LAO PN 8/2002**; private owners' consent too (para. 2).
    - Unauthorised felling may be prosecuted under **Crimes Ordinance s.60** (Cap. 200) or **Forests and Countryside Ordinance s.21** (Cap. 96) (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 政策：不得無故砍伐或損毀樹木；施工期間設**保護圍欄**（第 1 段）。
    - 受地契管制的私人土地或政府土地上的樹木：須經**地政專員**取得政府**事先同意**（LAO PN 8/2002）；私人擁有的樹木亦須業主同意（第 2 段）。
    - 未經許可砍伐可按《刑事罪行條例》第 60 條或《林區及郊區條例》第 21 條檢控（第 3 段）。
