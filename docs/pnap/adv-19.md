## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Building construction produces over **a quarter** of solid waste going to landfills; plan waste management early (para. 1).
    - Design measures (para. 2): **precast and prefabricated** elements (façades, stairs, slabs, kitchens, bathrooms), sprayed plaster, **balanced cut and fill**, **PFA** concrete, combined service trenches, **purchasers' choice** of finishes, less unsustainable timber, **system formwork**; site measures follow.

=== "中文"

    - 建築工程產生的固體廢物佔堆填區**四分之一以上**；應及早規劃廢物管理（第 1 段）。
    - 設計措施（第 2 段）：**預製及預先組裝**構件（外牆、樓梯、樓板、廚房、浴室）、噴塗批盪、**挖填平衡**、**煤灰**混凝土、合併設施坑道、**由買家選擇**裝修、減少使用非可持續木材、**系統模板**；其後為施工期間措施。
