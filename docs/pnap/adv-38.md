## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BD's **pre-acceptance mechanism** gives a curtailed, non-site-specific assessment of **innovative building materials and technologies (IBMT)** (building, drainage and structural; not geotechnical) against BO standards (para. 1).
    - The supplier or manufacturer appoints an **AP / RSE / RGE** to evaluate the IBMT and submit a **pre-acceptance application** with the App. A form and supporting documents (para. 2). Not for products already in PNAPs, codes or the **Central Data Bank** (para. 3). BD decides **within 45 days** with an in-principle acceptance letter or comments (para. 4); the letter states **limits** and whether it suits **minor works** (para. 5). It does **not replace approval and consent**; projects may need a **s.42 modification** (para. 6). Valid **up to 5 years**, then renew (para. 8).

=== "中文"

    - 屋宇署的**預先認可機制**就**創新建築材料與技術**（建築、排水及結構，不包括岩土）作出精簡、非針對個別地盤的評估，確認符合條例標準（第 1 段）。
    - 供應商或製造商須委任**認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師**評估並提交**預先認可申請**，附附錄 A 表格及證明文件（第 2 段）。不適用於已載於作業備考、守則或**中央資料庫**的產品（第 3 段）；屋宇署於 **45 天**內發出原則上接納信或意見（第 4 段），信中列明**限制**及可否用於**小型工程**（第 5 段）；**不能代替**批准及同意，項目或須申請**第 42 條變通**（第 6 段）；有效期**最多 5 年**，屆滿須續期（第 8 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-acceptance of **innovative building materials and technologies (IBMT)**: building, drainage and structural (not geotechnical), non-site-specific. 創新建築物料及技術。 |
| 2 | Supplier appoints an **AP / RSE / RGE** to evaluate and submit the **PAA** (form in Appendix A plus documents). 預先認可申請。 |
| 3 | Not for items already accepted in PNAPs or codes (e.g. MiC) or in the **Central Data Bank**. 不適用範圍。 |
| 4 | Decision **within 45 days**: in-principle acceptance, or comments to answer. 45 天內決定。 |
| 5 | Acceptance letter states **requirements and limitations**, approval/consent needs, and suitability for **minor works**. 接納信內容。 |
| 6 | Not an exemption from approval and consent; plans may need a **BO s.42** application; pre-acceptance is **not a pre-requisite**. 非豁免。 |
| 7 | Project professionals ensure use complies with the BO, approved plans and the letter's limits. 項目責任。 |
| 8 | Valid **maximum 5 years**; renew on the Appendix B form. 有效期 5 年。 |
