## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - BD's **pre-acceptance mechanism** gives a curtailed, non-site-specific assessment of **innovative building materials and technologies (IBMT)** (building, drainage and structural; not geotechnical) against BO standards (para. 1).
    - The supplier or manufacturer appoints an **AP / RSE / RGE** to evaluate the IBMT and submit a **pre-acceptance application** with the App. A form and supporting documents (para. 2). Later paras. cover assessment, validity and use in projects.

=== "中文"

    - 屋宇署的**預先認可機制**就**創新建築材料與技術**（建築、排水及結構，不包括岩土）作出精簡、非針對個別地盤的評估，確認符合條例標準（第 1 段）。
    - 供應商或製造商須委任**認可人士／註冊結構工程師／註冊岩土工程師**評估並提交**預先認可申請**，附附錄 A 表格及證明文件（第 2 段）；其後各段涵蓋評估、有效期及在項目中使用。
