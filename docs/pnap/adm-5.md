## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Paper submissions for **new buildings and A&A** (except order-related works) go to the **New Buildings Divisions counter, 7/F, 14 Taikoo Wan Road**; others to **BD HQ, G/F North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices** (para. 1).
    - Counters open **Mon–Fri 8:30 am – 5:30 pm**; fold plans as in App. A; electronic submissions per **ADM-17** and **APP-60** (paras. 2–4).

=== "中文"

    - **新建築物及改動加建**的紙本呈交（與命令有關的工程除外）送交**太古灣道 14 號 7 樓新建築部收發櫃位**；其他送交**西九龍政府合署北座地下屋宇署總部**（第 1 段）。
    - 櫃位**星期一至五上午 8:30 至下午 5:30** 開放；圖則按附錄 A 摺疊；電子呈交見 **ADM-17** 及 **APP-60**（第 2–4 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: paper submissions to BD. Electronic submissions follow [ADM-17](adm-17.md) and [APP-60](app-60.md). 紙本呈交。
