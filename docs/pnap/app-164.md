## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Pending amendments to the Drainage Regulations, BD requires under **BO s.28(1)** (para. 2):
    - (a) **Floor drains** in domestic kitchens, toilets and pantries are **replenished** by waste water from a basin, bath or shower, without backflow.
    - (b) **Anti-syphonage cross-vents** from stack to main vent, sloping up **≤ 67.5°** with diameter ≥ the vent: every **5 storeys** (buildings > 5 to ≤ 20 storeys) or every **3 storeys** (> 20 storeys) from the lowest branch, plus one below the lowest branch above each offset bend.
    - (c) **No branch connections** in the offset restricted zone: **600 mm** (≤ 5 storeys) or **1000 mm** (> 5 storeys) above the bend and **2500 mm** horizontally. (d)/(e) Ground-floor fitments (and the floor above for > 20 storeys) discharge **separately** to a manhole. (f) Vent pipe ends **≥ 3 m** from the boundary and from windows, openings and fresh-air intakes.
    - Applies to new or major-revision drainage plans submitted **after 31 Aug 2021**, relevant A&A and minor works (App. B) (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 在修訂排水規例前，屋宇署根據**條例第 28(1) 條**要求（第 2 段）：
    - (a) 住宅廚房、洗手間及茶水間的**地台去水**須由洗手盆、浴缸或花灑的廢水**補充水封**，且不會回流。
    - (b) 污水立管與主防虹吸管之間設**防虹吸交叉通氣管**，向上傾斜 **≤ 67.5°**，直徑不小於主通氣管：5 層以上至 20 層的建築物每 **5 層**一個，20 層以上每 **3 層**一個，由最低支管層起計；並在每個偏移彎位以上、最低支管以下設一個。
    - (c) 偏移彎位的限制區內**不得接駁支管**：彎位以上 **600 毫米**（≤ 5 層）或 **1000 毫米**（> 5 層），水平 **2500 毫米**；(d)/(e) 最低層的衞生設備（20 層以上另加上一層）須**另行**接駁至沙井；(f) 通氣管末端距地界及窗、開口、進氣口 **≥ 3 米**。
    - 適用於 **2021 年 8 月 31 日後**呈交的新排水圖則或重大修訂，以及相關改動加建及小型工程（附錄 B）（第 3–4 段）。

## Key table: key dimensions 主要尺寸 {#key-table-key-dimensions}

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| Cross-vent slope 交叉通氣管斜度 | ≤ 67.5° upwards from stack |
| Cross-vent interval 間距 | ≤ 5 storeys (6–20 storey buildings); ≤ 3 storeys (> 20 storeys) |
| Offset restricted zone 偏移限制區 | 600 mm (≤ 5 storeys) / 1000 mm (> 5 storeys) above bend; 2500 mm horizontally |
| Separate discharge 獨立排放 | Lowest floor (4–20 storeys); lowest two floors (> 20 storeys) |
| Roof vent pipe 屋頂通氣管 | ≥ 3 m from boundary, windows, openings, air intakes |

## Paragraph by paragraph (full) 逐段詳解 {#paragraph-detail}

Checked against the BD copy, revision November 2023 (para. 9 added, Appendix A amended). 已按 2023 年 11 月版本核對。

| Para. 段 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| 2(a) | Floor drain traps (kitchen in a domestic unit, toilet, pantry) replenished by used water from a basin, bath or shower, without backflow. Footnote 1: the trap must comply with Drainage Regulation 25. |
| 2(b)(i) | Cross-vent from stack to main anti-syphonage pipe slopes up at **≤ 67.5°**, internal diameter **≥ the main vent** (Fig. 1). |
| 2(b)(ii) | Interval **≤ 5 storeys** for buildings of more than 5 and up to 20 storeys; **≤ 3 storeys** for buildings over 20 storeys, starting from the lowest storey with a branch connected (Figs 2–3). Footnote 2: this depends on the **building's** storeys, not the number the stack serves. Stacks serving only podiums or basements use the podium or basement storeys. |
| 2(b)(iii) | A cross-vent below the lowest branch and above the bend of each **offset** (Fig. 4). None needed between offsets with no branches. Footnote 3: an offset is a change from vertical to horizontal, including discharge into a manhole. |
| 2(c) | **Restricted zone** at every offset: no connection within **600 mm** above the bend (≤ 5 storeys) or **1000 mm** (> 5 storeys), and **2500 mm** horizontally (Fig. 4). Measured from the **invert of the tail of the bend**. Does not apply to the separate pipework under (d) and (e). |
| 2(d) | Buildings of **more than 3 and up to 20 storeys**: fitments on the floor where the stack discharges to a manhole get **separate pipework** direct to a manhole (Fig. 5). |
| 2(e) | Buildings **over 20 storeys**: that floor **and the floor above** get separate pipework (Fig. 6). The two floors' pipework may be combined if the Regulations, [APP-93](app-93.md) and APP-164 are met. |
| 2(f) | Roof vent open end **≥ 3 m** from a common boundary, any window, opening or fresh air intake (Fig. 7). |
| 3 | Applies to new or major-revision drainage plans (new buildings and A&A) submitted **after 31 August 2021**. Projects with **superstructure consent after 31 Aug 2021** must meet 2(b), (c) and (f) with amended plans, or a deferred minor amendment under [ADM-19](adm-19.md) paras 22–24. Others are encouraged to comply. Also applies to aboveground drainage under the **MWCS** started after 31 Aug 2021. |
| 4 | A&A and minor works: applies when installing a new floor drain, stack, offset, connection to a stack, a fitment on the lowest or second-lowest floor of a stack, or a vent pipe, or altering a roof vent (examples in Appendix B). |
| 5 | Advice: carry vent pipes **≥ 3 m above accessible roofs**. 通氣管宜高出可達天台 3 米。 |
| 6 | Advice: avoid **hopper heads** (they need clearing of debris and vegetation). 避免使用漏斗頭。 |
| 7 | Advice (DSD practice, heavier rainfall) for rainwater pipes ≤ 100 mm — see the table below. |
| 8 | In drainage repair or comprehensive maintenance, advise clients to adopt these standards, including converting open hopper systems to **closed systems with traps** (regs 12(2), 12(3), 25). |
| 9 | **New (2023)**: the BA will favourably consider modifying reg. **30(2)(b)(ii)** so the anti-syphonage pipe may connect to the branch soil / waste pipe within **750 mm** of the trap outlet (Fig. 8). |

**Rainwater pipes (para. 7)** 雨水管

| Internal diameter 內徑 | 40 mm | 65 mm | 80 mm | 100 mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Max. horizontal roofed-over area served 最大服務面積 | 9 m² | 33 m² | 58 m² | 106 m² |
