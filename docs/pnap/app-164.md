## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Pending amendments to the Drainage Regulations, BD requires under **BO s.28(1)** (para. 2):
    - (a) **Floor drains** in domestic kitchens, toilets and pantries are **replenished** by waste water from a basin, bath or shower, without backflow.
    - (b) **Anti-syphonage cross-vents** from stack to main vent, sloping up **≤ 67.5°** with diameter ≥ the vent: every **5 storeys** (buildings > 5 to ≤ 20 storeys) or every **3 storeys** (> 20 storeys) from the lowest branch, plus one below the lowest branch above each offset bend.
    - (c) **No branch connections** in the offset restricted zone: **600 mm** (≤ 5 storeys) or **1000 mm** (> 5 storeys) above the bend and **2500 mm** horizontally. (d)/(e) Ground-floor fitments (and the floor above for > 20 storeys) discharge **separately** to a manhole. (f) Vent pipe ends **≥ 3 m** from the boundary and from windows, openings and fresh-air intakes.
    - Applies to new or major-revision drainage plans submitted **after 31 Aug 2021**, relevant A&A and minor works (App. B) (paras. 3–4).

=== "中文"

    - 在修訂排水規例前，屋宇署根據**條例第 28(1) 條**要求（第 2 段）：
    - (a) 住宅廚房、洗手間及茶水間的**地台去水**須由洗手盆、浴缸或花灑的廢水**補充水封**，且不會回流。
    - (b) 污水立管與主防虹吸管之間設**防虹吸交叉通氣管**，向上傾斜 **≤ 67.5°**，直徑不小於主通氣管：5 層以上至 20 層的建築物每 **5 層**一個，20 層以上每 **3 層**一個，由最低支管層起計；並在每個偏移彎位以上、最低支管以下設一個。
    - (c) 偏移彎位的限制區內**不得接駁支管**：彎位以上 **600 毫米**（≤ 5 層）或 **1000 毫米**（> 5 層），水平 **2500 毫米**；(d)/(e) 最低層的衞生設備（20 層以上另加上一層）須**另行**接駁至沙井；(f) 通氣管末端距地界及窗、開口、進氣口 **≥ 3 米**。
    - 適用於 **2021 年 8 月 31 日後**呈交的新排水圖則或重大修訂，以及相關改動加建及小型工程（附錄 B）（第 3–4 段）。

## Key table: key dimensions 主要尺寸

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| Cross-vent slope 交叉通氣管斜度 | ≤ 67.5° upwards from stack |
| Cross-vent interval 間距 | ≤ 5 storeys (6–20 storey buildings); ≤ 3 storeys (> 20 storeys) |
| Offset restricted zone 偏移限制區 | 600 mm (≤ 5 storeys) / 1000 mm (> 5 storeys) above bend; 2500 mm horizontally |
| Separate discharge 獨立排放 | Lowest floor (4–20 storeys); lowest two floors (> 20 storeys) |
| Roof vent pipe 屋頂通氣管 | ≥ 3 m from boundary, windows, openings, air intakes |
