## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - BO s.17 conditions may require **qualified supervision** at certain stages, e.g. major **excavation and foundations**, **concreting**, **compliance testing**, **prestressing** (tensioning, grouting, lock-off), key **structural steelwork**, and **specialist works** (para. 1).
    - Supervisors need relevant knowledge and must understand the design basis; AP, RSE, RGE and RC ensure it is done (BO s.4(3), 9(3); B(A)R 37, 41) (para. 2). Geotechnical works: **APP-28** (para. 3).

=== "中文"

    - 條例第 17 條的條件可要求在某些階段進行**合格監督**，例如大型**挖掘及基礎**、**澆灌混凝土**、**合規測試**、**預應力**（張拉、灌漿、鎖定）、主要**結構鋼工程**及**專門工程**（第 1 段）。
    - 監督人員須具相關知識並熟悉設計基礎；認可人士、註冊結構工程師、註冊岩土工程師及註冊承建商須確保妥善執行（條例第 4(3)、9(3) 條；B(A)R 第 37、41 條）（第 2 段）；岩土工程見 **APP-28**（第 3 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **structural, foundation and excavation works** where the BA imposes **qualified supervision** under **BO s.17** on approval or consent. Geotechnical works: [APP-28](app-28.md). 結構、地基及挖掘工程的合資格監督。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | s.17 conditions may require qualified supervision of stages or operations, e.g. (a) major **excavation and foundations**; (b) **concreting**; (c) **compliance testing**; (d) **prestressing** (pre-/post-tensioning, grouting, lock-off); (e) key **structural steel** fabrication, erection, examination; (f) **specialist works**. 例子。 |
| 2 | Supervisors need relevant knowledge and experience and must know the **design basis**; AP / RSE / RGE / RC ensure it is done (BO s.4(3), 9(3); B(A)R 37, 41). 人員要求及責任。 |
| 3 | Geotechnical works: old PNAP 83 ([APP-28](app-28.md)). 岩土工程另見。 |
