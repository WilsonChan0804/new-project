!!! warning "Check against the FS Code 2011 與消防安全守則不一致"
    For the **lift lobby** on a refuge floor cum sky garden, APP-122 para. 4(a) says doors **≥ 1 hr** and walls **≥ 2 hr**. The later **[FS Code C17 commentary](../codes/fsc-part-c-fire-resisting-construction.md#c15c17-linked-buildings-fire-doors-refuge-floors)** says doors **and** walls **-/120/120**. Design to the **stricter FS Code** requirement, or confirm with BD.
    庇護層兼空中花園的**升降機門廊**：APP-122 第 4(a) 段要求門 ≥ 1 小時、牆 ≥ 2 小時，但較新的**消防安全守則 C17 說明**要求門及牆均為 **-/120/120**。應按**較嚴格的守則**設計，或向屋宇署確認。

## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - A **refuge floor** may double as a **communal sky garden** when not needed for refuge (para. 2).
    - Conditions: comply with the refuge floor requirements. **Planters, equipment and furniture do not count** in the net refuge area or minimum dimension. Furniture must be **fixed** and **non-combustible**, give off no toxic gas and not obstruct exits. Rubbish bins need **self-closing lids**. **No naked flames** (e.g. barbecues). The other **JPN 1** sky garden criteria also apply.
    - **Wholesale conversion** of existing buildings: a refuge floor (or the **main roof**) that cannot fully meet the technical requirements may be accepted with **enhanced FSI** and greenery. A roof refuge must be a **green roof with ≥ 50% planted** (para. 3).
    - **Lifts** may open onto the refuge-cum-garden floor via a **protected lobby** (doors FRP ≥ **1 hr**, walls ≥ **2 hr**). Non-fireman's lift doors at that level **lock automatically on fire alarm** (para. 4).
    - Use **live plants**. Avoid artificial plants, and use highly flammable species with caution. Provide a **maintenance and management manual** (paras. 7–9).

=== "中文"

    - **庇護層**在無需作庇護用途時，可兼作**公用空中花園**（第 2 段）。
    - 條件：符合庇護層規定，計算淨庇護面積及最小尺寸時**不計花槽、設備及裝置**；裝置須**固定**、**不可燃**、不散發有毒氣體、不阻礙出口；垃圾筒設**自動關閉的蓋**；**不可有明火**（例如燒烤）；並須符合 **JPN 1** 其他空中花園準則。
    - **改裝整幢現有建築物**：未能完全符合技術規定的庇護層（包括**主天台**），如提供**優化消防裝置**及綠化可獲接納；以天台作庇護層須為**綠化屋頂，種植面積 ≥ 50%**（第 3 段）。
    - **升降機**可經**防護門廊**（門耐火時效 ≥ **1 小時**、牆 ≥ **2 小時**）開向庇護層兼花園；非消防員升降機在該層的外門須於**火警警報時自動上鎖**（第 4 段）。
    - 使用**活生植物**，避免人造植物，並慎用高易燃品種；須提供**保養及管理手冊**（第 7–9 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "Para. 2 — Conditions for refuge floor cum sky garden 庇護層暨空中花園的條件"
    …the Building Authority (BA)… is prepared to accept the incorporation of communal sky gardens in refuge floors subject to compliance with all the following conditions: (a) Requirements on refuge floors… are complied with. In assessing the net area for refuge and the minimum dimension of the area for refuge, no account should be taken of the space occupied by any planters, equipment and furniture in the refuge floor; (b) All garden furniture and equipment in the refuge floor are firmly fixed; (c) All furniture, equipment and rubbish bins and other artificial features, including the finishing materials used in these gardens are non-combustible materials and do not emit toxic gas in case of fire, and are so sited that they will not obstruct the exit routes and lift openings. Rubbish bins, where provided, are fitted with self-closing lids; (d) The garden is not to be used for activities involving the use of naked fire e.g. barbecuing. A notice to this effect should be posted at prominent locations at the garden; and (e) Other criteria applicable to communal sky gardens specified in the JPN No. 1 are complied with.

    **官方中文本：** 建築事務監督在研究所有相關事宜後，同意只要符合以下所有條件，便可在庇護層闢設公用空中花園：(a) 除下文提及的特殊規定外，符合……有關庇護層的要求。在計算庇護處的淨面積和最小尺寸時，不應計入花槽、設備以及裝置佔用的空間；(b) 庇護層花園內所有裝置和設備都要安裝得牢固穩當；(c) 在這些花園內的所有裝置、設備、垃圾筒和其他人工設施（包括所使用的飾面物料）皆是不可燃物料，在火警時不會散發有毒氣體，並且應置於不會阻礙出口路線和升降機口的位置。垃圾筒（如有提供）設有可自行關閉的蓋子；(d) 花園內不可進行有明火的活動，例如燒烤。應在花園顯眼的位置張貼有關告示；及 (e) 符合《聯合作業備考》第1號內其他適用於公用空中花園的準則。

!!! quote "Para. 4 — Lift access 升降機通道"
    Where sky gardens are incorporated in refuge floors, relaxation on the restriction to provide lift door openings onto such floors may be granted subject to the following: (a) The lift landing doors should open onto a protected lobby with doors having an FRP of not less than 1 hour and walls having an FRP of not less than 2 hours; and (b) The lift operation is arranged in such a manner that the landing doors at the refuge floor, other than those for fireman's lifts, will be automatically locked when the fire alarm is on.

    **官方中文本：** 如在庇護層設置空中花園，只要符合下列情況，對這類樓層升降機門口的限制或會放寬：(a) 升降機外門應開向一個防護門廊，該門廊須有不少於1小時耐火時效的大門，以及不少於2小時耐火時效的牆壁；及 (b) 除了消防員升降機外，其他位於庇護層的升降機的外門在火警警報時會自動上鎖。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    A refuge floor is a mostly empty floor that is only used in a fire. APP-122 lets it become a **useful communal garden** for the rest of the time, **without weakening its fire role**. In practice that means:

    - Keep the refuge area **clear and big enough**, without counting planters and furniture.
    - Make sure nothing in the garden can **burn, smoke or block** the escape route.
    - Control **lifts**. Normally only the fireman's lift may serve a refuge floor, but with a protected lobby and auto-locking doors other lifts can stop there for garden users.

    The concession works together with **JPN 1** (sky gardens exempted from GFA) and **APP-151** item 7. A refuge floor cum sky garden is also counted in the JPN 1 sky garden number and spacing rules (e.g. a 55-storey building with two refuge floors may have two refuge-floor-cum-sky-gardens and one independent sky garden).

=== "中文"

    庇護層一般是只在火警時使用、大致空置的樓層。APP-122 容許它在平時成為**實用的公用花園**，**同時不削弱其消防功能**，即：

    - 保持庇護面積**暢通及足夠**，花槽及裝置不計。
    - 確保花園內沒有任何物件會**燃燒、冒煙或阻礙**逃生路線。
    - 管制**升降機**：一般只有消防員升降機可到達庇護層，但設防護門廊及自動上鎖的外門後，其他升降機可停該層供花園使用者使用。

    本寬免與 **JPN 1**（空中花園豁免總樓面面積）及 **APP-151** 第 7 項一併運作；庇護層兼空中花園亦計入 JPN 1 的空中花園數目及間距規定（例如 55 層建築物設兩層庇護層，可有兩層庇護層兼空中花園及一個獨立空中花園）。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Counting **planter areas** in the 50% net refuge area. They are excluded.
        - **Combustible decking, timber benches or artificial turf** that is not non-combustible.
        - Letting **ordinary lifts** open directly onto the refuge floor without a protected lobby and fire-alarm locking.
        - Forgetting that JPN 1 now **prohibits coupling a refuge floor** with a sky garden that is connected to the clubhouse (RRF) (JPN 1 App. A 1(c)(xvi)(d)).

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 把**花槽面積**計入 50% 淨庇護面積：不應計算。
        - 使用**可燃的木地台、木長凳或人造草**。
        - 讓**普通升降機**直接開向庇護層，而沒有防護門廊及火警自動上鎖。
        - 忘記 JPN 1 現時**不准許**連接會所的空中花園與庇護層合併（JPN 1 附錄 A 1(c)(xvi)(d)）。
