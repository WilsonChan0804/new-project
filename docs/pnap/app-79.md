## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Adopt **Geoguide 5** standards when specifying maintenance in site formation submissions (B(A)R 8(1)(bb)(E)) (para. 1).
    - The designer prepares a **Maintenance Manual** (Geoguide 5 Ch. 2), submitted **in duplicate with Form BA14**; BA returns a copy for the owners (paras. 2–3).
    - Engineer Inspections include a review of previous stability assessments; a Layman's Guide is available; routine slope maintenance may be **minor works** (paras. 4–6).

=== "中文"

    - 在地盤平整圖則訂明維修要求時須採用**岩土指南第 5 冊**的標準（B(A)R 第 8(1)(bb)(E) 條）（第 1 段）。
    - 設計者須擬備**維修手冊**（指南第 2 章），**一式兩份連同表格 BA14** 呈交；建築事務監督會發還一份轉交業主（第 2–3 段）。
    - 工程師維修巡查須覆檢以往的穩定性評估；另有簡易版指南；斜坡例行維修可屬**小型工程**（第 4–6 段）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: maintenance requirements for **man-made slopes, retaining walls** and features on natural hillsides in **site formation submissions** (B(A)R 8(1)(bb)(E)), following **Geoguide 5**. 斜坡及擋土牆保養要求。

## Key clauses 條文一覽 {#key-clauses-table}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Regular inspection and maintenance are essential; adopt **Geoguide 5** when specifying maintenance in SF submissions. 採用 Geoguide 5。 |
| 2 | Designer prepares a **Maintenance Manual** (Geoguide 5 Chapter 2); the SF submission states it will be submitted at **Form BA14** stage. 保養手冊。 |
| 3 | Submit **in duplicate** with BA14; one copy returned after acknowledgement for the owners / maintenance parties. 一式兩份。 |
| 4 | **Engineer Inspections for Maintenance** include a review of the previous **Stability Assessment**. 工程師檢查。 |
| 5 | **Layman's Guide to Slope Maintenance** on CEDD's website. 簡易指南。 |
| 6 | Routine slope maintenance may be **minor works** ([APP-147](app-147.md), B(MW)R Schedule 1). 小型工程。 |
