## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Every registered contractor appoints at least one **Authorised Signatory (AS)**; RGBCs and RSCs give **continuous supervision** per the supervision plan (para. 1).
    - The AS signing Forms **BA7, 10–14, 14A, 18, 20** must be the person responsible; **one AS per project**, deemed to be the BA10 signatory (para. 2).
    - From **1 Jul 2004**, forms without the AS name are incomplete; a mismatch between the supervision plan and forms means rejection unless the change was approved or notified (para. 3). Later paras. cover temporary absence.

=== "中文"

    - 每名註冊承建商須委任最少一名**獲授權簽署人**；註冊一般建築承建商及註冊專門承建商須按監工計劃書進行**持續監督**（第 1 段）。
    - 簽署表格 **BA7、10–14、14A、18、20** 的獲授權簽署人須為負責人；**每項目只接受一名**，以簽署 BA10 者為準（第 2 段）。
    - 自 **2004 年 7 月 1 日**起，沒有註明獲授權簽署人姓名的表格視為不完整；監工計劃書與表格上的姓名不符會被拒絕，除非已獲批准或已通知更改（第 3 段）；其後各段處理暫時缺勤。
