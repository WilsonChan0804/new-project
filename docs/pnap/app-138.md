## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - Every registered contractor appoints at least one **Authorised Signatory (AS)**; RGBCs and RSCs give **continuous supervision** per the supervision plan (para. 1).
    - The AS signing Forms **BA7, 10–14, 14A, 18, 20** must be the person responsible; **one AS per project**, deemed to be the BA10 signatory (para. 2).
    - From **1 Jul 2004**, forms without the AS name are incomplete; a mismatch between the supervision plan and forms means rejection unless the change was approved or notified (para. 3). Changing the AS needs an approved addition to the registration, or notice on PNRC 59 Appendix A (para. 4). If the RC cannot act, **works must stop** (B(A)R 22(2)) (para. 5). **Temporary AS**: notify **≥ 7 days** before (sudden illness: proof **within 3 days**), countersigned by the AP; another AS on the **same register**; **14 days** each time, **30 days** in total (para. 6); beyond 30 days change the AS (para. 8).

=== "中文"

    - 每名註冊承建商須委任最少一名**獲授權簽署人**；註冊一般建築承建商及註冊專門承建商須按監工計劃書進行**持續監督**（第 1 段）。
    - 簽署表格 **BA7、10–14、14A、18、20** 的獲授權簽署人須為負責人；**每項目只接受一名**，以簽署 BA10 者為準（第 2 段）。
    - 自 **2004 年 7 月 1 日**起，沒有註明獲授權簽署人姓名的表格視為不完整；監工計劃書與表格上的姓名不符會被拒絕，除非已獲批准或已通知更改（第 3 段）。更換獲授權簽署人須先獲批准加入註冊或按 PNRC 59 附錄 A 通知（第 4 段）；承建商未能行事須**停工**（第 22(2) 條）（第 5 段）。**暫代獲授權簽署人**：最少 **7 天前**通知（突然患病須於 **3 天內**提交證明），由認可人士加簽；須為**同一名冊**的獲授權簽署人；每次 **14 天**，合共最多 **30 天**（第 6 段）；超過 30 天須更換（第 8 段）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

| Para. 段 | Rule 規定 |
|---|---|
| 1 | The RC (RGBC / RSC) gives **continuous supervision** per the supervision plan (B(A)R Part V, TM, Site Supervision Code). 持續監督。 |
| 2 | The AS who signs Forms BA 7, 10–14, 14A, 18 and 20 is the person responsible; **one AS per project**, deemed to be the one who signs **BA 10**. 每項目一名獲授權簽署人。 |
| 3 | AS name on **every specified form**; from **1 Jul 2004** forms without it are incomplete; names on the supervision plan and forms must match. 須註明姓名。 |
| 4 | Change of AS: single-AS firms need an approved addition (PNRC 38); multi-AS firms notify on **PNRC 59 Appendix A**, countersigned by the AP. 更換程序。 |
| 5 | If the RC becomes unable to act, **stop the works** until another RC is appointed (B(A)R 22(2)). 須停工。 |
| 6 | Temporary AS: (a) **PNRC 59 Appendix B**, AP countersigns; (b) **≥ 7 days** before, or proof **within 3 days** for sudden illness or accident; (c) another AS of the same RC on the same register, or an AS of another RC (with its authorisation); (d) **14 days** maximum, extendable with proof, **30 days** in total. 暫代條件。 |
| 7 | Nominee not meeting 6(c): not accepted; works may have to stop. 不符合則不接納。 |
| 8 | Absence **over 30 days**: change to another AS or add a new AS. 超過 30 天須更換。 |
| 9 | The temporary AS supervises and signs **site inspection records** only, not supervision plans or specified forms. 暫代職責。 |
