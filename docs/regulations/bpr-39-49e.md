!!! note "How these regulations are met in practice 實務上如何符合"
    Regs. 41, 41A–41D set **performance** requirements. The detailed, deemed-to-comply rules are in the **Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011**: [Part B](../codes/fsc-part-b-means-of-escape.md) for means of escape and [Part D](../codes/fsc-part-d-means-of-access.md) for firefighting access and EVA.
    第 41、41A–41D 條屬**表現**規定，詳細的當作符合規定見《2011 年建築物消防安全守則》[B 部](../codes/fsc-part-b-means-of-escape.md)（走火通道）及 [D 部](../codes/fsc-part-d-means-of-access.md)（消防救援進出途徑及緊急車輛通道）。

!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: **Part V (regs. 39–44)** to all buildings, with exemptions: reg. 41A not for single-household domestic buildings ≤ **3 main storeys**; reg. 41B not for one-staircase buildings ≤ 6 storeys / 17 m, domestic ≤ **30 m**, or ordinary non-domestic ≤ **15 m** (or ≤ 30 m and ≤ 7,000 m³); reg. 41C only for **industrial / warehouse** buildings and large **basements**. **Part VI (regs. 45–49)**: domestic buildings. **Part VIA (regs. 49A–49E)**: places of public entertainment. 第 V 部適用於所有建築物（有豁免）；第 VI 部住用；第 VIA 部娛樂場所。

    **How to comply 如何符合**: the regulations state the duty; the deemed-to-comply detail is in the **FS Code**: [Part B](../codes/fsc-part-b-means-of-escape.md) (escape), [Part C](../codes/fsc-part-c-fire-resisting-construction.md) (fire resisting construction), [Part D](../codes/fsc-part-d-means-of-access.md) (firefighting access, EVA). Temporary buildings: [regs. 50–70](bpr-50-70.md). 細節見消防安全守則。

## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Reg. 39 staircases**: buildings over 1 storey need a staircase. The **main staircase** is at least **2 m** clear height and **900 mm** wide, treads ≥ **225 mm**, risers ≤ **175 mm**, ≤ **16 steps** per flight, with handrails. Over **4 storeys**, it continues to the **roof** unless there is a second staircase.
    - **Reg. 41**: means of escape suited to the use. Over **6 storeys**, or top floor over **17 m** above ground, a **second staircase** is needed.
    - **Regs. 41A–41C**: **access staircases** for firemen (over 1 storey); **fireman's lifts** for buildings over 2 storeys, so that no part of a served floor is more than **60 m** from one (with exemptions); **firefighting and rescue stairways** for industrial / storage buildings over **30 m** or **7 000 m³**, and large basements.
    - **Reg. 41D**: every building needs **emergency vehicular access (EVA)** to BA's requirements, unless exempted (terrain or low fire risk), in which case other fire safety measures apply.
    - **Regs. 42–44**: landings / lobbies at least the **staircase width**; any part of homes, schools and places of public assembly within **24 m** of a staircase or exit; an alternative exit next to **revolving doors** and turnstiles.
    - **Part VI (regs. 45–49)**: kitchens in domestic buildings; tenement house rules; **1.5 m** gap from retaining walls over **4.5 m**; **no dangerous trades** in domestic buildings.
    - **Part VIA (regs. 49A–49E)**: a **place of public entertainment** must be in a building used only for that purpose, except **cinemas** (max. **2 000** persons in a mixed-use building, non-domestic part only). No domestic accommodation in a PPE; tiers ≤ **35°** with ≥ **3 m** headroom.

=== "中文"

    - **第 39 條樓梯**：超逾 1 層的建築物須設樓梯；**主樓梯**淨高最少 **2 米**、淨闊 **900 毫米**，級面 ≥ **225 毫米**、豎板 ≤ **175 毫米**，每段 ≤ **16 級**，並設扶手；超逾 **4 層**須延伸至**屋頂**，除非設有第二道樓梯。
    - **第 41 條**：按用途提供逃生途徑；超逾 **6 層**或最高樓層高於地面 **17 米**，須有**第二道樓梯**。
    - **第 41A–41C 條**：供消防員使用的**通道樓梯**（超逾 1 層）；超逾 2 層的建築物須設**消防員升降機**，使所服務樓層任何部分距升降機不超過 **60 米**（設有豁免）；高逾 **30 米**或體積逾 **7 000 立方米**的工業／貯存建築物及大型地庫須設**消防和救援樓梯間**。
    - **第 41D 條**：每幢建築物須按建築事務監督的規定設**緊急車輛通道**；如因地勢或低火警風險獲豁免，須符合其他消防安全措施。
    - **第 42–44 條**：樓梯平台／門廊不少於**樓梯闊度**；住宅、學校及公眾集會場所任何部分距樓梯或出口不超過 **24 米**；**旋轉門**及回轉欄旁須設另一出口。
    - **第 VI 部（第 45–49 條）**：住用建築物的廚房；唐樓規定；與高逾 **4.5 米**的擋土牆之間須留 **1.5 米**；住用建築物**不得兼作危險行業**。
    - **第 VIA 部（第 49A–49E 條）**：**公眾娛樂場所**須位於只作該用途的建築物，**電影院**除外（在混合用途建築物最多 **2 000** 人，只限非住用部分）；公眾娛樂場所內不得有住用設備；層級斜度 ≤ **35°**，淨高 ≥ **3 米**。

## Regulation by regulation 逐條摘要 {#regulation-by-regulation}

### Part V Staircases, fire escapes and access for firefighting 第 V 部 樓梯、走火通道及消防救援進出途徑

| Reg. 條 | Title 標題 | Summary 摘要 |
|---|---|---|
| 38 | (Repealed) 已廢除 | — |
| 39 | Staircases 樓梯 | Staircase for buildings > 1 storey; main staircase to the **roof** if > 4 storeys (unless a second staircase); main staircase: **2 m** clear height, **900 mm** clear width, tread ≥ **225 mm**, riser ≤ **175 mm**, ≤ **16** steps per flight, handrails (tubular **38–50 mm** dia.; rectangular **40–50 mm** wide, ≤ 50 mm deep), leads to a street or open space, glazed roof door. 主樓梯尺寸及扶手。 |
| 40 | Staircases in buildings for separate occupation 獨立佔用建築物的樓梯 | Common staircase in a building for > 2 tenants: **natural lighting at each storey** above G/F and ventilation at least at the top. 每層須有天然照明，頂部須通風。 |
| 40A | Escalators 自動梯 | Escalators are **in addition** to required staircases and means of escape, and do not count as normal egress for reg. 43. 自動梯不計作樓梯或逃生途徑。 |
| 41 | Means of escape 逃生途徑 | MOE as required by the use; **second staircase** if > **6 storeys** or top floor > **17 m** above ground at the main staircase exit. 第二道樓梯。 |
| 41A | Access staircases for firemen 供消防員使用的通道樓梯 | Adequate number of protected access staircases (by use and floor size), except houses ≤ 3 storeys for a single household. 足夠數目並受保護的通道樓梯。 |
| 41B | Fireman's lifts 消防員升降機 | Buildings > 2 storeys: enough fireman's lifts so that no part of a served floor is > **60 m** away; at least every alternate floor served; ≤ **3** fireman's lifts per liftwell and no other lifts; protected, exclusive fireman control. **Exempt**: single-staircase buildings ≤ 6 storeys / 17 m; domestic ≤ **30 m**; certain non-domestic ≤ **15 m**, or ≤ 30 m and ≤ **7 000 m³**. 60 米；豁免情況。 |
| 41C | Firefighting and rescue stairway 消防和救援樓梯間 | Industrial / bulk storage / warehouse buildings > **30 m**, or > **7 000 m³** and > 1 storey; basements > **7 000 m³** and > 2 storeys or > **9 m** deep: FRS (access staircase + fireman's lift) within **60 m** of every part; only fireman's lifts (≤ 3) in it; serves the roof if accessible. 工業建築物及大型地庫。 |
| 41D | Emergency vehicular access 緊急車輛通道 | EVA for FSD vehicles to BA's requirements by building use; BA may **exempt** (impracticable terrain or low fire risk) and require other measures. 可豁免但須有其他措施。 |
| 42 | Landings, lobbies and passages 樓梯平台、門廊及通道 | Minimum dimension ≥ the **width of the staircase** they lead to. 不少於樓梯闊度。 |
| 43 | Distance from staircase 與樓梯的距離 | Any part of a habitation building, school or place of public assembly ≤ **24 m** from a staircase, passage or other normal egress. ≤ 24 米。 |
| 44 | Revolving doors and turnstiles 旋轉門及回轉欄 | Alternative exit close by; turnstiles in places of public assembly kept clear of the exit line. 旁邊須設另一出口。 |

### Part VI Domestic buildings 第 VI 部 住用建築物

| Reg. 條 | Title 標題 | Summary 摘要 |
|---|---|---|
| 45 | Kitchens 廚房 | Kitchen in every domestic building / separately let dwelling (unless exempted); walls faced with tiles, ≥ **12.5 mm** cement render or non-absorbent material to **1.2 m**; cooking slab (unless gas / oil / electric) and sink with water supply. 牆身 1.2 米高須鋪瓷磚或批盪。 |
| 46 | Tenement house 唐樓 | Depth front to rear ≤ **10 m** (unless exempted); rear window each storey with glass ≥ **1.5 m²** and opening ≥ **1.5 m²**; kitchen ≥ **3.75 / 4.5 / 5.5 m²** for units ≤ 45 / ≤ 70 / > 70 m², min. **1.5 m** wide. Tenement house = living rooms for more than one tenant. 唐樓深度、窗及廚房面積。 |
| 47 | Building abutting on retaining wall 緊連擋土牆的建築物 | No domestic building against a retaining wall (or massive rock face) > **4.5 m** high; keep **1.5 m** clear from its bottom. 須留 1.5 米。 |
| 48 | Retaining wall forming part of a building 擋土牆構成建築物一部分 | Waterproofed and insulated against damp and condensation (subject to reg. 27). 防水及隔熱。 |
| 49 | No domestic use with dangerous trades 不得兼作危險行業 | Domestic buildings must not also be used for dangerous goods manufacture / storage, motor repair, vulcanizing, car painting, paint mixing or dry-cleaning (unless exempted); caretaker's quarters ≤ **50 m²** allowed in such buildings. 管理員宿舍 ≤ 50 平方米。 |

### Part VIA Places of public entertainment 第 VIA 部 公眾娛樂場所

| Reg. 條 | Title 標題 | Summary 摘要 |
|---|---|---|
| 49A | Situation of a PPE 公眾娛樂場所的位置 | A PPE must not be in a building used for any other purpose. 不得位於作其他用途的建築物內。 |
| 49B | Cinemas 電影院 | Exception: a cinema may be in a mixed-use building, only in the **non-domestic** part, max. **2 000** persons (aggregate for all cinemas); not with motor repair, petrol station, godown, factory, school, child care centre, elderly or disability home; may share MOE, entrance, foyer, waiting space to BA's satisfaction. 電影院例外規定。 |

| 49D | Floors and tiers 樓面及層級 | Gradient ≤ **35°**; ≥ **3 m** clear height above every floor / tier. 斜度 ≤ 35°；淨高 ≥ 3 米。 |
| 49E | Waiting accommodation 等候用設備 | Adequate waiting space for persons awaiting admission. 須設足夠等候地方。 |

## Key table: main staircase (reg. 39(3)) 主樓梯尺寸 {#key-table-main-staircase-reg-393}

| Item 項目 | Requirement 規定 |
|---|---|
| Clear height 淨高 | ≥ **2 m** |
| Clear width 淨闊 | ≥ **900 mm** |
| Tread (going) 級面 | ≥ **225 mm**, measured at the centre of the flight 在梯段中央量度 |
| Riser 豎板 | ≤ **175 mm** |
| Steps per flight 每段級數 | ≤ **16** without a landing 不加平台不得多於 16 級 |
| Handrail 扶手 | One or both sides; tubular **38–50 mm** OD; rectangular **40–50 mm** wide, ≤ **50 mm** deep 圓管 38–50 毫米；方形闊 40–50 毫米 |
| Roof 屋頂 | Continued to roof if > **4 storeys** (unless secondary staircase); roof door with **glazed upper panels** 超逾 4 層須通往屋頂 |

<small>For buildings to which reg. 73 applies, main circulation staircases on regulated routes need treads ≥ **300 mm**, risers ≤ **150 mm** and dual-level handrails (Fourth Schedule, see [Part X](bpr-71-73.md)). Means of escape staircases are sized under FS Code Part B. 適用第 73 條的建築物另有較嚴格要求。</small>

## Key table: firefighting access triggers 消防救援設施的觸發條件 {#key-table-firefighting-access-triggers}

| Provision 設施 | Required when 何時須設 | Not required 毋須設置 | Reg. 條 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Second staircase 第二道樓梯 | > **6 storeys**, or top floor > **17 m** above ground | — | 41(2) |
| Access staircase 通道樓梯 | Building > **1 storey** | House ≤ 3 storeys, single household | 41A |
| Fireman's lift 消防員升降機 | Building > **2 storeys**; ≤ **60 m** to every part of served floors | 1-staircase ≤ 6 storeys / 17 m; domestic ≤ **30 m**; ordinary non-domestic ≤ **15 m**, or ≤ 30 m and ≤ **7 000 m³** | 41B |
| Firefighting and rescue stairway 消防和救援樓梯間 | Industrial / storage / warehouse > **30 m**, or > **7 000 m³** and > 1 storey; basement > **7 000 m³** and (> 2 storeys or > **9 m** deep); ≤ **60 m** to every part | Other buildings | 41C |
| EVA 緊急車輛通道 | Every building | If exempted by BA (terrain / low risk), with other measures | 41D |

<small>Height for regs. 41B(4) and 41C(1)(a) is measured under [reg. 23(1)](bpr-23-23a.md). 高度按第 23(1) 條量度。</small>

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

!!! quote "Reg. 41 — Means of escape 逃生途徑"
    (1) Every building shall be provided with such means of escape in case of emergency as may be required by the intended use of the building. (2) Without prejudice to paragraph (1), every building which exceeds 6 storeys in height or in which the level of the floor of the uppermost storey is more than 17 m above the level of the ground at the point of discharge of the main staircase shall, in addition to the main staircase, be provided with a second staircase as means of escape in case of emergency.

    **官方中文本：** (1) 每幢建築物須設有該建築物擬作用途所需的緊急情況用的逃生途徑。(2) 在不損害第(1)款的原則下，每幢高度超逾6層的建築物，或最高樓層的樓面水平在主樓梯出口處的地面水平之上多於17米的建築物，除主樓梯外，須有第二道樓梯，作為緊急情況用的逃生途徑。

!!! quote "Reg. 41B(1) — Fireman's lifts 消防員升降機"
    Subject to paragraph (4), every building exceeding 2 storeys in height shall be provided with at least such number of lifts designed and installed to be used by firemen in the event of a fire (fireman's lift) as will ensure that no part of the floors that they serve are more than 60 m from such a lift.

    **官方中文本：** 除第(4)款另有規定外，每幢高度超逾2層的建築物須設有為供消防員在發生火警時使用而設計和裝置的升降機(消防員升降機)，而該等升降機的數目最少須足以確保升降機到達的各層沒有任何部分距離該等升降機多於60米。

## Worked example 計算例子 {#worked-example}

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    **(A) Office tower** (UC 4a), 28 storeys, roof of the highest usable floor **100 m** above the lowest street; 3 basement car park levels, **8,000 m³**.

    1. **Second staircase** (41(2)): > 6 storeys, so at least 2 required staircases; number and width from occupant capacity ([FS Code B4, B8, B12](../codes/fsc-part-b-means-of-escape.md#clause-by-clause)). 第二道樓梯。
    2. **Fireman's lift** (41B): non-domestic > **30 m**, not exempt; every part of served floors ≤ **60 m** from it, at least every **alternate floor**, ≤ 3 per liftwell ([D8, D9](../codes/fsc-part-d-means-of-access.md#clause-by-clause)). 消防員升降機。
    3. **FRS** (41C): not an industrial / warehouse building, so not for the tower; but the **basement** > 7,000 m³ and > 2 storeys, so FRS within 60 m of every part of the basement ([Table D1](../codes/fsc-part-d-means-of-access.md)). 地庫須設消防和救援樓梯間。
    4. **Refuge floors**: > 25 storeys, so refuge floor at no more than 25 storeys apart ([FS Code B18](../codes/fsc-part-b-means-of-escape.md#clause-by-clause)). 庇護層。
    5. **EVA** (41D): serve ≥ ¼ of the perimeter within 10 m ([D22–D23](../codes/fsc-part-d-means-of-access.md#clause-by-clause)), or seek exemption with an FSAR. 緊急車輛通道。

    **(B) Domestic block**, 25 storeys, **75 m**: second staircase (41(2)); fireman's lift (41B: domestic > 30 m); no refuge floor needed at 25 storeys or less (> 25 needs one, or the roof for 25–40 storeys under B18.5); every flat kitchen tiled to 1.2 m (45); caretaker's flat allowed (49(2)) only where dangerous trades are exempted. 住宅大廈。

    **(C) Cinema** of 1,500 seats on 3/F of a shopping centre: allowed under **49B** (non-domestic part, ≤ 2,000 persons, no school, child care centre or warehouse in the building), tiers ≤ **35°**, 3 m headroom (49D), waiting space 0.5 m² × ⅙ of seats ([B27.8](../codes/fsc-part-b-means-of-escape.md#clause-by-clause)). 商場電影院。

## Explanation 淺白解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    Part V is the **legal base** for fire escape and firefighting design. The regulations themselves are short and mostly say "adequate", so the **FS Code** fills in the detail. In design you apply the FS Code, and the regulations tell you the **thresholds** (6 storeys / 17 m for a second staircase; 2 storeys and 60 m for fireman's lifts; 30 m or 7 000 m³ for an FRS in industrial buildings).

    Part VI keeps **homes separate from dangers**: away from tall retaining walls, and not in the same building as dangerous trades. Tenement house rules (reg. 46) mostly matter for older buildings and A&A works.

    Part VIA keeps **crowds safe**. A place of public entertainment normally needs its own building, but cinemas are allowed in shopping centres up to 2 000 seats.

=== "中文"

    第 V 部是走火通道及消防救援設計的**法律基礎**。規例本身較簡短，多數只要求「足夠」，因此由**消防安全守則**補充細節。設計時應用消防安全守則，規例則訂出**門檻**（第二道樓梯：6 層／17 米；消防員升降機：2 層及 60 米；工業建築物的消防和救援樓梯間：30 米或 7 000 立方米）。

    第 VI 部使**住宅遠離危險**：遠離高擋土牆，亦不與危險行業同處一幢建築物。唐樓規定（第 46 條）主要與舊樓及改動加建工程有關。

    第 VIA 部保障**人群安全**：公眾娛樂場所一般須獨立成幢，但電影院可設於商場內，上限 2 000 座位。

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Counting an **escalator** as a required staircase or exit (reg. 40A).
        - A residential tower just over **30 m** without a fireman's lift. The domestic exemption (reg. 41B(4)(b)) stops at 30 m, measured under reg. 23(1).
        - A cinema in the **same building as a school, child care centre or elderly home** (reg. 49B(4)).
        - A house platform built against a slope wall **over 4.5 m** high without the **1.5 m** gap (reg. 47).
        - Staircase risers over **175 mm** in A&A works (e.g. a new internal staircase in a duplex).

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 把**自動梯**當作規定的樓梯或出口（第 40A 條）。
        - 住宅大廈剛超過 **30 米**卻沒有消防員升降機：住用豁免（第 41B(4)(b) 條）只限 30 米，並按第 23(1) 條量度。
        - 電影院與**學校、幼兒中心或安老院**位於同一建築物（第 49B(4) 條）。
        - 屋宇緊貼高逾 **4.5 米**的擋土牆而建，沒有留 **1.5 米**（第 47 條）。
        - 改動加建工程（例如複式單位新建室內樓梯）的豎板高於 **175 毫米**。
