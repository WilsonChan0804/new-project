## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - **Temporary buildings** (reg. 50) are buildings permitted on a **temporary basis**: needed for a short time, built of **short-lived materials**, or **contractor's sheds**. The BA may also include buildings on licensed land. Reg. 50(2) lists the short-lived materials (thin boarding, fibre board, plasterboard, canvas, unprotected sheet steel, any other combustible material, etc.).
    - The BA may issue a **permit** for a temporary building, with a time limit and conditions (reg. 51). Readily combustible temporary buildings must be at least **3 m** from other buildings and the site boundary, and this space must be kept clear (reg. 52).
    - **Contractor's sheds** need a permit application from the contractor. Sheds for habitation need a concrete floor or one raised ≥ **1 m** (reg. 53). Contractors must provide kitchens, latrines and proper drainage for workers (reg. 54), and make live cables safe (reg. 55).
    - **Hoardings, covered walkways and gantries**: a building owner who will erect, alter or demolish a building, or excavate, must submit plans and apply for a **permit** (regs. 64–65). They must be erected **before** works start and kept in repair; hoardings are close-boarded (except isolated sites); no advertisements other than the project description and names (reg. 66).
    - If owners or contractors fail, the BA may do the work and **recover the cost** (regs. 56, 67). A security deposit of up to **$500** may be required (regs. 57, 69). Permits may be cancelled (regs. 58, 65(2), 70).

=== "中文"

    - **臨時建築物**（第 50 條）指以**臨時性質**獲發准許證的建築物：只在短時間內需要、以**非耐用物料**建造，或屬**承建商屋棚**；建築事務監督亦可把建於持牌土地上的建築物包括在內。第 50(2) 條列出非耐用物料（薄木板、纖維板、石膏板、帆布、無保護的鐵片、任何其他可燃物料等）。
    - 建築事務監督可就臨時建築物發出**准許證**，並訂明期限及條件（第 51 條）。以易燃物料建造的臨時建築物須距其他建築物及地盤界線最少 **3 米**，該空間須保持暢通（第 52 條）。
    - **承建商屋棚**須由承建商申請准許證；作居住用途的屋棚須鋪混凝土地台或高於地面 ≥ **1 米**（第 53 條）；承建商須為工人提供廚房、廁所及妥善排水（第 54 條），並使帶電電纜安全（第 55 條）。
    - **圍板、有蓋人行道及門架**：擬建立、改動或拆卸建築物或進行挖掘的建築物擁有人，須呈交圖則並申請**准許證**（第 64–65 條）；須在工程**開始前**豎設並保持妥善維修；圍板須以密封木板建造（偏遠地盤除外）；除工程說明及有關人士姓名外不得展示廣告（第 66 條）。
    - 如擁有人或承建商未有遵辦，建築事務監督可自行進行工程並**追討費用**（第 56、67 條）；可要求繳存不超過 **$500** 的保證金（第 57、69 條）；准許證可被取消（第 58、65(2)、70 條）。

|---|---|---|
| 50 | Definition of temporary buildings and short lived materials 臨時建築物及非耐用物料的定義 | Temporary building = permitted temporarily: short-time need, short-lived materials, or contractor's shed (BA may include buildings on licensed land). Short-lived materials listed in reg. 50(2). 定義及物料清單。 |

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "Reg. 52 — Siting of temporary building 臨時建築物的定位"
    (1) No temporary building constructed of readily combustible materials shall be erected within 3 m of— (a) any other building; or (b) the boundary of the site within which it is located. (2) Such space of 3 m shall be kept clear.

    **官方中文本：** (1) 任何以隨時可能燃燒物料建造的臨時建築物，不得在以下地方的3米範圍內建立 —— (a) 任何其他建築物；或 (b) 其所位處地盤的界線。(2) 該3米的空間須保持暢通。

!!! quote "Reg. 64(1) — Plans of hoardings 圍板圖則"
    Every building owner who intends to— (a) erect, alter, or demolish any building; or (b) carry out any excavations, shall submit to the Building Authority plans of such hoardings, covered walkways and gantries as may be necessary for the safety and convenience of passers-by in the street, occupiers of adjoining premises, or any workmen employed on the work.

    **官方中文本：** 每名建築物擁有人如擬 —— (a) 建立、改動或拆卸任何建築物；或 (b) 進行任何挖掘工程，須向建築事務監督呈交就街道上行人、毗鄰處所的佔用人，或任何受僱進行該工程的工人的安全及方便而需要的圍板、有蓋人行道及門架的圖則。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    These Parts deal with **short-life structures**: site offices, workers' sheds and other temporary buildings, and the hoardings and gantries that protect the public around a site. The aims are **fire separation** (3 m for combustible structures), **basic hygiene** for workers, and **public safety** on the street.

    Permits are time-limited and can be cancelled. For hoardings over a street, the **Highways Department** excavation / road opening permits and Lands Department requirements apply as well.

=== "中文"

    這幾部處理**短期構築物**：地盤辦公室、工人屋棚及其他臨時建築物，以及在地盤周圍保護公眾的圍板及門架。目的在於**防火分隔**（可燃構築物須相距 3 米）、工人的**基本衞生**，以及街道上的**公眾安全**。

    准許證設有期限並可被取消。在街道上方豎設圍板，亦須符合**路政署**的挖掘／掘路許可證及地政總署的規定。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Starting demolition or excavation **before** the hoarding / gantry permit is issued and the hoarding is in place (reg. 66(1)).
        - Advertising on hoardings beyond the project description and names (reg. 66(4)).
        - Combustible site offices placed **within 3 m** of the boundary or other buildings (reg. 52).
        - Treating a "temporary" structure as exempt from approval. Without a BA permit, it is an unauthorised building work.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 在圍板／門架准許證發出及圍板豎設**之前**便開始拆卸或挖掘（第 66(1) 條）。
        - 圍板上展示工程說明及姓名以外的廣告（第 66(4) 條）。
        - 可燃的地盤辦公室設於界線或其他建築物 **3 米範圍內**（第 52 條）。
        - 以為「臨時」構築物毋須審批：沒有准許證即屬僭建。
