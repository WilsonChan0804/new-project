## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Section 2** defines the core terms. **Building** is very wide (it includes hoardings, piers, walls, tanks, signboards, etc.). **Building works** include site formation, ground investigation in scheduled areas, foundations, repairs, demolition, A&A and drainage works. A **new building** includes an existing building ≥ ½ rebuilt by volume, or with ≥ ½ of its main wall area reconstructed.
    - The BA keeps **registers** of **authorized persons** (architects, engineers, surveyors), **registered structural engineers**, **registered geotechnical engineers** and **registered inspectors** (s.3), and of **general building, specialist and minor works contractors** (s.8A).
    - **s.4**: anyone having building works or street works carried out must appoint an **AP as co-ordinator**, plus an **RSE** and **RGE** where required. They must **supervise** to the **supervision plan**, report any contravention shown on approved plans, and comply with the BO. (Minor works under simplified requirements follow s.4A, 4B instead.)
    - **s.9 / 9AA**: appoint an **RGBC** for general works, an **RSC** for specialized works and a **prescribed registered contractor** for minor works. The contractor gives **continuous supervision** to its supervision plan.
    - **Discipline** (s.5–7, 11–13): a disciplinary board may **remove** a name from the register, **suspend**, **reprimand**, or **fine** up to **$250,000** (or **$150,000** for minor works and window inspections). Findings are gazetted. Appeals go to the **Court of First Instance**.

=== "中文"

    - **第 2 條**界定核心詞彙：**建築物**的涵蓋範圍很廣（包括圍板、碼頭、牆、貯槽、招牌等）；**建築工程**包括地盤平整、附表所列地區內的土地勘測、基礎、修葺、拆卸、改動加建及排水工程；**新建築物**包括以體積計不少於一半重建，或主牆表面面積不少於一半須重建的現有建築物。
    - 建築事務監督備存**認可人士**（建築師、工程師、測量師）、**註冊結構工程師**、**註冊岩土工程師**及**註冊檢驗人員**名冊（第 3 條），以及**一般建築、專門及小型工程承建商**名冊（第 8A 條）。
    - **第 4 條**：由他人代為進行建築工程或街道工程的人，須委任**認可人士作統籌人**，並按規定委任**註冊結構工程師**及**註冊岩土工程師**；他們須按**監工計劃書監督**工程、通知建築事務監督已批准圖則所示工程會導致的違規情況，並遵從本條例。（按簡化規定進行的小型工程則按第 4A、4B 條。）
    - **第 9／9AA 條**：一般工程須委任**註冊一般建築承建商**，專門工程須委任**註冊專門承建商**，小型工程須委任**訂明註冊承建商**；承建商須按監工計劃書**不斷監督**。
    - **紀律處分**（第 5–7、11–13 條）：紀律委員會可命令從名冊**除名**、**暫停**、**譴責**或**罰款**最高 **$250,000**（小型工程及驗窗為 **$150,000**）；裁斷須刊憲；可向**原訟法庭**上訴。

## Key terms (s.2) 主要定義 {#key-terms-s2}

| Term 詞彙 | Meaning (summary) 定義（摘要） |
|---|---|
| **Building** 建築物 | Very wide: the whole or part of any domestic or public building, PPE, arch, bridge, chimney, dock, factory, garage, hoarding, pier, retaining wall, wall, tank, signboard and many other structures. 涵蓋範圍很廣。 |
| **Building works** 建築工程 | Any building construction, **site formation**, **ground investigation in scheduled areas**, **foundation**, **repairs, demolition, alteration, addition**, every kind of building operation, and **drainage works**. 包括修葺、拆卸、改動、加建及排水工程。 |
| **New building** 新建築物 | Any building erected hereafter, and an existing building **≥ ½ rebuilt by volume**, or altered so that **≥ ½ of the main wall area** is reconstructed. 一半重建即屬新建築物。 |
| **Dangerous building** 危險建築物 | A building whose condition causes risk of injury to occupiers, users of neighbouring buildings or the public. 有導致損傷危險的建築物。 |
| **Domestic / non-domestic / composite building** 住用／非住用／綜合用途建築物 | Domestic = for habitation (including hotels, guest-houses, hostels, dormitories); composite = partly domestic, partly non-domestic. 居住包括酒店、賓館、宿舍。 |
| **Site formation works** 地盤平整工程 | Excavation on sloping land, filling, landslip preventive / remedial works, groundwater drainage works. 斜坡挖掘、填土、防治山泥傾瀉工程。 |
| **Street** 街道 | Any square, court, alley, highway, lane, road, road-bridge, footpath or passage, whether a thoroughfare or not. 包括巷、路、行人路及通道。 |
| **Owner** 擁有人 | Includes Government lessees and licensees, mortgagees in possession and persons receiving rent. 包括承租人、管有承按人及收租人。 |
| **Minor works / simplified requirements** 小型工程／簡化規定 | Building works designated as minor works in the B(MW)R, carried out under the simplified requirements without prior approval (see [APP-147](../pnap/app-147.md)). 小型工程監管制度。 |
| **Prescribed building professional (PBP)** 訂明建築專業人士 | AP, RSE, RGE or RI. 認可人士、註冊結構工程師、註冊岩土工程師或註冊檢驗人員。 |
| **Prescribed registered contractor (PRC)** 訂明註冊承建商 | RGBC, RSC or RMWC. 註冊一般建築承建商、註冊專門承建商或註冊小型工程承建商。 |
| **Supervision plan** 監工計劃書 | Plan of safety management of building / street works, prepared under the technical memorandum (s.39A). 安全管理計劃。 |
| **Scheduled areas** 附表所列地區 | Areas in **Schedule 5**: (1) Mid-levels, (2) North-western NT, (3) railway protection areas, (4) Ma On Shan, (5) sewage tunnel protection areas, with special geotechnical controls. 附表 5：半山區、新界西北、鐵路保護範圍、馬鞍山、污水隧道保護範圍。 |

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

!!! quote "s.4(1), (3) — Appointment and duties of AP, RSE, RGE 認可人士等的委任及職責"
    (1) Subject to subsection (1A), every person for whom building works or street works are to be carried out shall appoint— (a) an authorized person as the co-ordinator of such building works or street works; (b) a registered structural engineer for the structural elements of such building works or street works if so required under this Ordinance; and (c) a registered geotechnical engineer for the geotechnical elements of such building works or street works if so required under this Ordinance. … (3) Any authorized person, any registered structural engineer and any registered geotechnical engineer appointed or nominated under subsection (1) or (2) shall— (a) supervise the carrying out of the building works or street works, as the case may be, in accordance with the supervision plan; (b) notify the Building Authority of any contravention of the regulations which would result from the carrying out of any work shown in any plan approved by the Building Authority in respect of the building works or street works; and (c) comply generally with this Ordinance.

    **官方中文本：** (1) 除第(1A)款另有規定外，每一名將由他人代為進行建築工程或街道工程的人 —— (a) 須委任一名認可人士，作為有關的建築工程或街道工程的統籌人；(b) (如本條例有所規定)須就該建築工程或街道工程中關於結構的部分，委任一名註冊結構工程師；及 (c) (如本條例有所規定)須就該建築工程或街道工程中關於岩土的部分，委任一名註冊岩土工程師。……(3) 任何獲根據第(1)或(2)款委任或指定的認可人士、註冊結構工程師及註冊岩土工程師，須 —— (a) 按照監工計劃書監督建築工程或街道工程(視屬何情況而定)的進行；(b) 就以下情況向建築事務監督作出通知：建築事務監督就該建築工程或街道工程批准的任何圖則所顯示的任何工程，如予進行會導致違反規例；及 (c) 全面遵從本條例的規定。

!!! quote "s.2 — New building 新建築物"
    new building means any building hereafter erected and also any existing building of which not less than one half measured by volume is rebuilt or which is altered to such an extent as to necessitate the reconstruction of not less than one half of the superficial area of the main walls;

    **官方中文本：** 新建築物指今後建成的任何建築物，以及任何以體積計不少於一半是重新興建的現有建築物，或任何曾予改動而其程度致使主牆的表面面積不少於一半需要重新建造的現有建築物；

## Explanation 淺白解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    The BO works through **registered people**. BD does not supervise sites itself. The owner appoints an **AP** (co-ordinator), an **RSE** and **RGE** where needed, and a **registered contractor**. Each has statutory duties and can be disciplined.

    The **supervision plan** (prepared under the Technical Memorandum, s.39A) is the link: the AP / RSE / RGE supervise to it, and the contractor provides continuous supervision to it. Deviating from it without reasonable cause is a disciplinary ground.

    The **"new building"** definition matters for A&A works. If you rebuild half the volume or half the main walls, the whole building must meet current rules for new buildings.

=== "中文"

    《建築物條例》透過**註冊人士**運作：屋宇署並不親自監督地盤。業主委任**認可人士**（統籌人）、按需要委任**註冊結構工程師**及**註冊岩土工程師**，以及**註冊承建商**；各人均有法定職責，並可被紀律處分。

    **監工計劃書**（按第 39A 條的技術備忘錄擬備）是其中的聯繫：認可人士等按此監督，承建商按此不斷監督；無合理因由偏離屬紀律處分理由。

    **「新建築物」**定義對改動加建工程很重要：如重建一半體積或一半主牆，整幢建築物須符合現行新建築物的規定。

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Not replacing an AP / RSE / RC promptly when one stops acting (s.4(2), s.9(3)–(4)). Works should stop until the new appointment is notified.
        - Specialized works (e.g. foundations, demolition, site formation) carried out by an RGBC that is **not registered in that category**. An **RSC** is needed (s.9(2)).
        - Owners "arranging" minor works without the required PBP / PRC, which is an **offence** under s.4A / s.9AA.
        - A&A works so extensive that the building becomes a **new building**.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 認可人士／結構工程師／承建商停止行事後沒有即時另行委任（第 4(2)、9(3)–(4) 條）；在通知新委任前應停工。
        - 專門工程（例如地基、拆卸、地盤平整）由**未註冊該類別**的一般建築承建商進行，應委任**註冊專門承建商**（第 9(2) 條）。
        - 業主「安排」小型工程而沒有委任所需的訂明建築專業人士／訂明註冊承建商，屬第 4A／9AA 條**罪行**。
        - 改動加建工程規模大至令建築物成為**新建築物**。
