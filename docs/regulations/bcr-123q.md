## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - The new B(C)R is **performance-based**: short functional requirements (adequate strength, stability, factor of safety, no damage to neighbours). The detailed methods are in BD's **Codes of Practice** (structural use of concrete, steel and glass, foundations, wind effects, site formation, etc.).
    - **Loads (Part 3)**: dead load, imposed load and wind load (return period **≥ 50 years**). **Minimum imposed loads in Table 1**, e.g. domestic **2 kPa**, offices **3 kPa**, shops / assembly without fixed seats **5 kPa**, plant rooms **7.5 kPa**, car parks (≤ 3 t) **3 kPa**. Partitions in unknown positions ≥ **⅓ of their weight per m** spread per m², and ≥ **1 kPa** in offices.
    - **Reductions** (s.8, Table 2): up to **40%** for columns / foundations carrying many floors (25% max for heavy factories); beams supporting ≥ **45 m²**: 5% per 45 m², max. 20%. No reduction for storage, vehicles, machinery, dynamic loads or unlocated partitions.
    - **Protective barriers** (ss. 9, 36–38, Table 3): horizontal line loads **0.75 / 1.5 / 3 kN/m** by crowd category; vehicle barriers by impact formula. Barriers needed at edges and where levels differ by **> 600 mm**, designed against falling through and climbing over.
    - **Industrial buildings and warehouses** must display **floor loading notices** at staircases (s.11). No overloading (s.12).
    - **Foundations, site investigation, site formation, retaining walls** (Parts 5–7): SI to recognised standards before foundations; **proof tests** on representative foundation units; retaining walls checked for sliding, overturning, bearing and deep-seated failure, with filters, drainage and channels; bulk excavation in Scheduled Area No. 1 limited.
    - **External walls, cladding, curtain walls** (Part 8): **non-combustible**, impervious materials; flexible joints and corrosion-protected fixings for cladding; curtain walls fixed by cast-in anchors or to steel, with drainage of seepage; **access for maintenance** of walls, cladding, curtain walls and roofs.
    - **Fire resisting construction** (s.35), **lifts and escalators** (ss. 39–41: safe space, restricted machine spaces, warning notices), wells, chimneys and flues, vermin, ducts (Part 12).

=== "中文"

    - 新《建築物（建造）規例》以**表現為本**：只訂明簡短的功能要求（足夠強度、穩定性、安全系數、不損害鄰近物業），詳細方法載於屋宇署的**作業守則**（混凝土、鋼結構及玻璃的結構使用、地基、風力效應、地盤平整等）。
    - **荷載（第 3 部）**：恆載、外加荷載及風荷載（重現期 **≥ 50 年**）。**表 1 最小外加荷載**，例如住用 **2 千帕**、辦公室 **3 千帕**、商店／沒有固定座位的集會場所 **5 千帕**、機房 **7.5 千帕**、停車場（≤ 3 公噸）**3 千帕**。位置未定的間隔牆按每米重量的 **⅓** 分布，辦公室不少於 **1 千帕**。
    - **折減**（第 8 條、表 2）：承托多層的柱／基礎最多折減 **40%**（重型工廠最多 25%）；承托 ≥ **45 平方米**樓面的樑：每 45 平方米 5%，最多 20%；貯物、車輛、機器、動力效應及位置未定的間隔牆不得折減。
    - **防護欄障**（第 9、36–38 條、表 3）：按人群類別施加 **0.75／1.5／3 千牛／米**水平線荷載；車輛欄障按撞擊公式。邊緣及相鄰水平相差 **> 600 毫米**時須設欄障，並防止墮下及攀越。
    - **工業建築物及倉庫**須在樓梯展示**樓面荷載告示**（第 11 條）；不得超載（第 12 條）。
    - **地基、地盤勘測、地盤平整、擋土牆**（第 5–7 部）：興建地基前按認可標準進行地盤勘測；代表性基礎單位須作**驗證測試**；擋土牆須檢查滑動、傾覆、承載及深層破壞，並設濾層、排水及明渠；附表所列地區第 1 號的大型挖掘須受限制。
    - **外牆、覆蓋層、幕牆**（第 8 部）：**不可燃**及不透水物料；覆蓋層須有伸縮縫及防銹固定件；幕牆以預埋錨固或固定於鋼構件，並排走滲水；外牆、覆蓋層、幕牆及屋頂須有**維修通道**。
    - **耐火結構**（第 35 條）、**升降機及自動梯**（第 39–41 條：安全空間、限進空間、警告告示）、水井、煙囱及煙道、蟲鼠、管道（第 12 部）。

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "s.37 — Provision of protective barrier 設置防護欄障"
    (1) A protective barrier must be provided at the edge of a balcony, verandah, floor, roof, staircase, landing or projection to restrict or control the movement of persons, objects and vehicles. (2) If the difference between 2 adjacent levels (whether or not within a building) exceeds 600 mm, a protective barrier must be provided at the higher level to restrict or control the movement of persons, objects and vehicles.

    **官方中文本：** (1) 在露台、外廊、樓面、屋頂、樓梯、樓梯平台或伸出物的邊緣，須設置防護欄障，以限制或管制人、物件及車輛的移動。(2) 如2個相鄰水平(不論是否在建築物內)之間的差距超逾600毫米，則須在較高水平的地方設置防護欄障，以限制或管制人、物件及車輛的移動。

!!! quote "s.35 — Fire resisting construction 耐火結構"
    A building must be designed and constructed so as to, in case of fire— (a) inhibit the spread of fire within the building and to the buildings nearby; (b) provide adequate resistance to the spread of fire and smoke— (i) between different buildings; and (ii) in the building between different uses; (c) maintain the stability of the building to— (i) allow adequate time for safe evacuation; (ii) allow adequate time for rescue and firefighting operation; and (iii) avoid any consequential damage to the buildings nearby; and (d) provide adequate resistance to the spread of fire over the roof of the building to any other building having regard to the location of the building.

    **官方中文本：** 建築物的設計及建造，須能在火警發生時 —— (a) 阻止火勢在該建築物內蔓延，以及阻止火勢蔓延至附近的建築物；(b) 有足夠抵禦火勢及煙的能力，使火勢及煙不致 —— (i) 在不同建築物之間蔓延；及 (ii) 在該建築物內不同用途之間蔓延；……

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    In 2021 Hong Kong replaced its old, prescriptive construction regulations (Cap. 123B, with many detailed numbers) with a short **performance-based** regulation (Cap. 123Q). The regulation now says **what must be achieved**. The **Codes of Practice** say **how**, and can be updated faster as technology changes.

    The **Schedule tables stay prescriptive**, because designers need definite numbers: Table 1 imposed loads, Table 2 reductions and Table 3 barrier loads. These are the most-used parts in daily structural design.

    For **A&A works**, check whether a change of use increases the imposed load (e.g. office 3 kPa to shop 5 kPa, or storage). This links to [APP-117](../pnap/app-117.md) and BO s.25 change of use.

=== "中文"

    2021 年香港以簡短的**表現為本**規例（第 123Q 章）取代舊有、內容詳細的訂明式建造規例（第 123B 章）。規例現時訂明**須達到甚麼**，**作業守則**則說明**如何達到**，可因應技術發展更快更新。

    **附表的表格仍屬訂明式**，因為設計師需要確實數字：表 1 外加荷載、表 2 折減及表 3 欄障荷載，是日常結構設計最常用的部分。

    **改動加建工程**須檢查更改用途會否增加外加荷載（例如辦公室 3 千帕改為商店 5 千帕，或改作貯物），這與 [APP-117](../pnap/app-117.md) 及《建築物條例》第 25 條更改用途有關。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Citing **repealed Cap. 123B** regulation numbers in new submissions.
        - Converting an office (3 kPa) to a **shop, restaurant, gym or storage** without checking the higher Table 1 load.
        - Applying imposed load **reductions to storage floors, car parks or plant rooms** (s.8(5)).
        - Barriers in malls, stations or stadiums designed for 1.5 kN/m where the area is **susceptible to overcrowding** (3 kN/m).
        - Missing the **floor loading notices** in industrial buildings and warehouses (s.11).
        - Combustible cladding or curtain wall materials (s.28, 30). They must be **non-combustible**.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 新呈交文件仍引用**已廢除的第 123B 章**條號。
        - 把辦公室（3 千帕）改為**商店、食肆、健身室或貯物**而沒有檢查表 1 較高的荷載。
        - 對**貯物樓層、停車場或機房**採用外加荷載折減（第 8(5) 條）。
        - 商場、車站或運動場等**容易過度擠迫**地方的欄障按 1.5 千牛／米設計（應為 3 千牛／米）。
        - 工業建築物及倉庫沒有展示**樓面荷載告示**（第 11 條）。
        - 覆蓋層或幕牆使用可燃物料（第 28、30 條）：必須**不可燃**。
