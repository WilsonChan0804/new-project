## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - **Reg. 2** defines the basic terms used throughout the B(P)R, such as **balcony**, **canopy**, **storey**, **site coverage**, **open air**, **usable floor space** and **street**. L.N. 52 of 2026 added **residential unit**, **single-household building** and **specified building**.
    - **Reg. 3A**: every opening in an external wall **above the ground floor** needs a barrier at least **1 100 mm** high, with the bottom **150 mm solid**, designed against falling through and climbing over.
    - **Reg. 4**: buildings must not obstruct or endanger street users or cause a nuisance. Exhaust must not discharge over a street **below 2.5 m**.
    - **Reg. 5**: every building needs **access from a street**. The BA may require an access lane or road within a new site.
    - **Part II** limits projections over streets. Most must be at least **2.5 m** above ground. Maximum projection is **500 mm** for eaves / mouldings, **300 mm** for pipes, **750 mm** for drying racks, A/C frames and ventilation ducts. Canopies need **5.5 m** clearance over roads (or within 600 mm of the kerb) and **3.3 m** over footpaths, and project at most **1/10 of the street width or 3 m**.

=== "中文"

    - **第 2 條**界定整套規例使用的基本詞彙，例如**露台**、**簷篷**、**樓層**、**上蓋面積**、**露天地方**、**實用樓面空間**及**街道**；2026 年第 52 號法律公告新增**住宅單位**、**單一住户建築物**及**指明建築物**。
    - **第 3A 條**：**地面層以上**外牆的每個開口須設高度不少於 **1 100 毫米**的欄障，最低 **150 毫米**須為實心，並須防止墮下及攀越。
    - **第 4 條**：建築物不得阻礙或危及街道使用者或造成滋擾；廢氣不得在 **2.5 米以下**排放至街道。
    - **第 5 條**：每幢建築物須有**從街道通往的途徑**；建築事務監督可要求在新地盤內闢設進出路徑或通路。
    - **第 II 部**限制伸出街道的伸出物：大部分須在地面以上最少 **2.5 米**；伸出上限為屋簷／裝飾線條 **500 毫米**、喉管 **300 毫米**、晾衣架、冷氣機支架及通風管道 **750 毫米**。簷篷在道路上方（或行人路外緣 600 毫米內）須有 **5.5 米**淨空，在行人路上方須有 **3.3 米**，伸出長度最多為**街道闊度的十分之一或 3 米**。

## Key terms (reg. 2) 主要定義

| Term 詞彙 | Meaning (summary) 定義（摘要） |
|---|---|
| **Balcony** 露台 | A structure projecting from a wall to carry a floor or roof load, cantilevered or on brackets. 自牆伸出以承托樓面或屋頂荷載的構築物。 |
| **Canopy** 簷篷 | Projects **> 500 mm** from a wall at **≤ 7.5 m** above ground, for rain or sun protection, carrying **no floor load**. 伸出 > 500 毫米、高度 ≤ 7.5 米、不承托樓面荷載。 |
| **Verandah** 外廊 | A structure projecting from a wall and **supported by piers or columns**. 以墩或柱支承的伸出構築物。 |
| **Storey** 樓層 | From the upper surface of a floor to the upper surface of the floor above; for the top storey, to the mean height of the ceiling or roof. 由樓面上表面量至上一層樓面上表面。 |
| **Site coverage** 上蓋面積 | The area of the site covered by the building. 地盤被建築物覆蓋的面積。 |
| **Open air** 露天地方 | Vertically uncovered space, ≥ **1.5 m** in every horizontal dimension; if enclosed on 4 sides, ≥ **1 m² per 6 m** of mean wall height. 垂直無遮蓋，每邊 ≥ 1.5 米。 |
| **Usable floor space** 實用樓面空間 | Floor space excluding staircases, lift landings, sanitary fitment space and lift / A/C machinery space. 不包括樓梯、升降機平台、衞生設備及機器所佔空間。 |
| **Street** 街道 | Includes footpaths and private and public streets. **Road** = a street excluding footpaths. 包括行人路及私家／公共街道；道路不包括行人路。 |
| **Detached / semi-detached building** 獨立／半獨立建築物 | Has a clear open space ≥ **2.3 m** wide along its full depth and at the rear. 須有闊 ≥ 2.3 米的空地。 |
| **Specified building** 指明建築物 (2026) | Any building other than a single-household building, a building wholly under Transitional Housing / Light Public Housing, or a Part VII temporary building or contractor's shed. Used by the elderly-friendly design rules (reg. 73). 用於第 73 條利便長者設計規定。 |

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "Reg. 3A — Protection of openings 開口的防護"
    (1) Every opening placed on an external wall above the ground floor of any building shall be protected by a barrier which shall be not less than 1 100 mm high and the lowermost 150 mm of such barrier shall be built solid.
    (2) A barrier provided under paragraph (1) shall be so designed as to minimize the risk of persons or objects falling, rolling, sliding or slipping through gaps in the barrier, or persons climbing over the barrier.

    **官方中文本：** (1) 每個設於任何建築物地面層之上外牆的開口，須以欄障防護，欄障高度不得少於1 100毫米，而最低的150毫米須為實心。(2) 根據第(1)款設置的欄障的設計，須盡量減低任何人或物件自欄障的縫隙墮下、滾下、滑下或跌出或任何人攀越欄障的危險。

!!! quote "Reg. 10(1), (2), (4) — Canopies over streets 街道上方的簷篷"
    (1) Every canopy erected within 600 mm of the outer edge of a footpath, or projecting over a road, shall have a clear space of not less than 5.5 m beneath every part thereof. (2) Every canopy erected over a footpath shall have a clear space of not less than 3.3 m beneath every part thereof. … (4) The maximum projection of any canopy (including cornices, mouldings or other features) erected over any street shall be— (a) one-tenth of the width of the street; or (b) 3 m, whichever is the less: Provided that no portion of any such canopy shall be within 4.5 m, measured horizontally, of a line drawn vertically from a point in the centre line of the street nearest to such portion of the canopy.

    **官方中文本：** (1) 每個在行人路外緣600毫米範圍內豎設或在道路上方伸出的簷篷，其每一部分之下須有不少於5.5米的淨空間。(2) 每個在行人路上方豎設的簷篷，其每一部分之下須有不少於3.3米的淨空間。……(4) 在任何街道上方豎設的任何簷篷(包括飛簷、裝飾線條或其他裝置)的伸出長度最多為 —— (a) 該街道闊度的十分之一；或 (b) 3米，兩者以較少者為準：但該等簷篷的任何部分不得位於自該街道的中心線最接近簷篷該部分之處劃的垂直線與簷篷等高之處的4.5米範圍內。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    Part I sets the **vocabulary**. Many arguments about GFA, projections or lighting come down to these definitions, e.g. whether a structure is a **canopy** (no floor load, ≤ 7.5 m high) or a **balcony** (carries a floor load).

    Part II protects **people using the street**. Anything over a street must be high enough not to hit pedestrians or vehicles (2.5 m, or 3.3 m / 5.5 m for canopies) and must not reach too far towards the middle of the street. Canopies must not be used as balconies, so no doors may open onto them.

    Reg. 3A is one of the most often used rules in design. It sets the **1.1 m barrier** at every opening above G/F. Details of protective barriers are in the B(C)R and the related PNAPs.

=== "中文"

    第 I 部訂立**詞彙**。很多有關總樓面面積、伸出物或照明的爭議，最終取決於這些定義，例如某構築物屬**簷篷**（不承托樓面荷載、高度 ≤ 7.5 米）還是**露台**（承托樓面荷載）。

    第 II 部保障**街道使用者**。任何伸出街道的物件須夠高，以免碰撞行人或車輛（2.5 米，簷篷則為 3.3 米／5.5 米），亦不得伸向街道中央太遠。簷篷不得當作露台使用，因此不得有門通往簷篷。

    第 3A 條是設計上最常用的規定之一：地面層以上每個開口須設 **1.1 米欄障**。保護欄障的細節見《建築物（建造）規例》及相關作業備考。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Measuring projections over the street from the **wrong line** (use the lot / street boundary) or forgetting the **4.5 m from centre line** limit for canopies on narrow streets.
        - A/C frames or drying racks projecting more than **750 mm** over a street, or lower than **2.5 m**.
        - A full-height window or door opening with **no 1.1 m barrier** (reg. 3A).
        - Utility room doors opening over a street that **obstruct** the footpath when fully open (reg. 15(2)).
        - A canopy with a door onto its top (reg. 12). This also raises GFA and UBW issues.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 由**錯誤的界線**量度伸出長度（應以地段／街道界線計），或在狹窄街道忽略簷篷「距中心線 **4.5 米**」的限制。
        - 冷氣機支架或晾衣架伸出街道多於 **750 毫米**或低於 **2.5 米**。
        - 落地窗或開口**沒有 1.1 米欄障**（第 3A 條）。
        - 向街道開啟的雜用房門在完全開啟時**阻礙**行人路（第 15(2) 條）。
        - 簷篷設有門通往其頂部（第 12 條），亦會引起總樓面面積及僭建問題。
