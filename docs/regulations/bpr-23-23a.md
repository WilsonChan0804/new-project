!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Applies 適用**: every calculation of **height, site area, GFA and SC / PR** under [regs. 19–22](bpr-18a-22.md), for all buildings (new and A&A); reg. 23A for **hotel** buildings and hotel parts of composite buildings. 所有建築物按第 19–22 條計算高度、地盤面積及總樓面面積；第 23A 條適用於旅館。

    **Not the same as 不等同**: **lease** GFA (checked by LandsD) and **OZP** GFA (PlanD), which may count differently, see [JPN 4](../pnap/jpn-4.md); **green and amenity features** exempted under **BO s.42** rather than reg. 23 ([APP-151](../pnap/app-151.md), [JPN 1](../pnap/jpn-1.md), [JPN 2](../pnap/jpn-2.md)). 地契及分區計劃大綱圖的總樓面面積另計；環保及適意設施經條例第 42 條寬免。

## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Building height** is measured from the **mean level of the street** (≥ 4.5 m, specified street) to the **mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor space**. Where streets are at different levels, use the **lowest** (reg. 23(1)).
    - **Site area** excludes streets and service lanes but **includes** areas dedicated for public passage (reg. 23(2)).
    - **GFA** = area **within the external walls** at every floor (**including basements**) + **balconies** + **thickness of external walls** (reg. 23(3)(a)).
    - The BA **may disregard** floor space used **solely** for carparking, loading/unloading, refuse facilities, telecom and broadcasting access, and lift / A/C / heating machinery (reg. 23(3)(b)).
    - **Hotels** may be treated as **non-domestic**, and pick-up/set-down areas and back-of-house supporting facilities may be disregarded. The use **cannot be changed** without BA approval (reg. 23A).

=== "中文"

    - **建築物高度**由**街道平均水平**（闊度 ≥ 4.5 米的指明街道）量度至**最高實用樓面空間之上屋頂的平均高度**；如街道水平不一，以**最低**的街道為準（第 23(1) 條）。
    - **地盤面積**不包括街道及通道巷，但**包括**撥作公眾通道的面積（第 23(2) 條）。
    - **總樓面面積** = 每層樓面（**包括地庫**）**外牆以內**的面積 + **露台** + **外牆厚度**（第 23(3)(a) 條）。
    - 建築事務監督**可不計算****純粹**用作停車、上落貨、垃圾設施、電訊及廣播接達設施，以及升降機、空調、暖氣機械的樓面空間（第 23(3)(b) 條）。
    - **酒店**可被視為**非住用**，其上落客區及後勤輔助設施可不計算總樓面面積；未經建築事務監督批准**不得改變用途**（第 23A 條）。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

!!! quote "Reg. 23(1) — Height of building 建築物高度"
    For the purposes of regulations 20, 21 and 22, the height of a building shall be measured from the mean level of the street or streets on which it fronts or abuts being a specified street or specified streets not less than 4.5 m wide, or where the building abuts on specified streets not less than 4.5 m wide having different levels, from the mean level of the lower or lowest of such streets, to the mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor space in the building.

    **官方中文本：** 就第20、21及22條而言，建築物的高度是由建築物所臨向或緊連而闊度不少於4.5米的一條或多於一條指明街道的平均水平或(如建築物緊連多於一條闊度不少於4.5米而水平不一的指明街道)由該等街道中最低的街道的平均水平，量度至建築物最高的實用樓面空間之上的屋頂的平均高度。

!!! quote "Reg. 23(2) — Site area 地盤面積"
    In determining for the purposes of regulation 20, 21 or 22 the area of the site on which a building is erected— (a) no account shall be taken of any part of any street or service lane; and (b) there shall be included any area dedicated to the public for the purposes of passage.

    **官方中文本：** 為施行第20、21或22條而釐定建有建築物的地盤所佔面積時 —— (a)無須考慮任何街道或通道巷的任何部分；及 (b)撥供公眾作通道用途的任何面積須包括在內。

!!! quote "Reg. 23(3)(a) — Definition of GFA 總樓面面積定義"
    Subject to subparagraph (b), for the purposes of regulations 19, 20, 21 and 22, the gross floor area of a building shall be the area contained within the external walls of the building measured at each floor level (including any floor below the level of the ground), together with the area of each balcony in the building, which shall be calculated from the overall dimensions of the balcony (including the thickness of the sides thereof), and the thickness of the external walls of the building.

    **官方中文本：** 除(b)段另有規定外，就第19、20、21及22條而言，建築物的總樓面面積為在每層樓面水平(包括地面水平以下的任何樓面)量度所得的建築物外牆以內面積，連同建築物內每個露台的面積(以露台整體尺寸計算，包括其圍邊的厚度)，以及建築物外牆的厚度。

!!! quote "Reg. 23(3)(b) — Floor space the BA may disregard 可不計算的樓面空間"
    In determining the gross floor area for the purposes of regulations 20, 21 and 22, the Building Authority may disregard any floor space that he is satisfied is constructed or intended to be used solely for parking motor vehicles, loading or unloading of motor vehicles, or for refuse storage chambers, refuse storage and material recovery chambers, material recovery chambers, refuse storage and material recovery rooms, refuse chutes, refuse hopper rooms and other types of facilities provided to facilitate the separation of refuse to the satisfaction of the Building Authority, or for access facilities for telecommunications and broadcasting services, or occupied solely by machinery or equipment for any lift, air-conditioning or heating system or any similar service.

    **官方中文本：** 為施行第20、21及22條而釐定總樓面面積時，建築事務監督如信納任何樓面空間有以下情況，即可不計算該樓面空間的面積：該樓面空間是純粹為下述用途而建或擬純粹用於下述用途的，即停泊汽車、汽車上落客貨、或垃圾房、垃圾及物料回收房、物料回收房、垃圾及物料回收室、垃圾槽、垃圾漏斗室以及為方便分隔垃圾而提供並令建築事務監督滿意的其他類型的設施、或為電訊及廣播服務而設的接達設施；或該樓面空間是純粹由任何升降機、空調或暖氣系統或任何相類設施的機械或設備所佔用的。

!!! quote "Reg. 23(4) — Caretaker's flat in a non-domestic building 管理員住所"
    …the Building Authority may treat as a non-domestic building a composite building in which the only domestic part of the building is a place of residence, not having more than 50 m² of usable floor space, for a caretaker… or a residence comprising the top storey of the building, or both.

    **官方中文本：** ……如任何綜合用途建築物內唯一的住用部分，是管理員……住用的地方(實用樓面空間不多於50平方米)，或是以該建築物頂層構成的住所，或兩者兼有，則建築事務監督可視該綜合用途建築物為非住用建築物。

!!! quote "Reg. 23A(2)–(3) — Hotels 旅館"
    (2) …the Building Authority may— (a) treat as a non-domestic building a building that he is satisfied is constructed or intended to be used as a hotel (hotel building); or (b) treat as a non-domestic part of a composite building the part in the building that he is satisfied is constructed or intended to be used as a hotel…
    (3) …the Building Authority may disregard any floor space… constructed or intended solely for use as— (a) a place for picking up and setting down persons departing from or arriving at the hotel by vehicle; or (b) any of the following— (i) a laundry, a carpentry workshop, a mechanical or electrical workshop; (ii) an area for storing dry goods, food, beverages, linen or furniture; (iii) facilities for the welfare of staff including staff canteen, changing room and rest room for staff; or (iv) other supporting facilities as may be approved by the Building Authority.

    **官方中文本：** (2)……建築事務監督可將 —— (a)任何他信納是建作或擬用作旅館的建築物(旅館建築物)視作非住用建築物；或 (b)任何在綜合用途建築物中他信納是建作或擬用作旅館的部分(建築物中的旅館部分)視作該建築物中的非住用部分。
    (3)……建築事務監督如信納其中的任何樓面空間是純粹為下述用途而建或擬純粹用於下述用途的，即可不計算該樓面空間的面積 —— (a)供人離開或到達該旅館時上落汽車之用；或 (b)任何下述用途 —— (i)洗衣房、木工工場、機械或電氣工場；(ii)貯存乾貨、食物、飲料、布料製品或傢俬的地方；(iii)職工福利設施，包括職工飯堂、更衣室及休息室；或 (iv)建築事務監督批准的其他輔助性設施。

## Clause by clause 逐款條文 {#clause-by-clause}

| Provision 條款 | What it requires 規定 | 中文要點 |
|---|---|---|
| **23(1)** | For regs. 20–22, building **height** is measured from the **mean level** of the specified street(s) ≥ **4.5 m** wide it fronts or abuts (the **lower / lowest** if levels differ) to the **mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor space** | 高度由闊度 ≥ 4.5 米指明街道的平均水平（不同水平取較低者）量至最高可用樓面上蓋天台的平均高度 |
| **23(2)(a)** | Site area excludes any part of a **street or service lane** | 地盤面積不計街道或後巷 |
| **23(2)(b)** | Site area **includes** any area **dedicated to the public for passage** | 撥作公眾通道的面積計入地盤面積 |
| **23(3)(a)** | **GFA** (regs. 19–22) = area within the **external walls** at **every floor** (incl. below ground), **plus balconies** (overall dimensions incl. sides) and **external wall thickness** | 總樓面面積：每層外牆以內面積（包括地庫），加露台（連圍邊）及外牆厚度 |
| **23(3)(b)** | For regs. 20–22 the BA **may disregard** floor space used solely for **parking**, **loading / unloading**, **refuse** storage and material recovery chambers / rooms, refuse chutes and hopper rooms and other refuse separation facilities, **telecom and broadcasting access facilities**, or occupied solely by **lift, A/C, heating or similar machinery** | 可不計：停車、上落貨、垃圾及物料回收設施、電訊廣播設施、升降機／空調／暖氣等機房 |
| **23(4)** | The BA may treat a composite building as **non-domestic** where its only domestic part is a **caretaker's** residence (≤ **50 m²** usable) and / or a residence on the **top storey** | 唯一住用部分為管理員住所（≤ 50 平方米）及／或頂層住所，可當作非住用建築物 |
| **23A(1)** | **Hotel** = premises held out as providing sleeping accommodation to any persons willing to pay | 「旅館」定義 |
| **23A(2)** | The BA may treat a **hotel building** as non-domestic, or the **hotel part** of a composite building as a non-domestic part | 旅館建築物或旅館部分可視作非住用 |
| **23A(3)(a)** | May disregard space solely for **picking up / setting down** hotel guests by vehicle | 可不計車輛上落客地方 |
| **23A(3)(b)** | May disregard **back-of-house**: (i) laundry, carpentry, M&E workshops; (ii) stores for dry goods, food, beverages, linen, furniture; (iii) **staff welfare** (canteen, changing, rest rooms); (iv) other approved supporting facilities | 可不計後勤設施：洗衣房、工場、貨倉、員工設施等 |
| **23A(4)** | Without the BA's **prior approval**, no **change of use** of a hotel treated as non-domestic to a non-hotel use | 未經批准不得改作非旅館用途 |
| **23A(5)** | Deemed change of use if, since first use, **no hotel licence** (Cap. 349) is in effect or an **exclusion order** is in force | 沒有酒店牌照或有豁除令即當作改變用途 |
| **23A(6)** | Disregarded space under (3) must not be used for other purposes without prior approval | 獲寬免的地方不得改作他用 |
| **23A(7)** | The BA may order the proprietor, owner or occupier to **discontinue** a contravening use within a set time | 可發命令停止用途 |
| **23A(8)** | Contravening (4) or (6): **level 6 fine and 2 years**; defence of not knowing and could not reasonably discover | 違反：第 6 級罰款及監禁 2 年 |
| **23A(9)** | Failing to comply with a (7) order without reasonable excuse: **level 5 fine and 1 year**, plus **$5,000 per day** | 不遵從命令：第 5 級罰款、監禁 1 年，另每日 5,000 元 |

## Worked example 計算例子 {#worked-example}

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    **Site 地盤**: lot 1,050 m², of which **50 m²** is an existing service lane; fronts a 15 m street (mean level +5.0 mPD) and a 6 m street at the rear (+8.0 mPD). The owner **dedicates 30 m²** at the corner for public passage.

    1. **Site area** (23(2)): 1,050 − 50 (lane, excluded) = **1,000 m²**; the 30 m² dedicated area stays **included**. 後巷不計，撥作通道部分計入。
    2. **Height** (23(1)): both streets ≥ 4.5 m, so measure from the **lower** mean level, +5.0 mPD, to the mean roof height over the highest usable floor (+65.0 mPD) = **60 m**. This height selects the PR / SC row in the [First Schedule](bpr-18a-22.md). 由較低街道平均水平量起。
    3. **GFA** (23(3)(a)): 20 typical floors × 600 m² measured to the outside of the external walls, including balconies of 20 m² per floor = 20 × 620 = **12,400 m²**.
    4. **Disregarded** (23(3)(b)): 2 basement car park floors (1,800 m²), refuse storage and material recovery rooms, lift machine room and A/C plant room may be **disregarded**; the A/C plant room counts towards the **10% cap** ([APP-151](../pnap/app-151.md), [APP-2](../pnap/app-2.md)). 可不計，部分受 10% 上限。
    5. **Balconies**: count under 23(3)(a) unless exempted as a green feature under [JPN 1](../pnap/jpn-1.md) by s.42 modification. 露台須另申請寬免。
    6. **Hotel variant** (23A): if the tower is a hotel, the BA may treat it as non-domestic and disregard the drop-off, laundry, stores and staff canteen ([APP-40](../pnap/app-40.md)); changing it to flats later needs **prior approval** (23A(4)), and running without a hotel licence counts as a change of use (23A(5)). 酒店寬免及改變用途限制。

## Explanation 淺白解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    Reg. 23 gives the **definitions** behind the numbers in regs. 20–22:

    - **Height** decides which row of the First Schedule you use. It is measured to the roof over the **highest usable floor**, so roof-top structures above that roof (e.g. plant rooms that are not usable floor space) generally do not count in this height.
    - **Site area** is the denominator of plot ratio. Streets and service lanes inside the lot boundary are excluded, but land dedicated for public passage stays in.
    - **GFA** is a "gross" measure. It is measured to the **outside face** of external walls and includes basements.

    "May disregard" in 23(3)(b) is a **discretion of the BA**. The BA uses its practice notes (APP-2, APP-35, APP-84, APP-111 etc.) to decide whether the space is really "solely" for that use and of a reasonable size. Since 2011, non-essential plant rooms are also tied to APP-151's pre-requisites and 10% cap.

    **Hotel concession (23A):** treating a hotel as non-domestic gives it the higher non-domestic PR. In return, the owner **must not change the use** without BA approval. If the hotel has no valid licence under Cap. 349, that is treated as a change of use (reg. 23A(5)).

=== "中文"

    第 23 條提供第 20–22 條所用數字背後的**定義**：

    - **高度**決定使用附表 1 的哪一行。高度量度至**最高實用樓層**之上的屋頂，因此其上的天台構築物（例如不屬實用樓面空間的機房）一般不計入此高度。
    - **地盤面積**是地積比率的分母。地段界線內的街道及通道巷須扣除，但撥作公眾通道的土地仍須計入。
    - **總樓面面積**屬「總」面積，量度至外牆**外邊**，並包括地庫。

    第 23(3)(b) 條的「可不計算」屬**建築事務監督的酌情權**。建築事務監督會按作業備考（APP-2、APP-35、APP-84、APP-111 等）判斷該空間是否「純粹」作該用途及面積是否合理。自 2011 年起，非必要機房亦須符合 APP-151 的先決條件及 10% 上限。

    **酒店寬免（第 23A 條）**：酒店被視為非住用，可享較高的非住用地積比率；作為交換，業主未經批准**不得改變用途**。如酒店沒有《旅館業條例》（第 349 章）下的有效牌照，即被當作改變用途（第 23A(5) 條）。

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Measuring height from the **wrong street**. Where streets have different levels, use the **lower or lowest** specified street ≥ 4.5 m.
        - Forgetting that **basements count** as GFA unless the use is disregarded under 23(3)(b).
        - Plant rooms must be used **solely** for the machinery. Mixed-use or oversized rooms are not disregarded.
        - Hotel concession areas under 23A(3) **cannot be converted** to other uses later without BA approval (reg. 23A(6)).

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 由**錯誤的街道**量度高度：街道水平不一時，須以闊度 ≥ 4.5 米的指明街道中**最低**者為準。
        - 忘記**地庫須計入**總樓面面積，除非其用途屬第 23(3)(b) 條可不計算的類別。
        - 機房必須**純粹**放置機械，混合用途或面積過大的機房不會獲豁免。
        - 根據第 23A(3) 條不計算的酒店面積，日後未經建築事務監督批准**不得改作其他用途**（第 23A(6) 條）。
