!!! tip "Does it apply? 是否適用"
    **Reg. 71**: any window, vent or airshaft that the regulations require to face the external or open air (regs. 30–36, 40, 46). **Reg. 72 (barrier free)**: every building persons with a disability may be expected to use, to the extent in **Third Schedule Part 4**; **not** single-household buildings **≤ 13 m** or Part VII temporary buildings. **Reg. 73 (elderly-friendly)**: **specified** residential and commercial buildings (and buildings containing such units), and single-household buildings **> 13 m**. 第 72 條：13 米或以下單一住戶及臨時建築物除外；第 73 條：指明住宅及商業建築物，及 13 米以上單一住戶建築物。

    **Guidance 指引**: design figures in the [DMUA 2026](../codes/dmua-2026.md) and [BFA 2008](../codes/bfa-2008.md); PNAP [APP-41](../pnap/app-41.md) (barrier free) and [APP-175](../pnap/app-175.md) (elderly-friendly). Definitions of specified building and residential unit: [B(P)R Parts I–II](bpr-parts-1-2.md). 設計手冊及作業備考。

## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **Reg. 71**: the BA may allow a window, vent or airshaft that must face the external / open air to face an **unenclosed verandah, balcony or other unenclosed place** instead, if lighting, ventilation and health standards are not prejudiced. Conditions may be attached.
    - **Reg. 72**: buildings that persons with a disability have, or may be expected to have, access to must be designed for their **access and use**. Compliance with **Part 2 of the Third Schedule** is deemed to comply. **Part 4** limits how far reg. 72 applies to domestic and composite buildings. Not applicable to **single-household buildings ≤ 13 m** high or Part VII temporary buildings.
    - **Reg. 73 (2026)**: **specified buildings** that are residential or commercial (or contain residential / commercial units), and single-household buildings **over 13 m**, must be designed to help **elderly persons**. Deemed compliance is through the **Fourth Schedule**: Part 2 for the building's regulated routes, Part 3 for main entrance doors and wet areas of flats and houses.
    - **Fourth Schedule key numbers**: indoor regulated routes **≥ 1 200 mm** clear, ≥ **120 lux**, no wall projections > **90 mm** below 2 m; main circulation staircases tread ≥ **300 mm**, riser ≤ **150 mm**, **dual-level handrails**; doors ≥ **850 mm** clear; thresholds ≤ **15 mm**; accessible lifts ≥ **1 200 × 1 400 mm** with mirror finishes; **automatic main entrance door** for residential buildings; **slip-resistant** floors in bathrooms, kitchens and toilets.

=== "中文"

    - **第 71 條**：須面向室外／露天地方的窗、通風口或通風豎井，建築事務監督可准許改為面向**未圍封的外廊、露台或其他未圍封地方**，但不得有損照明、通風及健康標準；可附加條件。
    - **第 72 條**：殘疾人士有或預期會有進出的建築物，其設計須便利殘疾人士**進出和使用**；符合**附表 3 第 2 部**即當作符合；**第 4 部**限制第 72 條對住用及綜合用途建築物的適用範圍；不適用於高度 **≤ 13 米的單一住户建築物**及第 VII 部臨時建築物。
    - **第 73 條（2026 年）**：屬住宅或商業建築物（或內有住宅／商業單位）的**指明建築物**，以及高度**超過 13 米**的單一住户建築物，其設計須**利便長者**；符合**附表 4** 即視為符合：第 2 部適用於建築物的受規管路徑，第 3 部適用於住宅單位及屋宇的主要入口門及濕區。
    - **附表 4 主要數字**：室內受規管路徑淨闊 **≥ 1 200 毫米**、照明 ≥ **120 勒克斯**、2 米以下牆身伸出物 ≤ **90 毫米**；主要往來樓梯級面 ≥ **300 毫米**、豎板 ≤ **150 毫米**、**雙層扶手**；門淨闊 ≥ **850 毫米**；門檻 ≤ **15 毫米**；暢通易達升降機 ≥ **1 200 × 1 400 毫米**並設鏡面；住宅建築物主要入口須設**自動門**；浴室、廚房及洗手間地面須**防滑**。

## Key clauses 主要條文 {#key-clauses}

!!! quote "Reg. 72(1) — Persons with a disability 殘疾人士"
    Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4) and notwithstanding any other provisions (other than the provisions under this regulation and regulation 73) in these regulations, where a building is one to which persons with a disability have, or may reasonably be expected to have, access, that building shall be designed to the satisfaction of the Building Authority in such a manner as will facilitate the access to, and use of, that building and its facilities by persons with a disability.

    **官方中文本：** 除第(3)及(4)款另有規定外，並且儘管有本規例的任何其他條文(本條及第73條的條文除外)的規定，任何建築物如有或在合理情況下預期會有殘疾人士進出，則該建築物的設計須能便利殘疾人士進出和使用建築物及其設施，並達到建築事務監督滿意的程度。

!!! quote "Reg. 73(1)–(2) — Elderly persons 長者"
    (1) This regulation applies to— (a) a specified building— (i) that is a residential building; (ii) that is a commercial building; or (iii) that is neither a residential building nor a commercial building but in which there is a residential unit or commercial unit; or (b) a single-household building of over 13 m in height above ground level. (2) Despite any other provisions (other than regulation 72) in these regulations, a building to which this regulation applies or a residential unit in a building that falls within paragraph (1)(a) must be designed to the satisfaction of the Building Authority in a manner that will facilitate the access to, and use of, the building or unit and its facilities by elderly persons.

    **官方中文本：** (1) 本條適用於 —— (a) 符合以下描述的指明建築物 —— (i) 屬住宅建築物；(ii) 屬商業建築物；或 (iii) 並非住宅建築物或商業建築物，但屬內有住宅單位或商業單位的建築物；或 (b) 高度超過地面水平以上13米的單一住户建築物。(2) 儘管有本規例任何其他條文(第72條除外)的規定，本條適用的建築物，或第(1)(a)款所指的建築物的住宅單位，其設計須能利便長者進出和使用該建築物或單位及該建築物或單位的設施，並達到建築事務監督滿意的程度。

## Worked example 例子 {#worked-example}

!!! example "Illustrative only 只作說明"
    **(A) 30-storey residential tower** (a specified residential building):

    - **Reg. 72, Third Schedule Part 4**: domestic > 4 storeys, so **all common areas** are designed for persons with a disability (access route, ramps, doors, accessible lift, accessible toilet in common facilities, signs); plant rooms and maintenance-only spaces excluded. Accessible parking per Sch. 3 Div. 3. 所有公用地方須無障礙。
    - **Reg. 73, Fourth Schedule Part 2** (common parts): direct route from a prominent point on the lot boundary to the main entrance; route **≥ 1,200 mm**, slip-resistant, **≥ 120 lux**; main circulation staircase tread **≥ 300**, riser **≤ 150 mm**, **dual-level handrails**; doors **≥ 850 mm**, threshold **≤ 15 mm**; accessible lift car **≥ 1,200 × 1,400 mm** with a mirror-like panel; **automatic door** at one main residents' entrance. 公用部分長者友善設計。
    - **Part 3** (each flat): main door **≥ 850 mm**, threshold ≤ 15 mm; **slip-resistant** bathroom, kitchen and toilet floors. 單位內要求。
    - **Reg. 71**: a kitchen window opening onto an **unenclosed balcony** may be permitted if lighting, ventilation and health are not prejudiced, with conditions ([JPN 1](../pnap/jpn-1.md) balconies). 面向露台的窗。

    **(B) Single-household house**: **10 m** high, reg. 72 and reg. 73 do **not** apply. **15 m** high, reg. 72 applies (exemption is only ≤ 13 m), and reg. 73(1)(b) applies **Parts 2 and 3** of the Fourth Schedule. 獨立屋高度決定是否適用。

    **(C) Office A&A**: reg. 72 applies to the altered **non-domestic** parts (all parts); reg. 73 applies only if it is a specified commercial building. 辦公室改動工程。

## Explanation 淺白解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    Reg. 72 is the legal base of **barrier free access**. Meet the Third Schedule and you are deemed to comply. In homes, it mainly covers **common areas**, not the inside of flats.

    Reg. 73 goes further for an **ageing population**. It reaches **inside flats** (main entrance door width and threshold, slip-resistant wet areas) and raises standards on common routes: gentler stairs, two-height handrails, wider corridors, brighter lighting, bigger lifts and automatic entrance doors.

    Reg. 71 is a long-standing relief: a window opening onto an **open balcony** can still count for lighting and ventilation, at the BA's discretion.

=== "中文"

    第 72 條是**暢通無阻通道**的法律基礎：符合附表 3 即當作符合。住宅方面主要涵蓋**公用地方**，而非單位內部。

    第 73 條為**人口老化**進一步提高要求：延伸至**單位內部**（主要入口門闊度及門檻、濕區防滑），並提高公用路徑標準：較平緩的樓梯、雙層扶手、較闊走廊、較光照明、較大升降機及自動入口門。

    第 71 條是一項長期的寬免：窗向**開敞露台**開啟，經建築事務監督酌情批准，仍可計作照明及通風。

## Practical notes 實務提示 {#practical-notes}

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Assuming the BFA rules cover flat interiors. Reg. 72 does not, but **reg. 73 / Sch. 4 Part 3 now does** for main entrance doors and wet areas.
        - Using the old staircase standard (175 mm riser) on a **main circulation staircase** that is a regulated route under Sch. 4 (≤ 150 mm riser, ≥ 300 mm tread).
        - Fire hose reels, signage boxes or meters projecting more than **90 mm** into corridors below 2 m.
        - A raised threshold over **15 mm** at the flat entrance or on corridor doors.
        - Houses over **13 m** high: both reg. 72 and reg. 73 now apply.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 以為無障礙規定涵蓋單位內部：第 72 條不涵蓋，但**第 73 條／附表 4 第 3 部現已涵蓋**主要入口門及濕區。
        - 附表 4 受規管路徑上的**主要往來樓梯**仍沿用舊標準（豎板 175 毫米），應為豎板 ≤ 150 毫米、級面 ≥ 300 毫米。
        - 消防喉轆、標誌箱或錶房在 2 米以下伸入走廊多於 **90 毫米**。
        - 單位入口或走廊門的門檻高於 **15 毫米**。
        - 高度超過 **13 米**的屋宇：現時須同時符合第 72 及 73 條。
