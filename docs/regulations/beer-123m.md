## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Applies to **commercial buildings** (offices, shops, department stores, PPEs, places of assembly, restaurants) and **hotels** (Schedule); not domestic, industrial, schools, car parks or bulk storage. In mixed buildings, only those parts, excluding refuge floors and car parks (s.3).
    - Buildings must be **energy efficient** to the BA's satisfaction; a suitable **overall thermal transfer value (OTTV)** of external walls and roofs is deemed to comply (s.4). See APP-67 for OTTV limits and method.
    - Plans must show external wall and roof materials, colours and details; per-floor **window schedules** by orientation, glass type and thickness; U-values of composite walls and roofs; and the **OTTV statement and calculations** (s.5).
    - **Projections ≤ 1.5 m** that help achieve the OTTV (e.g. **sunshades**) are disregarded for site coverage, plot ratio and open space under B(P)R 20, 22, 31 (s.6).

=== "中文"

    - 適用於**商業建築物**（辦公室、商店、百貨公司、公眾娛樂場所、集會場所、食肆）及**酒店**（附表）；不包括住用、工業、學校、停車場或大量貯存；混合用途建築物只適用於該等部分，不包括庇護層及停車場（第 3 條）。
    - 建築物須具令建築事務監督滿意的**能源效率**；外牆及屋頂有適當的**總熱傳送值**即視為符合（第 4 條）；限值及計算方法見 APP-67。
    - 圖則須顯示外牆及屋頂的物料、顏色及細節；按方向列出每層**窗戶表**、玻璃種類及厚度；複合牆及屋頂的熱傳送值；以及**總熱傳送值聲明及計算**（第 5 條）。
    - 有助達致總熱傳送值、伸出外牆 **≤ 1.5 米**的伸出物（例如**遮陽板**）在 B(P)R 第 20、22、31 條計算上蓋面積、地積比率及空地時不計算在內（第 6 條）。


## Key clause 主要條文

!!! quote "Section 4 Energy efficiency 第 4 條"
    (1) A building to which this Regulation applies shall be so designed and constructed as to achieve energy efficiency to the satisfaction of the Building Authority. (2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), a building shall be regarded as being designed and constructed in compliance with that subsection if its external walls and roofs have a suitable overall thermal transfer value.

    **官方中文本：** (1)本規例適用的建築物在設計和建造上須具令建築事務監督滿意的能源效率。(2)在不損害第(1)款的一般性的原則下，如某建築物的外牆及屋頂有適當的總熱傳送值，該建築物須視作在設計和建造上符合該款的規定。
