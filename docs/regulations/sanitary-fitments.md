## How to calculate 計算步驟 {#method}

| Step 步驟 | What to do 做法 | Reg. 條 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify the **use** of each part (residential, workplace, restaurant, arcade, PPE, cinema, etc.). Mixed buildings: calculate **each use separately**. 確定每部分用途；混合用途分開計算。 | 3 |
| 2 | Measure the **usable floor area (UFA)** — floors **less** staircases, public circulation, lift landings, kitchens in self-contained flats, lavatories, and lift / A/C / utility machinery spaces. Arcades: less staircases, lift landings, lavatories and plant only (passageways **count**). 計算實用樓面面積（扣除樓梯、公用通道、升降機大堂、自成一套單位廚房、洗手間及機房；商場通道計入）。 | 3, 7A(4) |
| 3 | Persons = UFA ÷ **occupancy rate** (or number of **seats**). 人數 = 實用樓面面積 ÷ 比率（或座位數）。 | see table below |
| 4 | Split by the **deemed male : female ratio** (1:1 or 1:1.5). 按當作男女比例分配。 | each reg. |
| 5 | Apply the **table** for each sex; "**or part of those persons**" means round **up**. 查表；「不足之數亦作計算」即向上取整。 | Tables 1–28 |
| 6 | Put male and female fitments in **separate rooms** used exclusively by each sex (except residential, ≤ 10-person workplaces and < 25-person restaurants). 男女設備分房（例外見下）。 | 5(4), 6(2), 7(2), 7A(2), 7B(2), 7C(2), 8(1C) |
| 7 | Add **accessible** WC cubicles and unisex toilets under the [BFA Design Manual](../codes/bfa-div-10-12.md) (Third Schedule B(P)R). 另加暢通易達設施。 | B(P)R 72 |

!!! note "Rounding of persons 人數取整"
    The regulation gives rates but not a rounding rule for the person count or the sex split. This page (and the calculator) rounds **persons up** and each sex **up**, a common conservative practice. Check with your AP / BD for borderline cases. 規例未列明人數及男女分配的取整方法，本頁一律向上取整（保守做法），臨界情況請與屋宇署確認。

"PPE" excludes sports stadia and cinemas. ## Residential buildings (reg. 4, Tables 1–4) 住宅建築物 {#residential}

Choose the table by how WCs are arranged. 按水廁安排選用表格。

**Table 1 – WCs not separated by sex** 不分男女

| Persons 人數 | WC fitments 水廁 |
|---|---|
| ≤ 8 | 1 |
| > 8 | 1 plus 1 for every 12 persons (or part) over 8 |

**Table 2 – separate WCs for each sex** 分男女

| Male (Part 1) / female (Part 2) persons 男／女人數 | WC fitments 水廁 |
|---|---|
| ≤ 8 | 1 |
| > 8 | 1 plus 1 for every 12 persons of that sex (or part) over 8 |

**Table 3 – separate WCs, with urinals for males** 分男女並設尿廁（男）

| Male persons 男 | WC 水廁 | Urinals 尿廁 |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 12 | 1 | 1 |
| > 12 | 1 plus 1 per 12 (or part) over 12 | 1 plus 1 per 12 (or part) over 12 |

**Table 4 – basins and baths / showers** 洗手盆及浴缸或花灑

| Persons 人數 | Lavatory basins 洗手盆 | Baths or showers 浴缸或花灑 |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 8 | 1 | 1 |
| > 8 | 1 plus 1 per 12 (or part) over 8 | 1 plus 1 per 12 (or part) over 8 |

- **Tenement house** (reg. 4(2)): water supply points may replace basins and baths / showers; 1 point = 1 basin + 1 bath / shower; number ≥ basins required; in a separate compartment **≥ 0.75 m²** or in a WC; a kitchen sink tap does **not** count. 唐樓可以供水點代替，供水點須設於不少於 0.75 平方米的獨立隔間或水廁內。
- Location of fitments to BA's satisfaction (reg. 4(3)). 位置須令建築事務監督滿意。

## Equivalents, latrines and water supply (regs. 9, 10A) 等同設備、廁所及供水 {#equivalents}

| Rule 規則 | Content 內容 | Reg. 條 |
|---|---|---|
| Soil fitments prohibited (reg. 89) 禁止便溺設備 | Latrine fitments replace WCs, bucket urinals replace urinals, in at least the same numbers. 以廁所設備及便桶尿廁代替，數目相同。 | 9(1) |
| **Trough WC** 槽式水廁 | Every **1 m** = 1 WC fitment. 每 1 米當 1 個水廁。 | 9(2) |
| **Trough urinal** 槽式尿廁 | Every **0.5 m** = 1 urinal. 每 0.5 米當 1 個尿廁。 | 9(3) |
| Water supply 供水 | Permanent, sufficient supply for flushing and for waste fitments / showers: from **waterworks**; a well on site with BA's written permission; or another source BA permits. WSD certificate on availability (issued within **10 days**); well yield tested **1 Dec – 30 Apr** and certified by the AP. 須有永久充足供水；水井須批准並測試。 | 10A |

## When fitment numbers change (A&A checklist) 改變衞生設備數目時的檢查清單 {#aa-checklist}

| Check 檢查 | Notes 說明 | Where 參考 |
|---|---|---|
| 1. New use or new UFA? 新用途或面積改變？ | Recalculate persons for the **new** use (e.g. office → restaurant: 9 m² → 1.5 m², ratio 1:1 → 1:1.5). A change of use may also need a BA15 notice. 按新用途重算；改變用途或需 BA15。 | [BA15](../forms/nb-aa.md#ba15) · BO s.25 |
| 2. Reducing fitments? 減少設備？ | Must still meet the minimum for the whole use served; check whether the fitments are shared by other units / floors (common toilets). 減少後仍須符合整體最低要求。 | Tables above |
| 3. Accessible provision 暢通易達設施 | Accessible WC cubicles: **1** per floor where **≤ 20** WCs, **2** where **> 20** (per sex in multi-cubicle toilets); at least one **accessible unisex toilet** on every floor with toilets. 暢通易達水廁間及無分性別洗手間。 | [BFA Div. 10–12](../codes/bfa-div-10-12.md) |
| 4. Lighting and ventilation of toilets 洗手間照明及通風 | Window or mechanical ventilation for every lavatory / WC. 洗手間須有窗或機械通風。 | [B(P)R 36](bpr-24-37.md) · [Lighting & ventilation guide](../guides/lighting-ventilation.md) |
| 5. Drainage works 排水工程 | New soil / waste pipes, traps, vents and floor drains must meet Part III (trap seal ≥ 50 mm, soil pipe ≥ 80 mm, vent ≥ 80 mm) and Part IV drainage. 須符合第 III 及 IV 部。 | [Cap. 123I plumbing & drainage](bsfpwr-123i.md#key-table-plumbing-and-drainage-numbers-falls-of-drains) · [APP-93](../pnap/app-93.md) |
| 6. Approval route 審批途徑 | Drainage works generally need **approval and consent** (drainage plans, B(A)R reg. 8); some above-ground drainage alterations are **minor works** (Type D, Schedule 1 B(MW)R), e.g. via MW03 / MW01. Sunken slabs and cleaning access: APSEC 2023–2024. 一般需批准及同意；部分地面以上排水屬小型工程。 | [B(MW)R](bmwr-123n.md#key-table-the-eight-types-of-minor-works) · [MW forms](../forms/mw.md) · [BA5](../forms/nb-aa.md#ba5) |
| 7. Testing 測試 | Drainage tested before backfilling / covering. 回填前測試。 | [APP-58](../pnap/app-58.md) · reg. 73 |
| 8. Fitments installed before OP? 佔用許可證前安裝？ | Basic fitments should be installed before OP; deferral only by **modification** for restaurants / hotels or purchaser-chosen fittings. 一般須於佔用許可證前安裝。 | [APP-114](../pnap/app-114.md) |
| 9. Higher standards? 較高標準？ | [ADV-28](../pnap/adv-28.md) (pre-2015 voluntary female-provision standards, now largely enacted by L.N. 191 of 2015). | [ADV-28](../pnap/adv-28.md) |
| 10. Licensing 牌照 | FEHD restaurant licences, schools (EDB), places of public entertainment (FEHD) may set further toilet requirements. 發牌部門或另有要求。 | — |
