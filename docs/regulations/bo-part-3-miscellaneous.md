## At a glance 重點摘要 {#at-a-glance}

=== "English"

    - **s.31**: no building or structure in, over, under or upon a **street** unless it complies with **B(P)R Part II**, is a **signboard** that causes no danger, hazard or obstruction (to structure, traffic, fire, traffic signs, MOE / firefighting access, lighting and ventilation, the public), or has a **s.42 exemption**. The BA may order removal of projections in the public interest within **3 months**, with **compensation** for lawful projections (disputes go to arbitration).
    - **s.32–32B**: street names and building numbers; BD may do ancillary works and charge for **abortive visits** when access is refused.
    - **s.33**: when BD recovers the cost of inspections, investigations or works, it may add a **surcharge of up to 20%**, plus materials and **supervision charges**, with **10% p.a. interest** from 1 month after the certificate is served.
    - **s.35–36I**: service of notices; BD's **records** (microfilm / electronic), public access, certified copies admissible as evidence.
    - **s.37**: **no liability** on Government or officers because works are approved or inspected under the BO, and personal immunity for acts done in good faith.
    - **s.38–39A**: the Secretary for Development makes **regulations** (the B(A)R, B(P)R, B(C)R, B(MW)R, etc.) and issues the **Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans**.
    - **s.39B**: no one may **obstruct an owners' corporation** in complying with an order or notice on common parts.
    - **s.39C**: **validation** of certain UBW. BD will not serve a s.24 order or s.24C notice only because works lacked approval, if they were completed before the cut-off dates and inspected / certified (and strengthened if needed) under the B(MW)R. This covers the household minor works scheme (before **31 Dec 2010**), signboards, and the **Schedule 8** items (supports for services, RBS, A/C frames, fence walls, railings, poles, etc.).

=== "中文"

    - **第 31 條**：任何建築物或構築物不得建於**街道**之內、上方、之下或之上，除非符合 **B(P)R 第 II 部**、屬不會構成危險、阻礙等的**招牌**（對結構、交通、火警、交通標誌、走火通道／消防進出途徑、照明通風、公眾），或已獲**第 42 條豁免**。建築事務監督可基於公眾利益命令在 **3 個月**內移除伸出物；合法伸出物須作**補償**（爭議交仲裁）。
    - **第 32–32B 條**：街道命名及建築物編號；屋宇署可進行附帶工程，並在被拒進入時收取**前往施工未遂的費用**。
    - **第 33 條**：屋宇署追討檢驗、勘測或工程費用時，可加收**不超過 20% 的附加費**，另加物料及**監工費**，並由證明書送達 1 個月後起收取**年息 10%**。
    - **第 35–36I 條**：通知送達；屋宇署的**紀錄**（縮微／電子）、公眾查閱、核證副本可作證據。
    - **第 37 條**：政府或公職人員**不會因**根據本條例批准或檢查工程而承擔法律責任；真誠執行職務的人員獲個人豁免。
    - **第 38–39A 條**：發展局局長訂立**規例**（B(A)R、B(P)R、B(C)R、B(MW)R 等）及發出**監工計劃書技術備忘錄**。
    - **第 39B 條**：任何人不得**阻礙業主立案法團**遵從就公用部分送達的命令或通知。
    - **第 39C 條**：若干僭建物的**檢核**：如工程在截止日期前完成，並按 B(MW)R 檢查及核證（如需要並作加固），屋宇署不會僅以未經批准為理由送達第 24 條命令或第 24C 條通知。涵蓋家居小型工程（**2010 年 12 月 31 日**前）、招牌及**附表 8** 項目（屋宇裝備支架、無線電基站、冷氣機支架、圍牆、欄杆、桿柱等）。

## Key table: s.31(1)(aa) signboard criteria 招牌準則 {#key-table-s311aa-signboard-criteria}

A signboard over a street is allowed only if its location and dimensions do **not** constitute: 招牌的位置及尺寸不得構成：

| # | Criterion 準則 |
|---|---|
| (i) | A danger to the structure of any building 危及任何建築物的結構 |
| (ii) | A hazard to traffic 交通危險 |
| (iii) | A fire hazard 火警危險 |
| (iv) | An obstruction of traffic signs, signals, road markings or traffic monitoring equipment 阻礙交通標誌、燈號、道路標記或交通監察設備 |
| (v) | An obstruction or reduction of the width or height of any **means of escape** or **means of access for firefighting** 阻礙或減少逃生途徑或消防進出途徑的闊度或高度 |
| (vi) | An obstruction of the lighting or ventilation of any building below the regulation standard 阻礙建築物的照明或通風至低於規例標準 |
| (vii) | A danger to the public 危及公眾 |

## Explanation 淺白解釋 {#explanation}

=== "English"

    Part 3 is a collection of supporting powers. Three items matter most in practice:

    - **s.31 street projections**: anything over a street needs to fit B(P)R Part II, or be a safe signboard, or get an exemption. This is the BO base for the **signboard control** system.
    - **s.33 cost recovery**: if BD does the work in default, owners pay the **cost + up to 20% surcharge + supervision + 10% interest**. That is a strong reason to comply with orders.
    - **s.39C validation**: a practical amnesty for small, common UBW (A/C frames, drying racks, small canopies, signboards, amenity items). If inspected, certified and strengthened, BD will not order removal **just because there was no approval**.

=== "中文"

    第 3 部收錄多項輔助權力，實務上最重要的有三項：

    - **第 31 條街道伸出物**：任何伸出街道的物件須符合 B(P)R 第 II 部、屬安全的招牌或取得豁免；這是**招牌監管**制度的法律基礎。
    - **第 33 條追討費用**：如屋宇署代為進行工程，業主須支付**費用 + 最高 20% 附加費 + 監工費 + 年息 10%**，因此有充分理由遵從命令。
    - **第 39C 條檢核**：為常見小型僭建物（冷氣機支架、晾衣架、小型簷篷、招牌、適意設施）提供的實際寬免：經檢查、核證及加固後，屋宇署不會**僅因未經批准**而命令清拆。
