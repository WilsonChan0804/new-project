## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Part 2A **does not apply** to domestic buildings of **3 storeys or less** (s.30A).
    - **MBIS** (s.30B): for buildings aged **30 years or above** (age by OP date, or other evidence). BD serves **statutory notices** on owners to carry out a **prescribed inspection** and, if needed, **prescribed repair** of the **common parts**, **external walls**, **projections** and **signboards**. The same part cannot be served again within **10 years** of the previous notice.
    - **MWIS** (s.30C): for buildings aged **10 years or above**. Notices require inspection and repair of **windows**. Owners of windows used exclusively by them are served individually. Re-serving the same window is not allowed within **5 years**.
    - Owners must appoint a **registered inspector (RI)** for MBIS (s.30D), or a **qualified person (QP)** for MWIS (s.30E), to inspect and to supervise repairs. The RI / QP must inspect **personally**, report **emergencies**, and the RI reports **UBW** found in common parts, external walls, roofs, podiums, yards, slopes and adjoining streets.
    - Notices may be **registered in the Land Registry**. If not complied with, BD may inspect and repair, and recover the cost from the owner (the owner at completion, if registered).
    - The contractor carrying out the repair (RGBC or RMWC) must make that part **safe** (s.30F).

=== "中文"

    - 第 2A 部**不適用**於**不超過 3 層**的住用建築物（第 30A 條）。
    - **強制驗樓計劃**（第 30B 條）：適用於樓齡 **30 年或以上**的建築物（按佔用許可證日期或其他證據斷定）。屋宇署向擁有人送達**法定通知**，規定對**公用部分**、**外牆**、**伸出物**及**招牌**進行**訂明檢驗**及（如有需要）**訂明修葺**；同一部分在上一份通知後 **10 年**內不會再次送達。
    - **強制驗窗計劃**（第 30C 條）：適用於樓齡 **10 年或以上**的建築物；通知規定檢驗及修葺**窗戶**；由擁有人專用的窗會個別送達；同一扇窗 **5 年**內不會再次送達。
    - 擁有人須委任**註冊檢驗人員**（強制驗樓，第 30D 條）或**合資格人士**（強制驗窗，第 30E 條）進行檢驗及監督修葺。註冊檢驗人員／合資格人士須**親自**檢驗並報告**緊急情況**；註冊檢驗人員亦須報告在公用部分、外牆、天台、平台、天井、斜坡及毗鄰街道發現的**違例建築工程**。
    - 通知可**在土地註冊處登記**；如不遵辦，屋宇署可代為檢驗及修葺，並向擁有人（如已登記，則為完工時的擁有人）追討費用。
    - 進行修葺的承建商（註冊一般建築承建商或註冊小型工程承建商）須確保該部分**安全**（第 30F 條）。

## Key table: MBIS vs MWIS 強制驗樓與強制驗窗比較

| | **MBIS** 強制驗樓 | **MWIS** 強制驗窗 |
|---|---|---|
| Section 條 | 30B, 30D | 30C, 30E |
| Building age 樓齡 | **≥ 30 years** 30 年或以上 | **≥ 10 years** 10 年或以上 |
| Excluded 不適用 | Domestic buildings ≤ 3 storeys 不超過 3 層的住用建築物 | Same 同上 |
| Scope 範圍 | Common parts, external walls, projections, signboards (not windows) 公用部分、外牆、伸出物、招牌（不包括窗） | All windows; exclusively used windows served on that owner 所有窗；專用窗向該擁有人送達 |
| Who inspects 檢驗人士 | **Registered inspector (RI)** 註冊檢驗人員 | **Qualified person (QP)**: AP, RSE, RI, RGBC or RMWC (window items) 合資格人士 |
| Who repairs 修葺 | RGBC or RMWC, supervised by an RI 承建商，由註冊檢驗人員監督 | RGBC / RMWC (the QP may also be the contractor) 承建商（合資格人士可兼任） |
| Re-serving interval 再次送達間隔 | **10 years** 10 年 | **5 years** 5 年 |
| Reporting duties 報告責任 | Emergencies; **UBW** in common parts, external walls, roof, podium, yards, slopes, streets 緊急情況及僭建物 | Emergencies 緊急情況 |
| Default 不遵辦 | BD inspects / repairs and recovers cost; notice registrable 屋宇署代做並追討費用；可登記 | Same 同上 |

## Section by section 逐條摘要

| s. 條 | Title 標題 | Summary 摘要 |
|---|---|---|
| 30A | Application 適用範圍 | Part 2A does not apply to domestic buildings ≤ **3 storeys**. 不適用於 3 層或以下住用建築物。 |
| 30B | Prescribed inspection and repair of buildings 建築物的訂明檢驗及修葺 | Buildings ≥ 30 years; notices for common parts, external walls (non-common), projections, signboards; dates for appointing the RI, completing inspection and repair; must repair if dangerous; registrable; default works and cost recovery; 10-year interval; windows excluded. 強制驗樓。 |
| 30C | Prescribed inspection and repair of windows 窗戶的訂明檢驗及修葺 | Buildings ≥ 10 years; notices for windows; dates for appointing the QP, inspection and repair; registrable; default works; 5-year interval. 強制驗窗。 |
| 30D | Appointment and duties of registered inspectors 註冊檢驗人員的委任及職責 | Appoint an RI to inspect and one to supervise repair (may be the same); inspect personally; proper supervision; materials comply; ensure safety; report emergencies and UBW; replace if unable to act. 註冊檢驗人員職責。 |
| 30E | Appointment and duties of qualified persons 合資格人士的委任及職責 | Appoint a QP to inspect and to supervise repair; personal inspection (or prescribed representative for companies); may also act as the contractor; report emergencies; **no nomination** of a substitute during illness / absence. 合資格人士職責。 |
| 30F | Duties of RGBC and RMWC 承建商的職責 | The repair contractor must ensure the part repaired has been rendered safe. 確保修葺部分安全。 |

## Key clauses 主要條文

!!! quote "s.30A and s.30B(1), (3) — MBIS 強制驗樓"
    30A. This Part does not apply to a domestic building not exceeding 3 storeys in height. 30B(1) This section applies to any building aged 30 years or above. … (3) The Building Authority may by notice in writing served on any owner of a building require a prescribed inspection and, if necessary, prescribed repair in respect of the common parts of the building to be carried out within a specified time.

    **官方中文本：** 第30A條 本部不適用於不超過3層高的住用建築物。第30B(1)條 本條適用於樓齡達30年或以上的建築物。……(3) 建築事務監督可藉向建築物的任何擁有人送達書面通知，規定在指明的限期內，對該建築物的公用部分進行訂明檢驗及(如有需要)訂明修葺。

!!! quote "s.30C(1) — MWIS 強制驗窗"
    This section applies to any building aged 10 years or above.

    **官方中文本：** 本條適用於樓齡達10年或以上的建築物。

## Explanation 淺白解釋

=== "English"

    Hong Kong has many ageing buildings, and falling concrete, windows and signboards are a real risk. Part 2A makes **regular inspection a legal duty of owners**, instead of waiting for BD to find danger.

    In practice BD **selects target buildings** each year and serves notices. Owners (usually through the Owners' Corporation) appoint an RI, who inspects and reports. Repairs follow under the RI's supervision. Compliance is then reported to BD.

    MBIS inspections commonly lead to UBW findings, because the RI must report them. Expect follow-up removal orders under s.24.

=== "中文"

    香港有大量老化樓宇，石屎、窗戶及招牌墮下是真實風險。第 2A 部把**定期檢驗定為業主的法定責任**，而不是等待屋宇署發現危險。

    實務上，屋宇署每年**選定目標樓宇**並送達通知；業主（通常透過業主立案法團）委任註冊檢驗人員檢驗並提交報告，然後在其監督下進行修葺，再向屋宇署呈報已遵辦。

    由於註冊檢驗人員必須報告違例建築工程，強制驗樓往往會發現僭建物，其後可能有第 24 條清拆令跟進。

## Practical notes 實務提示

=== "English"

    !!! tip "Common pitfalls"
        - Missing the **appointment deadline** for the RI / QP stated in the notice. Each date in the notice is a separate requirement.
        - Using an AP or RSE for MBIS who is **not on the inspectors' register**. MBIS needs an **RI**.
        - Ignoring windows inside flats under MWIS. Windows exclusively used by an owner are that owner's responsibility (s.30C(4)).
        - Assuming MBIS covers windows. They are excluded from s.30B (s.30B(13)) and handled under MWIS.
        - Selling a flat with a registered, uncomplied notice. The cost can be recovered from the owner at completion of BD's works.

=== "中文"

    !!! tip "常見錯誤"
        - 錯過通知所述委任註冊檢驗人員／合資格人士的**限期**：通知內每個日期都是獨立的規定。
        - 強制驗樓委任**未列入檢驗人員名冊**的認可人士或結構工程師：強制驗樓須委任**註冊檢驗人員**。
        - 強制驗窗忽略單位內的窗：擁有人專用的窗由該擁有人負責（第 30C(4) 條）。
        - 以為強制驗樓包括窗戶：第 30B 條不包括窗（第 30B(13) 條），由強制驗窗處理。
        - 出售附有已登記但未遵辦通知的單位：費用可向屋宇署完工時的擁有人追討。
