## At a glance 重點摘要

=== "English"

    - Sets the **fees** for registering **minor works contractors** under B(MW)R: applications, registration, renewal, restoration, additional items, classes or types, additional authorised signatories, and reviews.
    - **Part 2: individuals for Class III items** (RMWC (Individual)): see table; review fees are refunded if the BA substitutes its decision (s.8).
    - **Part 3: companies by types and classes** (RMWC (Company)): fees scale with the **highest class**; each extra proposed authorised signatory costs more (s.10). Registration fee **$595** for all classes (s.11).

=== "中文"

    - 訂明根據 B(MW)R 註冊**小型工程承建商**的**費用**：申請、註冊、續期、恢復、增加項目、級別或類型、新增獲授權簽署人及覆核。
    - **第 2 部：個人第 III 級項目**（註冊小型工程承建商（個人））見下表；如建築事務監督以另一決定取代原決定，覆核費退還（第 8 條）。
    - **第 3 部：公司按類型及級別**（註冊小型工程承建商（公司））：費用按**最高級別**計算；每名額外建議獲授權簽署人另加費用（第 10 條）；註冊費各級別均為 **$595**（第 11 條）。
