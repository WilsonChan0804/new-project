# Issues and tasks together

An issue says what is wrong; a task says who fixes it by when. The two are linked and each offers to close the other.

- <strong>A task from an issue:</strong> in the issue window press <strong>+ New task from this issue</strong>. The task list and group of this project come first, the owner is the issue's <strong>Assigned to</strong>, and the due date is the issue's. The issue, its files and its Revit element go on the task. Tick <strong>Open the task when made</strong> to go to it.
- <strong>Task done → issue:</strong> when you <strong>Mark complete</strong> a task whose linked issues are still open, Tasks asks <strong>Resolve its issues too?</strong> Tick the ones to change and choose Resolved or Closed. They are changed as if you did it in the viewer: history, emails, the chat card, and a comment naming the task.
- <strong>Issue resolved → task:</strong> set an issue to Resolved or Closed and <strong>Save</strong>; if its tasks are still open, the viewer offers to mark them done. The tasks also get a line in their activity.
- The task window shows each linked issue's status now; the task list shows <strong>#n open</strong> or <strong>#✓</strong>.
