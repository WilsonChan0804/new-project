# Working offline

On site with no signal, a project downloaded to the device keeps working: sheets, the 3D model, issues and markups.

1. With a connection, open the project and press <strong>Offline</strong> in the top bar, then <strong>Download for offline</strong>. The download shows its progress and can be continued if it stops.
2. Without a connection the top bar says <strong>Offline - copy of &lt;date&gt;</strong>. Look at sheets and the model, raise and answer issues, draw markups.
3. Changes made offline wait on the device (<strong>N changes waiting</strong>) and upload by themselves when the connection is back (<strong>All changes uploaded</strong>).

- The Dashboard, Admin, Board, Tasks, Messenger, comparing with earlier versions and adding PDFs need a connection.
- On iPad and iPhone, <strong>Add to Home Screen</strong> first: Safari may clear a website it has not seen for some weeks, but keeps the Home Screen app.
