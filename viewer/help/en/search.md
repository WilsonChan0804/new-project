# Search

One box for everything: press <strong>Ctrl+K</strong> or <strong>/</strong> on any page, or click the magnifier at the top.

- It finds <strong>projects</strong>, <strong>tasks</strong> (title, description, T-12), <strong>issues</strong> (#12, title, description, sheet, assignee, Revit element and id), <strong>sheets</strong>, saved <strong>3D views</strong>, <strong>messages</strong>, and <strong>files and links</strong> on tasks and issues.
- Type one or more words; every word must match. Results are grouped by kind; ↑ ↓ and <strong>Enter</strong> open one, <strong>Esc</strong> closes.
- You only see what you may open. Your last searches are listed when the box is empty.
