# What the LWK Viewer is

The LWK Viewer shows a project's Revit sheets and 3D model in a web browser, so anyone on the team can review, measure, mark up and raise issues without Revit. It runs on desktop, iPad and iPhone, and every issue raised is shared live with the whole team and can be picked up back in Revit.

Around the drawings and the model it also holds the team's work: a list of projects, task lists (in place of Lark Tasks), whiteboards and a messenger, all linked to the same sheets, 3D views and issues, with one search across all of them.

<strong>Who it is for</strong>

| Who | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Architects and designers | Check drawings and the 3D model, raise design and coordination issues, answer issues assigned to them |
| Project leads and directors | Review progress on a tablet, follow open and overdue issues on the Dashboard |
| BIM coordinators | Publish models and sheets from Revit, run nightly exports, manage issues from inside Revit |
| Consultants and site staff (Viewer role) | Look and read only, on any device |
| Team leads | Plan the team's task lists, follow who does what by when, discuss in project channels |
| Project and site admins | Add people, set roles, manage projects |

<strong>What you can do</strong>

- <strong>Sheets (2D):</strong> open every published sheet as a sharp PDF, search the words on all drawings, mark up with 20+ tools, compare with earlier versions, and export PDFs with markups.
- <strong>3D:</strong> orbit, pan and walk through the model and its links, cut floor plans and sections, measure with snapping, check element properties, and save views.
- <strong>Issues:</strong> raise an issue on a sheet or in 3D with a snapshot, assign it, set a due date, comment with @mentions, and track it to closure. Issues exchange with Revit, Navisworks and BIMcollab through BCF.
- <strong>2D + 3D side by side:</strong> click a plan on a sheet and the 3D view goes there; walk in 3D and see where you are on the plan.
- <strong>Revit link:</strong> the LWK Issues window in Revit lists the same issues, zooms to them and sends replies back.
- <strong>Projects:</strong> every job with its people, drawings, model, tasks, issues, files and chat on one page.
- <strong>Tasks:</strong> team task lists with List, Kanban, Gantt and Dashboard views, imported from Lark and linked to issues, sheets and 3D views.
- <strong>Board:</strong> a shared whiteboard per project for notes, mind maps and sketches.
- <strong>Messenger:</strong> project channels, group chats and direct messages, with task and issue updates posted in, links to tasks, issues, sheets and 3D, and messages to and from WhatsApp.
- <strong>Issues and tasks together:</strong> make a task from an issue; finishing one offers to close the other.
- <strong>Revit elements:</strong> a 3D issue keeps its Revit ElementId, ready for Select by ID, BCF and the LWK add-in.
- <strong>Search:</strong> one box (Ctrl+K) for tasks, issues, sheets, 3D views, messages and file links.
- <strong>Offline:</strong> download a project to a tablet and keep working on site with no signal.
