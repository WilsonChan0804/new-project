# Phones and tablets

The same address works on iPad and iPhone in Safari; the viewer detects the device and loads a lighter 3D model so it fits in the device's memory.

<strong>What is different on a phone or tablet</strong>

- The side panels become drawers: <strong>☰ Models</strong> / <strong>☰ Sheets</strong> on the left and <strong>ⓘ Details</strong> / <strong>Issues</strong> on the right. Tap outside a drawer to close it. A dot on Details means something was picked.
- 3D loads a phone copy of each model, made on the server after every publish. On big projects, geometry is fetched in pieces as you look around ("streaming"), coarse first, then finer.
- <strong>Light mode</strong> is on: repeated typical floors load only near where you are looking.
- Small items such as furniture are drawn only near the camera, and far parts use simpler shapes.
- Cut solids show the orange hatch but no brown outline.
- The top bar scrolls sideways: swipe it to reach Board, Tasks, Chat and Dashboard.
- <strong>Search</strong> fills the screen. In <strong>Messenger</strong>, tap a message to show its buttons; Enter makes a new line and the round button sends.

<strong>Gestures</strong>

| Gesture | 3D | Sheets |
|---|---|---|
| One finger | Orbit | Draw with the current tool (pans after you have used an Apple Pencil) |
| Two fingers | Pinch to zoom, drag to pan | Pinch to zoom, drag to pan |
| Double tap | Focus on that surface | - |
| Long press | New issue at that point | Right-click menu |
| Two-finger / three-finger tap | - | Undo / redo |

<strong>Tips</strong>

- Use landscape on iPad for the most drawing space.
- If a model was published minutes ago, its phone copy may still be being prepared; the page offers to prepare it and you can try again a few minutes later.
- If Safari reloads the page by itself, the device ran out of memory: untick models you do not need in <strong>Models</strong>, stay on one floor, and send a report (see Troubleshooting).
