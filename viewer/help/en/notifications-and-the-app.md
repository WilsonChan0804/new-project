# Notifications and the app

New chat messages reach you without reloading any page, and the viewer can be installed as an app.

- <strong>While a viewer page is open</strong> (any page, a tab in the background, or the app): new messages show as notifications on Windows, Mac and Android; a click opens the chat at the message. The chat you are looking at stays quiet. The tab title shows the unread count, e.g. <strong>(3) LWK Viewer</strong>, and so does the app's icon.
- <strong>The bell</strong> in the top bar: <strong>Turn on notifications</strong> (the browser asks once), then choose <strong>Every message</strong>, <strong>@mentions and direct messages</strong> or <strong>Nothing</strong>.
- <strong>Mute a chat:</strong> Chat info &gt; <strong>Mute notifications</strong>. A muted chat still tells you when you are @mentioned.
- <strong>Not only messages:</strong> you are also told when a task is made yours, when you are @mentioned on a task or an issue, when an issue is assigned to you or your query is answered, and when tasks you own are due. The bell's <strong>For you</strong> list keeps the latest ones (a red number shows how many are new); click one to open it, <strong>Mark all read</strong> clears the number.
- <strong>Install the app:</strong> the bell &gt; <strong>Install the app</strong> (Edge, Chrome), or the install icon at the right of the address bar. On Android: menu &gt; <strong>Add to Home screen</strong>. On iPhone and iPad: Safari &gt; Share &gt; <strong>Add to Home Screen</strong>. The app opens in its own window, with shortcuts to Chat, Tasks, Calendar, Folders and Projects (right-click the icon).
- <strong>When no page is open (push):</strong> the bell &gt; <strong>Turn on for this device</strong>. This works when the viewer is opened by its <strong>https</strong> address; ask your admin. Messages then arrive on that phone or computer even with the app closed.
- <strong>iPhone and iPad:</strong> first add the viewer to the Home Screen (Safari &gt; Share &gt; <strong>Add to Home Screen</strong>) and open it from there; then in the bell turn on notifications and <strong>Turn on for this device</strong>, and press <strong>Send a test</strong>. If it does not arrive, the message says why (for example the server cannot reach Apple). After a server update, turn push off and on again once.
