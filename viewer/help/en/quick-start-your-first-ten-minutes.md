# Quick start: your first ten minutes

Sign in, open a sheet, look at the same place in 3D, and raise one test issue.

1. <strong>Sign in.</strong> Open the viewer address, enter your email and password, and change the temporary password when asked.
2. <strong>Pick the project.</strong> Use the project name at the top (a drop-down appears when you are on more than one project), or pick it on the <strong>Dashboard</strong>.
3. <strong>Open a sheet.</strong> On <strong>Sheets</strong>, click a sheet in the left list, or type a room name, grid or note in <strong>Search all sheets</strong>. Scroll to zoom, hold the middle mouse button to pan, press <strong>Fit</strong> to see the whole sheet.
4. <strong>Go to 3D.</strong> Click <strong>3D</strong> at the top left. Hold the right mouse button to orbit, the middle button to pan, and scroll to zoom. Double-click a surface to zoom in on it.
5. <strong>Look at a floor.</strong> Choose a level in the <strong>Floor plan</strong> drop-down: the model is cut 1.2 m above that floor and seen from the top. <strong>Page Up / Page Down</strong> step between floors; <strong>Whole building</strong> goes back to 3D.
6. <strong>Click an element</strong> to see its properties in the <strong>Selection</strong> panel on the right.
7. <strong>Raise a test issue.</strong> Click <strong>Add issue</strong>, click a surface, type a title such as "TEST - please ignore", then <strong>Save issue</strong>. It appears in <strong>Issues in 3D</strong> for everyone.
8. <strong>Try 2D + 3D.</strong> Back on Sheets, open a plan and press <strong>2D + 3D</strong>; click on the plan and the 3D view moves there.
9. <strong>Make a task from it.</strong> Open the test issue, press <strong>+ New task from this issue</strong>, check the list, owner and due date, then <strong>Make the task</strong>.
10. <strong>Say hello in Chat.</strong> Open <strong>Chat</strong>, pick the project's channel, type <strong>#</strong> and pick your test task, then press <strong>Send</strong>.
11. <strong>Search.</strong> Press <strong>Ctrl+K</strong> (or the magnifier at the top) and type a word from your issue title: the issue, the task and your message are all found.
12. <strong>Delete your test issue and task</strong> when you are done: the issue from its detail window (<strong>Delete</strong>), the task from its window (the bin button).
