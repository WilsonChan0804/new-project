# Walking through the model

Walk mode puts you at eye level inside the building, following floors, stairs and ramps.

<strong>Start:</strong> click <strong>Walk</strong>, then click a floor (clicking a wall places you on the floor in front of it). Or right-click a spot and choose <strong>Walk from here</strong>. Walking turns off the floor plan and section box.

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | iPad / iPhone |
|---|---|---|
| Forward / back | W / S or ↑ / ↓ | On-screen pad |
| Step sideways | A / D | On-screen pad |
| Turn | ← / →, or drag with the mouse | Drag with a finger, or the pad |
| Look up and down | Drag with the mouse | Drag with a finger |
| Faster | Hold Shift (3× speed) | - |
| Step forward or back | Scroll wheel | - |
| Floor above / below | Page Up / Page Down | - |
| Up / down (Gravity off) | E / Q | - |
| Stop walking | Esc or <strong>Stop</strong> | <strong>Stop</strong> |

<strong>Walking panel settings</strong> (remembered):

- <strong>Eye</strong> height, 0.8 to 3 m (default 1.6 m).
- <strong>Speed</strong>, 0.5 to 8 m/s (default 1.5 m/s).
- <strong>Gravity</strong> (on): stay on the floor, climb stairs and ramps, ignore desk-height steps.
- <strong>Walls block</strong> (off): stops you walking through walls. Doors are closed in the model, so doorways block too when this is on.

On the Sheets page, <strong>2D + 3D</strong> with <strong>Walk</strong> lets you walk in 3D while the plan beside it shows where you are.
