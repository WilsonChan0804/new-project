# Calendar

<strong>Right-click a day</strong> (hold it on a phone) to add on that day an <strong>event</strong> (all day), a <strong>meeting</strong> (from 10:00, choose the chat to invite) or a <strong>task</strong> due that day.

<strong>Calendar</strong> shows what is coming up for you, by date: <strong>meetings</strong> sent in the Messenger (with your reply), <strong>your tasks</strong> on their due dates and <strong>issues assigned to you</strong> on their due dates.

- A month on a computer, a list on a phone (<strong>Month</strong> / <strong>List</strong>). The tick boxes show or hide meetings, tasks and issues. <strong>‹ ›</strong> and <strong>Today</strong> move through the months; on a phone, swipe.
- Click an entry for its details, then open the chat, the task or the issue. Replies to a meeting are made in its chat.
- <strong>+ Event:</strong> choose a chat; the event form opens there, so everyone in it is invited.
- <strong>Add to Outlook:</strong> <strong>Open in Outlook on this PC</strong> subscribes Outlook to your calendar (choose <strong>Yes</strong>), or copy the address and use Outlook's <strong>Add calendar &gt; From Internet</strong>. Outlook then shows the entries beside your own calendar and updates them by itself. Outlook on the web and on phones can only reach it when the viewer is reachable from the internet.
- The address is yours alone: <strong>Make a new address</strong> if it was shared by mistake (the old one stops working).
