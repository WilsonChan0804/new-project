# Messenger

<strong>Chat</strong> opens the Messenger: the team's conversations, next to the work they are about.

<strong>Kinds of chat</strong>

| Chat | What it is |
|---|---|
| Project channel | One per project. Everyone on the project is in it. New tasks, assignments, completions, task comments and new or changed issues are posted into it as cards |
| Topic | A sub-channel of a project channel for one subject (Facade, MEP coordination, Site visits). Listed under its channel, and as tabs under the channel's name: <strong># General</strong> is the channel itself |
| Group | Any group of people, named by whoever starts it |
| Direct message | Two people |
| Task discussion | The comments of the tasks you follow, answered from here |

- The pencil at the top starts a new chat. Right-click a chat (or hold it on a phone) for <strong>Details</strong>, <strong>Mark as read</strong>, <strong>New topic</strong> (project channels), <strong>Leave</strong>, <strong>Import WhatsApp chat ...</strong>, <strong>Send chat link to WhatsApp</strong> and <strong>Delete chat</strong>, for those who may delete it.
- <strong>Opening a topic:</strong> anyone in the project channel presses <strong>+ Topic</strong> on the tabs under its name (or <strong>New topic</strong> in its menu, or the pencil &gt; <strong>Topic in a project channel</strong>). Everyone in the channel is added.
- <strong>Who can delete a chat:</strong> a direct message, either of the two people (it goes for both); a group, its admins (who started it) or a site admin; a project channel or topic, the project's admins or a site admin. Deleting a channel deletes its topics too.
- <strong>Chat info</strong> (the people button): the description, the members (add, remove, message), <strong>Pictures and files</strong>, <strong>Leave this chat</strong> and <strong>Delete this chat</strong>.
- The number on <strong>Chat</strong> in the top bar of every page shows unread messages; <strong>@</strong> means you were mentioned.

<strong>Writing</strong>

- <strong>Enter</strong> makes a new line; the <strong>Send</strong> button sends (on a keyboard, <strong>Ctrl+Enter</strong> sends too).
- Paste or drop screenshots, or use the paper clip for pictures and files up to 50 MB each. OneDrive, SharePoint and ACC links show as cards; a video link shows a play card, and a pasted OneDrive or SharePoint <strong>Embed</strong> code plays right in the chat.
- <strong>@</strong> a person. <strong>#</strong> a task, <strong>!</strong> an issue, <strong>$</strong> a sheet, <strong>%</strong> the 3D model or a saved view: a list opens as you type. <strong>Choose the project first</strong> in the row of projects at the top of the list (in a project channel its own project is chosen). The <strong>#</strong> button beside the paper clip opens the same lists with tabs.
- The picked item shows in the message as a named link that opens it.
- 🙂 adds emoji.
- <strong>Formatting (Aa):</strong> the box shows the words as they will be sent, as in Lark. <strong>Aa</strong> shows the format buttons: <strong>B</strong>, <strong>S</strong>, <strong>I</strong>, <strong>U</strong>, numbered and bulleted lists, quote, link, code and block of code; <strong>Ctrl+B</strong>, <strong>Ctrl+I</strong>, <strong>Ctrl+U</strong> and <strong>Ctrl+Shift+X</strong> work on the selection. On a phone they appear in their own row after <strong>Aa</strong>. Forwarded to WhatsApp, a message keeps its bold, italic and strikethrough.
- <strong>The buttons under the box:</strong> <strong>Aa</strong>, 🙂 emoji, <strong>@</strong> a person, <strong>#</strong> a task, issue, sheet or 3D view (it shows in the box as a chip), the paper clip, <strong>+</strong> and <strong>Send</strong>, which is always on screen, on a phone too.
- <strong>The + button:</strong> <strong>Poll</strong>, <strong>Event / meeting</strong>, <strong>Task</strong>, or a link to a task, issue, sheet or 3D view.

<strong>Polls, events and tasks</strong>

- <strong>Poll:</strong> a question with 2 to 10 choices; tick <strong>Allow more than one answer</strong>, <strong>Anonymous</strong> or a closing date if you like. Tap a choice to vote, again to take it back. The writer can <strong>Close poll</strong> (and reopen it).
- <strong>Event:</strong> title, date, time (or <strong>All day</strong>), where and the Teams link. Everyone in the chat is invited (by email too when mail is set up) and answers <strong>Going</strong>, <strong>Maybe</strong> or <strong>Can't go</strong>. <strong>Add to calendar (.ics)</strong> puts it in Outlook, Google or the phone. The writer can <strong>Change</strong> or <strong>Cancel event</strong>; the chat is told.
- <strong>Task:</strong> choose the task list and group (this project's first), owners (this chat's people first), due date and priority. The task goes into the list and its card into the chat. ⋯ More &gt; <strong>Make a task</strong> on any message does the same, with the message as the description, the people @mentioned as owners and a link back to it.

<strong>A message</strong>

- Hover a message (tap it on a phone): 🙂 <strong>React</strong>, ↩ <strong>Reply</strong>, ✏ <strong>Edit</strong> (your own) and ⋯ <strong>More</strong>: <strong>Copy</strong>, <strong>Pin</strong>, <strong>Make a task</strong>, <strong>Forward to another chat</strong>, <strong>Send to WhatsApp</strong>, <strong>Copy link to this message</strong> and <strong>Delete</strong> (your own).
- <strong>Edit</strong> changes the message in place: <strong>Ctrl+Enter</strong> saves, <strong>Esc</strong> cancels; others see "(edited)". <strong>Edit</strong> and <strong>Delete</strong> are only for the person who sent the message (not the chat's admin, not a site admin); others see "message deleted".
- <strong>Pinned messages:</strong> anyone in the chat can pin a message, up to 100 a chat. The bar under the chat's name shows the latest one; click it to see the message and move on to the next; <strong>All</strong> lists them, with <strong>unpin</strong>.
- <strong>Reactions</strong> show under the message with a count; tap one to add or take away yours.
- A message that could not be sent stays with <strong>send again</strong>.

<strong>WhatsApp</strong>

- <strong>Out:</strong> <strong>Send to WhatsApp</strong> on a message sends its words, links and files. On a phone, pictures go as themselves through the share sheet; on a computer it opens WhatsApp Desktop or WhatsApp Web.
- <strong>Copied messages:</strong> messages copied in WhatsApp and pasted into the box are offered as one "From WhatsApp" quote.
- <strong>Exported chat:</strong> in WhatsApp, the chat &gt; <strong>More</strong> &gt; <strong>Export chat</strong>, and send yourself the .zip (with media) or .txt. Then in a chat's menu choose <strong>Import WhatsApp chat ...</strong>: the messages arrive as one quote with the pictures in place.
- <strong>Android:</strong> with the viewer installed as an app, use WhatsApp's <strong>Share</strong> &gt; <strong>LWK Viewer</strong> and choose the chat. (iPhone does not offer this: copy and paste, or export.)
- There is no automatic two-way link with WhatsApp.
