# Feedback and what to try first

This is a trial: please try the tasks below and tell us what works, what is confusing and what is missing.

- ☐ Sign in and change your password
- ☐ Find a sheet by searching for a room name or note
- ☐ Mark up a sheet with a cloud and text, then turn it into an issue
- ☐ Open the same area in 3D and cut a floor plan
- ☐ Measure a room wall to wall in plan, with <strong>Straight</strong> on
- ☐ Raise an issue in 3D, assign it to a colleague, and comment with an @mention
- ☐ Try <strong>2D + 3D</strong> and walk a corridor while watching the plan
- ☐ Open the viewer on an iPad or iPhone and look at one floor
- ☐ Make a task from your test issue, then complete it and resolve the issue from the Tasks page
- ☐ Send a message in your project's channel with a # task and a $ sheet link
- ☐ Open a topic in your project channel, pin a message, and send a poll and an event
- ☐ Find your test issue with Ctrl+K
- ☐ In 3D, click an element, press Copy Revit ID and select it in Revit with Select by ID
- ☐ Delete your test issues and tasks when you finish

Send feedback, questions and problem reports to [name / Teams channel]. Include the Performance report for anything to do with speed or crashes.
