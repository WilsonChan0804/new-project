# Tasks

<strong>Tasks</strong> are the team's task lists, in place of Lark Tasks, linked to the drawings, the model and the issues.

### Lists, groups and views

- <strong>Task lists</strong> belong to a team (for example LWK-MANILA). Inside a list the <strong>groups</strong> are usually the jobs (SKW, Kai Tak 2A3 ...). A group linked to a project shows <strong>Project</strong>, <strong>Sheets</strong> and <strong>Dashboard</strong> buttons; link them in <strong>...</strong> &gt; <strong>Groups and projects</strong>.
- <strong>Quick access</strong> on the left: <strong>Owned</strong> (open tasks that are yours), <strong>Subscribed</strong>, <strong>All</strong>, <strong>Created</strong>, <strong>Assigned</strong> (by you to others) and <strong>Completed</strong>, across every list you can see. <strong>People</strong> shows the organisation by team, office or company, with each person's open and overdue tasks.
- <strong>List:</strong> a grouped table. Edit in place, open sub-tasks with ▸, drag rows to reorder or move them to another group. A task with linked issues shows <strong>#2 open</strong> until they are all resolved, then <strong>#✓</strong>.
- <strong>Kanban:</strong> cards in columns; drag a card to another column. The board fills the window, so the sideways scroll bar is always at the bottom of the screen; <strong>Shift + mouse wheel</strong>, dragging an empty part of the board, or the <strong>‹ ›</strong> buttons at its edges also scroll it.
- <strong>Gantt:</strong> bars on a timeline; drag a bar, or drag its ends to change the dates. Each change asks <strong>Change the dates?</strong> first: <strong>Save</strong> keeps it, <strong>Cancel</strong> puts the bar back (tick <strong>Don't ask again today</strong> to skip the question on this device). The legend explains the colours; milestones are gold.
- <strong>Dashboard:</strong> total, completed, incomplete and overdue, by owner, by project, average duration and due soon. <strong>Activities:</strong> everything that changed, by whom.
- Filter by owner and priority, sort, <strong>Group by</strong>, and <strong>Customize</strong> which columns show. Lists can have their own columns (person, select, text, date, number), for example Modelers and Project Manager.

### A task

- Click a task to open its window: title, <strong>Owners</strong>, start and due <strong>Dates</strong>, <strong>Group</strong>, <strong>Priority</strong> and the list's own columns. <strong>Mark complete</strong> finishes it.
- <strong>Completion method:</strong> <strong>OR</strong> = done when any owner completes it; <strong>AND</strong> = every owner completes their own part.
- <strong>Sub-tasks:</strong> type in <strong>+ Add sub-task</strong> and press Enter. A sub-task can have sub-tasks of its own, as in Lark, up to 5 levels: open the sub-task and add them there, or use the <strong>+</strong> that shows on a row in the List. Drop a task on the right half of another task's title to make it its sub-task. Deleting a task deletes everything under it (<strong>Ctrl+Z</strong> brings it all back).
- <strong>Linked issues</strong> show their status now (Open, In progress, Resolved). <strong>+ Link an issue</strong> picks one of the project's issues.
- <strong>Files, sheets and 3D views:</strong> paste a OneDrive, SharePoint or ACC address, or use <strong>+ Task</strong> on the Sheets or 3D page to keep the sheet or view you are looking at. Revit elements kept on a task show as <strong>Elements to change</strong>.
- <strong>Comments:</strong> @name tells someone; paste a screenshot or attach files (up to 50 MB each). Your own comments have <strong>edit</strong> and <strong>delete</strong>.
- <strong>Follow</strong> emails you when the task changes; <strong>Share</strong> sends it to a chat; the link button copies its address.
- The ▲ ▼ buttons, or <strong>K</strong> / <strong>J</strong>, go to the previous / next task. On a phone, <strong>Back</strong> returns to the list.

### Members of a list

- <strong>Owner</strong> (changes the list's settings and members), <strong>Editor</strong> (adds and changes tasks) and <strong>Viewer</strong> (reads only). Site admins see every list.

### Importing from Lark

1. In Lark, open the task list and choose <strong>...</strong> &gt; <strong>Export</strong> (Excel).
2. In Tasks, use <strong>+</strong> &gt; <strong>Import a Lark task list (.xlsx)</strong> to make a new list, or <strong>...</strong> &gt; <strong>Import from Lark (.xlsx) / CSV</strong> to bring it into the open list.
3. Groups, sub-tasks, owners, followers, dates, done status, priority, completion method, milestones, Modelers and Project Manager all come in, with a link back to each task in Lark.
4. Importing the same export again updates the tasks instead of adding them twice. Names with no account yet are kept as names; once the accounts exist, use <strong>...</strong> &gt; <strong>Match names to accounts</strong>.
