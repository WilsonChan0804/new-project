# Section box, section plane and floor plans

Use the section box to cut a part of the building open, and a section plane to cut along any surface; cut solids show an orange hatch, as on a drawing.

### Section tools

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| <strong>Box</strong> (tick) | Turns the section box on, fitted round the whole project |
| <strong>Plane</strong> | Click a surface: the model is cut on that face |
| <strong>Align</strong> | Click a wall: the box turns square to that wall |
| <strong>Flip</strong> | Keeps the other side of the section plane |
| <strong>Reset</strong> | Removes all cuts |
| <strong>Solid fill on the cut</strong> | Cut walls, slabs and columns show an orange hatch (on by default) |
| <strong>Plan look for floor plans</strong> | In a floor plan, surfaces are paler and floors below fade out (on by default) |

The Floor plan drop-down (see 3D: moving around) is a ready-made section box for one storey.

### Moving a cut

- Each face of the box has a <strong>coloured dot on a short stem</strong>: red for X, green for Y, blue for height. The single section plane has an orange dot.
- <strong>Mouse:</strong> hover over a dot, or over the edge of a cut face (the face lights up orange), then drag. The cut only moves along its own direction.
- <strong>iPad / iPhone:</strong> drag a dot with one finger. Two fingers always move the view, never the cut, so you can pinch and orbit freely while a section is on.
- On a computer, a brown outline traces the cut edges once the cut stops moving. Phones and tablets show the hatch only.

### Handy shortcuts

- Right-click a wall or floor: <strong>Section on this face</strong> cuts right there; <strong>Square section box to this face</strong> lines the box up with it.
- Right-click empty space: <strong>Turn section on/off</strong>.
