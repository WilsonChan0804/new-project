# Before you start

You need an account, a supported browser and the viewer's web address; nothing is installed on your computer.

<strong>This manual is also online:</strong> the <strong>?</strong> at the top of every page opens it in the viewer at the chapter for that page, with search and English / 廣東話. It is always the latest version.

<strong>Your account</strong>

- An admin creates your account and sends you an invitation with a <strong>temporary password</strong>.
- Open the viewer address, sign in with your <strong>Email</strong> and the temporary password, then choose your own password (at least 8 characters) on the <strong>My account</strong> page that opens.
- You stay signed in for 30 days on each browser. After 10 wrong passwords in 15 minutes the sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.
- Your role is set per project: <strong>Project admin</strong>, <strong>Member</strong> or <strong>Viewer</strong> (see For admins). A Viewer sees a banner saying they can look and read but not change anything.

<strong>Browsers and devices</strong>

| Device | Browser | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Windows or Mac | Chrome or Edge (recommended), Safari, Firefox | A mouse with a middle button (wheel) makes 3D much easier |
| iPad | Safari | Apple Pencil works for markup; landscape recommended |
| iPhone | Safari | Best for looking and reading; 3D loads a lighter copy of the model |

<strong>Confidentiality</strong>

- Treat the viewer like the project folder: only people on the project team should be added.
- Do not share your password, forward invitation emails, or post links outside the team.
- Screenshots and exported PDFs follow the same rules as any other project document.
