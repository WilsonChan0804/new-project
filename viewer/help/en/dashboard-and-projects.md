# Dashboard and projects

The Dashboard is the project's issue scoreboard: how many are open, overdue, who holds them and what changed this week.

<strong>Getting around</strong>

- The top bar on every page has the same pages in the same place: <strong>Projects</strong>, <strong>Sheets</strong>, <strong>3D</strong>, <strong>Board</strong>, <strong>Tasks</strong>, <strong>Chat</strong> (with the number of unread messages) and <strong>Dashboard</strong>. On the right: the <strong>Search</strong> magnifier, <strong>+ Task</strong> (on Sheets and 3D: keeps this sheet or 3D view on a task), <strong>Admin</strong> (admins only), <strong>Account</strong> and <strong>Sign out</strong>. On a phone the top bar scrolls sideways.
- The <strong>project drop-down</strong> (Sheets, 3D, Board, Dashboard) lists the project names from the Projects page, e.g. "SKW · HKA-P-01681-ARC". A project made of several models, such as MOS Site 1 and Site 2, appears as a group with its parts under it.
- The connection chip shows <strong>online - your name</strong>, <strong>N waiting to save</strong> (the connection dropped; your changes are queued and sent when it returns), or <strong>offline</strong>. Do not reload the page while changes are waiting: the queue is kept only until you leave the page.

<strong>What the Dashboard shows</strong>

| Part | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Filters | <strong>Closed within</strong> (Any time to Last 12 months), <strong>Type</strong>, <strong>Assigned to</strong>, <strong>Office</strong>, <strong>Only mine</strong>, <strong>Include "not an issue"</strong> |
| This week | Issues raised, closed, open and overdue this week, and open issues by person; <strong>Send to Teams</strong> posts it to the project channel |
| Summary figures | Issues, Open, In progress, Resolved, Closed, Overdue, Due in 7 days, Unassigned, Avg days open, Avg days to close |
| Charts | Raised and closed by week (last 12 weeks), by status, type, priority, where raised (sheet or 3D) and office |
| Responsibility table | Each assignee's open, in progress, overdue and due-soon counts, oldest open issue and last activity; click a row to filter |
| Lists | <strong>Overdue</strong>, <strong>Due in the next 14 days</strong>, <strong>Recently changed</strong>, <strong>Going quiet</strong> (untouched for 14 days or more) |

- <strong>Export CSV</strong> downloads the filtered issues for Excel. <strong>Report</strong> opens a printable issue report with pictures.
- The page refreshes itself every 2 minutes; <strong>Refresh</strong> updates it now.
- <strong>Tasks of this project:</strong> when the project is on the Projects page, the Dashboard also shows its tasks from every team's list: open, overdue and sub-tasks done.

<strong>Notifications</strong>

- <strong>Email</strong> (when the server's mail is set up): you are emailed when you are made owner of a task, when someone @mentions you in a task or a chat, and when a task you follow gets a comment or is completed; once a day you also get your tasks due tomorrow and overdue.
- <strong>Teams:</strong> if an admin has connected the project's Teams channel, it posts there when an issue is raised, assigned, changes status or due date, or gets a comment. @mentions in comments become Teams mentions.
- A weekly summary can be posted to the same Teams channel (Monday by default).
- <strong>Messenger:</strong> the project's channel shows new tasks, assignments, completions and comments, and new or changed issues, as cards.
