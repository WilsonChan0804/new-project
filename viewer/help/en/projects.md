# Projects

<strong>Order:</strong> projects are listed by status - <strong>Active</strong> first, then <strong>On hold</strong>, <strong>Completed</strong> and <strong>Archived</strong> - and by name within each. The Projects page and every project drop-down (Sheets, 3D, Board, Dashboard, Folders, Messenger) use this order. On the Projects page, click a column heading (Project, Status, Tasks, Completion, Overdue, Issues) or use <strong>Sort</strong> to order the list another way; click again to reverse it. Your choice is remembered.

The project drop-down at the top of Sheets, 3D, Board and Dashboard is two: first the <strong>project</strong> (name · code), then <strong>its model or sheet set</strong> (for example Site 1 / Site 2, or on Sheets "Revit sheets" and each uploaded PDF set).

In a project's panel, <strong>Project information</strong> keeps the project no., client, address, building type, site area, GFA, storeys, stage, start and completion dates and a description (<strong>Edit</strong>, for the project's owners and admins). Click or drop a picture at the top of the panel to give the project a <strong>picture</strong>; it also shows in the list.

The <strong>Projects</strong> page lists the team's jobs, each with its people, drawings, model, tasks, issues, files and chat in one place. It is for reading; admins set projects up on <strong>Admin &gt; Projects and members</strong> (the <strong>Manage</strong> button takes you there).

<strong>The list</strong>

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| Project | Its short name and code, e.g. SKW · HKA-P-01681-ARC |
| Open | <strong>Sheets</strong>, <strong>3D</strong> and <strong>Dashboard</strong> buttons. A project made of several models (MOS Site 1, Site 2) shows a small menu of its parts on each button |
| Tasks | Progress (done of all), overdue tasks and the open tasks, from every team's list that has a group for this project |
| Issues | A bar of the issues by status, with open / total; overdue in red. Added up over all the project's models |
| Folders | Its OneDrive, SharePoint or ACC folders |
| Chat | The project's channel in the Messenger, with unread messages |

- Search projects or people, and filter by status, at the top.
- Click a row for the <strong>details</strong>: members by team, issues by status (click a figure to open the Dashboard), the tasks with their sub-tasks, and <strong>Models and sheets</strong>: each part with its own Sheets, 3D and Dashboard links.
- <strong>One project, several models.</strong> A job exported as several Revit models and sheet sets stays one project with several parts. Its members are everyone on any part, its issue counts add up all the parts, and the project drop-downs show it as a group.
