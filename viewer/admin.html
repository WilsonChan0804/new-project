        How to get the address (2 minutes, no IT needed)
        1. In Teams, go to the project's channel, click ... (More options) next to its name, choose Workflows.
        2. Pick "Post to a channel when a webhook request is received", give it a name (e.g. LWK Viewer), click Next, check the team and channel, click Add workflow.
        3. Copy the address it shows, paste it above, Save, then Send a test.

        Auto publish which Revit file is published to which project, from which PC, and when
        
      
        Every PC reports its publish settings here - when they are
        saved in Revit (LWK tab > Auto Publish or Publish) and after every run. A row in red means the
        same model is published at night from more than one PC: the two overwrite each other, so one of them
        should be switched off (in Revit on that PC: Auto Publish > Night export on / off). The settings themselves
        are changed in Revit on the PC that publishes; "Take off the list" only removes a line that is no longer true.
        
      
        
        
        
        
        
      
        
      
        
    
        
  
        

  
        
    
        
      
        Devices how phones, tablets and computers cope with the 3D view
        
      
        Each 3D page reports once its models are in and when it is
        left. Crash means the page stopped while on screen without closing - on a phone or tablet almost
        always Safari running out of memory. Memory figures are the viewer's own estimate of what the models take on
        the graphics card. Click a row for everything that was reported.
